Chris 是 Drive Capital 的创始人，这家风投机构 2012 年从旧金山搬到了俄亥俄州的哥伦布市，专门投资硅谷以外美国城市的创业公司。他的核心判断非常反直觉：美国本身才是世界上最好的新兴市场，而硅谷风投圈把绝大部分精力砸在湾区那一小块地方，反而制造了一个巨大的投资[[真空|真空]]。

## 为什么说美国是"新兴市场"

Chris 的逻辑起点不是情怀，是一个数字对比。他说如果你把美国各州当成独立国家来看，大量州的 GDP 放在全球都是前十的水平——比如希腊的 GDP 和底特律一个城市一样，而俄亥俄、密歇根这些州的经济体量远超很多国家。但真正能拿到世界级风投服务的，连加利福尼亚州都不算，本质上就是旧金山这一两个城市 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:33" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's like you start to just unpack this and you start to, you know, I think if you start to just reframe the conversation around, you know, let's not think of it being just one America, like let's think of each of these states as if they were countries." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

传统观念说"所有聪明人都会去湾区""所有发明都出自斯坦福"，Chris 认为这经不起数据检验。密歇根大学一年的研究预算是 20 亿美元，俄亥俄州立大学是 15 亿美元，而且全美各地都在复制这个量级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:21" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And it identifies this cognitive dissonance where you have this very popularly held belief that all the invention in America has done it Stanford and MIT, and then you look at, say, the University of Michigan, and you realize that the research budget of the University of Michigan is $2 billion a year." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这些资源以前没用上，是因为技术获取本身是稀缺的——在 2006 年[[云计算|云计算]]普及之前，你要自己买服务器、在裸金属层写代码，会做这事的人全在湾区 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:22" data-who="Unknown" data-en="What we would do in 2000, think right before the iPhone. You had to build your own cloud infrastructure and you had to buy servers and rack them and stack them. And then you had to write the software code at that bare metal layer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。云计算把这个知识门槛打掉了，一根网线加一张信用卡就能拿到世界级的计算资源，所以中国和欧洲的创业生态崛起，美国其他城市也一样可以 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:01" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And it was truly a watershed moment for, I mean, really for the world, where suddenly cloud computing took what was previously this very scarce knowledge set. And democratized access to it, where suddenly, now with an internet connection and a credit card, you could access a world-class super compute infrastructure from any corner of planet Earth." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他认为 AI 是加速这一切的催化剂。[[LLM|LLM]] 的成本在暴跌，意味着住在美国经济腹地的创始人，如果懂制造业、懂医疗、懂物流，现在可以用 AI 把这些领域的痛点变成可规模化的公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="76:46" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And so the ability to be on top of an ecosystem like Georgia Tech or a University of Michigan or wherever, I think we have a very, very strong advantage in doing that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。湾区的人不懂制造业，因为那里没有制造业 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:00" data-who="Unknown" data-en="is better served to be built in other places because there are more people in Columbus that know about manufacturing than in San Francisco. Like people in the Bay Area don't understand manufacturing because there's no manufacturing that's done there." aria-label="回原文"></button>——领域知识加上新技术，才是中西部城市的真正优势。

## 从红杉学到的三件事：苦干、主题化、第一性原理

Chris 2006 年加入红杉资本，他说在那里学到的东西可以浓缩成三件。

第一是职业道德。红杉很多 GP 是移民，这是刻意为之的——Zoom 在疫情期间上市后的一周，负责的投资人 Pat Grady 做了 75 个会议 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:37" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I remember when The week after Pat Grady had Zoom go public amidst COVID, this meteoric successful investment, he did something like 75 meetings the next week. It's that kind of mentality that the firm has where it's a you're only as great as your next investment." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这家机构的信条是"你只和你的下一个投资一样伟大" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:55" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's that kind of mentality that the firm has where it's a you're only as great as your next investment. And it's driven by this understanding that This is an extraordinarily hard thing to do and to, to be successful at this challenge." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二是必须[[主题化投资|主题化投资]]，绝不能当"会议工厂"。他们每年收到超过 8000 个主动投递的项目，如果照单全收，你的日历会被随机填满——今天见网络安全公司，明天见 AI 基础设施公司，后天见社交网络公司，每个创始人都很有魅力，每份 BP 都有一张向上向右的图，但你根本没法分辨好和杰出 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:18" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You can't control who emails you next, right? So you'd fill your calendar, you'd be super busy, and you'd meet with us, you'd go from meeting with a cybersecurity company, then you'd go meet with an AI infrastructure company, then go meet with an inference company, then you go meet with a social networking company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

正确做法是锁定一个主题深扎 90 天，比如只看机器人公司，你会被迫拉远视角去看全球的上市公司、中国公司、研究阶段的项目，然后你可能会发现"这个领域最好的答案其实是买特斯拉股票"——那你就得放弃这个主题，等下一个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:19" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Is very very high and i think that like that's you know but that is one of the secrets ingredients in the secret sauce which is like by being focused and going deep for like 90 days in a theme and you know what you will what you will start to be able to do is if you're just meeting with robotics companies for example you're doing robotics robotics robotics robotics" aria-label="回原文"></button>。这个纪律的好处是，等你真的投了一家机器人公司，你能给创始人一张完整的地图：客户痛点是谁、顶级工程师在哪、哪些细分市场已经有营收 traction、后续轮谁会投 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:55" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I know you're not there yet, but your product is closest to filling these pain points. I've also talked to all these other companies in this space and I know who the top engineers are." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他认为投资组合里 90% 的价值来自投后工作，而不是选股 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:38" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's very much the opposite of that words. I think 90% of the value that accrues in our portfolio comes from the work that we do with the companies after we've invested." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第三是[[第一性原理|第一性原理]]决策。不要用"Google 是这么做的""Facebook 是那么做的"来推理，因为那些场景的要素跟你面前的完全不同 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And what I mean by that is, when you start looking at a decision, it's so easy for a group of people to start reasoning with anecdotes. You know, Facebook does it this way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。做决策时只看这个房间里的信息，找你独有的杠杆点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:18" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You've got to find the other points of leverage that you have. So we define that as first principles, like using only the information that is within this room to make this decision as the thing that's there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## "真空"是什么，怎么找

Chris 把"数据说一件事、但主流叙事说相反的事"定义为真空。比如红杉当年因为一家公司总部在旧金山以北 10 英里的佩塔卢马就直接 pass——10 英里，开车 20 分钟 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="10 miles north of San Francisco. And the example you're describing, it was a company based in Petaluma, which I'll never forget. It was about 10 miles north of San Francisco." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这就是真空：市场转变了，但认知没跟上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:08" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And as the market shifts, it created a vacuum. And that really happened around cloud computing, where we started to see more and more companies that were getting started and going public even in these other American cities." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

找真空的方法不是看新闻，是做没人在做的硬研究。比如把"俄亥俄州当成一个国家来看它的 GDP"——这个框架一旦转过来，一连串发现就出来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:46" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And, you know, suddenly it just kind of dawns on me. I was like, What is the GDP of Ohio?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 投资组合构建：不走"万亿公司"路线

这是 Chris 策略里最反硅谷的一条。

先看数据：过去 20 年，美国风投支持的公司里，退出估值超过 500 亿美元的一共只有 12 家，平均两年都不到一家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:20" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, venture-backed companies in America. For like M&amp;A and IPOs? Maybe 20?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。如果你把基金回报押在这种结果上，你本质上在赌一件从未发生过的事 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:47" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You've set the tables against yourself. At this point, you're saying, I am planning on something to happen that has never happened before. I'm not saying it won't." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他把门槛降到 20 亿美元——过去 20 年超过 300 家，差不多每周一个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="61:19" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We said, how many $2 billion outcomes have there been in the last 20 years? It's over 300 between IPOs and M&amp;A events. That's like a couple a week, I guess, or one a week." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但问题是，如果一只基金只占 1% 或 5% 的股份，20 亿的退出也回不了本。

所以他的解法是两个杠杆：一是控制基金规模，不大; 二是在硅谷以外的地方集中持股——因为那里的风投少，一笔交易不会被五六家机构分食，Drive 可以一口气拿 25% 到 30% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="62:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Our deals, our investments end up not being syndicated very often. So the result is that a founder might part with 25%, 30% to the VCs. But instead of 10% going to Sequoia, 10% going to Andreessen, 10% going to Lightspeed, we get to consolidate all that and say, we'll just buy 30%, which means we have a larger concentration in our funds, but we have way larger ownership sizes." aria-label="回原文"></button>。通过后续轮持续加码，有些公司他们最终持有高达 40% 的股份 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="63:52" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think over time, we might accrue a larger position in the portfolio. That is, in some cases, we've owned as much as 40% of an individual business by participating in multiple rounds." aria-label="回原文"></button>。40% 乘以 10 亿美元退出，一只 4 亿美元的基金就回本了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="64:01" data-who="Unknown" data-en="That is, in some cases, we've owned as much as 40% of an individual business by participating in multiple rounds. And now if you own 40% of a business and they sell for a billion dollars and your fund size is 400 million bucks," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他说这个策略"不性感"——媒体不会报道 10 亿美元的退出，他们只写万亿和千亿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="64:22" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think that strategy, that investment product, it's not sexy. And you write the article, try and get the newspaper, write the article on the billion-dollar outcome." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但产生回报和卖报纸是两件事。

还有一个附带好处：创始人只需要管理一个董事会成员、打一个电话，不用在三四个 VC 之间协调 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="63:23" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It actually makes the companies more successful because they don't have to manage a syndicate. They have to manage one board member. And they have to manage one VC." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 募资：转化率思维和"去别人不去的地方"

Drive 第一支基金募资时，联系了几千个 LP，拿到 223 次会议，转化了 19 个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:49" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, it was unpopular, for sure. And I think our conversion rate in our first set of LP outreach was something like 223 meetings. And to get 223 meetings, we had to reach out to like, I don't know, a couple thousand different LP prospects to get the 223." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Chris 说募资就是销售——建漏斗、跑漏斗、接受你的[[转化率|转化率]]，然后用转化率倒推你需要做多少工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:11" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I think one of the things you discover in fundraising is it's sales. And sales is to do it right. You've got to build a funnel." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他发现一个关键区分：成熟的 LP（投了二三十年风投的）极难撬动，因为他们的配额满了，要进就得踢一个出去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:07" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think, and this is still true to this day, is that what we've discovered at Drive is that there is a, there is a mature LP base that's been investing in venture for like, you know, 30 years, 50 years, 20 years, 10 years, whatever their number is." aria-label="回原文"></button>。相对新的 LP、还没选满管理人的，才是好目标 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:20" data-who="Unknown" data-en="That doesn't happen very often. By contrast, if you find an LP who is relatively new to venture and they haven't picked all of their managers, we have a really good shot at getting into those accounts." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他自己的差异化方式是物理出现。别人开 Zoom，他飞过去。

他算过一笔账：一个 LP 一年见 2000 个 GP，但如果这个会议在某个偏远城市、而且是创始合伙人亲自来，可能不到 50 个 GP 愿意跑这一趟——那他就从两千分之一变成了五十分之一 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Because I, you know, if you were to immediately like try and convert that very quickly, okay, so if I'm going to be that one in 2000, well, how many of those meetings do you have in person?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。再筛掉那些派 junior 来的，就变成二十五分之一 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Well, how many of those meetings in this weird place are with the founder and general partner? Oh, like only half of those. So now I've got to be one in 25." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他搬去哥伦布那天的故事很能说明心态：搬家公司已经到了，电话响了，最大的一家 LP（5000 万美元）撤资了，首次关账直接吹了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:04" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Right then my phone rings. And the phone rings and I get the news that, you know, our single largest LP commit in that 175, which was a $50 million check, had changed their mind and they were out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他一岁女儿坐在搬家箱前大哭的照片，就是他当时内心的写照 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:55" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And hung up the phone and at that very moment, for whatever reason, my daughter, who was one year old at the time, was, you know, she was throwing a fit and she's like crying, she's on the floor having a tantrum." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但他还是让搬家公司出发了——"如果我在这个时刻不坚持，以后怎么说服创始人跟我共事" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="51:17" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I had this kind of introspection to look at it and say, well, what are you gonna do? <script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
