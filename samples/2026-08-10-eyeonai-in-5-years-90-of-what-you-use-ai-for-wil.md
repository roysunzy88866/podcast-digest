## 从 USDT 到去中介化

Paolo 的逻辑起点不是 AI，而是金融。Tether 做了 12 年数字美元，攒了 5.73 亿用户，每季度涨 3000 万以上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There are plenty of digital dollars, but only USD, our digital dollar, was able to achieve the holy grail of distribution and financial inclusion and impact in the world." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的核心观察是：全世界有四五十亿人拿不到基本的银行服务，不是因为他们有问题，而是因为他们太穷，银行觉得没利润 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:00" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And it's not like they are unbanked because they are bad people. They are unbanked because they are too poor for being of interest of the banking system. They live in countries where their national currency is devaluating so fast against the US dollar." aria-label="回原文"></button>。阿根廷比索五年对美元贬了 94.5%，土耳其里拉贬了 81%，委内瑞拉玻利瓦尔贬了 99.8% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Think about Argentina. The Argentinian peso lost 94.5% of its value against the US dollar in the last five years. The Turkish lira lost 81% of its value against the US dollar in the last five years." aria-label="回原文"></button>——这些地方的人天然需要一种跟美元挂钩的东西。

Tether 的做法是：不收交易费，靠持有的美元国库券的利息赚钱 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:45" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And we are not taking money from anyone. Actually, well, we are taking money we're earning on the interest rate of the United States. That's it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他管这叫"人类历史上最大的普惠金融成功故事" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:59" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Over the last 12 years, our company built the most used digital dollar in the world with 573 million users, growing by 30 plus million users per quarter. I call it the biggest financial inclusion success story in the history of humanity." aria-label="回原文"></button>。更重要的是，他从中提炼出一个信念：**金融被过度中介化了，整个技术领域也是** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But the most important learning point of that is that not only finance is heavily intermediated, is overly intermediated. The entire technology is overly intermediated." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 互联网本该是点对点的

Paolo 把这套[[去中介化|去中介化]]逻辑推到了互联网架构层面。他的论点是这样的：互联网生来就是点对点的，你连上网会拿到一个 IP 地址，本质上就是你的"家庭地址" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You know, internet, I feel internet was born to be peer-to-peer, point-to-point. When you connect your computer to the internet or when you connect your smartphone to the internet, you get an IP address." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但后来互联网变了——所有连接都被路由到数据中心去中转 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:43" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And suddenly internet changed. And instead of let's connect people directly, became let's route their connections through data centers. Why?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。你住在罗马，给同在罗马的妈妈发一条 WhatsApp 消息，这条消息要先跑到法兰克福或爱尔兰再绕回来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:43" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="If you have a person living in Rome, like imagine like you are a person living in Rome and you're, you know, it's very likely that your family lives in Rome and you send a message, you know, I send a message, let's say I live in Rome, my family's in Rome, my mom lives in Rome." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

每一张照片、每一段视频都是如此。过去 20 年各国政府在互联网基础设施上花了巨量资金，去路由那些根本不必要中转的数据包 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Every photo, every video, everything that I send goes to Ireland from Rome, Ireland, Rome. Imagine how much all the governments spent in internet infrastructure in the last 20 years to route packets data that is completely unnecessary when internet was built to be point-to-point and peer-to-peer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为什么？因为中介方——也就是托管数据的平台——靠持有和中介人们的数据来赚钱，这是错误的激励结构 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Because also in that case, the intermediaries created the gravitational force so that being intermediaries of people's connections, i.e. people data, they could earn money hosting, holding, and intermediating people's data." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他认为 BitTorrent 早就证明了技术上的答案：点对点协议可以扩展到数亿用户和海量数据 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Well, there is, you know, in early 2000, a new protocol was born, was called BitTorrent, and was the iteration of all the file sharing protocols. And was so great it could scale to hundreds of millions of users and exabytes and exabytes and exabytes of data in the perfect way." aria-label="回原文"></button>，只是没人把这个思路从文件共享推广到更广泛的应用。

## QVAC 到底做了什么

QVAC（Quantumverse Automatic Computer）就是把这个思路搬到 AI 上。要让人在手机上跑 AI，Paolo 说需要解决两个问题：**[[推理|推理]]**（你问模型问题、它回答你）和**微调**（模型根据你的数据学习，变成"你的"AI）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:43" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="In order to have a platform that is able and capable to scale and evolve on smartphones, you want to do two things. The first one is inference. The process of inference is, you know, you ask something to a model, you get a reply." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

推理这侧，他们在 Llama CPP（开源 AI 最知名的推理引擎）之上做了大量优化，现在能扩展到几乎所有消费级 [[GPU|GPU]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:50" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so we understood that, of course, inference was already a proven task to do on local devices. But with QVAC, we took the Llama CPP, that is probably the most known inference engine for open source AI, and we built on top of it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

关键的突破来自微软的 [[BitNet|BitNet]]——一种一位量化（1-bit quantization，用极少的位数来表示模型权重，大幅降低计算量）的模型架构 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="On top of that, the other breakthrough that was done by Microsoft was called BitNet. The problem is that BitNet is a one-bit model. So usually, models have different levels of quantization." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但原始 BitNet 太依赖英伟达高端 GPU，跑不了消费设备。QVAC 团队改了 BitNet，让它能跑在任何消费级 GPU 上——三星手机上的 Snapdragon GPU、Adreno GPU、Apple GPU 都行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="...suitable because it was too much relying on NVIDIA high-end GPUs. And so we modified the first BitNet to make it adaptable to run on any consumer device, like in any consumer GPU, like the Snapdragon GPUs that you find on the Samsung phones or the Adreno GPUs or the Apple GPUs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

微调这侧，他们建了一个通用的 [[LoRa|LoRa]]（低秩适应，一种只调模型小部分权重就能定制化的小成本微调方法）微调框架 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That process is called fine-tuning. And usually the best way, the best technique to do fine tuning is LoRa, that is low rank adaptation. So basically you take an existing model and you adjust only a portion of the weights based and you fine tune them in order to be respectful and slightly adjust those weights so that they could respect and customize based on your own behavior." aria-label="回原文"></button>。不只是 BitNet 模型，他们支持数百个模型，开发者可以用同一套框架在任何消费级 GPU 上微调任何模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Second, we also created in QVAC... A common fine-tuning, LoRa fine-tuning framework, so that not only for the BitNet models, but any model that we support, and we support hundreds of models, we give to the developer the same framework to fine-tune any model on all the consumer GPUs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。实际效果：你可以在自己笔记本上让模型读你所有邮件，学会用你的语气和习惯回复，数据完全不出你的设备 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:01" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Imagine this. Imagine if you could have a model on your laptop, on your smartphone, that could read privately through all your emails and design and learn how to respond in the best Craig's way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 手机 AI 能覆盖多少需求？

Paolo 的核心主张是：大多数人根本不需要数据中心级别的 AI。95% 到 99% 的人用 AI 就是搜索、翻译、拍购物收据做月末记账、基础教育 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:42" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But the reality is that between 95% and 99% of the people in the world, they will never do that. They use AI for basic things, for search or to take a photo of a grocery list or the grocery receipt and have at the end of the month some accounting." aria-label="回原文"></button>——这些事情今天的手机模型已经能做了。

他举了个具体例子：谷歌的 MedGemma 医疗模型有 270 亿参数，被认为是最先进的；Tether 团队做了一个 40 亿参数的模型，性能超过了它 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And one of the most popular ones is the flagship, is considered a state-of-the-art model, was MedGemma from Google. MedGemma, 27 billion parameters. Our team was able to produce a 4 billion parameters model that was exceeding the performance" aria-label="回原文"></button>。40 亿参数意味着可以在高端智能手机上跑；他们还有一个 17 亿参数的版本，能在非洲平均水平的手机上跑 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And the accuracy of the 27 billion per meter model of Magema. So 4 billion model, I mean, 4 billion per meters model means that can run on a smartphone, can run on a smartphone of a good smartphone." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的预测是：2026 年手机本地模型能覆盖 50% 的普通 AI 用例，三年后到 70%，五年后到 90% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So if today, I believe that today with the QVAC, we've created a platform, an open source platform, because open source is very important, so that people don't have to trust that they can verify the platform themselves." aria-label="回原文"></button>。届时 95% 的人口能在手机上跑 90% 的 AI 用例 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:21" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And in five years, it will go to 90%. So if in five years, 95% of the population are able to run 90% of the use cases of AI on a smartphone. Then why the hell we are building tens of gigawatts of data centers when, you know, and the subscriptions to these AI models in centralized data centers cannot be justified because then the subscription could cost millions, not hundreds of dollars." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Apple 每年出新 GPU，跑 Llama 模型的速度比上一代快两倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:47" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Every year they're releasing the M3, the M4, M5 GPU of their iPhone 16, 17, and now 18. And every year these GPUs can run these Llama models. Two times faster than the previous iteration." aria-label="回原文"></button>——硬件趋势也在往这个方向走。

## 数据中心会不会过剩？

主持人提了一个很直接的反问：企业和政府的需求呢？那些需要海量算力的场景 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:32" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But while you're talking about consumer use of AI, The big transformational market is enterprise and government use. And those will likely run on data centers because the problems that they're solving, the speed at which they have to solve them, are going to require massive compute." aria-label="回原文"></button>？

Paolo 的回答分两层。第一层：ASIC（专用芯片，为特定计算任务专门设计的硬件，效率远超通用 GPU）会改变游戏规则。

比特币挖矿从 CPU 到 GPU 再到 ASIC，AI 也在走同样的路 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is a very interesting question, right? Coming from the Bitcoin world, you might know that Bitcoin mining was first running on CPUs, then moved to GPUs, and then moved to ASICs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。好的 GPU 跑 Llama 3.2 是每秒 150 个 token，AI 专用的 ASIC 能跑到每秒 17,000 个 token <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="All four AI were born so that right now, if you have a good GPU, you can run Llama 3.2 at 150 tokens per second. The ASICs for AI were able to run Llama 3.2 at 17,000 tokens per second." aria-label="回原文"></button>。五年后，一家大银行花 5 万到 10 万美元买十几块 ASIC，就能在本地跑一个极其强大的 AI 集群，能耗降 98% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:22" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I think there is a world where even if you are a bank, let's say a big bank, You could buy in five years 10, 15 powerful ASICs, spend $50,000, $100,000, but you can run an AI cluster that is extremely, extremely powerful." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二层：主权和安全。意大利的银行不会想把数据全跑在美国的基础设施上，法国也不会，公共行政部门更不会 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:33" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The more, even on enterprise, I think that they will have their own dedicated hardware. And so there will be, also because think about it, like you don't want every Italian bank, they will, sorry to make all references to Italy, but I'm Italian by birth." aria-label="回原文"></button>。所以"主权云"（直接在某国建小型数据中心、就地部署软件）已经是一个真实趋势 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:09" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="If you read... One of the coolest new product lines for data centers or cloud providers is actually the sovereign cloud. Means that you go into a country, you install, you build a small data center directly for the country, deploy there your software." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他把结论说得很直接：就算企业和政府需要一些集中式算力，那也是小众市场，花几十亿美元，可能还得政府补贴 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Those, I think medicine will have huge breakthroughs thanks to AI, but it will become a niche. What will run on centralized data centers on these behemoths will be a niche, will cost billions and billions of dollars, and will be probably subsidized by governments because that is of fundamental importance." aria-label="回原文"></button>。绝大多数人、几十亿人，会在手机上用 AI，保持自己数据的隐私 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="What will run on centralized data centers on these behemoths will be a niche, will cost billions and billions of dollars, and will be probably subsidized by governments because that is of fundamental importance." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 对当前 AI 投资潮的担忧

Paolo 对现在 AI 行业的金融工程有很深的怀疑。他指出许多大 AI 公司在大幅补贴订阅成本——收 200 美元，实际成本可能在 1,000 到 5,000 美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And it's clear that many of these big AI companies are subsidizing the cost of their subscriptions. So a $200 subscription will cost from one to $5,000. That's the reality." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为什么敢这么干？因为还是私企，可以藏着；而且需要冲用户增长来推估值——花 50 亿补贴换来 500 亿估值增长，这笔账在私企阶段算得过来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:47" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So if they have to subsidize, let's say that you are like a big AI company. That's again, not the name names. ## 本集带走

- **去中介化是 Tether 的统一主线**：从稳定币去掉金融中间商，到 QVAC 去掉云数据中心中间商，逻辑一脉相承
- **BitNet + LoRa 是 QVAC 的技术核心**：改写微软的一位量化模型让它跑在消费级 GPU 上，加通用微调框架让任何模型都能在本地个性化，数据不出设备
- **手机 AI 的覆盖速度可能超预期**：Tether 用 40 亿参数模型打败了 270 亿参数的 MedGemma，17 亿参数模型能在非洲普通手机上跑
- **ASIC 会进一步削弱数据中心优势**：AI 专用芯片跑推理的速度是通用 GPU 的 100 倍以上，能耗降 98%，企业五年内可能用十几块 ASIC 就能在本地跑强集群
- **当前 AI 订阅大量补贴、不可持续**：收 200 美元实际成本可能上千，私企阶段靠补贴冲估值，上市后成本会转嫁给散户
- **小模型互协作 vs 单体大模型**：Paolo 认为万亿参数单体模型不可持续，未来是百万个小模型各司其职、按需微调
- **机器人是本地 AI 的重要落地场景**：延迟和安全要求决定了机器人不能依赖云端决策，必须在本地有计算能力
