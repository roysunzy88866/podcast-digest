如果你关注 AI,大概率看过 AI 帮人写代码、做客服。但 [[Samsara|Samsara]] 的联合创始人兼 CEO [[Sanjit Biswas|Sanjit Biswas]] 在这集访谈中要讲的是另一个世界:[[物理 AI|物理 AI]]——也就是把 AI 应用到那些必须在现实物理环境中运转的行业,比如卡车运输、建筑工地、电网等等。Samsara 是一家市值约 200 亿美元、年经常性收入超过 20 亿美元的公司,每年处理约 25 万亿个数据点,其系统每天都在覆盖美国 99% 的道路。嘉宾把物理 AI 的核心挑战、Samsara 的产品架构(从硬件层到 AI [[智能体|智能体]]层)、以及他对人机混合与[[自动化卡车|自动化卡车]]的前瞻判断娓娓道来。

## 物理 AI 的挑战:不在数字比特里,而在泥泞的现场

为什么 AI 浪潮是从聊天机器人和代码开始,而不是从建筑工地开始?Sanjit 认为,这是因为数字世界天然拥有大量现成的、可供模型训练的比特数据;而物理世界要混乱得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:20" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So think about all the trillions of tokens that were needed to get these models bootstrapped. The physical world is much messier and there's also a hardware component to it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

物理世界的数字化没有低垂的果实。它不仅要面对不可靠的网络环境,还要把硬件设备真正安装、部署到前线,并说服数以百万计的一线工人把新技术融入日常工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:34" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="It has to be deployed to the front lines. And that requires a lot of sort of. messy work, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。虽然门槛极高,但传统行业占据了全球 GDP 的 40% 到 50% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:53" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="All that being said, it's a massive part of the global economy, right? All these industries, they make up about 40, 50% of world GDP. And so it's an area where you can have tremendous impact, but you have to really roll up your sleeves and get much more involved than kind of connecting to a large database that may have already existed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。更重要的是,这是一个极不宽容的环境,系统失灵的后果可能是真实的物理伤亡,因此「风险」既是最大的挑战,也是最大的机会 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:08" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="Can we find ways to make it less risky using data? So we see that the risk is the opportunity as opposed to the challenge. All right." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

说完了物理 AI 为什么难做,接下来我们看看 Samsara 具体是怎么靠软硬件结合来做这件事的。

## 硬件层:像「眼睛」和「耳朵」一样的传感器

Sanjit 把 Samsara 的系统分为硬件、软件/AI 和智能体(action)三个层级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:07" data-who="Matt Turk" data-en="So just to use an analogy and stop me if that doesn't seem right, but that would be the sensors, so the ears and the eyes. Then you have a software layer. I guess now is AI, which would be the brain." aria-label="回原文"></button>。在硬件层,他们制造了种类繁多的传感器去采集物理数据。

首先是[[资产标签|资产标签]](Asset Tag),它带有加速度计和工业级蓝牙,十分坚固,「你可以用卡车从它上面碾过去,它会继续运行」,电池能用好几年 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:03" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So you can beat this thing up. You can drive over it with the truck, and it'll continue to operate. That layer, it has hardware, but also has firmware that's running on it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们最近甚至推出了一款类似贴纸的一次性追踪标签,能持续约 45 天,刚好够单次物流使用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:52" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="That's the one you just launched in Vegas a few weeks ago. Exactly. So these last about 45 days or so." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

其次是[[车辆网关|车辆网关]](Vehicle Gateway)。它就像一个插在卡车上的黑匣子,通过诊断端口读取发动机的各种信号,比如油耗、故障码、甚至司机的脚是在油门还是刹车上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:32" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So it collects diagnostic information from... the engine computers, and that's everything from how much fuel is it consuming to does it have fault codes to was the driver's foot on the accelerator or the brake." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最后是核心的 [[AI 行车记录仪|AI 行车记录仪]](AI Dash Cam)。它不仅能录下向外的高清视频,还向内对准司机。借助在边缘端(即设备本地)运行的 AI 模型,它可以实时检测司机的疲劳或看手机等危险行为,并用音频警报进行干预。Sanjit 指出,这其实是一种负强化,能帮助司机打破看手机等坏习惯 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:13" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="And it happens many, many times. You tend to break the habit because it's sort of negative reinforcement. That was a huge breakthrough for us six, seven years ago as we introduced AI at the edge." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 从边缘到云端:数据的闭环与护城河

硬件采集完数据,接下来是怎么把它们组织起来,变成 AI 能消化的信号。

Samsara 的架构是「边缘+云端」混合。低延迟、关乎实时安全的计算(如防碰撞预警)放在边缘设备上做;大量的数据存储、分析则放在超大规模云数据中心 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:11" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So just massive amounts of data storage. This is all kind of sitting in modern hyperscaler cloud. So it's not that we have like one big warehouse slash data center, but, you know, pretty large system." aria-label="回原文"></button>。在云端,他们使用了大量的[[生成式 AI|生成式 AI]] 和 VLM(视觉语言模型,能同时理解画面和文字含义的模型)。比如,过去如果司机猛踩刹车,人工抽查可能会想当然认为是司机分心;但现在模型可以看懂整段视频,判断出司机其实是为了躲避突然窜出的狗,从而给司机发送「干得漂亮」的正面反馈 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:49" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="If you can watch that as a video clip, you can now say, hey, we're actually going to give the driver some positive feedback because they did a really good thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当主持人问到:在 AI 模型日趋同质化的未来,Samsara 的护城河究竟在哪里?Sanjit 的答案是:物理世界独有的运营数据 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:27" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So we're very excited about what we're seeing on that front. The operational data that I was talking about, the physical world kind of digitization side of thing is where we come in, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他强调,互联网上能抓取到的数据根本无法覆盖现实中具体的建筑工地或某条道路的突发状况。更关键的是,Samsara 形成了强大的[[数据网络效应|数据网络效应]](不同客户间的数据可以共享价值):比如他们的系统每天都在全美 99% 的道路上行驶,能收集到所有的坑洼位置。芝加哥市就利用这些数据,判断冬季后哪些路段破损最严重,应该按什么优先级去修补 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:18" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="And that is super useful for the city. So like this city of Chicago, for example, they want to know which potholes happen after the winter weather season. What order should we go after them in, in terms of severity?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

了解了底层数据与架构,接下来看 Samsara 是如何迈出「替人干活」这一步的。

## 智能体的落地:从安全的「低垂果实」做起

在最近的大会上,Samsara 发布了 [[Agents Studio|Agents Studio]]。如果说此前的 LLM(大语言模型)助理只是个能用几句话回答「哪辆车需要维修」的问答机器人,那智能体的突破在于它能在更长的时间跨度内自主规划并执行任务 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:40" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="Those tended to be single turn or maybe like a few turn interactions. Like you just go back and forth with the chatbot window on the side. The breakthrough that, of course, happened last year is these agents can operate over much longer time horizons." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但 Sanjit 坦言,在物理世界引入智能体必须非常谨慎。因此他们挑选的最初落地场景,是那些「就算搞错也不会死人」的低风险、非核心操作领域。他举了一个非常有代表性的例子:保修智能体(Warranty Agent)。

当卡车的故障码一出现,智能体会自动去查阅厚重的服务手册,核对企业与汽车制造商谈定的专属保修协议,判定这个故障在当前车龄下是否在保修范围,接着自动开列工单,并排查其他车是否有类似隐患 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:05" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So one of the demos I did on stage is we have a warranty agent. when it sees a fault code, it can basically crack the service manual, look at your specific OEM negotiated warranty agreements, and then correlate the two and say, yes, this specific issue, given the age or the number of miles that have been driven on this vehicle, is actually covered under warranty." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Sanjit 表示,这是一项过去需要耗费人工一两小时的繁琐流程,现在被压缩到了不到一分钟,而且「现实中大部分保修索赔根本没人有时间去弄」 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:17" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="The reality, by the way, is most of those warranty claims just are unfulfilled, right? Nobody has the time to go do all that work they just mentioned and do the paperwork." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他强调,纯粹的智能体推理与预设的工作流[[护栏|护栏]]必须结合。没有护栏,智能体很容易陷入死循环或走神 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:16" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="Like we have to kind of keep it on track. So it's some combination of operational context, which comes through workflow and guardrails with also this now kind of new agentic reasoning ability." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

既然 AI 已经开始逐步接管繁琐的后勤任务,下一个问题是:它会怎么看待和评价在前线干活的人?

## AI 与一线工人:对抗「老大哥」隐喻的透明度法则

当 AI 行车记录仪时刻紧盯着司机,会不会变成可怕的「老大哥」?Sanjit 认为,应对这个社会性焦虑的核心在于透明度和价值对齐 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:25" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="It's like super boring, by the way, to like sit and watch drivers, right? So when you kind of are transparent about what the system's doing and what it's not doing, and then how the data is used, that is like how you get the buy-in and you earn the trust of that entire organization." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

首先,摄像头是公开可见的,绝非偷偷录音。其次,这套系统对司机最大的价值是「洗清嫌疑」。像家得宝这样的大车队,经常遭遇虚假的碰瓷索赔。有了高清视频证据,司机终于能自证清白。Sanjit 指出,司机 90% 的时间都在做好事却无人知晓,系统提供的正面反馈打破了单向监控的对立感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:43" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="Drivers love that because I have to say 90% of the time they're doing a great job and nobody's seeing it. Right. And so that has been a huge unlock is this positive reinforcement of we're analyzing the whole drive." aria-label="回原文"></button>。引入系统后,家得宝的车险理赔数量下降了 65% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:58" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="If you have that conversation early in a transparent way, it tends to be quite constructive because I mentioned Home Depot earlier. They saw like a 65 percent reduction in their claims, like auto claims." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

主持人追问:无论如何,AI 正在成为人类工作质量的判官。Sanjit 回应称,最终的落脚点还是回归到「安全」,即确保每个人都能像来上班时一样平平安安地回家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:24" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="So in other words, like we want everyone to go home the same way they came to work, right? That is an important concept that I think people don't think about. If you're working construction, you're working oil field services or something like that, you take a lot of risk when you do your job every day." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

聊完了眼下司机与 AI 的协作,我们不妨抬头看看,当彻底没有人的自动化机器涌入物理世界,未来会怎样?

## 未来判断:机器人的分工与自动化卡车的现实

展望未来五到十年,Sanjit 认为我们会看到更多机器人涌入物理世界,但这是一种「混合模式」。他预测,在未来五年内,建筑工地或道路铺设中那些高度重复的工作(例如在第三班次即深夜到清晨去平整场地),完全可以交给机器人去做 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:08" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="Right. We see robots being able to do that in the next five years. Now, all the rest of it, though, it's still pretty messy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而人类依然不可或缺,因为现场充满了不可预知的突发状况,需要人类的经验来判断「我在等建材的时候还能先干点什么别的」。

关于万众瞩目的自动驾驶卡车,他显得比外界炒作的要冷静得多。他认为,Robotaxi([[自动驾驶出租车|自动驾驶出租车]])的普及会快得多,因为城市内的交通场景高度相似;但卡车运输的难点在于它不仅是点对点的长途干线运输,更多是混乱的长尾场景——比如 HVAC 技工、电工去不同社区干活,或者定制化的水泥搅拌车。这些设备的特殊性和非标场景,意味着自动驾驶卡车可能需要 10 到 20 年才能完全渗透扩散 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:05" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="It's like the really messy long tail. So for that reason, we think the adoption might be a bit slower, but it's not like a no, it's just, it might take 10, 20 years." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

回到宏观现实,数据中心和 AI 的狂飙突进,反而倒逼了传统基建的爆发。Sanjit 分享了他上周在一家大型能源公用事业公司听到的震撼数据:过去 125 年建成的电网容量,将在未来 5 年内翻两番(其中 90% 的增量需求来自数据中心) <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:40" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="They said, you know, over the last 125 years, we built a certain amount of grid capacity in megawatts or gigawatts, really. In the next five years, we're going to triple that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这种历史级的基建缺口,引发了全美电工等技能工人的严重短缺。连 Meta 都在推出计划,培训更多人成为电工 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:10" data-who="Sanjit Biswas" data-en="now. So you see companies like Meta doing initiatives to reskill people, train them on how to become a good electrician. And then how can you take the people who are trained and make their jobs as efficient as possible?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

1. **物理 AI 没有捷径**:它必须仰仗艰脏累的硬件部署和实地变更管理,你无法在网上爬取到某条特定泥路今天下雨后的真实数据;但这恰恰构成了拥有庞大实体车队的公司的数据护城河。
2. **智能体落地要分先后**:Samsara 把智能体首先应用在了「非致命」的后勤与流程上(如保修审批),在智能体推理与预设规则之间建立平衡,而非让 AI 裸奔。
3. **AI 热潮的真实底色是传统基建**:AI 需要算力,算力需要电网,电网需要电工。未来五年要新建超越过去百年总和的电网容量,这意味着「重操旧业」(如成为电工、卡车司机)正迎来新一轮的时代红利。

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);

    });
  }
