## 预算正在从企业软件流向哪里

企业客户的预算是有限的。当公司意识到智能体（agent，能自主执行多步骤任务的 AI 程序）可以接管原来靠企业软件走的工作流时，钱就不再花在 SaaS 订阅上了。

同一天，Workday 跌 10%，Salesforce 跌 9%，ServiceNow 跌 8%，Adobe 跌 6%——全是企业软件公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:11" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I was just looking while you were talking and on the same day. Today, as IBM crashed, unless my agent is hallucinating, Workday slid down 10%, Salesforce 9%, ServiceNow 8%, Adobe 6%." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而涨的是芯片供应商，因为大家在抢硬件，怕将来被 AI 推理的价格上涨挤出市场 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:34" data-who="Unknown" data-en="What did gain was... The NVIDIA Intel chip providers rising as people, again, panic by some of these hardware solutions. And there is a lot of, I think, distraction related to the cybersecurity side of this as well, which is partially related to the hardware, but more related to maybe some of this distraction around mythos and cybersecurity and how agents violate cybersecurity." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

另一层压力来自地缘政治。美国对高端 AI 模型实施出口管制，中国则开始意识到自己[[开源模型|开源模型]]的廉价优势，也在考虑设限 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:29" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It kind of skimmed by. Pretty quietly, I thought, and that was China is now recognizing the value it has in its open models, which are much cheaper to use than these expensive proprietary models in the US." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这意味着企业原来"用不起美国专有模型就换中国开源模型"的备用策略，两条路都在收窄 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:40" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think it's creating, to your point, a certain level of panic. In executive level companies where they're trying to figure out how to mitigate the risk both in terms of the proprietary US models, the expense associated with that, but the fallback strategy of going to Chinese models if you're in an industry that allows for that is now closing off potentially as well." aria-label="回原文"></button>。结果就是高管层恐慌性重新分配资本，牺牲掉历史上被视为关键运营的企业软件支出 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:07" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And so that is putting a lot of pressure from multiple sides on companies on what they're going to do. And so they are definitely funneling internal budget to try to mitigate that risk at the expense of a lot of other things that have historically been kind of keystone operations in their organizations." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 智能体不是五年后的故事

这听起来像远景，但已经有企业在跑了。Daniel 提到他公司在过去几周接触到的客户中，一家已经有 70,000 个智能体在运行，另一家有 6,000 个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:06" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And just to validate your point, like even in the last couple of weeks, we've talked to one One company that already has 70,000 agents running, one that has, I think it was like 6,000 or something." aria-label="回原文"></button>。很多人还停留在跟 Claude Code 一对一交互的阶段，觉得成千上万个智能体跑在企业基础设施里很遥远——但这个速度比想象中快得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:24" data-who="Unknown" data-en="What companies are... So I think there are many people out there that are maybe still on a one-to-one basis with their Claude Code or with their Hermes agent or whatever it might be." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

经济账也变了。智能体从你笔记本上的"个人助理"变成云端长期运行的进程——跑几小时、几天甚至几周 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:42" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Folks, but as we transition from agents being like my personal assistant on my laptop, which I interact with back and forth, which obviously limits the speed at which and the amount of context that agent can consume." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这时候如果用按需付费的 API 调用大模型，成本跟自托管一个小模型完全不是一个量级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:53" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Hours and days and weeks maybe, right? Then all of a sudden the economics do change around like, hey, if I'm using a pay-as-I-go API endpoint, that's going to be a vastly different economics than a small self-hosted model, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。这就是为什么[[微调|微调]]（fine-tuning，用自有数据针对性调整模型）这个话题又回来了：之前大家觉得模型够好不需要微调，现在被两头挤压，又开始认真考虑自己训练或微调小模型来降低推理成本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:23" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But also I'm starting to hear about the recognition that there may be a need, especially as models are now getting, you know, we're getting smaller models that are used for a lot of specific purposes and edge cases to actually go and do training of models or getting back to fine tuning." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 企业软件公司该怎么办

两条路开始浮现。

第一条是暴露接口，让外部智能体能调你的数据和功能。比如通过 [[MCP|MCP]]（一种让 AI 智能体连接外部工具的标准协议）把自家的数据平台和功能开放出去，价值假设是"我的数据组织方式本身就有壁垒" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="like how do you lead your company ahead into this world and what what becomes important what strategy is is important as you move into this world i've seen some take the approach of hey well you know like a net suite or something like that they're saying to some degree saying well we're gonna" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二条是自己建一套专有智能体，不接入通用的智能体生态，把能力封闭在自己体系内 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:11" data-who="Unknown" data-en="provide a connector into the AI world right which is often MCP right so their value is maybe they're assuming their value is in their data platform how they organize data the the functionality that you're that they provide but surfacing that in an agentic way so that's you know through the MCP side so that's one take that you have you have others then" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但 Chris 指出，更根本的变化在于交互方式本身：未来跨系统的绝大多数交互将是[[智能体对智能体|智能体对智能体]]的，不经过任何人类操作的图形界面 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:14" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And then as we got into the cloud era, and then even the beginning of the AI era, as we've gone to, you know, app interfaces where we've interacted, but going as we look at agents doing all these different things." aria-label="回原文"></button>。没有人会去点你的网页后台。

企业软件公司必须思考：如何在没有人类进 GUI 的情况下，通过权限设置和 MCP 配置让智能体完成业务交易 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:01" data-who="Unknown" data-en="How do I move from where I'm at right now into a world in which agentics are incorporated from a business transaction consideration? It's going to happen without anyone going into your GUI." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这本身就是一个新的行业机会 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And that's quite complex. And I think there's a whole industry right there of how do you manage agentics at massive scale with resources and MCPs. And so like if you're an entrepreneur out there and you haven't" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 人的位置在哪

Chris 强调，试图守住旧角色不是好策略——他引用 George Lucas 的说法，这就像汽车时代还在坚持马车 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:24" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I saw a quote earlier today from George Lucas saying, if you're not really on top of This with agentics, you're kind of holding on to the heart, the heart, the cart, the cart and horse in a day where the automobile is happening." aria-label="回原文"></button>。正确的做法是认清智能体真正擅长什么，然后找到人能插入的位置 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:45" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think that still happens a lot because I get into all these conversations, especially with older people closer to my age that are seeing the world change out from under them very quickly." aria-label="回原文"></button>。变化的速度在指数级加速，比一年前快得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:27" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, the world's changing really faster than it ever has right now. It's speeding up much faster than it was a year ago. And so it's a good moment for people to self-reflect a bit." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Anthropic 最近发了一篇论文，讨论模型内部形成的"工作空间"——功能上类似人脑的工作记忆 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:20" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So there may be more on that in the future. Yeah, I think, you know, one of the things in the paper that you're referencing that was pointed out was that Anthropic had noticed that models are creating what they're calling workspaces where they're essentially, the notion of the workspace is fulfilling the same function as working memory in a human brain." aria-label="回原文"></button>。不管你怎么定义意识，模型在路由和报告信息方面的功能能力在提升，而这正是智能体转型的关键能力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:01" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I think one of the points of the paper, if I'm understanding it right, whatever your take on sort of consciousness and cognition, is that there is this functional capability of these models and improving functional capability of these models to route and report information." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

> 【背景】Anthropic 这篇论文全名为"Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models"，探讨语言模型内部是否存在类似全局工作空间的结构。

## 本集带走

- **企业软件正在被智能体直接替代，不只是"被 AI 影响"**：预算从 SaaS 订阅转向硬件采购和自建智能体基础设施，IBM、Workday、Salesforce 同日暴跌是同一个逻辑。
- **微调回来了**：两头挤压（美国模型贵、中国模型可能被限）让企业重新考虑用小模型 + 微调来降低长期推理成本，而不是直接调 API。
- **智能体对智能体的交互是下一个范式**：企业软件公司如果还在优化人类点的 GUI，就已经偏了。要想的是怎么通过 MCP 等协议让智能体直接完成业务流程。
- **这不是五年后**：已经有企业跑了 70,000 个智能体。还在一对一用 Claude Code 的人，对这个规模没有感知，但市场已经在定价这个未来。
- **别守旧角色**：变化速度指数级加速，正确的策略是认清智能体擅长的部分，找到人能插入的新位置，而不是抱着旧岗位职责不放。
