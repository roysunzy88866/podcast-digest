# AI解数学题≠理解数学

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Daniel Litt</b> · 多伦多大学数学教授 · 2026-09-02</div>

这一集是 a16z 基础设施合伙人 [[Lisha Lee|Lisha Lee]] 和多伦多大学数学教授 [[Daniel Litt|Daniel Litt]] 聊 AI 在数学领域的真实能力。Daniel 是一位活跃的研究者，一直在公开追踪并评论 AI 数学能力的演变。

Daniel 目前最喜欢的一个完全由 AI 自主完成的结果，是五月中旬宣布的 [[Erdos 单位距离问题|Erdos 单位距离问题]]的解决方案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:34" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So it's quite possible that if you talk to a different mathematician, you'll get, like, different answers here. So my favorite, like, fully autonomous result by an AI so far is the solution to the Erdos unit distance problem, which I think was announced in mid-May." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这个结果有意思的地方在于，它不是简单的"最后一公里"——即人类做了大量深活、AI 踢最后一步——而是从一个意想不到的领域引入了已有的分类思想，证明了该领域普遍相信为真的猜想实际上是假的，随后人类数学家还拿这些想法找到了其他开放问题的反例 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:10" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So I think some of the results we've seen have had kind of the flavor if you kind of take some known techniques and apply them in maybe a clever way or you know they've kind of been some kind of results I would characterize as like last mile like where some recent work was done, quite deep work done by a group of human mathematicians and then the AI kind of took the final step." aria-label="回原文"></button>。不过 Daniel 强调，回头看这些 AI 产出的证明，它们的[[推理|推理]]过程看起来非常像人类数学家，并没有什么"非人"的奇招 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:42" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Yeah, okay, so first of all, I mean, you kind of characterized it as, like, inhuman in some way. I actually think the argument was very human. Ah, oh, I'd love to go for that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 模型到底在做什么

Daniel 把目前的能力边界分得很清楚。模型非常擅长两件事：一是应用所有已知技术，二是做大规模的并行计算——比如让它同时跑十个子智能体处理一千个例子 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:47" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="I haven't, you know, you can see even the best results the models are producing, you don't see that much of this kind of reasoning. It's more like they're very, very good at applying some known techniques, which is to be clear, that's like a very powerful thing to do to be very good at applying like all known techniques." aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 和 [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 的模型在他看来能力相当，[[ChatGPT|ChatGPT]] 只是更早变强，[[Claude|Claude]] 大概在 Opus 4.5 或 4.6 左右追上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:33" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Pretty sure I have it, yeah. One thing is that ChatGPT got better at math earlier. So for a long time, the Claude models were just not useful for research math." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但模型在几个关键维度上明显偏弱。一是[[直觉|直觉]]。

数学里很多猜想嵌在巨大的理论框架里，有大量证据支持它为真，不是靠找一个反例构造就能推翻的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:36" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="But in general, like, these conjectures fit into some very broad theoretical framework, which means that, look, we actually have a lot of evidence that they're true." aria-label="回原文"></button>。二是[[理论构建|理论构建]]。

Daniel 试过让模型做理论构建，给一些提示后能做出有趣的东西，但完全自主做不到 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:31" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So like I've tried to get both Fable and ChatGPT 5.6 Sol, I guess, to do some kind of theory building. And it's like they're not, they definitely are not good at it autonomously, at least with like whatever scaffolding I've set up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。三是提出正确的问题。很多时候找到猜想本身比证明它难得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:38" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="And this, like, doesn't really capture in a lot of ways what we don't know. Like, often finding the conjecture is, like, really, really hard. So, I don't know, a good example of this is, like, the Bertrand Swinerton Dyer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Daniel 讲了一个自己的亲身经历。他在一个项目里想证明某个引理，所有前沿模型都做不了。

他自己做了大量例子后，找到了一个更好的表述，然后模型很快就能证明这个更强的版本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:03" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="And so I like worked with, actually this was with Gemini DeepThink, which at the time was also on the frontier, no longer, where I kind of worked with it to improve the lemmas." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但如果你把原来那个引理直接扔给 ChatGPT，它会吐出十页毫无洞见的暴力计算 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:33" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So, like, our inability to prove it, like, led to an improvement in the result. Okay, now you can take the original lemma I had that the models weren't able to prove, put it into ChatGPT 5.6 Pro, and it will output, like, the worst proof you've ever seen, like, 10 pages of, like, just brutal calculations with no insight whatsoever." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

那种证明虽然"正确"，但不会带来任何理解上的发现。Daniel 承认自己其实知道那种暴力证法行得通，只是不愿意去做——恰恰是因为不愿意做丑陋的证明，反而逼出了更好的结果 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:06" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So, like, we found a better proof because we couldn't do, I mean, okay, I say we couldn't do the calculation, like, what actually happened, and here I understand that I'm being hypocritical about my complaints about ugly proofs before." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## "老虎机论文"与激励机制的危险

Daniel 对当前学术激励机制发出了很直接的警告。现在一个博士后如果想找工作，最快的方式是让模型去"拉老虎机"——比如让 [[Codex|Codex]] 上网找五个代数几何里的最新猜想然后去证明。

他自己跑过这个实验，一小时就得到了三篇正确但质量很差的论文 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:42" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So you don't even have to pick the theorem in advance. So here's an experiment you can do. You can take Codex." aria-label="回原文"></button>。预印本上已经出现了同一定理、同一证明、三四篇论文在几天内同时出现的情况——显然是有人在玩老虎机 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:36" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Like, there's no development of human capital or understanding. Like, sometimes, you know, we've seen examples where, like, three or four or five papers with the exact same proof of the exact same theorem have come out within a couple days of each other, which is clearly, you know, some situation where someone's playing the slot machine." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更深层的问题是，这可能导致数学探索的多样性崩塌。数学的很多进展来自于一千个不同的人追求各自的好奇心，知识边界在高维空间里以相对均匀的方式扩展，然后突然某个新想法被引入，级联式地解决一堆老问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:37" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Like, why limit yourself? For this audience, like, what is ugly? Because I have an intuition of what's ugly, but like, what is that for kind of spelling out?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

如果数学探索从属于模型想追求的东西，你得到的可能不是一个有一百万种不同直觉的数学家群体，而是一个被复制了一千次的同一个数学家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:25" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="And then like opportunistically, you suddenly get some applications or like answers to old questions we found. And it's like not clear to me that if, you know, we kind of subordinate mathematical exploration to what the model want to pursue, like if what you're getting is like one mathematician duplicated a thousand times or like actually, you know, a million different mathematicians doing a million different things." aria-label="回原文"></button>。模型的直觉从哪来？目前还是来自人类数学家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:44" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="I mean, one, where are they developing their intuitions? It's from practicing mathematicians. It's not clear where that other emergent, stronger, diverse intuition might be coming from." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Daniel 的核心论点是：数学的目标不是产出论文，而是产生理解。如果理解只存在模型权重里，对他来说"相当不令人满意" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:48" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Thank you for joining us. That said, the goal of mathematics is not to produce mathematics papers. It's to produce some kind of understanding." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

维持一个有广泛兴趣、有能力在前沿有意义地参与的人类数学家群体，需要庞大的基础设施和人才管道 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:17" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="And that requires a pretty substantial apparatus. It's like simply not the case that you can like study the questions I think are fundamental unless you've invested a huge amount of time and effort kind of getting to the point where you can meaningfully do so." aria-label="回原文"></button>。如果激励机制不改变，这条管道就会断裂。

## 为什么模型只能产出"短巧"证明

最近一些引人注目的 AI 证明都相对简短。Daniel 的判断很直接：不是因为短证明更好，而是因为模型目前还做不到长的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:47" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="I think my sense is that The reason they're not producing long, complicated proofs is that they cannot. Just like the ability to check correctness is not yet there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

让它生成长证明，它可能自己都不知道哪里错了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:20" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="So, like, they're much more reliable than they were six months ago for sure, but, like, it's still, you know, they will still sometimes produce things that are just wrong and they know they're wrong." aria-label="回原文"></button>。有人贴过一篇 800 页的 AI 生成证明，Daniel 说那不可能正确——当前模型的能力范围是校准过的，而且肯定没有人类读过、模型也检查不了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="54:07" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="And we do actually see very long generated proofs on the archive. So for example, someone recently posted a claimed proof of resolution of singularities and positive characteristic, which was 800." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

人类检查长论文的方式不是逐行看，而是理解论证的全局结构，做各种压力测试——比如"这个论证会不会推出某个我知道是假的东西"。这种"模糊单元测试"式的宏观检查，模型目前也做不好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:05" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Like, you know, you can't reliably check it line by line. What you would try to do to understand it is you try to, like, understand the overall global structure of the argument and you, like, stress test it in various ways." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **模型强在应用已知技术，弱在创造新理论**：它能从其他领域搬来你没读过的技术，但在需要发展全新理论框架的问题上几乎无能为力。
- **"丑证明"有时是逼出好理解的起点**：Daniel 的案例说明，模型直接给的那个暴力证法虽然正确但无洞见；恰恰是模型做不了、人也不愿意做的丑证明，逼出了更好的概念性解释。
- **警惕"老虎机论文"摧毁激励机制**：用模型批量生产正确但无理解的论文，既不发展人力资本，也可能让数学前沿的多样性坍缩成一个模型的副本。
- **短证明不是审美选择，是能力限制**：模型目前产不出可靠的长证明，因为自身还缺乏对长论证进行全局一致性检查的能力。
- **用 AI 要"上升"而非"让渡"**：用模型加深自己的理解，而不是把思考外包给模型——后者太容易了，而模型其实并不能真正思考 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:35" data-who="Daniel Litt" data-en="Use the AI models in a way that doesn't Basically, you need to be ascending and using the models to deepen your understanding. And it's so easy for human nature just to be lazy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
