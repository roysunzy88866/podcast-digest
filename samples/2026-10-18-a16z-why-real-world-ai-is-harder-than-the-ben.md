# 从弦理论到帮人租房：一位物理学家眼中的AI落地有多难

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Mario Martone</b> · Elise AI 应用研究负责人 · 2026-10-18</div>

[[Mario Martone|Mario Martone]] 花了 15 年研究黑洞和弦理论，如今是 [[Elise AI|Elise AI]] 的应用研究负责人。这家公司做的是住房和医疗行业的对话式AI。

让他意外的是，现在面对的问题，可能比研究宇宙还复杂。

## 为什么90%的准确率在论文里好看，在现实里是灾难？

学术界谈[[可靠性|可靠性]]，常说做到90%就够了。但 Elise AI 每月处理约 500 万通电话，一年约 6000 万通。

90% 意味着海量的失败，而每一通电话背后都是某个人的住房或医疗问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:42" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's kind of funny because like, you know, I still read some papers from academia and there you see really the difference. Like when they're talking about reliability, they talk, okay, if the LLM can take it up to 90% and then we say 90%, that means for like 60 million phone calls per month, per year, it means like an enormous amount of failures that is totally unbearable." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

「我们会被投诉淹没，还有对人们生活的影响。」所以这里的标准是：每多一个9，就多爬一座山 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:32" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It has to be 99.999. And they joke that every nine that you add is another hill to climb. That's harder than that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Elise AI 的做法是把顶尖学术人才和顶尖工程师混在一个团队里，用科学方法去评估系统——每天数百万次对话里AI做的决定，能不能被系统地理解其影响。

小规模测试时，五六段对话看起来都不错；到百万级规模，不同方法的差距才真正显现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="If you just like try it at small scale, like five or six conversations, all look quite good. But at scale, when you get to the millions, then you see that different methods diverge significantly." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 为什么不是所有问题都该扔给大模型？

很多新入行的人只有一把锤子：大语言模型。

Mario 承认这些模型看起来很聪明，但深挖下去，有些场景并不合适——比如需要确定性的场景（同样的输入必须得到同样的输出），大模型会失败；

需要精确概率的场景，大模型也很难做好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But then when you kind of like dig a little bit deeper in the surface, like you see that some of the crap is like not really good. And there are some problems like, for example, having, as you mentioned, determinism is not there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的团队每天要做的决定是：这个问题该扔给大模型、深度神经网络，还是一个非常简单的[[传统机器学习|传统机器学习]]模型？

比如预测租户续约风险，Elise AI 用的是自建的精密神经网络，跟大模型毫无关系——因为他们有海量数据，传统方法反而是更好的答案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:09" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so we have really a full spectrum of tools that we're using and we're using on full force. So for example, the predictions of renewals that we're using really elaborate kind of neural network that we built in the house, but they have nothing to do with LLMs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

判断标准有两条：一，什么方案影响最大；二，什么方案能以最快速度逼近最优结果 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:55" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I need to kind of like look at a problem, try to understand two things. Like one, what's the one the most impactful? And then two, because we also have to ship at a very fast pace, okay, what's the solutions that can really get us almost as close as possible to the optimal outcome, but in the fastest way possible?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 语音AI难在哪：一秒半之内干完几十件事

Elise AI 做语音做了3年，起步时技术根本不成熟。语音的难点在于，一秒半之内要完成几十次运算，不能有奇怪的[[延迟|延迟]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But voice is extremely difficult. Like you're talking about doing tens, not hundreds of operations in a span of like a second and a half. And you're also touching upon another thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

好消息是，整套语音技术栈是他们完全自建的，每个环节都清楚瓶颈在哪。

市场上出现新的语音转文字、文字转语音模型，或者[[开源模型|开源模型]]可以[[微调|微调]]了，他们就去匹配：

哪里最差、新方案能补哪里，就全力投入 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So you have a gist of possible solutions and you try to match them with the current stack. Basically, the idea is that, okay, if we obviously have something that is really bad on our current stock and it is a new solutions that you can apply, then you go in full force." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他们还搭了一条实验闭环：有人在 Slack 上说看到某个新模型，不用看公开的基准测试，直接在内部基准上跑一遍，看真实表现。

「那些外部基准你是不能信任的。」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:45" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The thing we do is like, okay, let's set it up. Let's run an experiment internally so we can actually see a performance of every model on our own internal benchmarks versus the other ones that you cannot trust." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 租房可以全自动，修水管还差一步

两个已经全自动化的完整流程：

一是租房。

从你在网站上看到心仪的公寓，到预约看房、AI 发给你门锁密码、你自己开门进屋、AI 给你讲解房子——全程不需要人介入，可以一路走到搬进去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:39" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="For example, the two main loops that we've automated, one is leasing people that are interested in apartments. They basically can do the entirety of starting from looking at the website and deciding that they are interested in apartments to moving in." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

二是租户报修。AI 能判断问题是否需要立刻处理，自动建工单、找供应商、打电话——最后一步仍需要一个真人上门拧螺丝。

这一环为什么不能自动化？因为需要机器人，大模型干不了体力活。Mario 猜也许一两年后能补上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There, there's still a gap that we've not been able to automate because we need robotics to fully automate that because, you know, some of the fixes require actual human labor and so LLMs can't really do that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

公司现在只有几百人，却每天自动化处理 100 万通电话和几十万封邮件短信。

难点在于：10 个人的团队如何每天搞清楚这些无人监督的对话质量如何 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But on the other hand, you need to really build some evaluations so that you know, like we have tons of conversations that go on without our inputs. And these impact, again, these are housing and healthcare." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 下一仗：让保障性住房更便宜

医疗已占美国GDP的40%，够忙一阵子了。Elise AI 的下一个大押注是降低保障性住房的运营成本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So we want to be driven by what is next big bet based on what our clients say. So one of the things, for example, that we are now facing very pretty heavily is to really cut down the costs of running affordable housing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这个领域在 Mario 看来几乎完全没有技术投入，基本不自动化，但影响最大——它关系到最脆弱的那部分人群。

如果能把成本显著降下来，租客的生活会立刻改善。

## 本集带走

- 学术论文里90%的准确率，放大到每月500万通电话就是灾难；现实产品对可靠性的要求是每多一个9都要拼一次。
- 大语言模型不是万能锤子：需要确定性或精确概率的场景，传统机器学习往往更好。选对工具本身就是核心工作。
- 自建全栈的好处是你确切知道瓶颈在哪；评估新模型别信公开基准，用自己的数据内部跑实验。
- 租房流程已经可以端到端全自动（含自助看房），报修只剩最后一步真人上门。
- 保障性住房是下一个大押注：几乎零技术渗透，但对社会最脆弱人群影响最直接。
