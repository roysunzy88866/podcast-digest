[[Stripe|Stripe]] 内部的编码[[智能体|智能体]]最近一周生成了 7000 个拉取请求。很多公司拿到这种智能体效率后的反应是裁人、缩运营支出——Stripe 选了完全相反的方向：用同样的人构建更多产品 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:35" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="It could be that we just have many fewer engineers and maybe that's what the world would look like. ## 不是「用更少的人做同样的事」，是「做更多事」

Will 把这比作杰文斯悖论（Jayvon's paradox）：当一项资产的生产力大幅提升时，你不会想要更少，你会想要更多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:27" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="They're having to do less, you know, manual work. And so, you know, it's almost trite in tech circles now, but the Jayvon's paradox, you know, an asset becomes more productive." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们内部的知识 AI 工具 CHI，六个月前由两人建成，现在 83% 的周活跃率、约 60% 的日活跃率，销售人员生产力提升了 20%。结论不是「我们需要更少的销售」，而是「销售回本变得更好了，我们需要更多销售」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:41" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And the main thing I actually think about on this front is making our people more productive. We have a tool internally called CHI, which is our knowledge AI tool." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

背后的逻辑是：一方面，能做的事变多了——四年前你造不出 Suno，造不出 Higgs field，现在可以了；另一方面，做这些事的成本因为智能体编码而大幅下降。两者叠加，新软件创建正在爆发 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:40" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="So what has changed? Well, I think two things. One is giving rise to so much opportunity for new business creation." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Stripe 的新 SaaS 平台客户群，2026 年比 2025 年大了 103% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:15" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="Our... New SaaS platform cohort is 103% larger than in 2026 than it was in 2025. So people are just saying, well, there's so many spaces that need a SaaS platform." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Stripe Minions：一次性生成，不迭代

Stripe 内部有个叫 [[Stripe Minions|Stripe Minions]] 的编码智能体系统，核心指标是：有多少 PR、多大比例的 PR 是由 Minions 创建的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:40" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And of course, we've got tons of developer tooling to help users iterate with agents. But Minions, we think of as one of our most important metrics internally is how many PRs and what percentage of our PRs are created by Minions." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Minions 的特点是「一次性」——你给它一个提示词，它去构建，跑完 CI/CD 和所有测试，你来审查。

不进入规划模式，不迭代。你说「这就是我想要的，去做吧」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:51" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="But Minions, we think of as one of our most important metrics internally is how many PRs and what percentage of our PRs are created by Minions. And the reason for that is that Minions are one shot." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

构建 [[Stripe Projects|Stripe Projects]] 的那位资深工程师 Alexander，现在一个人编排 16 个智能体，速度比以前快得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:17" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="But you just don't need that anymore. I was talking to one of our most senior engineers recently, in fact, the guy who built Stripe Projects, because Stripe Projects was..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Will 把这种方式比作注塑成型：你创建模具和模板（每个代码仓库里的智能体文件和 Markdown 文件非常关键），然后把大量工作交给智能体去填充，人类时间主要花在代码审查上——而智能体做代码审查甚至比以前的人类做得更好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:52" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And it's because... The direction that we're finding from an agentic engineering standpoint is kind of akin to injection molding. It's a bit reductionist, but when you look at sort of the growth in consumer packaged goods and toys and things like that, a lot of it just exploded post-war." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 组织怎么做：更扁、更宽、更像创始人的平台

Stripe 从亚马逊学 OKR 的变体，从微软学销售组织，从苹果学 DRI 文化，从 Alan Mulally 学日常经营机制。但到了今天，「已经没有人可以模仿了」——当单个工程师能做两年前两个团队做的事，组织该怎么变 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:07" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And it's interesting. I think a lot about institutional progress. And we study the great companies that have come before us and now are beside us." aria-label="回原文"></button>？

答案是让 Stripe 成为一个「面向内部创始人的平台」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:13" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And there's just no one to copy. What do you do when a single engineer can do what two teams of engineers could do two years ago? And sort of the theory of mind right now is that Stripe needs to become, more than it ever has been before, a platform for founders internally." aria-label="回原文"></button>。具体做法：Flutter（让团队更宽、管理层级更少），让工程师同时担当工程师、产品经理、设计师的角色，并且自己就能做前端开发和前端设计 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:22" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="Yeah. So engineers have always been, you know, Some of the most important product leaders at Stripe." aria-label="回原文"></button>。团队大小没有固定标准，但趋势是：八人的直线管理团队做着三倍的事，管理者与独立贡献者比例可能不变，但产出翻了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:00" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="Not really. I mean, we still have, I mean, you kind of want... In a Flutter org, you both want wider teams, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Will 认为世界上的软件工程师会变多，不是变少。Stripe 对应届生和早期工程师的定位变成了孵化器：进来找一个高[[主观能动性|主观能动性]]的项目，公司让路、铺路，让你加速 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:41" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And so Stripe then becomes for... New grads and early career engineers, more of an incubator of come in, figure out a high agency project that can help our users." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

比如支出管理本来在两年后的路线图上，结果一个工程师自己接手做起来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:07" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="But adjacencies sort of compound what we're doing today. So spend management is adjacent to treasury, and that might have been road mapped for two years from now." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Stripe Tax 的全球申报功能，复杂度远高于美国申报，但只用了美国申报三分之一的时间就建成了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:15" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And it took us a long time to get to US filing on Stripe tax, meaning that every state in the US will just automatically file for you. It's hard. It's a hard problem because you are dealing with so many different jurisdictions that accept filing in different ways." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 智能体商务：还缺原语，但方向清晰

智能体在互联网上做商务，目前还没有经历「寒武纪爆发」——没有大量反复出现的典型用例 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:21" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And how do you sort of view a framework for how that could be done in the future? So we talked about the Opus 4.5 Cambrian explosion moment. We haven't had that in agentic commerce today." aria-label="回原文"></button>。原因之一是缺少原语（primitives）。

Stripe 做了 [[Tempo|Tempo]] 机器支付协议：服务方可以返回 402 响应，告诉机器「这是购买我的方式」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:39" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And I think there's a few reasons for that. One is we're missing primitives. And that's something we're very focused on." aria-label="回原文"></button>。最近还推出了 Link agent wallet，智能体可以直接收集 Link 凭证去使用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:34" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="Okay, so Stripe Link, like you have like 400 million users on Stripe Link and ShopPay probably has some comparatively large number and you can use those and just automate the whole process." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Will 认为最令人兴奋的领域其实是 B2B：智能体去「采用」服务——比如一个智能体直接帮你配置好 Vercel 托管，不需要你去 Vercel.com 做任何操作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:49" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="The... I think one of the places we are the most excited about agentic commerce actually is B2B. So we launched Stripe Projects." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这甚至影响了投资判断：假设智能体是未来的购物者，那「这是智能体会想选的那个吗？」成了一个很好的筛选框架 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:18" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="So you can go, an agent can go adopt Vercel for hosting and they can do it without you needing to go to Vercel.com and do anything. This has actually informed our investment thesis in a few investments recently." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

结账页面最终会消失——不只对智能体，对人类也是。有了 Stripe Link 和 Shop Pay，你完全可以在产品展示页直接完成购买 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:51" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="I think there's some very non-speculative ways in which it'll be better. Like just checkout pages shouldn't exist for humans, you know, with Stripe link and, you know, shop with shop pay." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 微交易与稳定币：智能体经济的支付基建

智能体要做复杂任务——比如帮 Will 用 Claude Code 给侄女写首歌，他不想为了一次使用去开一个月费账户，他想要的是微消费 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:52" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="My... I was trying the other day, I was using Claude Code to try to compose a song for my niece's birthday. Oh." aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[微交易|微交易]]需要微支付支撑，而[[稳定币|稳定币]]让微交易变得真正可行：对人类来说稳定币使用体验还不友好，但给智能体一个预算，它可以自如地在美元和稳定币之间转换，不介意那些来回操作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:14" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And I think to make that work, you're just going to need to support microtransactions. I think the case against microtransactions has always been, well, you're trying to consume content and if you're trying to sell an article, you're always going to be squeezed between the subscriber business model and the free business model." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

稳定币本身就是一个更好的全球资金转移平台。印度 UPI 上 86% 的支付低于 5 美元，美国卡支付这个比例是个位数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:50" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And better for a couple of reasons. You know, there are certain countries that have rolled out really good payment systems. Most of them are nationalized, you know, UPI in India." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但各国的好系统都是国有的，全球经济需要一个「共同支点」——稳定币解决了这个政治问题，在任何地方都能用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:27" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="All of these cheap, fast payment schemes that are nationalized picks another one of yours, which is brilliant, meteorically. But you need a shelling point for the global economy, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Stripe 把稳定币做成了 Stripe Treasury 的原生功能，法币覆盖约 60 个国家，稳定币覆盖约 150 个国家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:08" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And so we are sort of resolutely focused on trying to change that. We talked earlier about Stripe Treasury and we decided to make stablecoins native to Stripe Treasury." aria-label="回原文"></button>。做美墨汇款的 Felix，几年内就在全球最大汇款走廊拿到了 5% 到 10% 的份额 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:42" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="And all of this You know, when you really lean into it, Felix Pogba, or Felix, I think, as they're called now, which started as a remittance company between the US and Mexico, and now, you know, has other corridors as well." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 品味怎么规模化

Stripe 以「品味」著称——对一家基础设施公司来说这很少见。Will 认为规模化品味靠两件事：一是自上而下反复强调，像「赢得初创公司再赢得他们」一样反复说；二是真正使用产品 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:02" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="But to your, you know, more proximate to your question, Product quality is really, really core to our culture and to our identity. And it's for a few reasons." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Stripe 投入大量资源模拟产品使用：生成一个看起来真实的账户，随机化增长率、制造争议和退款，让团队「住进去」体验用户真实感受。工程经理被要求带头做这件事，因为他们控制资源，能直接说「这感觉不好，下个冲刺必须修」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="51:32" data-who="Will Gabrick" data-en="You have to kind of prove to yourself that it does before you ever fly it. Or in NVIDIA, you know, they really hit escape velocity was when they, you know, went from needing to wait for the fab to give them the chips to try the chips to actually simulate." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **AI 时代的正确回应是「构建更多」**：智能体提升了单人物产力，别拿来裁员降本，拿来消化积压需求、做更多产品——这是杰文斯悖论在软件公司的体现。
- **Minions 模式 = 一次性生成**：给智能体一个清晰提示，让它跑完构建和测试，你来审查。不迭代、不进规划模式。前提是每个代码仓库里要有好的智能体文件和模板（「注塑模具」）。
- **组织变扁、工程师变「创始人」**：减少管理层级，让工程师同时承担产品经理和设计师角色，用工具让他们能自己完成前端。公司角色从管理者变成铺路者。
- **[[智能体商务|智能体商务]]的切入点在 B2B**：让智能体去「采用」和配置 SaaS 服务，比让智能体替人逛电商更近、更实用。投资和选型时可以问：智能体会选这个吗？
- **微交易 + 稳定币 = 智能体经济的支付层**：智能体不需要月费账户，需要的是一次性微消费。稳定币对人类不友好，但对智能体来说刚好——它们不介意转换操作的摩擦。
- **品味规模化的两个抓手**：自上而下反复讲 + 模拟真实用户数据让团队住进产品里。工程经理必须带头，因为他们能直接调动资源修问题。

  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
