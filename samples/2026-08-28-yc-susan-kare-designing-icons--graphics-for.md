# Macintosh 原始图标设计师谈像素设计的本质

Susan Kerr 是一位图标设计师和艺术专业背景的人，被高中同学 Andy Hertzfeld 拉进 [[Apple|Apple]] 的 [[Macintosh|Macintosh]] 团队，负责为这台新电脑设计系统字体和小图标。她去面试时夹着一堆从图书馆借来的排版书假装内行，还用方格纸画了一些位图图形当作品——没人问她任何字体设计的问题，但她还是拿到了这份工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So those were my credentials. And I went to the library in Palo Alto and took out some books about typography and some font sample books. And I actually brought them to my interview under my arm." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她带去面试的那个画着早期图标的笔记本，后来被 MoMA 永久收藏了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:17" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I don't think anybody even asked me any questions about font design, despite my prep. In a funny twist of fate, as much as I had wanted to be an artist, now that notebook with those early icons that I took to my interview is in the permanent collection of MoMA." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第一天到 Apple，她看到的屏幕上只有粗糙的字体和可以拖拽的圆角矩形，任务是让这些东西变得任何人都能看懂、不需要读手册 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:53" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So when I got to Apple the first day, this screen was shown to me to say, this is what it's your job to improve. And fonts and the round rects that are on the five and a quarter inch floppy drawing are..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。限制极其严苛：图标只有 16×16 的黑白像素，启动画面只有 32×32 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:02" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So this is 16 by 16 black and white. And I think the first images that I made using those constraints were things like this. And we thought about, you know, thought maybe a lasso would be a good way to surround an irregular region and some other art tools." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但她没把这当成坏事——"你可以在任何限制下发挥创造力，最好明白限制是什么，然后对此感到兴奋，而不是因为你没有一千种颜色而感到沮丧" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:44" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And this... You can be creative, but it's good to understand what the constraints are and then be excited about that as opposed to being upset that you don't have a thousand colors." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她设计的第一个系统字体叫 [[Chicago|Chicago]]，大写字母高度只有 9 个像素。诀窍是只用水平线、垂直线和 45 度角来消除锯齿感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:50" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And it's funny to me because I went to Paris for the first time fairly recently and there's a street artist known as Space Invader and he does a lot of mosaic work and he has borrowed liberally from early Macintosh stuff which made me really happy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当时很多电脑显示器的字体是等宽的，M 被挤得扁扁的、I 却很宽，Macintosh 能做比例间距字体（每个字母宽度不同，像印刷体一样）是一个很大的进步，虽然她当时没意识到 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:26" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But the others I could pretty much fit into the system. And we were so lucky on the Macintosh to be able to do proportionally spaced fonts. Because I could remember just seeing displays where the M was really squished and then the I was really wide." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Chicago 被用在标题栏和计算器里，后来第一台 iPod 也用了这个字体，她觉得这算是一种延续 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:02" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And so we used Chicago, we put it in the title bar, experimented a lot till we came up with those lines. And what we didn't realize was that when we designed the calculator that we were designing the first iPod, which also used Chicago, which made me happy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Andy 给她写了一个图标编辑器——没有画形状的工具，没有撤销，只能一个个切换像素的黑白，但可以同时看到放大版和实际 72 DPI 的效果，而且自动生成十六进制代码 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:54" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We forged ahead. Andy wrote, I did not have to design on graph paper once I got to Apple. Andy wrote an icon editor." aria-label="回原文"></button>。"你可以在没有橡皮擦的情况下进行迭代。

这有多好简直难以言表" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:34" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I thought this was just magical, even though it didn't have 20 levels of undo and you couldn't draw any shapes. You could iterate without an eraser. It's hard to overstate how great this was." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她甚至用 32×32 的像素给同事画肖像，一千个点如果迭代足够多次，就能得到一张有几分像的图 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:18" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And you can see in the title bar, we tried a bunch of different types of things during the time we were experimenting. And sometimes I liked to, people came and sat and I would do their portrait in 32 by 32." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最出名的图标是 [[Happy Mac|Happy Mac]]——电脑启动时显示的笑脸。灵感来自她 14 岁时得到的一个微笑按钮 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:06" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So I thought a smile was good. I'm pretty sure it came from my memory of a button I got when I was about 14. I like smiling and happy things." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她从漫画家 Scott McCloud 的书里找到理论支撑：当一张脸细节很多时，它看起来像某个具体的人；但如果你把它做得非常简单，每个人都可以把自己投射上去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:39" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It came out 10 years after the Mac originally did. And I pulled two illustrations from that book by Scott McCloud that kind of explains some of the thinking behind our icons, which is that when a face in particular has a lot of detail," aria-label="回原文"></button>。这成了她的图标设计原则——"一两个显著细节，但不要给它填塞很多额外的东西" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:47" data-who="Unknown" data-en="A salient detail or two, but don't lard it with a lot of extra stuff. Kind of similar to street signs, like the sign where there's a silhouette of two kids holding hands at a school crossing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。类似学校过马路标志上两个手牵手孩子的剪影：技术上完全可以加上格子午餐盒和鞋带，但那只会让人分心，夺走你追求的瞬间识别 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:03" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Kind of similar to street signs, like the sign where there's a silhouette of two kids holding hands at a school crossing. And there's not a technical reason that they couldn't have plaid lunchboxes and shoelaces or..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她也犯过错。打印图标画了一台带链轮孔的 ImageWriter 打印机，细节太多，很快就过时了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:23" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So we made a watch cursor instead of an hourglass. This was the icon for print. It's kind of a trap." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

"在图标中展示特定产品设计的图片不好，比如那个用于保存的三点五英寸软盘，它经不起时间的考验，最好使用一个隐喻，只要足够的细节" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:51" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It would have had a much longer lifespan if I had just stopped and made it simpler. So in general, I think it's not good in icons to show pictures of a particular product design because like the three and a half inch floppy for save even though some there's still a few of those kicking around but obviously it it didn't age well better to use a metaphor just enough detail" aria-label="回原文"></button>。还有炸弹图标——本意是给"完全系统故障"用的，设计时被告知"没人会看到这个"，所以她画了一个兔八哥风格的卡通炸弹。结果真有用户打来电话，问屏幕上出现炸弹是不是电脑要爆炸了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We made some mistakes too. I was told that as an alternative to the happy Mac, if in the extremely unlikely event something went wrong with the Mac, there should be an unhappy Macintosh." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Command 键（⌘）的符号也出自她手。本来想用苹果标志做快捷键，Steve Jobs 有一天过来说"我们的标志是珍贵的，我们不能有一堆苹果" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:30" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And there was an apple, going to be an apple key for these keyboard shortcuts. But Steve came in one day at the end of the day and said, you cannot do this. I think he called it an apple farm and said," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她去图书馆翻书，找到一张叫"feature"的图片，觉得像四叶草也像苜蓿叶式公路立交桥，而且在 16×16 里很好画 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:12" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So back to the library. And I came across this image in a book that was called a feature. So I thought, OK, it kind of looks like a four-leaf clover." aria-label="回原文"></button>。后来她去瑞典才发现，那个符号其实来自一座 1200 年的城堡废墟的俯瞰图——四座塔楼 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:39" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And there's the feature key because it's used there to indicate that you're near, like for sightseeing, like a monument or something else. Someone from Sweden must have heard me comment on this and said actually that symbol that's on the signs is taken from a 1200" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

离开 Apple 后她做了很多项目：给 Windows 3.0 用 16 色抖动做不那么俗气的界面 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:10" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But after I worked at Apple, I did a job for Microsoft on Windows 3.0. And color, even if 16 colors, and some of them weren't very good colors, but it was still so nice to have, to be able to use red for alerts and to differentiate at a glance a little easier when you had color as another..." aria-label="回原文"></button>；在 Facebook 做了差不多四年的"礼品店"，每天午夜上架一个新礼物卖一美元，她发现"可爱胜过一切，吻痕标记是有史以来最畅销的，如果我的性命取决于卖出一幅一美元的画，我会画企鹅" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:36" data-who="Unknown" data-en="That someone would want enough either to have or to share with someone that it was worth spending a dollar on. So we did this for not quite four years and had a new gift every day that launched at midnight on the East Coast." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

后半段她分享了几条影响她很深的建议。Paul Rand——她后来在 Next 公司请他做 logo，Rand 开价 10 万美元做一个，"你会喜欢的，而这就是实际发生的事" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:00" data-who="Unknown" data-en="he said we could hire five people or firms and pay them each 15k and then we would pick the best and continue to work with them so i invited some people and then i invited paul rand and he said" aria-label="回原文"></button>——告诉她字体不用多，"你只需要知道如何很好地使用它们" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:05" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's like you don't need that many. He was kind of brusque. You don't need that many fonts." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

做 logo 时 Rand 反对花大钱搞一个跟产品名无关的抽象符号，主张直接对产品名称本身做文章，比如他把 Next 放进一个立方体里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:07" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But when we were talking about logos, he really advocated Trying hard to think of a way to do something to the name of the product, kind of the way he put next in the cube." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还把 UPS logo 拿给六岁女儿看，女儿说"这是一个礼物"，Rand 就认为这证明了它是可理解的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:03" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And I love that UPS logo. And he said when he was doing it, he showed it to his six-year-old daughter and said, Catherine, what is this? And reportedly, she said, it's a present, Daddy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

建筑师 Daniel Nord 说："让它视觉化。展示，而不是讲述" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:40" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And this is how he said, Make it visual. I mean, I guess this is a metaphor too, but do the drawing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Alan Kay 关于用户界面的一句话她也一直记着——不要把所有东西放在同一层级，要嵌套，让东西可以自然地出现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:14" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And this is Bud quoting Alan Kay. As a kind of tenet about user interface and maybe not having everything at the same level and nesting things so that things can just appear that ease of use seems possible." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Andy Hertzfeld 教她怎么向 Steve Jobs 展示方案："不要给他看一样东西，因为他会说他不喜欢，迫使你重做；给他看几样，他可以说某个很糟糕，但仍然能挑出一个" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:13" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And Andy said this. He gave me this advice showing stuff to Steve. He's like, don't show him one thing because he'll say he doesn't like it because it maybe forces you to go try and do it again and be better." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **限制是朋友，不是敌人**：16×16 黑白像素看似极端，但搞清楚边界后兴奋地创作，比抱怨没有一千种颜色有效得多
- **图标要一两个显著细节，不要堆**：铅笔比镀铬反光笔更适合代表"写作"，因为越简单越普适，越多细节越像某个特定东西
- **别画具体产品**：软盘保存图标、带链轮孔的打印机图标都因为绑定了特定硬件而快速过时，用隐喻比用写实图更耐久
- **给决策者看多个选项，不是一个**：只给一个，对方只能说"不喜欢"；给几个，对方可以骂烂的但挑出好的，你也省了重做
- **可爱胜过一切**：在 Facebook 礼品店四年的数据里，泰迪熊、心形、吻痕、企鹅全部碾压钻石和 Rolex

> <span class="qz">我想说的是，如果我的性命取决于卖出一幅一美元的画，我现在知道我会画一只企鹅，因为我们有过多个企鹅限量版，而且它们都卖光了。</span>
