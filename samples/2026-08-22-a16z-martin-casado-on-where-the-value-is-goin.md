# Martin Casado：AI 时代，钱比以前好使了

但现在，投 10 美元进去，能直接产出回报——这个行业以前从来做不到这一点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:36" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="I don't know if you get $9 back on the other side of that. ## 前沿实验室会吞噬一切吗？

Martin 说他们内部认真讨论过这个问题，两条路径都有很强论据 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:38" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="Listen, we actually had an offsite recently, and one of the big discussions points in like, what is the future of models? And I think there's basically two paths that are meaningful to talk about." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**支持「实验室赢」的论据：** 三年数据看，实验室拿走了大部分收入；筹资能力惊人——假设 [[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 和另一个实验室筹了 2200 亿美元，比整个下游生态加起来还多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:15" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="And then they can turn that capital pretty directly into growth, which we've very rarely seen. So let's say OpenAI and Thratbook have raised $220 billion. That's more than the entire downstream ecosystem combined, which is really unbelievable." aria-label="回原文"></button>；前沿模型只需比对手好一点点就能维持定价权，因为用户在竞争均衡中使用它们 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:26" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="That's more than the entire downstream ecosystem combined, which is really unbelievable. There's another argument, and that argument is, like, listen, they've been able to maintain pricing power just by being an epsilon higher." aria-label="回原文"></button>；此外它们掌握全部算力供应，而供应是有限的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:00" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="So what about the arguments against then that the labs will not own everything? Oh, another one for it, by the way, is they own all the supply. Yeah." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**反对「实验室赢」的论据：** AI 可应用的表面积在快速扩大，很多领域需要服务团队和客户关系，单一组织做不到 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:06" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="Okay, so the arguments against. The arguments against is the surface area is really expanding. And I mean, really what's working are primarily" aria-label="回原文"></button>；[[开源|开源模型]]生态在成熟；而实验室领先的一个重要原因是资本太便宜，这几乎肯定会回归理性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:40" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="And there's a maturing ecosystem around them to serve those. I think a lot of the reason the labs are so far ahead is they have very cheap access to capital, which will almost certainly rationalize." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Martin 个人猜测供应限制会在 2028 年左右缓解，届时按金额算大实验室可能拿 80% 市场，但按 token（模型处理的文本单元）量算，60% 会是长尾和开源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:32" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="Then I think that will play in favor of everybody else. If I were to guess, this is Martine totally guessing, no idea. If I were to guess, I'd say" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 模型路由：被高估的套利，被低估的双边市场

[[OpenRouter|OpenRouter]] 这类模型路由器，看起来是让应用自动选最合适的模型，但 Martin 认为现实没那么简单。

这些模型比人们以为的更「粘」——大家都在说换模型，但实际很少发生，一部分原因是采购惯性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:26" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="I go back and forth on this. We've actually learned that these models are a lot stickier than people assumed. Like everybody talks about just swapping them out, but it actually doesn't happen very often." aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[智能路由|智能路由]]有两种思路：一是按质量选最优模型，Martin 认为这近乎是一个「AI 完全问题」（需要宇宙中最聪明的东西才能回答的问题），因为要判断「哪个模型最适合回答这个问题」，本身就需要最聪明的模型来判，那不如直接交给最聪明的模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:24" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="I think it's AI complete. By AI complete, okay, so let's imagine you're trying to answer the question, what question does the smartest thing in the universe need to answer?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。二是按成本效益选，这是当前路由的主要收益来源——保持质量的同时降成本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:40" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="So like that will happen. But I think today, if you look at like model routing and the gains, You know, as deployed by the application companies, you know, I mentioned Cursor, but, you know, we've got a huge portfolio of application companies." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但 OpenRouter 真正的价值不在于路由算法，在于它是一个[[双边市场|双边市场]]：对模型提供商说「我们有百万用户」，对开发者说「新模型出来你第一时间能用上」 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:11" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="So what, like, to what extent is like the value of OpenRouter that it will eventually be able to do this kind of like AI complete model routing? Listen, I think that you need a two-sided marketplace." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而且现在模型定价是动态的、有补贴的，下一个大模型出来往往各方面都更好（帕累托最优），你干脆就只用它了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:59" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="And it's a hard question to answer just because we don't even know how to benchmark these models to begin with. The pricing is dynamic. It's often subsidized." aria-label="回原文"></button>。补贴也有套利空间——有人注册 200 美元的套餐，三天耗尽 token 后取消并拿回 27 天的退款，然后用这些 token 去提供 20 美元的服务 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:03" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="...to, like, a $200 plan. They'll use all the tokens in, like, three days, and then they'll cancel, and they'll get prorated for the 27 days, even though they used all the tokens." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Cursor 为什么值 600 亿

Martin 确认 [[Cursor|Cursor]] 独立估值就达到那个量级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:23" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="To like an investor? Yeah. Yeah, for sure." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他强调 Cursor 的关键区分点：在 AI 领域，大多数公司要么做服务，要么做研究，但 Cursor 始终认为这是一个产品问题，不是模型架构问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:38" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="If you're not working on a new model architecture, it's kind of hard to actually interest the core teams. And they always kept the main thing the main thing, Cursor, which is changing how you write software engineering." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

创始人 30-40% 的时间花在招聘和建文化上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:56" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="They set culture incredibly well. I'd say the founders probably spend 30, 40% of their time hiring and setting culture. They were very focused on engineering and not like research and model architecture." aria-label="回原文"></button>。团队自己是开发者，给自己造工具、在自己身上测试 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:50" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="We had people from the Cursor team actually here on MTS and they're sharing a little bit about their internal culture and they're constantly building products internally for themselves and then testing it on themselves." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 别用财务指标看早期 AI 公司

Martin 反复强调一个观点：如果你只看资产负债表——利润率、流失率、收入质量——你会得出错误的结论 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:08" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="So like if you think... So, if you think about it primarily in the balance sheet and in finance, you worry about things like margins, churn, you know, revenue quality, which are all very legitimate things to worry about if you see no strategic value in the business, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。早期投资要看的是战略控制点：这家公司在新堆栈里是不是一个关键节点？

哪怕现在亏钱，它可能到达一个能指挥巨大价值转移的位置 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:41" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="...difference of opinions, but that's entirely divorced to the strategic value of a company. Like some value, some companies may lose money, but they'll get to a position where they can command a tremendous transfer of value." aria-label="回原文"></button>。现在价值在堆栈每一层都在积累——芯片、模型、应用、推理、服务、媒体，这是他职业生涯见过的最大财富释放 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:43" data-who="Martin Casado" data-en="All layers of the stack. I mean, NVIDIA's doing great. The model companies are doing great." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **AI 改变了资本效率**：投钱进去能直接产出回报，这是行业史上首次，小团队+大资本成为可行组合。
- **实验室不会自动吞噬一切**：供应限制缓解后，长尾和开源会夺回 token 量的大头；应用层也在侵蚀利润份额。
- **模型路由的价值在双边市场，不在算法**：模型比想象中粘，按质量路由近乎无解，当前核心收益是成本优化。
- **早期 AI 公司看战略控制点，不看利润表**：关键是它在新堆栈里是不是不可绕过的节点，而不是现在赚不赚钱。
- **产品公司稀缺且值钱**：AI 领域大多数人做研究或服务，专注产品本身就是区分点。
