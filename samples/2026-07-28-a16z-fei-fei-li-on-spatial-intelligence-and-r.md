<div class="pd-tldr"><b>一句话</b>World Labs 收购 Cynics，用仿真和数据补齐机器人最难的数据缺口。</div>

语言模型改变了 AI 理解文字的方式，但下一个前沿是让 AI 理解物理世界并在其中行动。在这场对话中，[[World Labs|World Labs]] 创始人 [[Fei-Fei Li|Fei-Fei Li]] 与 [[Cynics|Cynics]] 联合创始人 [[Yunzhu Li|Yunzhu Li]] 坐在一起，剖析了 World Labs 收购 Cynics（一家做机器人[[仿真|仿真]]的初创公司）背后的逻辑。全篇围绕一条主线：机器人真正走向现实世界的最大瓶颈是缺乏数据，而解决这个问题的关键钥匙是「仿真（即用数字技术模拟物理世界的运行）」。文章将从两家公司的合并逻辑讲起，接着拆解为什么仿真比真实世界数据更重要，最后谈谈他们对机器人的务实预判（别做全能人形机器人，先做仓库里的专用机器人）。

> 【背景】Fei-Fei Li 是知名的计算机视觉科学家。World Labs 是她创立的前沿模型初创公司，旨在构建「大型世界模型（Large World Models，一种让 AI 理解和生成三维物理空间的模型）」。Cynics 是一家专注为机器人开发「现实到仿真」流水线的公司。

## 一拍即合：从客户变战友
机器人要在真实物理世界中行动，一直是 AI 领域最难啃的骨头之一。World Labs 一直认为机器人是「[[空间智能|空间智能]]」的重要应用，而 Cynics 恰好在解决机器人领域最头疼的问题——训练和评估数据的极度匮乏 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:44" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="So for Cynics, the unique opportunity we see is that there has been a lot of bottlenecks. Right now, we see faced by the developments of general-purpose robots, especially around training and also around evaluations." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

两家公司的结缘很有戏剧性。它们并非因为创始人曾经是师生关系（Yunzhu 曾在 Fei-Fei 指导下做过博士后）而一拍即合，而是源于一次真实的商业互动。去年冬天，当 World Labs 发布了第一代生成式基础模型 [[Marble|Marble]] 后，Cynics 团队直接注册成为了客户 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:51" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="They came into WorldLabs as a customer. Really? When we released the first version of our generative model called Marble last winter, around November, December, Cinex just signed up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。在沟通过程中，双方意识到彼此的能力极其互补：Cynics 擅长密集重建（把真实环境高度保真地搬进数字世界），而 World Labs 的 Marble 模型擅长稀疏重建和空间生成 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:41" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="So this dense reconstruction right now is still a little bit on the heavier side. And what World Labs right now has been doing involves a lot of profound capabilities around sparse reconstruction." aria-label="回原文"></button>。如果把两者结合，就能构建出一个完美的「数字世界」来训练机器人。

既然收购是为了补齐做机器人的能力，那是不是意味着 World Labs 要自己造机器人、出硬件？并不是。World Labs 做的是针对环境的基础模型，未来大概率会演进为涵盖状态与动作输出的「全能模型（Omni models，指能同时接收和输出多种模态如图像、文本、动作的模型）」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:15" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="And as the technology has been evolving, some of the most exciting base models are omni models, right? They take multimodal input." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而 Cynics 打造的则是一套与具体硬件无关的基础设施——客户可以用单臂、双臂、带轮子的各种机器人，World Labs 的平台能将这些不同的硬件放进生成的数字世界中进行训练 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:22" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="It's building an environment which another company can place their robot brain to navigate and to learn. Yeah. So for our customers right now, they have all different kinds of robots." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

说完了两家公司为什么合并、定位是什么，接下来的核心问题是：为什么非要用仿真？只用真实世界的数据不行吗？

## 为什么真实数据不够用：仿真的独特价值
现在市面上流行一种做法：直接用海量的互联网视频来训练机器人。但在 Yunzhu 看来，这有一个致命弱点——缺乏「一致性」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:14" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="So in order to create words with Robot Candler, the words, as I mentioned, need to capture the essential structure of the problem. And one of the very important and necessary requirements for those words will be consistency." aria-label="回原文"></button>。现有的视频预测模型经常会出现物体凭空消失的荒谬错误，这种错误无法给机器人提供正确的物理反馈。

更重要的是，仿真（用数字技术模拟物理世界）扮演了一个现实数据无法替代的角色：[[反事实推理|反事实推理]]（推演那些在现实中尚未发生或不可能发生的事情）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:06" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="You know why? There's a very important role simulation plays that real world data doesn't play, which is counterfactual reasoning, is that you play out events that hasn't happened or cannot happen, or you don't have enough data to make it happen in real world." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Fei-Fei 举了世界杯的例子：每场比赛的战术规划本质上都是仿真，无论是画在白板上还是在电脑里。**人类在脑子里做大量仿真，是为了学会如何应对各种突发情况，机器人也一样。** 她还提到 Waymo（自动驾驶公司）的例子：Waymo 在实际训练中，比重度依赖现实数据更重度地依赖仿真 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:11" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="Weibo has officially said they use billions of hours of simulation. And actually, Waymo is more simulation-heavy than just real-world data-heavy. So these are real examples." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

既然理解了仿真为何不可或缺，那它具体能给产业带来什么好处？这直接引出了它解决的两个核心痛点：

## 解决机器人的两大痛点：可靠性与效率
具体到产业应用，仿真解决的是「可靠性」和「效率」两大难题。

第一是可靠性。要让机器人在真实环境中可靠工作，需要让它在训练时见过足够多的场景变化。在仿真环境中，你可以系统性地改变光照、摩擦力、几何形状和物体类型，确保覆盖各种极端状态 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:15" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="how you can learn of how is that is robust so with simulation you can do systematic randomizations and control and variations of lighting frictions geometries object types and also all different kind of physical parameters to making sure you have sufficient coverage of the state space so this is what can give the robotic systems reliability" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二是效率。现在业界大量使用远程操作（人类推拉摇杆遥控机器人）来收集训练数据，但这种方式收集数据的速度，甚至比人类自己亲自去做还要慢 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:20" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="So right now, many people are doing teleoperation. And if you look at many of the teleoperation devices, imagining all the actual skeletons you are using, you are actually collecting the data at a speed that is actually slower than humans actually doing the task." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而在仿真世界里，你可以不受物理重力等定律的束缚，系统性地给机器人的行为加速，从而极大地缩短训练时间。

这种效率的提升还体现在「评估」上。评估（Eval，测试模型的实际表现达标率）是 AI 迭代的生命线。但在真实物理环境中测试机器人，受限于物理定律，迭代速度比语言模型慢了几个数量级，不仅慢而且危险、昂贵 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:18" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="for you to do the distinguishments. And you really think about also the robotic evaluations right now people are doing in the real environments. The iteration speeds is multiple orders of magnitude slower than iterations of those language models." aria-label="回原文"></button>。有了高度保真的仿真环境，客户就可以直接在数字世界里快速、安全地测试机器人的各项指标。

说到这里，我们已经明白仿真环境是让机器人又快又好工作的必需品。但这并不意味着短期内我们就能看到能在家里全能干活的机器人，这涉及到对机器人落地形态的务实判断。

## 务实预判：别迷恋全能的人形机器人
基于上述逻辑，两位嘉宾对机器人产业的短期未来给出了一个极其务实的判断：不要过度迷恋人形机器人。

Yunzhu 认为，历史趋势表明，机器人的落地一定是从「完全结构化环境」（如工厂自动化流水线），走向「半结构化环境」（如亚马逊仓库、餐馆、酒店），最后才能进入你我家这种充满狗、小孩和杂物的「非结构化环境」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:42" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="So that's a very good question. So if you look at, for example, all the progressions of robotic applications in the real environments, it has always followed the trend from going from fully structured environments into semi-structured environments and then into unstructured environments." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Fei-Fei 进一步指出了人形机器人的悖论：人形是为了在非结构化环境中生存而演化出的最通用形态，但「最通用」往往意味着它不一定是解决具体单一问题的最优解 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:51" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="this body shape that can be very general but not necessarily best at everything. And that is for the survival of unstructured environment. But from a business point of view, from a pragmatic technology point of view, that..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。从商业角度看，去攻克非结构化环境下的通用身体，是最难、最不切实际的问题。因此，Cynics 选择了更可持续的道路：做与硬件无关的基础设施，服务于各类专用的机器人和半结构化环境 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:36" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="is that to be more body agnostic so that their infrastructure can serve different bodies and different semi-structured environments. A common lens to look at exactly this question is an economic lens, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

主持人抛出了一个犀利的问题：考虑到人类大脑只需 30 瓦特就能高效运转，我们离造出具备人类能效比的机器人还有多远？是五年还是永远不可能？Yunzhu 的回答是：这将需要非常长的时间 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:25" data-who="Yunzhu Li" data-en="Is this like five years or this is like never? I think it's going to take a very long time. So if you're really thinking about robots in real environments, in the end, it will always be a system." aria-label="回原文"></button>。现实中的机器人是一个包含软硬件甚至细节到手指摩擦系数的复杂系统，需要不断迭代。**正如 Fei-Fei 在对话结尾所强调的：当今 AI 领域最难的事情，是保持一种「经过校准的适度乐观」。** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:34" data-who="Fei-Fei Li" data-en="But to achieve, for example, human-level efficiency and capabilities, it will take longer. Martin, the hardest thing in today's AI is to have the right measured optimism." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 本集带走
1. **机器人的瓶颈是数据**：与互联网上数据充沛的语言模型不同，机器人的训练和评估极度缺乏数据。解决这个难题的核心钥匙，是构建具备高度物理一致性的仿真世界。
2. **仿真的独特价值**：现实数据只能覆盖已发生的事，而仿真提供了「反事实推理」的能力，让机器人在虚拟的极端和突发场景中学习应对策略。此外，仿真能打破现实物理定律的时间束缚，大幅提升测试与迭代效率。
3. **落地要走务实路线**：相比于试图直接进入家庭这种高度非结构化的环境，优先攻克仓库、工厂等半结构化环境中的专用任务是更现实的商业选择。全能人形机器人在短期内面临极大的系统复杂性挑战。

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
