## 过去 50 年的技术进步被严重高估了

人们感觉过去几十年技术变革很快，但数据不支持。衡量技术对经济影响的指标是[[生产力增长|生产力增长]]，而过去 50 年的生产力增长实际上非常低——在美国，大概只有 1940 到 1970 年间的一半，是 1870 到 1940 年间的三分之一 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:24" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And in particular, economists have a way of measuring the rate of technological change in the economy that is productivity growth, which we could talk about what that means, but basically, it's sort of the mathematical expression of the impact of technology on the economy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

如果你对比 1970 年和今天，建成世界并没有那么不同：1960 年建的楼还在用，1930 年的桥还在用，1880 年建的城市还在那里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:48" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="If you contrast 1970 today, it's not that different. And look, you just see that you could just walk around and it's just like, &quot;Oh yeah, there's a bunch of buildings that were built in 1960, and there's a bridge that was built in 1930, and there's a dam that was built in like 1910, and there's a city that was founded in 1880.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。这正是 Peter Thiel 多年来论证的——我们在比特世界有很多进展，但在原子世界进展很少 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:45" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="I would probably argue that one quite a bit differently today than I did, and I would give his view, I think, a lot more credit. And it actually goes to the discussion that we did, the conversation we just had, which is the... The real form of what Peter was arguing was we have lots of process in bit, we have lots of progress in bits, but we have very little progress in atoms." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 进入的是"缺人"的世界，不是"人多"的世界

很多人担心 AI 导致大规模失业，但这个模型太简化了。首先，即使 AI 让生产力增长翻两番，也只是把我们带回 1870 到 1930 年间的就业流动水平——那个时代的人觉得世界充满机会 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:05" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so we're coming out of this kind of phase where we've had almost no technological progress in the economy, we've had remarkably little job churn as a result of that relative to any historical period." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

其次，很多国家的生育率已经低于 2，包括美国和中国，未来一个世纪人口会减少 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:24" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And then there's this other just incredible thing that's happening, which is the demographic collapse, it's sort of a Western phenomenon and increasingly global phenomenon, which is the rate of reproduction of the human species is in rapid decline." aria-label="回原文"></button>。再加上移民可能在收紧，剩下来的人类工人会变得越来越稀缺，不是越来越廉价 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:44" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="It seems to be kind of a broad-based thing happening with rise to nationalism, concerns about the rate of immigration and immigration historically in countries like the US, it's kind of ebbed and flowed over time based on how the national mood shifts." aria-label="回原文"></button>。如果 AI 真的强到引发乌托邦式的变革，结果是商品和服务价格崩溃，等于给所有人涨了工资，提供社会保障网也变得更便宜 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:21" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="The result of those gluts is you get collapsing prices. The collapsing prices mean that the thing today that costs you $100, now costs you $10, and now costs you $1." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 教育的本质机会：一对一辅导终于经济可行了

几个世纪以来，教育领域有一个公认的最优解：[[一对一辅导|一对一辅导]]。Bloom 的 2 西格玛效应表明，一对一辅导能稳定地让学生从第 50 百分位跃升到第 99 百分位 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:09" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="There's actually also statistical evidence, analytical evidence that this is correct. There's this massive question in the field of education, which is, how do you improve educational outcomes?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但除了皇室和贵族，没人负担得起。AI 让这变得可行——孩子可以对 [[LLM|LLM]] 提无限个问题、获得即时反馈、让它出题考自己、说不懂就让它讲简单点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:53" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="But to your question, it's never been economically feasible for anybody other than the richest people in society to be able to provide one-on-one tutoring for kids." aria-label="回原文"></button>。即使孩子还在传统学校体系里，家长也可以用 AI 辅导来增强它 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:21" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="People can just do this today. And so I think there's this massive opportunity for parents in many walks of life, with a little bit of time at focus, to be able to say, &quot; Okay, my kid's probably still going to go through a traditional education system, but I'm going to augment this with AI tutoring.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 产品经理、工程师、设计师之间的"墨西哥僵局"

这三个角色之间正在发生一场三方对峙：每个程序员都相信有了 AI 自己也能做产品经理和设计师，每个产品经理觉得自己也能写代码和做设计，每个设计师也这么想 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:40" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="So, each is aiming at the other two and you've got this kind of standoff situation. And so the way I've been describing this is there's like a Mexican standoff happening between those three roles: between product manager, designer, and coder." aria-label="回原文"></button>。讽刺的是，他们都有点对——AI 确实已经挺擅长做这三件事了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:23" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="So, they're going to be aiming the guns up the org chart, but that's the next phase. And what I think is so fascinating about this Mexican standoff is they're actually all kind of correct, I think." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但真正的机会不是谁取代谁，而是谁能在自己原本的领域足够深的同时，用 AI 横向扩展到另外两个领域。Scott Adams 说过：擅长两件事的叠加效应不止是翻倍，擅长三件事的叠加效应不止是三倍，因为你成了领域组合中极其稀缺的专家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="54:33" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="It took somebody who actually had both of those skills to be able to make Dilbert which is one of the most successful cartoons in history. And so the way Scott always described it was that from a career development standpoint, the additive effect of being good at two things is more than double." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Larry Summers 换了个说法：不要成为可替代的人——如果你只是一个设计师或只是一个程序员，理论上你可以被换掉；但如果你是那种组合技能的人，你就不可替代了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:25" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="Yeah, the combination, yeah. My friend, Larry Summers, had a different version of the Scott Adams thing, which is he used to tell people, he said, &quot; The key for career planning is,&quot; he said, &quot;don't be fungible.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 编程任务的演变：从手写代码到"跟 AI 争论"

"计算器"这个词最初指的不是机器，而是人——一屋子人手工做数学计算 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:47" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="So, the first... Do you know the original definition of the term calculator? Do you know what that referred to?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。后来有了机器码、汇编语言、高级语言 C、再到脚本语言如 Python——每一层都抽象掉了下面的细节，每一层出现时都有人质疑"这算不算真正的编程" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:02" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And you can still... There's still people, kicking today, whose job as a programmer was to build the punch cards. And then you got, actually, this big breakthrough, which was called assembly language, which was basically the way to do machine code but with some level of English added to it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

[[AI 编程|AI 编程]]是下一层抽象。现在顶尖程序员的工作变成了编排十几个并行的编码机器人，整天跟 AI 争论让它写出正确的代码 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:29" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="To your point, it's not necessarily to write the code by hand. But what it is now is, all right, if you talk to the world's best programmer of yesterday, what they'll tell you is, &quot;Oh, my job is I'm sitting there and I'm orchestrating 10 code bots, coding bots that are running in parallel.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但如果你自己不会写代码，你就没法评估 AI 给你的东西对不对 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:03" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="But if you don't know how to write the code yourself, you don't know how to evaluate what the coding bots are giving you. And so, you asked about the 10... Our 10-year old is super into computers and super into programming." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Marc 告诉他 10 岁的儿子：你仍然要学懂代码，因为当 AI 给你的结果不对时，你需要理解为什么 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:08" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so, you asked about the 10... Our 10-year old is super into computers and super into programming. He's using Claude, and ChatGPT, Copilot, and all of these things." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 最大的被低估的用处：教你

人们用 AI 太多集中在"让它帮我干活"，但同样重要的是"让它教我" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="58:38" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="That's right. That's right. And again, I can't overstress the following, for anybody listening to this, the thing about AI that I think people are just not getting enough benefit out of yet is just it will teach you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。如果你不是工程师，就坐在那里看 AI 在做什么、怎么想的——这本身就是学架构的过程 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:46" data-who="Lenny" data-en="Two tricks I've heard along those lines. One is to watch the output, what the agent is doing and thinking as it's doing the work. So if you're not an engineer, just sit there and watch it think and make decisions, and it's almost become this layer on top of learning to code is learning to see what the agent is doing and thinking because that teaches you about architecture." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当你卡住又脱困后，问 AI"我本可以怎么做才能一开始就避免这个错误" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:11" data-who="Lenny" data-en="And the other is, a couple podcast guests have mentioned this, when you get stuck and then you figure out how to unstuck yourself, you ask it, &quot;What could I have done differently?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。你甚至可以让一个 AI 写代码、让另一个 AI 来挑错，让它们互相对抗 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="61:45" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="It'll happily critique itself. By the way, this is a very fun thing where you can have one AI critique the other AI which is another thing which is you have one AI write the code, you have another AI debunk the code." aria-label="回原文"></button>。想提升自己的人，应该把每一个空闲小时都用来跟 AI 对话，让它培训你 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:19" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so again, this is this level of latent superpower. People who really want to improve themselves and develop their career should be spending every spare hour in my view at this point talking on AI, being like, &quot;All right, train me up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 护城河：现在就下结论太早了

回顾互联网 1993 到 2010 年的报道，人们在头 10 到 15 年做的自信预测几乎全错 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="69:51" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="Not like, &quot;You know, I think that's a really good question and let's debate it from eight different angles.&quot; What I found is if you look back on those predictions a few years later, and you can do this by the way, if you pull up coverage of the internet from 1993 through 1997, or for that matter even through 2005 or 2010, and you look at the kinds of confidence statements people were making in the first 10 or 15 years, I would say almost all of them were wrong, generally quite badly wrong." aria-label="回原文"></button>。AI 模型本身有没有[[护城河|护城河]]？

一方面，投入几十亿美元、需要稀缺工程师、还要应对政治和监管——看起来应该有。但另一方面，ChatGPT 出来一年半内就有五家美国公司和五家中国公司做出了同等能力的产品，开源也追上来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="72:05" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so maybe that will be the outcome. The other side of it is if you had told me three years ago that in the Christmas of ChatGPT that within basically a year to year and a half there would be five other American companies that would have basically exactly capable products, and then there would be another five companies out of China that would have exactly capable products, and then there would additionally be open source that was basically the same, I would have been like, &quot;Wow, the thing that seemed like it was black magic all of a sudden has become like commoditized really fast,&quot; which by the way, is exactly what happened." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Claude Code 一周半开发出 Cowork——既说明 AI 编程惊人地强，也说明这种产品的进入壁垒可能很低 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="75:47" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="Well, and there's two ways looking at that which is like, &quot;Wow, that's really...&quot; I mean, obviously that's really impressive that Claude Code was able to build Cowork in a week and a half." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这是一个复杂适应系统，技术、法律、创业者选择、资本可用性都是变量，现在就断言行业结构会怎样，很危险 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="73:46" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so my honest answer on this is I think we're in a process of discovery over time. The way I think about this kind of structurally is it's a complex adaptive system." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AGI 的定义本身就设错了上限

[[AGI|AGI]]（通用人工智能）的通俗定义是"AI 能像人一样好地完成所有经济相关[[任务|任务]]" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="83:37" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="We could debate that, we could talk about that more. But the prosaic definition of AGI that at least I think the industry purchases but it's kind of conversed on, and tell me if you agree with this, is when the AI could do every economically-relevant task as good as a person." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但人类技能水平之所以有个顶，是因为生物学的限制——人类智商作为一个物种封顶在大约 160，那是爱因斯坦级别 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="84:43" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="Human, I gave you an example. Human IQ, kind of what they call &quot;fluid intelligence,&quot; or the sort of G factor of fluid intelligence, IQ I think tops out in humans as a species, it tops out around 160." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

机器没有这个限制。现有 AI 模型测试已经在 130 到 140 的水平，数学方面可能已接近 160 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="86:17" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="And so, can you have a... And you already have people running these experiments to kind of do human-equivalent kind of IQ for existing AI models. And by the way, existing AI models are kind of testing around the 130, 140 level, which means they're going to get to the 160 level." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

很快会有 200、250、300 智商的模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="86:30" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="But I think we're going to have AI models relatively quickly that are going to be like 160, 180, 200, 250, 300. By the way, and I think that's great, right? I feel as great about that as I do about the fact that we occasionally get an Einstein." aria-label="回原文"></button>。"人类同等水平"只会是一个脚注——到了那天，真正的问题是：在机器比人更强的世界里我们能做什么 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="87:44" data-who="Marc Andreessen" data-en="So you see what I'm saying, which is, like, I think this idea of human equivalent is just going to be a footnote. (function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();

      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }

    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');

    });
  }
