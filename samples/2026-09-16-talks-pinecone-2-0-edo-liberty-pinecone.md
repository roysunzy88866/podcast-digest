# 给智能体一个「知识层」：让 AI 不再是入职第一天的聪明新员工

这一集聊的是一个很扎心的问题：为什么大公司里的 AI [[智能体|智能体]]（agent，能自主干活、调用工具的 AI）明明很聪明，干起活来却总像个什么都不懂的新人？主讲人是一位曾在 Yahoo 负责 Yahoo Answers 产品的从业者，如今在做一款叫 [[Nexus|Nexus]] 的[[知识层|知识层]]产品。开场他用一个心理学概念「[[心智理论|心智理论]]」（即我们理解他人知道什么、相信什么、会怎么行动的能力）点出了全场核心论点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:26" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I want to tell you a little bit about the new knowledge layer, and I'll start with telling you a little bit about theory of mind. How many of you know what theory of mind is?" aria-label="回原文"></button>：

人们对自己用的产品「知道什么、不知道什么」往往缺乏判断——就像当年有人跑来 Yahoo Answers 问「我胖吗？」，可一个问答论坛根本不可能知道你胖不胖 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:22" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We used then AI, which is the stone age of AI, you know, it's like NLP and other statistical models to answer questions. But the funny thing is people would come to Yahoo and ask questions like, am I fat?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。主讲人认为，今天的 AI 领域正在犯同样性质的错，只是更微妙：我们期望智能体干资深员工的活，却从没给过它们资深员工脑子里的东西 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:48" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But this is a funny way to say, yeah, people have a very bad theory of mind on sort of what the products that they use, what information they have and what they don't have." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 知识分三类，智能体恰恰缺第三类

主讲人把知识粗分为三类 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:01" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We are making that mistake with AI today in a more subtle and less silly way, but in a just as fundamental of a mismatch. And so I will categorize roughly three kinds of knowledge." aria-label="回原文"></button>：

- **通用知识**：公开领域里的东西，模型训练时就学过，比如怎么改进 Rust 代码、解释法律条款。这块模型已经很擅长。
- **特定知识**：公司私有的具体信息，比如代码库里实现了什么、某个客户合同写了什么。这块靠 [[RAG|RAG]]（检索增强生成，先搜资料再回答）、搜索、Pinecone、向量数据库等工具早就基本解决了。
- **[[部落知识|部落知识]]**：公司内部的「通用常识」——我们怎么做事、流程是什么、谁负责什么、指导原则是什么。资深员工有、新员工没有，而且没法靠搜索一次捞出来，因为它是很多东西的总和，不是某份文档能写清的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There's still a lot to go, but we, by and large, know how to do it. And then I would say there is tribal knowledge. Tribal knowledge is the general knowledge inside the company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

问题就在这：如今大公司里大多数智能体的心智状态相当于一个新员工——很聪明、有工具、被寄予厚望，但对你的公司目标、文化、优先级、流程一无所知，大多数任务从零开始，每次新开会话都像入职第一天的应届生 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:09" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so our theory of mind of most of our agents in big companies today should be something like a new hire. They're brilliant, they have tools, we have very high expectations of them, but they are by and large clueless." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

所以主讲人的结论是：智能体需要的未必是更聪明、工具更好，而是需要一个新东西——**知识层** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:47" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="They really start to wake up, and every time you start your session, they're like a new hire on their first day on the job. So I would argue that what they need is not necessarily to be smarter, to have better tools, but to have something new, which I would call a knowledge layer going forward." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 知识层必须做到的三件事

按主讲人的定义，一个知识层至少要满足三点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:08" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Just I'll define it generally before I explain to you what we do. So at the very least, it needs to be persistent. We talked about this as the company knowledge, processes, capabilities, and goals, and so on." aria-label="回原文"></button>：

1. **持久**：承载公司知识、流程、能力和目标，这种东西拼起来是巨大工程，应该只做一次（或极少重做），且全公司所有智能体都能访问。
2. **高度专业化**：负责 HR 的智能体、优化内核的智能体、处理客户问题的智能体，关心的信息各不相同。好的实践是让公司里的**领域专家拥有并维护各自的知识**，确保它们与公司目标一致。
3. **始终保持最新**——这是最难的。公司会变，过时的信息要被弃用。他举了个例子：公司换了 CEO，第二天全公司的人都知道新 CEO 是谁，哪怕过去 10 年的 9000 份文档写的都是别人——这就是「压倒一切的部落知识」，任何 RAG 搜索都会告诉你旧答案，但现在新事实才作数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The very next day, everybody in the company knows who the CEO is. Even though 9,000 documents going from a day before yesterday to the last 10 years say it's somebody else, this has become common tribal knowledge immediately." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他特别澄清：知识层不是要取代现有的一切——智能体照样能访问 LLM、工具、本地文件、RAG 和各种搜索；知识层是一个独立的实体，本质上不同的一种数据源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:01" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And everything else, even every RAG search will tell you otherwise, this is now the overriding fact. I will argue before we move on that I'm not suggesting that this knowledge layer should replace everything else we're doing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Nexus 怎么做：连接器、上下文、任务、查询

Nexus 大致由四部分组成 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:40" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I want to tell you more about Nexus. Nexus is a product that we're, of course, it's a knowledge layer, but I want to explain to you what it does. Of course, so the four parts are roughly connectors, what I would call context tasks and queries." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**连接器**做的是显而易见的事：把数据带进平台、尊重访问权限。话题枯燥，但做过的人都知道这玩意儿做对有多费劲 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Now, so Connectos, of course, do the obvious things. There's nothing much to talk about them. They bring data into the platform, respect access rights and control and so on." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**上下文**是第一个真正有意思的部分，可以理解为知识层针对某个主题（HR、运营、工程……

）保存的资产总和。它不只有数据源连接，关键有两样东西：

- **[[Manifest（清单）|Manifest（清单）]]**——主讲人称之为第一个真正新颖的想法 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:08" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But then it has the manifest. The manifest is the first truly novel idea in an important entity. The way that we today give context to our agents is with skills and plug-ins and commands and markdown, fuzzy, write ourselves." aria-label="回原文"></button>。今天我们给智能体提供上下文，靠的是技能、插件、命令、手写 markdown，模糊又费劲；有了知识层，你只需在 manifest 里告诉 Nexus 你关心什么：要完成什么任务、追踪哪些实体、在意什么信息——**剩下的追踪文件、组织数据全是知识层的工作** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="In a knowledge layer, you don't do that anymore. You tell the knowledge layer, you tell Nexus what you care about in a manifest. You say what kind of tasks you want to complete, what entities you want to track, what kind of information you care about." aria-label="回原文"></button>。可以把 manifest 理解为「元知识文件或一组文件」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then it has the knowledge files themselves. So think about the manifest as the meta knowledge file or set of files. And then there's the knowledge." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **知识本身**，分五种载体 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then there's the knowledge. The knowledge itself, think about this as five types of The first kind is a semantic map." aria-label="回原文"></button>：一张语义地图（目录式文件，组织所有信息的位置、关键 schema、术语表）；一堆 markdown 文件存决策、记忆等非结构化信息（他提到这和 Andrei Karpathy 提出的 LLMWiki 想法很像，算是「加强版」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:42" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Who here has heard about LLMWiki? Okay, so LLMWiki is this idea that Andrei Karpathy suggested to have agents basically manage some stash of markdown files to sort of keep their own notes of stuff." aria-label="回原文"></button>）；SQL 表格存表格数据和需要记住的事实；一个向量数据库支持语义搜索、文本搜索和过滤；外加图实体和图索引，记住实体、关系、因果链。**这些全都是系统替你创建和维护的，你不用碰、也不用懂** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Context contains also a bunch of SQL tables for tabular data, for aggregation, for facts that they need to memorize, a vector database to be able to semantically search and text search and filter a bunch of information and all the information that it itself decides is necessary to keep and remember, and graph entities and graph indexes to remember, again, entities, relationships, causal chains, and so on." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**任务**是能对这些对象做的动作，主要是四个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:50" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="What are the verbs? What can we do with those objects? First of all is, of course, import, which is bring new information in the system, new files, new events, new concepts." aria-label="回原文"></button>：import（导入新信息，如新 PDF、会议记录、董事会演示文稿）；curate（整理，最精细的函数之一——拿着新文件、manifest 和语义地图，判断「我需要更新哪些表格、图关系、文本索引」，把新信息消化进上下文）；search（内部函数，用简单函数抽象对上下文的所有访问，让智能体不必了解内部结构）。

**查询（[[NoQL|NoQL]]）**是第二个最有意思的部分 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Now comes the second, I think, most interesting part of the system. Which is NoQL. NoQL is our way to specify how we want information out of the context." aria-label="回原文"></button>。它是指定「怎么从上下文取信息」的方式，关键在于：智能体可以**用 token 或美元为单位给出预算**——你是要一个即兴的快速答案，还是要我们全力以赴搜遍一切给你最准的答案？ <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:47" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's not enough to just ask questions. If you're an agent, it's really important for you to give us the budget in tokens or in dollars, how much time you want us to invest in this." aria-label="回原文"></button> 它是给智能体用的工具，不是聊天界面，返回的是智能体友好的「有依据的文本 + 结构化格式」。

## 打开引擎盖：「运行时编码智能体」

Nexus 与其他系统最大的不同，是它自称**[[运行时编码智能体|运行时编码智能体]]** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You see generated code on the bottom right. What Nexus does, which is very different than other systems, it's what we call a runtime coding agent. Unlike other coding systems," aria-label="回原文"></button>。普通编码系统的任务是：给我一个大代码库帮我改，改完部署——产物是软件，运行的东西里不再有模型。

Nexus 相反：**在查询时**，引擎本质上在搭一个类似 Jupyter Notebook / Python REPL 的环境，现场写代码、执行、拿到答案就用，不符合预期就重写那段代码 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is not what's happening here. In query time, you should think about the engine essentially building something like a Jupyter Notebook, something like a Python REPL, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。最终你得到的是一段完成任务、能保存、能重跑的代码——答案是代码写出来的，而不是查数据库查出来的，因此极其灵活。

有个很能说明问题的数字：因为接口被代码抽象了，他们给智能体的提示词从约 150,000 token（给智能体配齐所有工具）降到了不到 1,000 token（只说明从 Nexus 取数的全部接口） <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="If you replace, if you write software this way, the amount of prompting that you need is significantly reduced. We went down from having something like 150,000 tokens to give our agents all the tooling they need to less than 1,000 tokens to specify all the interfaces they need to get information out of Nexus." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

现场对比也很有说服力：同一个任务，走 Nexus 的智能体对上带着全部工具、但像「入职第一天员工」一样得自己找数据、搞清任务、写一堆代码的智能体——后者不仅慢得多、token 贵得多，**准确性反而差得多**，因为它没有公司里正确的上下文和目标 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:42" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then on the right is the same task given to an agent with all the tooling to complete the task. But remember, this on the right, it's severely handicapped. It's like the employee on the first day on the job." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

早期客户的数据横跨不同领域、企业、任务类型，结论一致 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:27" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We work obviously with a bunch of early access customers already. You see some results here, but I'll just sort of fly through them and say that across different domains, across different enterprises, across different kinds of tasks, we universally see the same thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>：

- token 减少 77%–90% 很常见；
- 提速 20%–30%，有时达 77%；
- 同时准确性显著提高。

主讲人的总结很直白：「这真的是一记漂亮的制胜球」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And the most important thing is it actually becomes a lot more accurate as well in the same time. So this is really, it's a slam dunk. I'll just wrap up by saying that Nexus is coming out of early access and into public preview literally tomorrow." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Nexus 在演讲次日即从早期访问进入公开预览 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So this is really, it's a slam dunk. I'll just wrap up by saying that Nexus is coming out of early access and into public preview literally tomorrow. So go try it out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **智能体的短板不是智力是部落知识**：怎么做事、谁负责什么、指导原则是什么——这些资深员工脑中的东西，智能体没有，所以每次会话都像入职第一天从零开始。
- **判断标准是持久、专业、够新**：知识层要只建一次全公司共用、按领域（HR/工程/客服）分工由领域专家拥有维护，并且信息过时要像「换 CEO 第二天全公司都知道」那样立刻被新事实覆盖。
- **用 manifest 声明代替手写上下文**：你只说要完成什么任务、追踪哪些实体、关心什么信息，语义地图、markdown、SQL 表、向量库、图索引这些载体的组织全交给系统。
- **查询时给预算**：让智能体用 token 或美元声明这次取数投入多少——快速即兴答案还是全面深挖，由预算决定。

  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
