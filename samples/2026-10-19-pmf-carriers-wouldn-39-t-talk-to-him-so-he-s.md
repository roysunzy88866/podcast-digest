# 从儿童手表到 AT&T:他如何拿下 90% 的大客户订单

[[Hermann Frank|Hermann Frank]] 和联合创始人 Dennis 创办了 [[Gigs|Gigs]]——一家让任何公司都能把手机套餐嵌入自家产品的公司。

听起来简单，但电信是被巨头垄断了几十年的行业，没人相信一个初创公司能撬动它。

这期播客里，他讲了怎么绕过壁垒、怎么找到第一个客户，以及怎么从 [[AT&T|AT&T]] 一路打到 [[Revolut|Revolut]] 和 [[Klarna|Klarna]]。

## 电信行业为什么值得重做一遍

时间回到 2018 年前后。Hermann 想创业，但没想好做什么，只确定一条：**找一个过去 20 年没被互联网改造过的行业**。电信跳了出来。

这个行业有多夸张？他举了个例子：Sam Altman 说要投 7 万亿美元建 AI 基础设施时，所有人都觉得他疯了。

但电信行业光是最近 10 年，就投了超过 3 万亿美元在基础设施上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:22" data-who="Hermann Frank" data-en="And so, and just tell me a little bit about the original business and maybe just the timeline in terms of when you start thinking about this. Yes. So we kind of settled on telecom and then we decided to look into, okay, what might be reoccurring problems in that industry?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而且这里面的技术被严重高估定价。Nokia、Ericsson、Huawei 卖给运营商的是封闭黑盒系统，价格是标准化硬件的约 100 倍。

## 在公寓里建基站，用免费 Wi-Fi 证明自己

他们给自己定了个规矩：所有硬件必须能在 Amazon 或 Alibaba 上买到，不许定制。

然后他们把通常跑在大型数据中心的电信后端，塞进一台英特尔 Mac 电脑，在公寓里搭了两个基站，走过去信号切换正常。

接着他们去柏林的咖啡馆挨家谈：==你们的 Wi-Fi 不安全，我们来装个更好的，还能让顾客在整个街区都能连上==。

作为交换，他们免费拿到了网络回传容量。一年下来，超过 100 万用户——大部分是游客。这成了柏林第一大公共 Wi-Fi 网络。

## 想收费时，才发现真正的大问题

问题来了：==怎么向这 100 万用户收钱==？他们想让用户买一张 eSIM，在网络覆盖内免费漫游，覆盖外用国内 LTE 网络。

这就需要一个接口，能像 [[Stripe|Stripe]] 处理支付那样处理电信业务。

这个接口不存在。他们钻进兔子洞才明白：整个电信行业根本没有面向开发者的开放体验。

而一旦解决这个问题，AWS 之于主机、Stripe 之于支付的故事就会在电信重演——所有新增需求和创新都会建立在这层基础设施上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:20" data-who="Hermann Frank" data-en="Exactly. We would have to do a lot of hard work to produce that outcome. But we understood if we can produce that outcome, the same thing that happened with AWS and hosting and Stripe and payments will happen in telecom. Basically everything that's net new is going to be built on top of that infrastructure. And then you kind of capture all of net new demand in one of the world's largest industries and pretty much all of net new innovation in one of the world's largest industries. And like that prospect for us is like, okay, like you can build a globally dominant horizontal player, like a global network if you start at the API layer and you eat your way down from there. So that's how the idea for GIGS came about." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这就是 Gigs 的起点，大约在 2020 年中。他们扔掉 Wi-Fi 业务，申请进入了创业加速器，后来在 2021 年冬季批次开始。

> 【背景】原文之外的信息：该创业加速器即 Y Combinator(常简称 YC),Gigs 于其 2021 年冬季批次入选。

## 运营商不理他，那就先找个“中间商”

讲故事容易，落地难。他们去见投资人，四处碰壁——投资人问问电信圈的朋友，得到的回答都是：运营商永远不会让你们这么干。

这话一开始是对的，运营商根本不跟他们谈。

解决办法是找了一家“[[聚合商|聚合商]]”——这类公司先跟运营商签下大合同，再把容量分给太小、运营商懒得服务的小客户。

他们在这家聚合商上搭出了产品第一版，经济账很烂，基本不赚钱 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:55" data-who="Hermann Frank" data-en="Dive deep around that. I'm not sure what that means. Who's like, what's the aggregator? Aggregators are these actors in the marketplace that they get a deal with a carrier and then they find smaller customers to basically split this pool because their customers are too small to get served by the carriers themselves or in our case, too small and slightly undesirable." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但有意思的是，他们在这条管道上做出了增长。运营商发现这家一直平平的聚合商突然涨了起来，主动来问怎么回事。

这一下敲开了门——第一份运营商直签合同，来自 AT&T。

## 第一个客户：被退货逼疯的儿童手表公司

最早的真实客户来自一位顾问的介绍：一家在 Amazon 上卖儿童智能手表的公司。

他们的痛点是，手表要用物理 SIM 卡，用户随便插一张，结果被 PIN 码锁住、或运营商把卡锁定只能和手机配合使用、套餐不支持手表——30% 到 40% 的手表被退货，用户以为是手表烂，其实只是 SIM 卡放错了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:58" data-who="受访嘉宾" data-en="Physical SIM and then the SIM wouldn't have a pin but the watch would get blocked because of the pin or the carrier locked the SIM to only work with smartphones and then so it wouldn't work with a smartwatch because the plan was not designed for a smartwatch. So all these things would happen and people would think okay this smartwatch sucks but it wasn't the smartwatch it was they put it in the wrong SIM. So they were looking to just launch their own mobile service and what we did is we embedded the SIMs at the factory into the device and when you were activating the device like in the parental app on the phone you could just subscribe to the service. You pay and instantly activated the subscription and it solved all their problems that 30-40% returns on these devices on Amazon. It created a subscription revenue stream and they started growing much faster." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Gigs 的方案：在工厂里就把 SIM 嵌进设备，家长在手机应用里激活设备时，直接订阅服务、付款、秒开通。

退货问题解决了，还多了一条订阅收入。

别小看这个市场。今天儿童智能手表给 Gigs 带来中八位数的年收入，年增长约 150%。

它不是能撑起千亿公司的业务，但是完美的切入点：**痛得足够狠，又足够宽容，容得下一个还不成熟的产品慢慢打磨**。

## 为什么银行是卖手机套餐的最佳人选

下一个台阶是金融科技公司。第一批客户都是这个领域里的大公司，之后是 Klarna、Revolut、[[Cash App|Cash App]]。==为什么银行该卖手机套餐==？他的逻辑很清晰：

银行已经完成了最严格的身份认证（KYC），激活订阅比谁都简单；手机和钱包在心理上是同类产品，出门都得带。

更关键的是结构性错位——运营商 30 年前被迫做手机分期，一直想甩掉这门生意，而银行恰恰想要更多信贷业务和触点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:38" data-who="之前 Hermann Frank" data-en="makes a lot of sense and do you find the offering is any different like for example I'm thinking in terms of the credit card that I have from Wealthsimple has no foreign exchange fees or whatever so it becomes a more global credit card which I think is a pretty common offering for these guys are you seeing the same things in plans where you can use it from anywhere in the world or are the terms of the plans pretty similar to what you would get from AT&amp;T T-Mobile directly" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

现在银行终于能把手机和套餐打包一起卖了。

产品也够狠。Revolut 在英国的套餐被他称为英国最好的手机套餐：本地不限流量、欧盟漫游额度、全球漫游和短信包，价格还很便宜。

Cash App 在美国推出了首个真正的无限流量套餐。

## 讲故事的秘诀：借一个别人已经相信的例子

这是个 15 年以上的长线生意，融资必须讲好故事。

Hermann 的方法是“双焦”式叙事：3 到 6 到 12 个月的计划要讲得极其细致，同时把长远的终点画出来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:30" data-who="之前 Hermann Frank" data-en="what was that second one like if you had to tell me in 30 seconds if everything goes right here's what we become what does that look like so you basically you imagine like if we execute this to its full ability what is the end state going to be like what is reality going to look like at the end of this journey for me personally there were two events that helped me understand this one was an interview I saw about Microsoft founders back in the day and someone said that the GUI was not a given thing it was debated in the industry if that is going to be the winning interface but the Microsoft founders just went out and just told everyone is going to be so they kind of willed that outcome into reality so it's like this kind of Platonian projection right like you just make the world see how things could be and what they are and ultimately that becomes reality and another one was a couple years ago I was visiting this original SpaceX headquarters and their founding VP of engineering showed me this video of the Falcon 9 launching and being recaptured and he said Elon created this video relatively at the beginning of SpaceX the video looked exactly how the thing looked like on TV when it finally happened and because what he was trying to sell was just so hard to imagine and considered so impossible that he created this ultra realistic version of it to just inspire people that that's what it is and that is what great storytelling is you show the final outcome you show the result and you convince people it's real" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他最受启发的两个例子：微软创始人当年直接宣布图形界面就是未来，硬是把结果“说”成了现实；

还有一家火箭公司在早期就对外发布了一段火箭发射回收的超逼真视频，多年后真实画面和视频几乎一模一样。

**好的讲故事，就是展示最终结果，并让人相信它是真的**。

> 【背景】火箭视频的例子通常指 Elon Musk 与 SpaceX：其在猎鹰 9 号发射回收实现前，就发布过一段高度逼真的火箭回收概念视频，多年后的真实画面与之几乎一致。

Gigs 早期最幸运的一点：不需要自己发明叙事。他们就是“做手机套餐的 Stripe”。

这个标签让不懂电信的投资人瞬间理解了逻辑，甚至吸引了一批早期 Stripe 员工加入。

## 拿下大企业：靠的不是销售技巧，是“外交”

他把拿下大客户总结为一场多层外交：

要说服一家市值几十亿甚至上百亿的公司，你得让创始人或高管层喜欢你，让运营团队喜欢你，让开发者喜欢你 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:03" data-who="Hermann Frank" data-en="Yeah, there's quite a range of companies. And I think today it's like a mid-eight-digit AR volume for us. So it's not a tiny market. If you want to land an enterprise customer, multi-billion or tens of billions of dollars in market cap, what you need to do is you need to convince several layers in that organization that it's a great idea to partner with you. Many times, including the founders or the C-level. You need to get the operating team to like you. You need to get the developers to like you. And ideally, it's not just like one person doing all that. If you want an enterprise deal, you want to find out very quickly if the guy you're talking to is a lone wolf. Is this like exploratory? So the quicker you ask the team you're working with to introduce other participants in the company, first, you find out if this is real and if this is serious. Second, it allows you to build those connections." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

具体做法是分层推进：

自己的产品工程团队对接对方的产品工程团队，销售副总裁对接对方产品副总裁，他自己直接联系创始人。

不同高度同时覆盖，形成对这家公司的 360 度视野——这是他们能赢下 90% 订单的原因。

另一个关键打法：招从目标客户公司体系里出来的人。他们招了很多前 Stripe 员工，而“Stripe 帮”遍布各家目标客户。

一个在圈内受信任的人，能让名单上 30% 到 50% 的目标都愿意接电话 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:15" data-who="之前 Hermann Frank" data-en="I'd say one of the other things that has worked really well for us is hire people who came up with the people working at your target customers we hire a lot of X-stripes the stripe mafia is everywhere they work in pretty much every company want their decision makers there so if you have someone who can go to 30 50 percent of your target list and they are well known and respected and trusted everybody is going to take a call everybody is going to listen and if you're in a competitive process you might have an advantage and trust as well this multi threading multi altitude in combination with having people who came up with the people that you want to work with is why we win as many deals as we do and you the listener will have but if you have a great product and do these things worked really well for us" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

怎么判断一个商机是真是假？尽快让对方把你介绍给公司里更多人。

第一，立刻知道这事是不是认真的；第二，顺势建立起更多连接。

对方愿意把工程师、市场人员拉进下一通电话，就是好信号——他们没资源浪费。

## 最后一条建议：创始人亲自卖，卖得比别人劝你放手更久

对早期创始人，他的建议是[[创始人主导销售|创始人主导销售]]。

没有名气、没有背书的时候，你有的只有信念——客户必须能从你身上每一根纤维里感受到你相信自己的产品 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:33" data-who="Hermann Frank" data-en="but listen to why you also need to iterate but it's tough it's especially tough getting started and when you don't have reputation when you don't have references it is just you it's going to be your sales craft your conviction you need to believe 100% when you talk people need to hear with every fiber of your being you believe in your product and you will do everything you possibly as a customer because literally your life depends on it so it's a sheer force of will to get past these initial conversations and do not exit sales to relieve the founder like I'm a massive believer in founder led sales and to a point where maybe others telling you you shouldn't be doing this anymore like do it past that point but not like forever but the more you are like the moment we started exiting processes like you see entropy creeping and like no one is going to be as convincing and as quick on their feet as a founder you kind of need to sell a little bit ahead of time and no one has the mandate to do it more than the founder right you know which risk you're willing to take you know how far you're gonna bend reality on that particular sales call and it's on you your employees right your sales well like the very good ones will know how to emulate your bullishness or your your boss but like most people won't and so if you want to achieve the maximum outcomes stay on there as long as you can as long as it makes sense for the business because no one will be a" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

只有创始人知道哪些风险可以承担、现实可以弯到什么程度。一旦开始把销售交出去，你会看到熵增悄悄出现。

出色的销售能模仿你的气场，但大多数人做不到。所以在还合理的范围内，尽量自己卖下去。

## 本集带走

- 电信是过去 10 年投了超 3 万亿美元却几乎没被软件改造的行业，机会在 API 层。
- 进不了大运营商的门，可以先接入聚合商，用增长数据把运营商吸引过来——Gigs 就这样拿到了 AT&T。
- 第一个市场选痛得最狠的小切口（儿童手表 30–40% 退货率），比一开始追大客户更能打磨产品。
- 卖给大企业是多层外交：创始人、高管、运营、开发者，每一层都要有人覆盖，这让他们赢下 90% 的订单。
- 讲愿景可以借力：用别人已经相信的成功公司做类比，直到你有能力讲自己的故事。
