## 采矿怎么改：套件装上老机器，让它自己跑

Atoms 旗下有一家叫 [[Pronto|Pronto]] 的公司，做的是采矿运输环节的[[自主性|自主化]]。做法不是造新车，而是拿一套[[套件|套件]]——传感器、计算设备、机械执行器——装到矿场已有的机器上，那台机器就变成自主驾驶的了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="they uh it's it's like the world's largest iron ore mine and you go and you get in a helicopter just going over one of the sites takes 30 minutes wow okay and it's fascinating it's so fascinating and you're learning how the system works" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

有些机器已经用了 20 年，本身连[[线控驾驶|线控驾驶]]（用电信号代替机械连接操控）都不是，得靠执行器去推物理按钮 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:26" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And why does it take a long time to install? Because that machine may not even be drive by wire. So you have a mechanical system." aria-label="回原文"></button>。安装完还要校准、做安全验证，加上现场的变革管理——从"每天早上人来上班、按严格流程操作"变成"人在控制中心监督自主作业"，这是完全不同的运作方式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:47" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So there's a process of getting it up. Then there's change management because that site's going from... There are people that show up in the morning." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

运输在采矿里是"心血管系统"——矿场里车辆最多、跑得最勤的环节 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:21" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I mean, look, I would say the way we think about it is the haulage part of a mine is where most of the vehicles are. And we think of haulage as the cardiovascular system of a mine." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Pronto 先从运输切入，通过 API 和其他设备（钻探、爆破、装载、破碎）做编排协调，不一步吃下所有环节 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So we're obviously very connected to all the other machines, but we don't do all the other machines. So we're like in an ecosystem. So we can work with them where there's APIs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最终目标是"[[无人进入矿山|无人进入矿山]]"——矿坑里没有人，只有自主设备在运转 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I mean, look, we ultimately, I mean, if you go in the mining industry, there's like this term, it's called no entry mine. A no entry mine is a mine where there are no people in the pit." aria-label="回原文"></button>。目前一台装满矿石的运输车重 200 万磅，在越野路面上以 35 英里时速自主行驶 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:19" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's super fascinating. And then you're talking about loaded a 2 million pound machine that's moving potentially 35 miles an hour down the road. It's an off-road thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 生产力提升从哪来：两件事叠加

[[生产力|生产力]]提升不是单一因素，是两件事叠在一起：第一，每台机器每小时干得更多；第二，工时、排班和安全协议都变了——不需要人在危险环境里轮班，24 小时运转变得可行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So, yeah, I mean, There's two parts to the productivity gain. First is the machine per hour doing more." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Travis 估计，全部加起来最终可能提升 30% 到 40% 的生产力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So there's a lot of things like this that pile onto each other. My guess is you could even end up 30%, 40% more productive at the end of all of it. And when you do that, the opportunity speaks for itself." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这对金矿、锂矿效果最直接——多挖出来的矿就是钱。采石场不太一样，因为下游水泥需求是固定的，不能无限多挖，所以那边更多是降运营成本的逻辑，利润也更薄 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That's lithium. We also go all the way down to quarries. Quarries are different because quarries are basically, it's about cement, let's just say." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 市场拓展：不是去开会，是飞进丛林

做消费业务时，Travis 的打法是把年轻人派到一个新城做营销；做工业 AI 的市场拓展则是飞到亚马逊丛林深处、飞到沙特边境，信号被干扰了就让飞行员目视降落 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:10" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then, you know, I went from Brazil and then straight from there dropped into the border between Iraq and Saudi, on the Saudi side. So we have a phosphate mine that we're doing stuff there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他在巴西一个飞越单个矿区就要 30 分钟的巨型铁矿待了几天，客户叫 Valet <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:01" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And you see, like, we took, we were there for a couple days because we already have customers there. A customer is called Valet. It's a massive mining company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这类客户的决策流程类似企业软件——先做试点，证明自主系统达到了人类水平甚至超越人类水平之后，再从几台车扩展到整个车队的全部车辆 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:21" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so we're in that place on Pronto where that momentum is taking hold because there's enough proof points where it's just working in so many different places where people are like, all right, let's go." aria-label="回原文"></button>。"一旦它奏效了，它就会做大" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:55" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But once it works, and in mining that means human productivity, human level, better than human productivity. Once it works, It goes big." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 运输：机器人的"轮基"

Atoms 的运输业务被他叫作"[[机器人的轮基|机器人的轮基]]"——不做通用人形机器人，做专用工业轮式机器人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:08" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So number one is it's... So I call it wheelbase for robots, which is if you're going to do specialized robots that move and act in the physical world... They're either humanoids, which we're not." aria-label="回原文"></button>。在食品业务里，供应链进入工厂的是货运车辆，出来的是"自主墨西哥卷饼"——一个能保温的轮上盒子，把食物送到家门口，配送成本 75 美分，而不是现在外卖平台每次 12 美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:53" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We probably should just turn that into a robot that moves stuff and actually interfaces with our facility in a really cool way. When the food is coming out of our facilities, there's probably like a machine that holds food at temperature that's like a box on wheels." aria-label="回原文"></button>。更广泛地看，光是叉车就是一个巨大市场——他提到一家公司每年在叉车人工上花 35 亿美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I saw something that was like, think about just forklifts. I know a company, remain unnamed, that's spending three and a half, this is on the supply chain side, three and a half billion dollars a year on forklift labor." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 自动化对就业：价格下降创造新需求

关于"机器人抢工作"的问题，Travis 的逻辑很直接：被自动化的东西价格会下降，价格下降释放出消费者的剩余资金，这些钱最终只会流向人类（因为机器人没有银行账户），然后去消费其他东西 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:46" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Yeah. Okay, when the price of food goes down, remember, robots don't have bank accounts. When the price of food goes down, what happens?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。新的需求会催生现在还不知道的新岗位。前提是人类仍然有机器人做不到的事，"那就是高歌猛进的时刻" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:51" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There's going to be a hundred other new things that come out because there's this excess of capital and progress continues. And as long as humans still have things that we do that robots cannot, it's go-go time, man." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 招高管：解决问题比会管理重要

Travis 说他对高管的要求有两件事：能在规模上组织和管理，以及能做史诗级的问题解决者。这两者像左撇子和右撇子，很少人两头都强 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And there's two things you want an executive to do. You want them to be able to organize at scale, organize and manage at scale, lead at scale. You also want them to be epic problem solvers, the most strategic badass problem solvers alive." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他多年的结论是：解决问题比管理能力更重要。如果一个人只会管理但不会解决问题，"他会以超级有组织的方式做荒谬的事情" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:49" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But I have come to the conclusion over my years doing the stuff is the problem solving is the most important thing. If you get somebody who organizes and manages well but cannot solve a problem," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的管理风格是"首席问题解决者"——挑最具影响力、没人解决的问题来盯，直属下属也必须是受委任的问题解决者，这样一层层往下传 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But if I don't have the management capacity, then I'm effed. So the management style that I do is sort of problem solver in chief, which is I take the most impactful problems that are not being solved." aria-label="回原文"></button>。面试时则要模拟一起工作的感觉，让入职第一天就像第二周，降低上手风险 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:27" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That's the number one. And then on the interview process, simulate what it's like working together so that day one really feels like week two. And day one, you better be excited." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 监管：联邦优先权什么时候好？

被问到 AI 实验室争联邦优先权（联邦统一监管、压过各州自定规则）的事，Travis 说得很直白：当你想搞监管俘获、把竞争对手挤出去的时候，联邦优先权就是好的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's hard to see where the chips are falling. Federal preemption is good when you are pro-regulatory capture. OK." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他在 Uber 时从没推动过任何只对自己有利的规则，"我们一直试图打开市场，让最优秀的人获胜" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:45" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We basically never, ever ...proposed or pushed any rule that would be beneficial to us versus somebody else. We always were trying to open up the market and we said let the best man win and we just went for it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还提到，交通领域每一条系统性的坏规则，背后几乎都有起诉律师和保险公司在推——保险公司按精算表从事故中赚利润，"他们喜欢在他们计划范围内的事故" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:12" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="...was most likely pushed by the trial lawyers and the insurance companies. Wow. Every single bad rule that's weird and dumb, the insurance companies and the trial lawyers were in the game, big time." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 安全：做东西得是人类想要的

聊到阿西莫夫的机器人三定律和 AI 对齐问题，Travis 的起点不是技术方案，而是一个很朴素的创业经验：如果你造了没人喜欢的东西，你不会成功 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I have failed. And when I think about why I failed, it's usually because I was building something that nobody liked. So if you build something that people don't like, I don't think you're going to succeed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。延伸到 AI——如果你造了反人类的东西，你也走不远。

而且现在阶段的 AI 有一个特征："它们想要取悦我们" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But what do those AIs really, really want to do almost too much? They want to please us. So I just think if you're not making stuff that humans want," aria-label="回原文"></button>。在机器人拥有自己银行账户之前，机器人归人所有，人根据你创造的价值付费，所以"如果你在做人类不喜欢的事情，你就完了" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:18" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="They are paying based on the value you're bringing them, because they like your stuff. So if you are doing things that humans don't like, you're done. And trust me, I've done it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **套件改造而非造新车**：拿传感器、计算设备和执行器装到现有矿场机器上，包括那些 20 年前没有线控驾驶的老设备，让它变成自主运行的。
- **生产力提升是两件事叠加**：机器每小时干得更多 + 24 小时不间断运转（不需要人在危险环境轮班），合计可能提升 30%–40%。
- **试点→扩展的企业软件模式**：先证明自主系统达到甚至超越人类生产力水平，客户就会从几台车扩展到全部车辆。
- **招高管，解决问题能力压过管理能力**：只会管理不会解决问题的人，会"以超级有组织的方式做荒谬的事"；管理风格是首席问题解决者，一层层传导。
- **被自动化的事价格下降，释放的钱流向人类**：机器人没有银行账户，剩余资金最终由人类消费，催生新需求和新岗位。
- **联邦统一监管利好谁**：想搞监管俘获、挤掉竞争对手的时候，联邦优先权就是好东西。

    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
