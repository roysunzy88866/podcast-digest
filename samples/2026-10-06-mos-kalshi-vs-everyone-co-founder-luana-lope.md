把一家 2021 年才上线的产品，做到两年估值从 50 亿美元冲到 220 亿——[[Kalshi|Kalshi]] 靠的不是赌博，而是「交易未来」。

今天这期节目的主角是 Kalshi 的联合创始人、总裁兼 COO Luana Lopez Lara,她也是「世界上最年轻的白手起家女性亿万富翁」。

主持人 [[Bob Safian|Bob Safian]] 问她当亿万富翁什么感觉，她说生活其实没变：起床、健身、办公室待一整天、回家陪丈夫和狗；

这个身份「只是又一个指标，显示这家公司走了多远」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:35" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="But I think that what's most exciting is about it really is just like another... I think it's just another kind of metric maybe that shows how far the company has gone and how hard we've worked and it's kind of paid off in that way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她强调这不是一夜成功：

公司 2018 年就创立了，光是为了和联邦政府([[CFTC|CFTC]],美国商品期货交易委员会)一起搞清楚「怎么安全、受监管地把[[预测市场|预测市场]]带到美国」，就花了四年才推出产品。

大选前后爆发的增长，是「八年工作加上赢下对 CFTC 诉讼那一刻的复利」——「当我们的时刻来临时，我们准备得非常充分」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:54" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="So I think it's a mix of both. I think we were very prepared when our time came. That kind of hockey stick growth, do you have to pinch yourself?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

增长太快反而帮了他们：团队一直保持着早期创业公司的心态，「对创业公司来说最重要的就是速度」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:42" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="We just had to like really keep going on like building the product as fast as we could. Early stage team and early stage mentality, very intense in time of like work intensity, keeping the speed, which I think is the most important thing for startups is the speed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

两年前一年做不满 1000 万美元，现在一天的交易额就远超当年一整年 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:51" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Early stage team and early stage mentality, very intense in time of like work intensity, keeping the speed, which I think is the most important thing for startups is the speed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 交易所不是庄家：为什么它和 FanDuel 根本不同

各州正以「无执照赌博」追查 Kalshi——联邦上诉法院刚裁定俄亥俄州和田纳西州可用赌博法监管它，总部所在的纽约州更是单独起诉索赔 360 亿美元。

Luana 的回应是：这些诉讼各有各的法律论点，但退一步看，关键在于机制完全不同 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:58" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="The more important part, if you take a step back, is that like... The mechanics of how Cauchy operates in a sportsbook is completely different, right? And that's why they are regulated different ways." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Kalshi 是受联邦监管的**[[交易所|交易所]]**：用户和用户互相交易，Kalshi 不定价格、不定赔率、不与用户对赌，只收交易手续费；

就像纽约证券交易所——她反问，如果你只有在纽约才能买纽交所的股票，价格会差得多，市场也不会有[[流动性|流动性]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:21" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="What matters the most here is liquidity and making sure that we have national liquidity to build upon. Imagine if you have, for example, the New York Stock Exchange, but you could only buy stocks in the New York Stock Exchange if you're in New York." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而 sportsbook([[体育博彩庄家|体育博彩庄家]])的收入「等于客户的亏损，客户亏得越多他们赚得越多」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:45" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="A sportsbook is actually completely different. They make money, their revenue is equal to customer losses. The more the customers lose, the more money they make." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

所以激励机制完全相反：庄家会在你赢钱后限制你继续参与，「我们恰恰相反，我们想要赢家」、想要价格竞争 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:55" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="The incentive is not to make people lose because we don't make money when people lose. Because of that as well, we don't cap our winners. So, you know, if you go to a sportsbook or casino, you start making money, they'll make sure that you cannot participate anymore." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她的底气之一来自对手的嘴：DraftKings 的 CEO Jason 在最新财报里说，体育博彩和预测市场之间「没有任何相互蚕食，因为它们从根本上不同」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:43" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Like that's not the case of an exchange. And on the DraftKings defendable points, actually interesting because Jason, CEO of DraftKings, actually in their latest earnings said that they're seeing no cannibalization between sports betting and prediction markets because they are fundamentally different." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

至于[[投机|投机]]？她毫不讳言：「投机发生在所有金融市场——加密货币、股票、期权、期货，而且投机驱动流动性」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:16" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="You know, crypto, stocks, options, futures, there's speculation in all of them. And speculation is actually very important because it drives liquidity, right? If you, obviously every market that we're talking about, and it's actually one of the biggest growing parts of Kaoshi right now, is the hedging and kind of small business hedging." aria-label="回原文"></button>。庄家总是赢，交易所不是。

## 对冲：给小企业的「保险」

Kalshi 增长最快的业务之一，是小企业[[对冲|对冲]]。她最喜欢的故事：

加州一个山羊养殖户，面临一项会把劳动力成本抬高 4 倍的新立法，于是在 Kalshi 上「押注这件他不希望发生的事」，真发生了就能拿钱，像保险一样 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:51" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="One of my favorite stories actually is this goat farmer in California. And by the way, I don't know, I'm not a farmer, I don't know, but so I might say something wrong, but..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

如今全美每个州都有小企业在 Kalshi 上对冲天然气价格、柴油、天气、体育、政治 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:18" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="And that part of business is growing so much. Like we have like small businesses in every single state of the country hedging things like gas prices, diesel, weather, sports, politics." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最反直觉的发现：问这些小企业为什么不去买存在已久、流动性好的石油期货，每一次的回答都是「等等，那是什么？怎么操作？」

——「这就像一个给大机构玩的游戏，小企业以前根本没有渠道接触这些东西」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:50" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Because, like, just don't understand. It's like a game for, like, big institutions that are just, like, they try to hedge at some certain, like, a big scale, but you don't have access to these things before, like, small businesses don't have access to these things, and it's something that we are really excited to bring, and it's part, for example, of our partnership now with the U.S. Hispanic Chamber of Commerce." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这也是 Kalshi 与美国西班牙裔商会合作的原因：得州企业一直说选举带来的风险太多，需要有办法对冲 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:13" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Like, businesses in Texas have been... Telling me, like, we have so much risk associated with the elections. We need a way to hedge it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 为什么预测市场比民调准

预测市场有效的原理，Luana 拆成两层：

第一层是激励——「如果我告诉你，明天下雨我就给你 100 美元，你第一件事就是打开天气网站、看窗外、甚至打电话问你妈」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:46" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Yes, it's going to rain. But if I tell you I'll give you $100 if it's going to rain tomorrow, the first thing you're going to do is you're going to open your phone and you're going to open the website, like the weather.com, you're going to look outside, you're going to maybe call your, you know, your mom and ask, like, you know, do you think it's going to rain tomorrow?" aria-label="回原文"></button>——对了赚钱、错了亏钱，人们就会去研究、把信息带进市场；

第二层是价格竞争，所有人争最优价格，不认同现价的人会交易、推动价格，「直到每个人都认同那个价格是公平的」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:33" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="And that price competition, that trading of price competition, really makes the market get to the best forecasted value. That is, everyone in the market kind of agrees that price is fair." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

市场 24 小时开放，每条新消息都有人为了赚钱去修正价格，所以它成了「对未来的实时预测」。

对比很尖锐：你可以上电视说明天有衰退、赚一堆点击，「说错了无所谓；但在市场上，你说得对不对真的非常重要」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:14" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="And because of that, markets become kind of efficient and they become the best forecast and the real-time forecast of what's going to happen in the future. You can go on TV and say there's a recession tomorrow and you know you're gonna get a lot of clicks everyone's gonna watch because you're you know doomsday and whatever but it doesn't really matter if you're not right but on markets it really really matters if you're right." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Kalshi 自己发布的校准研究报告显示——所谓校准，就是市场说 70% 概率的事是否真的十次发生七次——结果非常好，越临近结算日准确率越高，「校准和准确性会达到 90 多的高位」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:42" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="That calibration and prediction marks is very good. And as you get closer and closer to the date or the resolution date, they get even like high 90s on calibration and kind of accuracy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

具体到临近的美国中期选举：民调是自上而下，某个编辑委员会汇总后告诉你一个数；

预测市场是自下而上，「数百万人所想所做」的聚合，是「第一次真正由人们主导、而非精英宣布的信息」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:11" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="And I think it's like one of the first times that you really see Kind of like information that's actually led by people versus like the elites just coming and saying, this is what's going to happen." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

针对「参众两院谁控制」这类大市场，「我觉得这基本上就是你能达到的最准确的程度了」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:20" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="With our big markets, like, you know, like who's going to take control of the Senate or control of the House? I think it's like as accurate as you're going to ever get." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她还要反复做教育：

概率不是确定性，1% 不是永不发生，「40% 不意味着处于劣势的那个人永远不会赢」——如果走出家门有 40% 概率被公交车撞，你就不会出门了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:42" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="In an election that's like a 60% chance of someone winning. If I told you, if you walk outside right now, there's a 40% chance to get hit by a bus, you're not going to walk outside because, you know, 40% is pretty high." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

由于成千上万个选举市场难以逐一跟踪，他们上线了「权力平衡指数」(K-POW),把所有选举汇总成一个可追踪情绪倾斜的指数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:28" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="All those markets. So one thing that we launched was like our balance of power index, like a K-POW. It aggregates all of that in one index that you can see kind of sentiments trending more Democrat, more Republican, and you can kind of track with time kind of how the aggregate or the index of all those elections coming together." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

还有一个数据：约 70% 的 Kalshi 访客只看数据、不下注——Luana 说实际更高，「现在可能是 78%」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:54" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="Is that true? I think it's actually higher, maybe 78% now. And it goes back to what we talked about on the final, like this betting versus not, like how many people would open a sports betting app to just look at the odds, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这些人把它当「替代 Twitter、替代《纽约时报》」的信息源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:23" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="But the amount of people coming to Couch to consume information is because they really understand the value of that information that they can't get anywhere else, which is this unbiased, market-driven, kind of real-time information." aria-label="回原文"></button>:「Twitter 激励的是标题党，最疯的东西点赞最多；

预测市场恰恰相反——说荒谬的话，你只会亏钱」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:49" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="On Couch or prediction markets in general, it's actually the opposite. If you're saying something absurd, you're just going to lose money. Kalshi recently introduced margin trading, letting users borrow money to make bigger trades." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 争议、内幕交易与监管

关于那条引发争议的伊朗最高领导人合约(最终回撤并补偿了超 200 万美元)：Kalshi 因受联邦监管，不允许战争、恐怖主义、暗杀类市场；

但「一个人失去权力有很多种方式」——马杜罗下台就不是暗杀，且有明确的油价影响。

他们的划线方式是：市场照常运作，但一旦真发生暗杀或恐怖事件，「撤销所有交易，没有人能从中获利」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:52" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="The impact, the clear economic impact that had, for example, in like price of oil. So what we do for these markets is actually, we let that market operate, but what we do is if there is an assassination or a terrorism or a case or something like that, what we do is we void every trade so no one can profit from it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

补偿的原因不是自认有错，而是产品没把撤销机制向用户解释清楚；此后他们对上架市场「更有选择性得多了」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:19" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="And I think that that's what got confused. Now, after this market, we actually have been way more selective with the markets that we list. I've seen some Federal Reserve studies that say that prediction markets are more accurate than almost any forecasting method." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

[[内幕交易|内幕交易]]方面，Kalshi 有整支监控团队，已经办出 George Santos(试图交易自己出席国情咨文)和白宫提词器操作员等多起执法案例。

提词器那个案子靠的是对交易本身跑 AI 机器学习找异常(比如新账户以极不同寻常的方式交易)，再由合规团队做全面调查 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:23" data-who="Luana Lopez Lara" data-en="We know the information from the person. We do a lot of like AI machine learning on the trades themselves to see if there are anomalies or things that are like..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

- **交易所和庄家的分界线在激励**：庄家收入=客户亏损、会限制赢家；交易所靠手续费，用户互为对手方，所以想要赢家和价格竞争。这是 Kalshi 全部法律与公关论述的地基。

- **对冲是被忽略的大市场**：小企业买不到传统期货工具(根本不知道它存在)，把政治、天气、价格风险做成可交易的合约，是 Kalshi 增长最快的业务之一。

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);

        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),

      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
