# 造出超级智能的人，彼此恨之入骨

Big Technology Podcast 请来了《纽约时报》记者 [[Kevin Roose|Kevin Roose]]，聊他的新书《The [[AGI|AGI]] Chronicles》。

他花了数年时间采访 [[OpenAI|OpenAI]]、[[Anthropic|Anthropic]]、[[Google DeepMind|Google DeepMind]] 内部的 150 多人，本想写一部严肃的技术史，结果发现这其实是一个关于仇恨、猜疑与信仰的故事——而主角们正在建造人类历史上最强大的技术。

## 一群连自己公司都管不好的人，要保护我们？

书里的核心矛盾很扎眼：Elon Musk 与 OpenAI 决裂、Sam Altman 被亲密同事赶下台、有 OpenAI 的人因为受不了而出走创办 Anthropic、Google 内部 Brain 和 DeepMind 内斗不断。

几乎每个主角都卷入过戏剧性的政治风波。

> 【背景】出走创办 Anthropic 的人指 Dario Amodei,他曾任 OpenAI 高管。

而就是这些人，一边告诉公众这项技术危险到需要防备，一边根本坐不到一张桌子上谈安全。

Roose 写了一句很妙的话：「Machines of Love and Grace would be built by humans of bitter spite.」——**慈爱优雅的机器，将由满怀怨毒的人类建造**。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:04" data-who="Roose" data-en="You wrote, Machines of Love and Grace would be built by humans of bitter spite. So I get your surprise and I get the background here. But again, like when we think about the big picture of AI, I'm trying to think about whether we should be reassured" aria-label="回原文"></button>

他原本以为这只是可口可乐对百事式的商战，后来发现更像罗马对迦太基：

10 年前 Dario Amade、Ilya Satzkever、Sam Altman 和 Elon Musk 还是 OpenAI 的同事，如今都在为不同的 AI 项目工作，很多人处于互相撕咬的状态。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:14" data-who="Roose" data-en="And I had thought this was more like Coke and Pepsi, like kind of a normal business rivalry, but it was actually more like Rome and Carthage. Like these people cannot stand each other." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 问题不在谁坏，而在圈子太小

Roose 的结论不是某个具体的人不可信，而是决策权太集中：过去十年，AI 的所有关键决定，大约出自几十个人之手。

不管这些人动机纯不纯，**这么小的一群人秘密掌控并治理这样的技术，本身就不可信**。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:07" data-who="Roose" data-en="I think there are certain groups like like I just mainly think that the group of people who have been building this stuff in secret for the last decade is too small." aria-label="回原文"></button>

他采访后的感受是更不放心了。

起初他觉得实验室里至少有大人、有负责任的人——AI 领袖从第一天起就公开说自己害怕这项技术，这比当年只谈好处的社交媒体强多了。

但很快他看清了格局：这是一场停不下来的竞赛，OpenAI 出新模型，Anthropic 必须跟上，Google 又必须跟上。

生物學里管这叫 Red Queen's race——不管你跑多快，别人总在更快，你拼命奔跑只为留在原地。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:03" data-who="Roose" data-en="And, you know, there's this concept of the Red Queens race, which is a term from evolutionary biology, which basically means like... You just are in sort of a survival of the fittest competition among your evolutionary rivals." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 仇恨从哪来？因为赌注是史上最重要

为什么这些人斗得这么凶？

Roose 的解释是：他们真心相信这是人类造过的最重要的技术，谁先造出来谁就是世界上最强大的实体。

如果争的只是下个季度的营收，不会有人这么拼命。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:35" data-who="Roose" data-en="If they actually do build something that is smarter than humans, and I don't see any real technical reason that they can't, I mean, that would be incredibly transformative." aria-label="回原文"></button>

书里有个好玩的细节：Demis Hassabis 始终想不通 Sam Altman 为什么要造 AGI，认定他背后藏着权力野心。而在 Dario 眼里，Sam 谈安全只是野心的遮羞布。

有意思的是，所有人都觉得别人是为了权力，只有自己动机纯粹。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:14" data-who="Roose" data-en="And I think for Demis, it's probably, yeah, some version of what you said, like he's, you know, he's in it for the power, or he's... Demis thinks Sam is in it for the power." aria-label="回原文"></button>

几个人的画像也很有味道。

Sam 的超能力同时是他的弱点：他是世界上最强的融资者和推销员，能一眼看出你要什么然后给你——或者至少让你以为给了你。

这也是他落下滑头的名声的原因。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:23" data-who="Roose" data-en="Several people described him as a person who can meet you and figure out exactly what you want and give it to you, or at least appear to give it to you. And he's very good at kind of high EQ reading the room and understanding what people want him to do in any given moment." aria-label="回原文"></button>

Dario 则相反，一位熟人评价他「是个连撒谎救命都不会的人」，这「双数日子是资产，单数日子是负债」。

他聪明、有原则，也固执得像道德绝对主义者。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:22" data-who="Roose" data-en="I... Someone who knows him quite well told me that Dario is a guy who could not tell a lie to save his life, which they said is an asset on even-numbered days and a liability on odd-numbered days." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 信念，才是 OpenAI 依然领先的原因

一个反直觉的问题：OpenAI 流失了那么多顶尖人才——Dario、Ilya、后来跳去 [[Meta|Meta]] 的一批人——==为什么还领先==？

Roose 的答案是：[[信念|信念]]。写到一半他发现，这不是一本关于技术的书，而是一本关于信仰的书。

OpenAI 和 Anthropic 是信念最强的两家公司，人人都真的相信自己正在造改变世界的[[超级智能|超级智能]]。

Google 什么都有——资源、人才、突破、transformer 都是它发明的——唯独缺信念，领导者并不像 Dario 和 Sam 那样真的信 AGI。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:05" data-who="Roose" data-en="They made all of the breakthroughs. They had all the ingredients except for belief. The leaders of that company did not believe and may still not believe in AGI the way that people like Dario and Sam and Demis do." aria-label="回原文"></button>

Google 内部甚至有过著名的备忘录大战：一位研究员发内部信说必须谈 AGI，否则既抢不到想造 AGI 的人才，也不会认真对待 AGI 的风险；

马上有人回信说别用这个词，不科学。这场笔仗 Google 员工多年后还在谈论。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="58:42" data-who="Roose" data-en="Yeah, they were going to get there. They still refer to it as like the memo battle, you know, where there was like one researcher writes this memo saying like, hey guys, like we got to talk about AGI because not only are we missing out on like good talent because they want to build AGI, they don't want to build like autocomplete for Gmail." aria-label="回原文"></button>

信念的另一个体现是敢下注。当初 OpenAI 大手笔投算力时，连 Dario 都觉得这帮人疯了。

现在所有人都跟进了，但 OpenAI 赢在它在该赌不赌得明的问题上，先赌了。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="58:03" data-who="Roose" data-en="Well, Dario is like, they're YOLOing away. They're YOLOing. Even Dario is like, these guys are crazy." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 造 AI 的人，自己也被 AI 吓到

书里最好的一类故事，是造技术的人被自己的技术吓到。

GPT-1 时代，OpenAI 的研究员围着 Alec Radford 的电脑屏幕，发现一个用乱七八糟的 Reddit 链接训出来的模型，居然能回答各种问题、切换各种语气，一群人在 Slack 里发爆头表情包。

后来[[推理模型|推理模型]]出现前夜，2023 年某个深夜，两位研究员看着 Q* 模型的第一批思维链，意识到模型不用重新训练、光靠推理就能变聪明，深夜给 Sam 和 Mira 发消息，感觉世界要完蛋了。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:38" data-who="Roose" data-en="Those are the moments that I was like, oh, these people are also surprised by how quickly their technology is progressing. You see that again with the reasoning models." aria-label="回原文"></button>

Roose 说，正是这一步，让模型从数不清 strawberry 里有几个 r，三年内做到解千禧年大奖难题。这也是他反对AI 只是炒作论的原因：

三年前连基本算术都不行的东西，现在已经这样了，**质疑者只是没看懂指数曲线**——他觉得有点像新冠早期，西雅图才 40 个病例的时刻。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:22" data-who="Roose" data-en="It feels a little bit to me like the beginning days of COVID where it was like you would see these reports of like people, you know, not shaking hands at venture capital firms or they're, you know, somebody would say, oh, there are 40 cases in Seattle." aria-label="回原文"></button>

他甚至因此改变了自己。他曾担心自己像当年对待加密货币一样被忽悠，但区别在于：

加密货币从没成为他日常生活的必需品，而 AI 工具他天天用、越用越多。用一用，你就不会再怀疑它是不是真的了。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:13" data-who="Roose" data-en="But I think with AI, like I have stopped. Doubting the transformative potential of the products because I use them. Like, I think I'm unlike many journalists who cover AI in that I like AI and I use it all the time, not to write, but to like do all, you know, I'm starting a company now." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 更吓人的是：他们不是失控，是在主动交权

有个流行的说法是实验室正在失去对模型的控制。Roose 认为恰恰说反了：**他们是在主动把控制权交给模型**。

Anthropic 披露，Claude 已经在主导其 26% 的 AI 研发项目——不是当编程助手，是当项目负责人。OpenAI 的目标则是造出一个全自动的 AI 研究员。

一旦这个闭环合上，新模型可能不再半年一代，而是以机器的速度迭代。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:07" data-who="Roose" data-en="Anthropic is sort of trying to give, and OpenAI has done versions of this too, they're trying to give people a sense of how much of their own research is being automated by their own tools right now." aria-label="回原文"></button>

Roose 的态度很明确：他信[[递归自我改进|递归自我改进]]是可能的，也认为这很危险，实验室应该停手。

但他同时是暂停派而非停止派：他希望 AI 治好癌症、帮他报销差旅，只是希望人类先慢下来，赢得研发控制方法的时间。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:50" data-who="Roose" data-en="I believe that the labs are racing to do it, and I think it's dangerous. I think they should stop. But there was a statistic that came out the other day that I would love to know what you think of this." aria-label="回原文"></button>

那什么能让大家真的慢下来？他的答案有点黑色幽默：可能得等一场灾难。

9·11 之后爱国者法案几周就通过，三里岛事故后美国核电站停建了几十年。

他并不希望灾难发生，但那可能是现状下唯一能改变节奏的情景。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:14" data-who="Roose" data-en="If there was some kind of black swan event, I mean, we saw regulators move fast after 9-11 to do the Patriot Act. We saw after the Three Mile Island nuclear incident, there was this incredible outcry that basically stopped" aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 本集带走

- AI 圈的恩怨不是普通商战，而是前同事间的深仇，核心决策圈只有几十人，这本身就是治理风险。
- OpenAI 屡经动荡仍领先的关键不是人才，而是从上到下真信 AGI 的组织信念——Google 恰恰输在这里。
- 连造模型的人都不断被自己技术的进步速度惊到：推理范式让模型三年内从数不清字母到解千禧年难题。
- 实验室不是失去对 AI 的控制，而是主动把研发交给 AI——Anthropic 称 Claude 已主导 26% 的研发项目。
- Roose 自称暂停派：不反对造，但认为能力已跑在安全研究前面，人类需要用灾难以外的方式争取到减速的机会。

  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
