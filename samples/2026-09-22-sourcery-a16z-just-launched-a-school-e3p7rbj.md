# a16z 办校：不看你成绩，只看你造了什么

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Goggin</b> · 连续创业者 · 2026-09-23</div>

大学之外再多一个选项，而且是一所不考试、不要成绩、只看你做没做出过东西的住宿制学院——这就是 a16z 今天官宣的 [[Horowitz Andreessen Academy|Horowitz Andreessen Academy]]:一个面向高中毕业生的旧金山住宿项目，想帮年轻人去科技公司找工作或直接创业。主导这件事的 [[Goggin|Goggin]] 是个连续创业者：13 岁就在高中「非法地不非法」地做生意(注册成非营利、收益全归学校)，后来联合创办了在线课程平台 Udemy,中间创过失败的公司 Sprig,现在经营课程平台 Maven。他自己就是那个「在大学里憋得难受、只想赶紧出去做东西」的人——三年打了七份工、申请的顶尖学校全被拒只进了 UC Berkeley,所以这一次他要给当年的自己造一个去处 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:44" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And this is the type of experience that we want to create for the student who comes to Horowitz Andreessen Academy. So how does your career in education building companies result to partnering with Mark and Eric on this?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 和大学最根本的区别：不修学分，只做项目

学院完全不做学历认证，不受普通大学的认证规则约束，只需在开付费项目前拿到监管批准 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:21" data-who="Goggin" data-en="I'll give you a couple examples. First of all, it's completely unaccredited, so we don't have to follow the same accreditation rules of a typical college. We do have to get regulatory approval before we launch our paid programs, but it's very different." aria-label="回原文"></button>。课程结构整个是倒过来的：普通大学围绕每学期修多少学分、做题、考试运转；这里把 75% 到 80% 的时间留给学生做项目，每周只上几天课、可能只有半天，课程 topics 包括产品感觉、AI 架构、谈判、人际能力等，且全是纯讲授——「来上课，学到该学的，然后去造你的项目」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:34" data-who="Goggin" data-en="Yeah, the curriculum is like one of the most fun parts, I will tell you, because when you redesign something from scratch, thinking about the AI future, it kind of like blows, it blew my mind like the things that we decided to do." aria-label="回原文"></button>。项目分三类：研究项目(比如有人去深圳、奥斯汀、洛杉矶走访制造工厂，同时线上学机器人课)、学习项目、以及构建/创业项目 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:06" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so they'll get to do projects that they desire. And I have three different categories of projects. I think they'll have research projects." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的理由很直接：在 AI 时代，任何需要学的东西都可以在工作中学，所以「直接动手做东西」比课堂有效得多。而且项目必须是学生自选的、和课堂内容只是间接对应——他举了大学商科 101 课的例子：老师给你规定好商业计划书的全部要素、还给你一份可选企业清单，「到最后，没有创造力、没有构建、没有[[主观能动性|主观能动性]]」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:41" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And the reason that they need to be indirect is because if you ever, you know, in college, you ever get like a signed project, it's like... You know, in accounting, let's do like in business 101 class, you know, it'd be like, write a business plan." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

防止学生跑偏的替代机制是脚手架：每周(可能每周多次)站到自己八人小组面前，汇报上周做了什么、下周要做什么，导师在场 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:54" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so every week, you know, and we might do this multiple times a week, we're going to test things out with the founding class and work with them to figure it out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他坦承这套模式并不新——牛津至今如此运转，YC、Alpha School、蒙特梭利都是他们的灵感来源。

## 承诺三件事，收三种钱

Goggin 说学院能做出的承诺是：让学生学会做一个有超高主观能动性(agency,指不等人安排、自己推动事情)的 [[AI 原生建造者|AI 原生建造者]]，并具备理解硅谷文化的人际技能；「如果我们能做到这三件事，这将是任何人能获得的最有价值的教育体验」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:42" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so those are the types of things that these students are buying into. And the promise that I think we can make is that you will learn how to be an AI native builder with super high agency, with the people skills to understand the culture of Silicon Valley." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为此他们做成了硅谷生态的合资生意，而非 a16z 独角戏：创始合作方包括 Anthropic、Coinbase、Meta、NVIDIA、OpenAI、Palantir、Replit、Stripe 等，提供课程支持、顾问反馈和算力额度；另有 30 多家招聘合作方 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:37" data-who="Goggin" data-en="How do those tie into them and what are they? Yeah. One of the first things that I talked about with Mark, Ben, and Eric was that we felt like this needed to be Silicon Valley's school." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他的观察是：AI 领域最顶尖的研究者已经不在大学校园里，而在这些公司——所以跟它们绑定，等于让学生坐上前沿进展的前排座位 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:23" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so if you're going to build an AI forward school, of course you need the leading companies in the space. It's also interesting that the leading researchers in AI are no longer at university campuses." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

融资 4000 万美元，而且一融就是覆盖好几年的钱——不是常规创业公司那种 18 个月的 runway。「办学校和办一个 AI 应用最大的区别之一，是你需要长久存续。

学生必须知道这所学校不会把钱花光」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:15" data-who="Goggin" data-en="Yeah, that's a great question. I think that first, just to be clear, instead of raising for 18 months, we raised for a few years. This was very intentional because there's a big difference between starting a school and starting a, you know, AI app." aria-label="回原文"></button>。收入三块：学费是大头(他们相信学校有价值所以敢收费)；企业合作费(这些公司想接触全星球最好的学生)；长期则通过学生创办或加入的企业股权变现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:10" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so I think that there's a, there's a, you know, there's multiple revenue streams for Horowitz and Dresden Academy. One is tuition. We think that's going to be the bulk of the revenue." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

具体产品：创始班奖学金，一年制、免学费（食宿自理）；合作伙伴里有不少旧金山更实惠的食宿选择，但仍是一笔开销；2028 年待监管批准后推两年制正式项目，学费将等于或高于顶尖精英私立大学——「目标是一所高价值机构，不是一所精英机构：要提供多到让学生觉得这学费绝对值的价值」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="69:31" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And then, of course, in 2028, pending regulatory approval, we are hoping to launch the two-year program. And the two-year program will have a tuition that is equal or more than the top tier elite private universities." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 不急着创业：故意留一到两年的探索期

他预判学院最大的困难是「留不住人」——这些学生质量太高，VC 会蜂拥而上直接塞钱。他的应对不是防守，而是把这当成核心课程：现在大量年轻人一拿到 15 万、25 万美元的支票就去创业，而他认为「这些建造者中只有非常非常小的一部分应该立刻创业」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:18" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And then on the other hand, you have, you know, angel investors or VCs that are giving out 150, 250K checks on a flyer, hoping that this is the next, you know, Michael Truel or something, right, who's the founder of Cursor." aria-label="回原文"></button>。创业是十年苦役，大多数不会成功，所以不该追着第一个还算不错的点子跑、更不该「仅仅因为钱在那里就拿」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:57" data-who="Goggin" data-en="It's not actually the right thing to go after the first decent idea you have. Startups are a 10-year slog and most of them don't work out. So when you go after it, you gotta be sure." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

跟 YC、A16Z Speedrun 这些「要求你创办公司」的项目相比，他坚持的差异点是：保留高中后教育应有的探索性——一群同龄同侪、终生友谊、和进入下一段人生之前想清楚「接下来十年做什么」的缓冲。有真想法的学生照样全力支持，两个月走人他毫不在意；但绝大多数人，先在学院泡一两年，让加密、机器人、AI 应用、经典 SaaS 的创始人们轮番来讲行业怎么运转。

> 【背景】Thiel Fellowship 指 Peter Thiel 创办的 Thiel 奖学金项目，属于「要求你创办公司」的一类。

## 招什么样的人：看斜率，不看履历

招生标准浓缩成一句话：[[证明过的工作|证明过的工作]](proof of work),不是成绩——「这里每一个出现的人都做出过什么东西」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:26" data-who="Goggin" data-en="The second thing they're buying into is they're moving to San Francisco and they're going to be amongst a peer set of students who are the best builders on the planet." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他举的例子：田纳西 16 岁的 Darsh,造了一个生物学 LLM(大型语言模型)，用于设计分子、后来转向设计蛋白质，正在击败一些领先模型；还有青少年时期积累大量 TikTok 粉丝、并参与建设 Alpha School 的 Elle <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:58" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And like I love this you did what phrase because it's when I was meeting these young builders you know and doing research for this company I was like what did you do?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更微妙的一层是动机筛选。他面试一个顶尖高中的学生，对方说：几乎所有同学参加课外活动完全是为了进大学，「而我参加是因为我喜欢」——这个差别在纸面上看不出来，所以学院会面试每一个人、深挖他们做过的东西，招那些因为热爱才动手的人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:40" data-who="Goggin" data-en="I'll tell you these kids know who they are, right? I was talking to a kid at Illinois Math and Science Academy, right? And by the way, a lot of these kids do go to top schools, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。录取流程：先交作品集和个人信息，再线下面试加 IQ、性格、写作测试；看的是「斜率」不是经验——高三年级才发现做东西这回事的数学奥赛选手照样欢迎 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="61:47" data-who="Goggin" data-en="You know, you don't have to start your first company at 18, 19, 20, you can start whenever. And as a result, our goal is going to be looking at slope and not like experience, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 正面刚「这代年轻人讨厌 AI」

主持人抛出一个尖锐现实：现在大学开学典礼上一提 AI,学生们集体嘘声。Goggin 分两半回答。一半是定位：「一所拥抱 AI 的学校，就是一所拥抱现实和未来的学校」——不喜欢 AI 的学生不适合这里，而这里也只需要一小撮人适合就能运转；「他们的大多数同龄人是 AI 末日论者(doomer,认为 AI 会带来灾难)并不重要,我们要的是 AI boomers(双关 boomer 建造者),那些期待 AI 乐观未来的人」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:51" data-who="Goggin" data-en="But there's only a small set of students who need to be a fit for this thing to actually work. And so it doesn't matter that most of their peers are AI doomers. We want the AI boomers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

另一半是怎么用。学院不但不禁 AI,还多给学分和算力让学生用 AI 造东西。

他拿自己运营 Maven 举例：个人规矩是文档第一稿必须自己写——不是拒绝 AI,而是「你仍然要把自己面对的问题想透彻」，写完再丢给 AI 伴侣帮着进一步想。他的类比是计算器：如果员工给你看一份新产品预测表，你却因为不熟悉公司的损益结构、判断不出 5% 的转化率提升对两年后增长率意味着什么，「我就没有资格当这家公司的 CEO」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:24" data-who="Goggin" data-en="I know it's very, very trite, but like I have access to a calculator today. But if I'm looking at a spreadsheet that my employee shows that's the projections for their new product initiative, if I cannot immediately identify how this is going to affect the bottom line of our business because I don't have an intimate understanding of our P&amp;L and how this 5% improvement in conversion rate is going to affect our growth rate in two years." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

对 AI 也是同理：让它替你做事的前提是你懂它在做什么。他更深一层的行业判断是：过去中层管理者(对上传达、对下协调)很有价值，现在崛起的是「[[超级 IC|超级 IC]]」——一个人同时协调 30 个、50 个智能体，甚至协调那些在协调别的智能体的智能体，「构建那种架构很难，你需要一所真正教你的学校，才能进前 1%」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:32" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And so we're just using that same philosophy now for a post high school. You should come in and learn how to orchestrate 30 agents at a time, 50 agents, I don't know, orchestrate agents that are orchestrating agents that are orchestrating agents, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Maven 现在的全部业务就是教中层管理者完成这个转变。

顺带反驳「AI 抢工作」叙事：他引用 Marc Andreessen 的「软件正在吞噬世界」，推演「AI 将吞噬世界」——但吞噬不等于消失，就像软件吃掉 CPG(消费品)行业后，今天你不开 Shopify 根本没法做消费品品牌，而品牌和产品依然存在，只是按互联网的新秩序重新设计了一遍。「世界不是消失，是改变」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:39" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And what I think is true about that, AI will do the same. AI will eat the world. And as AI eats the world, that doesn't mean the world is not going to exist." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的朋友们在所有头部 AI 公司一如既往地拼命工作，而且全都在拼命招人——人才战正在硅谷白热化。退一万步：就算末日论是对的，学院毕业生恰恰是最被需要的前 1%,所以「如果你信末日论，更有理由来」。

## 失败课：不庆祝失败，也不羞辱失败

Goggin 自己的失败清单不短：Udemy 时期和联合创始人闹翻离场、Sprig 融了很多钱但没做成。硅谷教他的一课是「重要的不是你的失败，而是你的成功」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="65:43" data-who="Goggin" data-en="And now, of course, I'm still raising money for Horwitz and Dresden Academy. One of the greatest lessons of the Valley is that it's not about your failure, it's about your success." aria-label="回原文"></button>——亏过他钱的投资人，转头继续投 Maven 和这所学院。

但他刻意反对「把失败变酷」的文化:「失败并不酷，失败很糟，你应该尽一切可能避免失败；然而如果你失败了，也没关系——站起来，再试」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="66:16" data-who="Goggin" data-en="This like mentality of like making failure cool. Failure is not cool. Failure sucks." aria-label="回原文"></button>。学生项目失败时，他们会祝贺学生并帮其复盘。

  //    pathname → spaNavigated 永远置不了 true。window 上用「未设置才记」守卫,只在**第一次**记真·落地路径,
  //    重执行/换页都存活;__pdSpa 一旦置 true 就 sticky。referrer 用整 origin 比对(new URL),防

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
