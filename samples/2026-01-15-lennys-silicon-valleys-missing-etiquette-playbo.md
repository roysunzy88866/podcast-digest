# Sam Lessin：硅谷创始人最缺的不是产品，是低心率

硅谷教创始人最多的一句话是"只管做产品"，但 [[Sam Lessin|Sam Lessin]] 觉得没人诚实——当你在让人把数据和生意托付给你的时候，怎么走进一个房间、怎么说话、怎么吃饭，这些事讽刺性地变得非常重要。他给[[礼仪|礼仪]]下了一个定义：这是一项让你以[[低心率|低心率]]出现在任何房间里的技能 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:10" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Yeah, this is the thing I think about a lot. Again, again, you and I are now old people, right? But you're young. I feel that. You feel it in your bones." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

核心心法就一个词：[[富足心态|富足心态]]。很多年轻创始人走进一个全是 VC 和 CEO 的聚会，心态是"这是我唯一的机会"，于是表现得像一只精力过剩的兔子，把人吓跑。

Sam 的建议是，即使你内心觉得这就是唯一一枪，你也要带着"这只是一个机会，不是全部故事"的自信和冷静出现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:00" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="But actually, again, in terms of putting people at ease and building relationships and etiquette, even if in your heart of hearts, you're like, &quot;This really is my one shot.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。你不缺这个机会，你还会有的。

## 进房间和握手

早到，但别早到一个小时——早到半小时就很怪了。目标是比对方先到，让对方让你等，这样你坐下时心率是低的。

迟到了就简单道歉然后继续，别慌慌张张花五分钟解释。握手要坚定但别捏碎对方的手。见到人时把对方名字重复一遍——"嘿，[[Lenny|Lenny]]，很高兴见到你"——这表明你在努力记住这个人，而不只是把他当成一张潜在支票 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:19" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Again, this is not practice on your friends. You want to repeat names back is a really, really valuable thing to think about when you're meeting someone and say, &quot;Hey, Lenny, it's great to meet you.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

一个很实用的技巧：如果你忘了对方名字，而你带了同伴，就把介绍反过来做。不说"Lenny，这是我的妻子 Jessica"，而是说"Jessica，我想把你介绍给…

…"然后话悬在那里，对方通常会自己伸出手说"我是 Lenny"。这给了你一个合理的推诿空间 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:02" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="There's a reason. The reason is, but it's at least enough plausible deniability of semi-bad etiquette that leverages the social situation to be a better etiquette, that it's a useful thing to think about as a small queue." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

还有一句万能开场白：说"见到你很高兴"（great to see you），而不是"很高兴认识你"（nice to meet you）。前者无论你们是否见过面都用得上，后者如果你们其实见过五次，就非常尴尬 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:17" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="And so for me, this is an important one to keep in mind. My wife is constantly making fun of me of saying like, &quot;Oh.&quot; And not knowing if I've met someone before not she's like, how can you ... I don't know what to do with you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

别人迟到了，不要表现出愤慨。Sam 是 VC，经常因为 30 分钟一节的 Zoom 连轴转而迟到。如果创始人因为迟到几分钟就摆脸色，接下来 25 分钟的气氛就全毁了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:26" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="I'll try to email them ahead, et cetera, but then it is what it is and we kind of get right into it. Every once in a while, you'll have some founder who is super indignant about it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 交谈：打乒乓球，别审讯

提问是好习惯，但别连续问六个问题，那不是交谈，那是审讯。把对话想象成打乒乓球：你打过去，对方打回来，你来我往。最好的对话是有人先给一个想法或观点，然后双方像打球一样来回 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:00" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="I do think questions are a great tool to engage someone, but don't make it six questions in a row and make sure there's always in some ways a give to get. If you can come in, the best conversations are coming, someone comes in and gives you an idea or has a point or sparks something, then it's like a game of ping pong, then you can kind of react to and it goes back and forth where there's openness and they're playing with you, not playing single player is almost the way I would think about it in a conversation." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

匹配对方的词汇水平。跟大学教授说话和跟 12 岁孩子说话，用词不一样，重点是根据对方的状态让对方感到放松，而不是刻意模仿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:45" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Look, we talk a lot about matching vocabulary. You're going to meet a lot of different people. You want to make people feel good and welcome." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

给别人留点念想。大多数社交的真实目标是让对方觉得"这人挺有意思，想再聊聊"——而不是"我听完了他整个人生故事，再也不想跟他说话了" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:36" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="If we're being transactional about it, what's the real goal? The real goal is to leave people in a position where they're like, &quot;Wow, that was a really interesting person." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

知道什么时候优雅地退出，和知道什么时候进入一样重要。如果对方说"我去拿杯喝的"，那通常不是邀请你跟着去酒吧 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:09" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="And then whatever once in a while the person will be like, if someone says to you in a conversation they wind it down, it's like, &quot;I'm going to go grab a drink.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

遇到名人，别拍马屁，也别装不认识。走上去说"你是我见过最重要的人"是错的，走上去说"你是谁"也是错的。把他们当正常人对待，别一上来就要邮箱和电话 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:55" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="It's ridiculous. On the other hand, going up and being like, &quot;You're the most important person I've ever met.&quot; Is wrong." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 吃饭：别点最贵的

不要点菜单上最贵的东西。对方可能不在乎那点钱，但他们会注意到——你会被贴上"对成本不敏感"的标签 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:09" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="In general, it's don't order the most expensive thing on the menu. Does it really matter? Especially to an investor, do they really care?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。理想情况下别先点，看别人怎么点，跟着走，排在中间或最后 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:35" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="And then I think, look, in the terms of asking, ideally, don't order first because I think if you see how someone ... Are we doing starters? How long is this meal going to be?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

别人请你吃饭，主动提出买单。大多数时候你会被拒绝——"我来，别担心"——但这个姿态要有。

除非对方点了 1 万美元的酒，那就不需要了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:05" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="And look, I think I would say in terms of this, which I always think is important is like, look, within reason, always offer to pay. Now, you should be turned down." aria-label="回原文"></button>。小费给足，20% 是底线，但别给到让人十年后只记得你给了多少小费的地步——小费不应该成为这顿饭最被记住的事 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:47" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="If you're out in a situation, I think you kind of want to tip in my mind to the level of no one you're effectively paying for would bat an eye that you're being stingy is the way I would think about it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

面包盘和饮料杯怎么分？看你的手：左手四根手指形成 B（Bread），右手四根手指形成 D（Drink）。餐巾放腿上，不要只放一条腿上，也不要挂在脖子上——总之别让餐巾的位置被人记住 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:42" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Oh, BD. You just got to look at your hands, right? Bs and Ds for which was your bread plate is kind of the way to think about it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 着装：合身比品牌重要

穿得比场合正式一个级别，不是两个。穿西装的话，可以脱掉外套降低正式感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:01" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="So one is just dress one Level up as a really simple tip. It's an easy way to win. Not two, not three, but one." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

合身是一切的答案——一件合身的 20 美元衬衫比一件不合身的 500 美元衬衫好得多。大多数人看不出东西多少钱，但能凭直觉看出合不合身 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:32" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="You got to have a little bit of that in your life in terms of how I myself behave sometimes. But look, in the end of the day, a well-fitting $20 shirt is way better than a misfitting $500 shirt." aria-label="回原文"></button>。作为初创公司创始人戴一块劳力士出现是很没品位的，那是在太用力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:53" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="It's like if you're a startup founder, you do want to dress to the level of the room, but you kind of shouldn't show up and you shouldn't have a Rolex. It's very classless, if that makes sense, to show up as a startup founder with a Rolex." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

不确定怎么穿，就问。提问不是丢脸，反而表现出自信、冷静和谦逊 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:18" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="It is absolutely fine to ask. In fact, if anything, it shows a level of confidence and calm and humility to ask if you don't know. So I actually think this is a great example." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 日程安排：别默认甩 Calendly 链接

Sam 出名地讨厌 Calendly，甚至为此发过一通长文，结果反而帮对方推动了一个月的增长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:32" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Oh, well, I think I'd like to say that I'm fairly famous for hating Calendly. I actually, I think I am not overstating it when saying there was a period where calendar where I personally had driven most of their growth for the month." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他的原则是：如果你是资历浅的、不那么忙的那一方，你应该问对方什么时候有空，然后自己去凑。发链接不是绝对不行，但那不应该是默认选项 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:13" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="I really strongly believe the default should not be Calendly. The default in most situations, especially if you recognize the power hierarchy or the busyness hierarchy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

改期的话，要给尽可能多的提前量，并且你提出的替代时间，前三个里必须有一个能行的。改期是你欠对方的，你应该比平时更配合对方的时间 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:49" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="If you really can't and you're going to use a scheduling agent or something, it just needs to actually have real options is basically, I think that comes up all the time." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

时区要反复检查。别提议一个对你来说是下午、对对方来说是凌晨 4 点的时间 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="51:25" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="It's worth the extra check to make sure you're not both scheduling, you're getting the numbers right, but then also really importantly, scheduling at reasonable times." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最重要的一条：尊重 [[EA|EA]]（执行助理）和 PA（个人助理）。Sam 讲了当年把公司卖给 Airbnb 时的经验——帮忙卖公司的人特意跟 Airbnb 的 EA 和办公室经理搞好关系，因为如果他们喜欢你，事情会顺利很多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:14" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="That means saying thank you when they schedule and follow up with them and things like that. I'll tell a story when we were selling our company to Airbnb, we had this guy helping us sell the company and he made it a big point to build a good relationship with the EA and office manager at Airbnb because if they like you, it helps." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

跟他们有眼神交流，说谢谢，用完咖啡杯问他们放在哪。不要把他们当成隐形人。

## 邮件和会议

邮件短一点，别发 10 段。想象你是收件人，感觉如何 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:15" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="You don't want to leave people hanging. But again, I just think it's like if you read an email, I'm sorry, if you write an email, imagine you're receiving it. How does it feel?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。不用表情符号——它对大多数人来说可读性差，有文化含义，还可能让人以为你是用 AI 生成的，因为 ChatGPT 爱用表情符号 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:52" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="I think emoji from my perspective, it's quite a step of familiarity, if that makes sense. From a business context, look, if someone sends you a smiley face, you can respond with it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

收件人和抄送人的顺序有讲究。如果邮件第一个是助理、第四个是 CEO，你大概搞错了——人们会默认排在最前面的是最重要的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:18" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Yeah, I do think you kind of want to think about ... There is a connotation to who you send it to and who you CC and the order in which people. Now, I don't want to overstate this, but put differently, it's like if you're sending an email and the first person on the email is the assistant and the fourth person on the email is the CEO, you've probably done it wrong." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

如果你是抄送列表里的第五个人，你不应该第一个回复 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:30" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="If you send an email to 10 people CC'd to the whole nine yards, there is a subtle etiquette to when you respond. If you are the fifth CC on an email, you're not expected to be the first response." aria-label="回原文"></button>。被密送（BCC）的话，绝对不要"全部回复" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="61:33" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="And that is actually what you're signaling is you can trust me because intuitively, there's this well of knowledge and cultural connection and whatever that we can share effectively." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

视频会议开摄像头，穿得体，背景合适。Sam 不喜欢虚拟背景——如果你在卧室里开会，没关系，但床要铺好。

关上你的衣柜门 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="64:05" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="Yeah. For meetings, virtual ones, camera on. And again, I say this as someone who sometimes violates this." aria-label="回原文"></button>。面对面会议结束后，主动问咖啡杯放哪里，别留下就走了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="65:20" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="One other tip you had was clean up after yourself if you're in a real meeting. The easiest way, and this is by the way, it's for my partner, Kevin Colleran, But the easiest way to come off badly is to not offer to put your coffee cup in the kitchen." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 幽默：自嘲最安全

幽默是展示你对社交环境掌控力的终极方式——能讲一个正好在线上的笑话，说明你在那个空间里很自在 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:40" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="But I think the thing about humor is there's this interesting subtlety to why it's so useful in social settings, which is one, it kind of shows you the ultimate mastery of a social situation." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但别只被记住是个讲笑话的人，也别讲一个没人笑的笑话，那是高风险动作失败了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:24" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="So you don't want to tell a knock-knock joke with adults, but the off-color sex joke that is hilarious, you better be pretty confident before you tell it in space." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

取笑自己，不要取笑别人——取笑别人需要很高的熟悉度，一旦对方不在同一个波长上，就很危险 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:28" data-who="Sam Lessin" data-en="It's just like you can make fun of yourself as much as you want. Again, making fun of other people shows an incredible level of familiarity. - **低心率是目标**：早到几分钟、简单道歉后继续、不慌张——一切具体做法都围绕"别让你的心率成为对方的负担"。

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
