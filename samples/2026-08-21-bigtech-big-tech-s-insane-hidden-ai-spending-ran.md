## 表外承诺到底怎么运作

以 [[Meta|Meta]] 在路易斯安那州的 Hyperion 数据中心为例——这个项目大约 1,700 个足球场大小。Meta 是建设方和使用方，但项目和 270 亿美元债务都不在 Meta 资产负债表上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:57" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So it says, Meta's gigantic Hyperion data center project in Louisiana, which is the size of about 1,700 football fields, helps explain how obligations wind up off tech companies' balance sheets." aria-label="回原文"></button>。钱由华尔街公司 Blue Owl Capital 管理的基金出，Meta 做担保：承诺租用，如果提前退出会补偿债券持有人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Though Meta is the builder, neither Hyperion nor the $27 billion in debt that's financing its construction show up on Meta's balance sheet. Funds managed by the Wall Street firm Blue Owl Capital, there you go," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这里面的逻辑是二元的：如果 AI 需求爆发、数据中心疯狂赚钱，Meta 不需要掏钱；只有出问题时 Meta 才要兜底 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Well, again, so this MetaHyperion one is like the perfect example. So Meta is guaranteeing to make bondholders whole if it doesn't stay for the entire two decades, but everyone is betting if AI demand explodes and if these data centers start printing cash and then they're able to pay bondholders and then some and generate additional cash, then Meta's not on the hook for anything." aria-label="回原文"></button>。从纯技术、法律、金融结构角度看，这可以被论证为"不是现金支出" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So it's binary in the sense of if data centers, if the whole story works, they're not spending, Meta's not spending cash. So again, as ridiculous as that might sound when I say it out loud, that's the argument that can be used in a very technicality, legal, financial, structuring, creative sort of way." aria-label="回原文"></button>——但说白了，这就是 Meta 既是客户又是担保人的创意金融架构 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:01" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="No, where it gets even more ridiculous is in many of these cases, like Meta is the customer and the guarantor. For now or forever. Well, no, no, not forever, not forever." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Meta 披露了 3470 亿美元尚未启动的租赁总义务，其中很多没反映在财务报表里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so it's got 347 billion in total leases that haven't kicked in yet, many of which are not reflected in its financial statement. That's insane. I mean, when you say it out loud again, when we're saying these things out loud, it does all sound insane." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而 Meta 手头现金加流动投资只有 910 亿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:10" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But why go through the extra effort to obscure and play these games to the point that they are at this point? Well, so looking, Meta has $91 billion of cash in liquid investments and marketable securities as well." aria-label="回原文"></button>，Google 是 1870 亿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Well, so looking, Meta has $91 billion of cash in liquid investments and marketable securities as well. What is it? Google, $187 billion." aria-label="回原文"></button>——钱远远不够直接花，所以他们选择了这条路。

## 为什么不直接花现金

两个原因叠加。第一，没钱硬要花就得从别处挪，或者去资本市场融资，会影响股价。

第二，如果把真实支出规模透明地展示给华尔街，股价会挨打 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:22" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Okay, but I think, all right, so let me go to, this is going into my second question. Isn't part of the reason to do this to obscure it from Wall Street, to obscure that spending from Wall Street so you don't take a hit?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[Ranjan|Ranjan]] 说得很直白："当然会，这就是你偏偏用这种方式做的原因" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="uh and and shown wall street how much they're actually spending versus the typical capex numbers like don't you think the stocks would have taken a significant hit if they would have shown like the true amount of spending that they're doing as opposed to this like obfuscated run around that we seem to be getting" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但这些交易结构像雏菊链一样缠绕在一起，连投行分析师都得费劲拼凑总数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:35" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Thank you. To me, the biggest issue of this is it's like the tangled web, the daisy chain element of it is why you have to have these reports and investment bank firms trying to actually total up what this potential spending is, is that it's not clearly laid out in any way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Ed Zitron 很早就在喊这个问题，摩根士丹利和《华尔街日报》现在才跟上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:40" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I mean, this has lived in Ed Zitron territory for a long time, and to his credit, he's been on this for a while. But it's interesting to now see the investment banks, Morgan Stanley, The Wall Street Journal, everyone finally catching up to the story." aria-label="回原文"></button>——不是因为华尔街笨，而是 AI 狂热太强，谁敢在 NVIDIA 和 [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 飙涨的时候唱空数据中心融资，谁就会被投资者视为失败者 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Again, if you missed NVIDIA, you do not look good. If you missed Anthropic on the private side and OpenAI, if you're the one trying to say this 12 to 18 months ago and managing money, you'd be looked at as a failure by your investors." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 最大的风险：所有人押同一注

这些公司不是在下一盘小棋。Ranjan 把它叫"赌上公司"级别的活动 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:59" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="we have covered on the show but you and i haven't had a chance to speak about it yet but but yeah the the idea that you're gonna that these are big bets i mean 340 was 347 billion dollars that meta has and obligations for leases that haven't kicked in yet" aria-label="回原文"></button>，主持人把它框架化为"所有支出都是一张 [[AGI|AGI]]（通用人工智能）的[[看涨期权|看涨期权]]" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:35" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I mean, it all goes back to what Paul Kudrowski said on the show a week and a half ago, which is effectively that what we're looking at is a call. Basically, the way that it works is it's what all the spending is a call option on AGI." aria-label="回原文"></button>——如果 AGI 到了，钱花得值；如果没到，就要清算。

但让他焦虑的不是"AI 行不行"，而是"所有人都在下同样的杠杆赌注" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:24" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="No, not just – I agree with you and not just the same bet. Like you mentioned, a levered bet. And we've seen in the past couple of weeks that leveraged bets on AI don't always work out, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。就算 AI 需求最终会爆发，时间线对不上就会出问题。

而且这不是对冲基金那种几分钟内的急崩——会是以分散的方式慢慢展开，比如人们发现养老金投资跌了一点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There's people losing money left. Maybe it's in a very distributed fashion and people notice their pension fund investments are down 10%, which is not good. But it's not like Meta's $91 billion of cash evaporates next week." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Meta 的广告业务和 Google 的云业务不会一夜消失 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:32" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So to me the question, and I don't even say this as highly anxious. I say this as I don't think this plays out completely smoothly. In that call option framework, and again, a call option, the right to buy, basically having unlimited upside for kind of like smaller premium, taking that kind of bet, it's, I don't think it's, the question to me is what does an unwind look like?" aria-label="回原文"></button>，但整个结构需要"完美执行"才能平稳落地 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I mean, I think we talked about on the show numerous times that you need perfect execution, really, by many of the players here for this all to work out. And if the execution is not perfect, then that's when things get, you know, somewhat interesting and probably very rough for some of the players involved." aria-label="回原文"></button>，就像美联储搞软着陆——需要太多事情同时做对 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That's what Fed is trying to get inflation down. It's like you need so many things to go right or you're going to have a problem. That's a good kind of like corollary." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Anthropic 七倍增长 vs OpenAI 高管出走

转向公司层面。Anthropic 的年化营收运行率在 7 月底达到 650 亿美元，截至 6 月 30 日的季度营收是 115 亿美元，比 2025 年同期的 7.87 亿美元增长了七倍多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Anthropic is on track to generalize annual revenue of more than $65 billion based on its current performance, up more than sevenfold from its pace at the end of last year." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Ranjan 指出一个细节：这些数字是以"知情人士"形式泄露给彭博社的，时机恰好卡在 Anthropic 秋季 [[IPO|IPO]] 之前 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:49" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="As part of its regular update with investors. But there's also the prospect that they're going public and the sourcing in the Bloomberg story is, according to people familiar with the matter, which sounds like it might have been delivered." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他在直销电商经历过 IPO 流程，那时候对任何人——包括妻子家人——说数字都会被银行家痛骂 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:27" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is – like whenever this happens, I – when I was at – in my past direct-to-consumer e-commerce experience was part of a potential IPO proceeding. The level of secure, like, if we were to ever even say figures to our wives and families and friends, we would get annihilated by the bankers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。所以这种"泄露"几乎肯定是精心策划的，目的是吓退 [[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 抢先上市 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:49" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And meanwhile, like... Every, conveniently, at any moment, I mean, we're all just seeing these numbers, like, this is a very, and again, Anthropic, Sandwich in the Park, they're comms masters, like, all this stuff is orchestrated." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

OpenAI 那边，第二季度营收环比只增长了 18%，亏损加深，一些股东对追赶 Anthropic 的进度不满 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And this is from the Wall Street Journal. Results that disappointed some shareholders who had hoped that the startup would show more progress catching up to rival Anthropic." aria-label="回原文"></button>。更刺眼的是人事：首席营收官 Denise Dresser 上任不到一年就走了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:23" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So this is from this journal story. Last week, the company replaced its chief revenue officer, Denise Dresser, after she spent less than a year on the job. Her departure followed a string of other exits, including former chief operating officer Brad Lightcap and Fiji Simo, once seen as the heir apparent to chief executive Sam Altman." aria-label="回原文"></button>，之前还有首席运营官 Brad Lightcap 和曾被视为 Sam Altman 接班人的 Fiji Simo 离职 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:27" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Last week, the company replaced its chief revenue officer, Denise Dresser, after she spent less than a year on the job. Ranjan 认为根本原因是 OpenAI 在编码领域被 Anthropic 打了个措手不及，被迫转向 Codex，还在追赶中 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Look, I think this is just the consequences of them, you know, really getting their butt kicked by Anthropic on the way to coding. Yes. Okay, here's the thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>——但产品和技术路线还没定型，本质上还是初创公司的混乱状态 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="55:19" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But the thing with OpenAI and Anthropic, by the way, that gives them the nature of a startup is... ## 旅行：被低估的 AI 能力试验场

最后聊了一个轻松但实质的话题：为什么旅行是评估 AI 能力的好场景。三个理由——第一，旅行需要处理海量信息（去哪、住哪、吃什么、怎么走），而互联网上充满联盟链接和虚假评论，信息环境极差 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="58:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The first one is when you're traveling, you need to use something to sort through a lot of information. A lot of information. What to do, where to stay, what to eat, how to get there, how to get home, what days things are open on, what they're not." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二，旅行本质上是一连串不断变化的任务链，和高风险工作项目的结构很像，但试错成本低 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The second thing is that with travel, what you're dealing with basically is a cascading series of small and big tasks that are always changing in some way. So I think as an eval, so to speak, for AI, it's actually really good because it resembles some more high stakes type of cases where you're like maybe at work and you're working a project." aria-label="回原文"></button>。第三，旅行处于"重要但不致命"的金发姑娘区域——AI 把酒店幻觉出来了有真实麻烦，但不是灾难 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:12" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Maybe not ace your work test, but you can feel more comfortable putting it towards more economically valuable tasks because travel is in what I'm going to call like the Goldilocks zone of important but not so important tasks." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Ranjan 本来是反对方，但这次从西班牙旅行回来后改口了。他给 AI 一组很具体的要求（特定价格范围的 Airbnb、要带泳池、要浅水浮潜适合小孩学游泳、要沙滩、不要游客化），AI 推荐了 Denia，结果"Amazing"，是那种真正发现隐藏宝石的旅行体验 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="62:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I basically gave AI a bunch of requirements, like really specific around, like I want best value for Airbnb in a specific price range with a pool at the Airbnb." aria-label="回原文"></button>。AI 还帮他生成了包含所有租车预订号和护照信息的 markdown 文件，随时可以查 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="63:09" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then also during the process as well, creating a markdown file with the context of all your rental car booking numbers, even now having passport information, everything, hopefully secure." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

    var fromSite = pdSameOrigin(document.referrer || '') || window.__pdSpa === true;

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
