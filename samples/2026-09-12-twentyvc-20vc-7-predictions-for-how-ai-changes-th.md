And that is how we can achieve what we are achieving at scale in 35 countries, including China, including Middle East, including Europe, where there are a lot of regulations in all of them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 怎么接管公司运营

最出名的案例是邮件营销：以前有个两人团队，负责人一离职，联合创始人 Alexandra 用三天搭了几套机器人，现在邮件营销带来接近一亿美元收入，团队人数是零 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:17" data-who="Matteo" data-en="So what happened is we were forced to that, meaning we had a team of two. Then the person leading the team was leaving. And at that point, my co-founder, Alexandra, jumped in and said, OK, let me see how I can use AI to really make this work." aria-label="回原文"></button>。数字营销团队则每天早上收到 AI [[智能体|智能体]]的建议报告，人只负责批准或拒绝 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:39" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Growth, because email is like that. Now also our digital marketing, our team received a report every morning from our AI agents suggesting what changes to do or not to do, and they approve or reject." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Matteo 说他现在开始分开统计「人类员工」和「AI 员工」——把 AI 员工算上，公司规模大概是三到四倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:18" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Then internally, we have probably by now hundreds, if not thousands of agents that we use internally at Hitsleep. So now I started measuring what I call the human employees, but then there is also the AI employees, and probably the AI employees are, I mean, without probably, we are probably three, four X bigger if you start including all our AI employees." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为此他六个月前专门设了一个过去不存在的「AI 内部工具团队」，专门造这些内部智能体。这个团队必须和数据工程团队绑得很紧，因为智能体要能精确访问按国家拆分的收入和 [[CAC|CAC]]（获客成本）数据，要确保智能体 100% 正确 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="58:16" data-who="Matteo" data-en="You don't expect horizontal groups to do that. No, because this team needs to be very connected to the data engineering team because the agents need to have access to the data backend, the data pipeline of everything we do." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他的组织哲学是「两人小组」：一个领域配两个世界级的人做冗余，但领域可以很大。比如付费媒体团队就两个人，做出两三亿美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:04" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Which function does not exist today that will be very important, or job? Well, we saw a change in email, but what I think is I started thinking of in terms of teams of two." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

财务团队本该 20 人，他们只要 4 个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:01" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Probably a company like ours should have, I don't know, a team of 20 people in finance. Our team is four people. I just really like to keep teams small and try to see if I can 5X the output." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他判断未来不会有职位消失，但同样的人会更横向地覆盖更多领域，公司能精简得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:45" data-who="Matteo" data-en="So which function today or which job today will be gone in five years' time and which will be created? Again, I don't think anything will be gone. I think you can do what you're doing with way less people or the same people be spread more horizontally to cover more areas, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他的预测：三年后 250 人做到 10 亿美元收入 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:57" data-who="Matteo" data-en="How many people will you be then? 250 people making a billion. Which function does not exist today that will be very important, or job?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

关于 [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 该不该在 IPO 时买，他的判断很具体：会有两个反向的向量——用量会涨，「5% 会变成 50%」，但成本会下降，净额是降的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:00" data-who="Matteo" data-en="I think there will be two vectors in the opposite direction. The usage will increase, and so the 5% will become 50%, but the cost will go down. And so net-net, I think it will go down." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还举了 Sam Allman 讲的电的类比：电力早期贵到付一小时电费要工作五小时，现在没人在乎晚上的电费 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:09" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And so net-net, I think it will go down. I was listening to an interview to Sam Allman recently, and he was saying that in the early days, to pay for one hour of electricity at night, you had to work five hours, just to give you a sense of how expensive electricity was in the early days." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他确实会买 Anthropic <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:22" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Knowing what you know now as a consumer and customer of Claude, would you buy Anthropic at the IPO? I would probably buy. I still think there is so much upside in all these companies." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 渠道打法：一次只做一个

Matteo 明确反对「同时铺所有渠道」。他的打法：从最大的渠道（通常是 Meta）开始，按公司规模定每周小额预算，给 CAC 设上限，在 CAC 达标内才加码，否则「就是在往窗外扔钱」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:54" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And I say, no, no, no. You go one by one. You start with Meta." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

增长团队永远想多花，但你不约束，年底发现搞砸了、开始收缩，第二年就没有同比增长——今天有纪律，明年才能继续涨 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:20" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And what happens then? Let's say by the end of the year, you realize you screw up. And so you start pulling back." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他说他们今年本来只要多花钱就能多涨 50%，但那样明年 CAC 就倒挂了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:44" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Good luck fundraising when you're going from 400 to 70. We could be growing 50% more if we wanted year over year this year just by spending more, but then next year you're in trouble because your cac is upside down." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

两个关键机制：一是**不看平台报的 CAC**——平台归因严重虚高，Meta 报的 CAC 可能比真实的低 20%，他们有自己的模型来定每个渠道的真实 CAC <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:25" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Why is that? Well, because you cannot trust there will be an excess of attribution from the same platform. We do the same with Meta." aria-label="回原文"></button>；二是**每半年做一次[[增量测试|增量测试]]**：挑两个表现相似的地区，在一个地区关掉某个渠道，看收入差异，立刻算出这个渠道的真实价值 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:53" data-who="Matteo" data-en="So to me, they could deploy $100 million if they are within CAC, the cap of the CAC. The important thing you need to do regularly, probably every six months, is an incrementality test." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

口碑占他们收入的约 40%，且持续复利式增长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:49" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And so that is another thing you want to assess, which is word of mouth. In our case, word of mouth keeps compounding. We are around 40% of our revenue, even at our size, which starts being really big, is 40%." aria-label="回原文"></button>。TikTok 是过去六个月才跑通的，关键是靠 TikTok 网红产出足够多的 UGC 内容 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:22" data-who="Matteo" data-en="How? It's usually a matter of amount of content that you can generate with TikTok influencers. So it's about getting a UGC program in place?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

户外广告是最大的错误——在中东投放时品牌还没准备好；户外广告行得通，但前提是提示后/未提示的品牌知名度达到一定水平，否则路人看到广告也不认识你 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:58" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And you're like, ah, I learned a lot of lessons from this. Out of home. Everything depends on the scale of the business in that country." aria-label="回原文"></button>。有意思的是他们没花钱投户外，而是造了一辆后部带卧室的卡车在硅谷巡游，可预约上门试用产品，转化率很高 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:09" data-who="Matteo" data-en="No. But we created a truck now that goes around Silicon Valley. And so you can book the truck and it comes to your home and you can try the product live." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Google 投放则受 AI 搜索冲击，他们新设了「AI SEO」监测项，每周两次看自己在 AI 搜索里的排名 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:18" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Yes. And so we introduced a new channel that now we monitor that is literally what we call AI SEO. Now in the report that I receive weekly, twice a week, there is also AI SEO." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 硬件创业的真相与中国市场

对想投硬件的投资人，他的忠告：公司头几个月创始人根本不清楚真实生产成本和退货率，他们告诉你的 BOM 和 COGS「很可能至少偏差 50%」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:25" data-who="Matteo" data-en="What are you like, dude, before you do it, you should know this. That probably the founders in the first year of the company or in the first probably, let's say three months, they don't fully understand what the cost of producing that device will be." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

中国市场是他的最难战场，因为「中国人不在网站上买东西」——没有我们所知的电商，一切都在微信、RedNote 这类超级应用里完成，他们只能从零学起 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:22" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Is it very different selling in China? Yes, because you cannot sell on 8slip.com. Literally, you don't sell on a website." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但他也承认，此时进入中国恰好是时机对了：已在 35 国销售、赞助了 Charles Leclerc、Elon 和扎克伯格都公开谈过他们，品牌已有信任度，能定出价格；「如果五年前做同样的事，我们会被人一个便宜 500 美元的山寨货毁掉」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:21" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And so you have a level of trust where you command a certain price. But if we were doing the same thing five years ago, we would be destroyed by a knockoff that is 500 bucks cheaper." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他反复强调「凡事都有时机」：零售不是不行，而是需要二阶条件成熟才能做，对 Eight Sleep 来说时机就是现在 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:42" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Well, let me step back for a second. One thing I'm learning as an entrepreneur is there is a time for things, right? Similar to what I was telling you about the out-of-home campaign." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 招聘、留人与夫妻创业

招聘市场他说「回到了 2021 年」，连产品经理的薪水都失控了，所以他们越来越转向欧洲和美国以外招人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:10" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Has it changed in terms of talent competitiveness? We are back to 2021, and that I think is becoming a problem in the US, meaning salaries are going out of control, competition." aria-label="回原文"></button>。比薪水更难的是留人。他给被挖角压力下的创始人的建议很硬：一个真在拿你和 Anthropic 之间「比较最终 offer」的人是危险信号——「这两份工作不可能有任何相似之处，这说明你很迷茫」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:45" data-who="Matteo" data-en="And so sometimes it's still happening today. Sometimes people say, oh, I'm at the final stage with Anthropic and Eight Sleep. And to me, that is a really bad red flag." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他提拔内部工程师做 VP 的做法也值得一提：先让他代理三个月、同时跑外部招聘做参照，人选定后还要求他「回去和妻子谈谈，她见证了你这三个月 130% 的投入」——因为要的是这个家庭一起承诺未来三四年 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="55:30" data-who="Matteo" data-en="I found that not the professional way to do it. But what I told him is, I'm going to give you the job, but I don't want you to answer tonight. You go and you talk to your wife because she has seen how hard you work over the past three months." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Alexandra 既是他的联合创始人也是妻子。他们的规则：工作走 Slack、生活走 WhatsApp，绝不混用；但更关键的是 Alexandra 立的规矩——「如果我是一个不坐在你旁边的高管，你会在晚上 10 点 15 分为这事打电话给他吗？

那就用你对待驻纽约运营 VP 的方式对待我」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:46" data-who="Matteo" data-en="I think the way she framed it was extremely smart. And she says, if I was an executive that was not sitting next to you, would you call them for the thing at 10, 15 p.m.?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。早期磨合最难，他学到的教训是：要求可以同样苛刻，但措辞和方式必须专业 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:21" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Yeah. And so that was one of her points where she pushed me in the early days and say, I have no problem in you being extremely demanding and pretending this by this time of the day, but the words that you use and how you say it, it cannot be just like I'm your buddy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 中国制造、机器人与更大胆的预测

他非常推崇中国公司：小米做 15 种不同的东西，他们证明了「聚焦」不是永远只做同一件事，而是把自己积累的能力用任何形式变现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:36" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Anything hardware-related, anything that is a smart home, but then they also get into components, like some of the components, like BYD develops certain components well outside the car and automotive." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他由此提出一个理论：AI 时代正确的公司会把自己变成控股公司，Eight Sleep 今天的主业未来只是旗下业务之一 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:32" data-who="Matteo" data-en="What drives that? So here is another theory that I have, and ours is just my opinion, but I think with AI, the right companies will transform themselves in holdings." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还认为所有中国车厂都会变成机器人公司——[[人形机器人|人形机器人]]将是「60 年代的汽车」，不转型的车厂连这个数万亿美元的市场也会一起丢掉 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:36" data-who="Matteo" data-en="Well, I worry even more about another factor. I think all the Chinese manufacturers, they will become robotic companies. <script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
