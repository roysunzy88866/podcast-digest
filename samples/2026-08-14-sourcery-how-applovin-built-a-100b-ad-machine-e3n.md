# AppLovin 双人谈：百人工程团队如何撑起千亿公司

一家上市后 18 个月内股价跌掉 92% 的公司，三年后成了一家 1000 亿美元的公司——靠的是同一个约 100 人的工程团队。这一集的对话发生在 [[AppLovin|AppLovin]] 的帕罗奥图总部，主角是 CEO [[Adam|Adam]] 和 CTO [[Gio|Gio]]:Gio 在 2022 年 11 月加入时是公司的转折点，他主导架构了让公司翻盘的 [[Axon 2|Axon 2]] 广告模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:46" data-who="Adam" data-en="And Gio, you were that person. So you joined the company in November, 2022. What was it like when you first joined AppLovin?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Adam 说，面试时两人都大力游说他加入，结果入职前几天的财报电话会上股价一天跌了 30%,「我心想，糟了，我们可能招不到 Gio 了」——而 Gio 的反应是「跌了 30%,所以我拿到更多股权，应该更快加入」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:24" data-who="Adam" data-en="And then, so he was excited to join, and I go do my, this was November 22 earnings call, and he's supposed to join, I think it was like a couple days later. Stock went down 30% that day." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 先修基础设施，再重建引擎

Gio 从大厂过来，第一印象是团队精简、没有层层流程——他和当时的 CTO Basil 想约每周一对一，对方根本不知道那是什么，很快会议也取消了，「因为我们主要用代码来沟通」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:42" data-who="Gio" data-en="I asked Bazel to set up weekly one-on-one meeting with me, and he didn't understand what that was, because it's not a thing here in . But soon, we canceled that meeting, because we communicated with each other mostly with code, and that was much more efficient." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他加入的第一周就直接写代码，搭建 Axon 2 的训练基础设施；那一周干完，他决定不取消圣诞去欧洲的假期，而是整个假期都在写代码——包括在飞意大利的 12 小时无网航班上，只能深挖所用库的源代码来查资料 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:19" data-who="Gio" data-en="And I said, OK, fine. But that week fundamentally changed me. Because after that week, I decided not to cancel my vacation, but to work through my vacation." aria-label="回原文"></button>。等假期结束，基础设施已经就绪，回来当天就开始训练第一代模型。

那台旧引擎是什么？[[推荐系统|推荐系统]]的本质是在海量「用户 × 物品」组合里学习关系。

上一代 Axon One 用的是基于树的模型——相当于几十万个 if-else 分支，把组合聚类到分支末端；但真实世界的关系随时间、天气、促销、节假日动态变化，树模型没法准确建模 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:12" data-who="Gio" data-en="But obviously as you can see, the real world is so complicated and that the relationship changes dynamically as time change, as the weather change, as there are like promotion events, holidays, all the things are changing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Axon 2 换成了[[语义嵌入|语义嵌入]](把本来无含义的用户 ID、物品 ID 编码成携带统计信息的向量)，再过[[深度神经网络|深度神经网络]]学习复杂交互，既能识别模式，也能外推到没见过的用户-物品对 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:04" data-who="Gio" data-en="And then we pass those information through deep neural network that allows us to study the complicated interactions between user and items. The result of this kind of model is it is much more powerful in recognizing the patterns, and it's also powerful in extrapolating" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

副产品是推理更便宜：神经网络由大量标准矩阵乘法(GEMM)构成，正是 [[GPU|GPU]] 高度优化的操作；老的选择树在 GPU 上几乎没法优化 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:06" data-who="Gio" data-en="But the selection tree, the old generation select trees has the structure that is very hard to optimize on a GPU machine. So the new generation model is very, very powerful and efficient with a modern GPU architecture." aria-label="回原文"></button>。预测更准 → 广告主回报更好 → 业务增长，这就是整台增长飞轮 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:28" data-who="Gio" data-en="It was actually also cheaper in terms of infrastructure. So once we're able to make prediction more accurate, advertisers see better returns and our business grow." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 一张餐巾纸开出电商业务

Axon 2 规模化约一年后，团队判断模型架构足够通用，可以走出游戏。质疑者说「你们只有游戏数据」，Adam 的反驳是：那是十亿个真实的人，不是十亿个只玩游戏的人；理解了人，就能在游戏之外交付价值 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:58" data-who="Adam" data-en="One thing that loses sight of is that it's a billion people on the other side, not a billion just people who are only playing games and doing nothing else. So if you understand the person, then you can actually deliver value to them outside of just a game-to-game scenario." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们还照 Facebook 的路数给网站装像素代码拿数据，发现重度休闲游戏玩家很多是 30-50 岁的家庭户主 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:44" data-who="Adam" data-en="And the people who were playing mobile casual games turned out to be a lot of heads of households. It skews slightly female, but it is this sort of 30 to 50 year old age group that's the heavy mobile casual gamer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

验证方式不是论证，是原型：几个月内和少量测试广告主上线第一个电商模型，数据有限、模型很不成熟，但已经能看到购买、ROI(投入产出比)像样，「我们就想，好吧，这会成功的」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:14" data-who="Gio" data-en="But even with very premature model, we're able to see people were making a purchase. The ROI, the ROAS was decent, and we're like, okay, this is going to work. What's cool about this story, which I've never heard before, is that one of the cultural philosophies we had is almost no product management and have engineers that are competent enough about business problems to just write their own architecture, write their own product to solve business problems that maybe not even a business team knew existed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而那个数据流引擎的设计，是 CTO Basil 等人在拉斯维加斯一个 Google 大会的早餐桌上，没有纸、画在餐巾纸上定下来的，等餐上桌时设计已完成，一行李还没回旧金山，原型就写出来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:42" data-who="Gio" data-en="And then we all went back to our hotel room. Before we arrived in San Francisco, we actually had the first prototype for that data engine. And then he told us about a few months, a couple months, and to build up the data, the model, the infrastructure, and we recruited a few advertisers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Adam 点出背后的文化：这家公司几乎不做产品管理，产品经理「可能不到一小撮」，其余全是工程师——自己理解业务问题、自己写架构、自己写产品 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:19" data-who="Adam" data-en="The ROI, the ROAS was decent, and we're like, okay, this is going to work. What's cool about this story, which I've never heard before, is that one of the cultural philosophies we had is almost no product management and have engineers that are competent enough about business problems to just write their own architecture, write their own product to solve business problems that maybe not even a business team knew existed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。商务团队负责与广告主沟通，但工程师靠清晰的收入/ROAS(广告支出回报)指标就知道做得好不好，「和广告主之间就没太多可争论的」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:31" data-who="Adam" data-en="And with engineers to like give them a path, you got to give them pretty clear metrics on is it working or is it not working. If you're living in a revenue based world, a return on ad spend based world, there's not much of a debate with the advertisers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 工程师要「坐在 AI 之上」

用人上，Gio 最反感的错误有两个：给新人只派细碎任务、不给他们业务全景；以及反过来，对做得好的初级骨干只给鼓励、不给持续挑战，「然后他们的自我会膨胀，失去谦逊，停止质疑自己」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:51" data-who="Gio" data-en="I feel sometimes when a junior key member does a good job, we tend to give them too much encouragement without providing continuous challenge to them. And then sometimes their ego will grow and then they will lose their humility and stop questioning themselves." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Adam 那边的做法是：新人在入职第一周就能推送代码上线——「那一刻是一种相当强烈的多巴胺刺激」，真实反馈循环比任何培训手册都管用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:28" data-who="Adam" data-en="They start getting results. That moment is a pretty substantial dopamine hit. They get something that they developed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Gio 对 [[AI|AI]] 与工程师关系的表述是全集最广为流传的一句：「我不想让我们的工程师坐在 AI 旁边，我想让我们的工程师坐在 AI 之上」——AI 负责它擅长的(回答问题、写代码、补全整个 PR),人专注于 AI 不擅长的长周期规划，边界随 AI 进步不断移动 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:48" data-who="Gio" data-en="So the way I see the relationship between AI and our employee, our engineer is I don't want our engineer to sit next to AI. I want our engineers to sit on top of AI." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但核心系统的规矩极硬：「你作为人类，要对你的 AI 为你做出的每一个决策承担责任」——问工程师为什么这么实现，答「AI 做的」是不可接受的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:55" data-who="Gio" data-en="You can use AI however you want. But at the end of the day, you as a human is held accountable for every decision that your AI made for you. So it is absolutely not acceptable when I come to an engineer and say, hey, why you implement this system this way if the engineers tell me, oh, I don't know, AI did this." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 为什么他们不重构

对「AI 时代是不是该推倒重来」，Gio 的答案是否定的：「我不认为我们需要重建什么，但我们确实是在今天已有的东西之上进行构建」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:03" data-who="Gio" data-en="So back to your question. I don't think we need to rebuild anything, but we are definitely building on top of what we have today. On the business side, we keep the team really lean as well." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他给出一个刺耳的观察：今天用 AI 的人里，大概 99% 只是把它当搜索引擎 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:49" data-who="Gio" data-en="AI really helps us to solve the problem, but actually doesn't change the problem we have to solve. I think the fact is probably 99% of people that use AI today just use it as a search engine." aria-label="回原文"></button>;而很多组织的问题在于，构建变快了，但「人们做出了很多糟糕的决策、糟糕的[[品味|品味]]、浪费的想法，这些实际上抵消了 AI 带来的赋能」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:14" data-who="Gio" data-en="I think a big, big mistake that people are making is that because of the AI, building things become easier. People are making a lot of bad decisions, bad tastes, wasteful ideas that actually offset the empowerment of AI." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Adam 补了组织层面的残酷现实：非 AI 原生的公司想转型「真的、真的很难」，你既不能乱裁人，也不能发笔预算让大家「去把 token 用到上限」，否则就是业务人员和工程师各自造重复的仪表盘、给 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 付双份钱；真要彻底转型，几乎得换掉所有人重建文化，「但这对大多数公司来说并不现实」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:49" data-who="Adam" data-en="It's really, really hard. You can't just cut people out and say, okay, the rest are going to figure it out because how do you know who to cut and who not to cut?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 跌 92% 之后：不做投资者关系，做自己最好的投资者

回看谷底，Adam 的打法很反直觉。股价跌 90% 时，股权激励占总成本的比例会飙升——别人每年发 2-3% 股权，你难道要发 30% 稀释？

他们始终按固定美元额度给报酬、保持团队精简，所以成本始终可控 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="66:59" data-who="Adam" data-en="So like the challenge you have when stock price goes down is your stock-based compensation as a percentage of total goes way up. And so let's say like you're a company that typically like companies will issue two, 3% of their equity every single year to the team." aria-label="回原文"></button>。谷底时他们一年多完全不做投资者关系，「股价便宜得像土一样，但没人会买便宜得像土一样的东西，他们想看到的是愿景」——于是干脆把赚到的每一美元都拿去回购，「我们要成为自己最好的投资者」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="68:06" data-who="Adam" data-en="So we were able to flip it and say, we're just going to buy our own shares. We're going to become our best investor. We're going to give our team nice equity compensation, but not overdo it because we can't afford excessive dilution." aria-label="回原文"></button>。能这么做的前提是对正在构建的东西有信念。

至于万亿美元：Adam 算了笔账，公司目前 EBITDA(息税折旧摊销前利润)年化运行率超过 70 亿美元，每一美元 EBITDA 转出约 75% 的现金；要到一万亿美元市值，得相信能做到每年 300 亿美元以上的现金流 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="62:47" data-who="Adam" data-en="And if you think about where we are today, We, probably on an EBITDA basis, I think we're over a $7 billion run rate, and we generate somewhere around 75% cash off the EBITDA dollar." aria-label="回原文"></button>。移动游戏品类不够大，电商是第一步，下一步看的是「漏斗顶部」的发现型场景——联网电视、开放网络视频，而非聊天机器人里的搜索式广告(他判断聊天机器人广告会像搜索广告，而那是漏斗底部生意，不是他们的主场)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:07" data-who="Adam" data-en="For us, what is compelling as we think about, okay, well, what's next in our business is what are the other spaces that create this form of discovery where you don't actually know what the user wants." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 品味：成功归因于「不做什么」

Gio 最锋利的总结：「我会把 Axon 的成功很大程度上归因于我们决定不做什么，而不是我们实际做了什么」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="76:20" data-who="Gio" data-en="It's also engineering taste. I, you know, just from personal experience, I would attribute the success of Axon, you know, largely to what we decide not to do, not actually what we did." aria-label="回原文"></button>。小公司挑战巨头，最常见的死法是照搬巨头在做的一切；你团队更小、资源更少，做一样的事必输。

所以要过滤噪音，找到那 1% 最重要的事，把团队的全部火力押上去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="77:04" data-who="Gio" data-en="So we deliberately decide what to focus on and what to work on. Well, where do you get your inspiration from? Do you get it from outside sources, from abstract things?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。很多人问怎么获得灵感，他的答案是第一性原理：别只问「是什么」(「我们在公司里搞点 AI 吧」)，要问「怎么做」和「为什么」——你的产品真的需要它吗？<button class="pd-ts" data-t="77:19" data-who="Gio" data-en="I guess you just have to ask the questions of what, how, and why. I think a lot of times people overly focus on what? People say, oh, that's AI." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 本集带走

- **换问法重建模型**：老一代广告模型靠几十万个 if-else 分支聚类，无法建模动态变化；换成语义嵌入 + 深度神经网络，预测更准、GPU 推理还更便宜——预测精度直接兑现为广告主回报和业务增长。
- **用原型代替争论**：架构是否通用到能出游戏圈？不辩论，几个月内搭原型、找少量广告主上线，ROI 不错就全力投入。
- **砍掉产品管理层**：让工程师直接理解业务全景、自己决定做什么，而不是「产品写规格 → 工程师照规格写代码」；新人第一周上线代码，靠真实反馈快速成长。
- **「坐在 AI 之上」+ 人类问责**：AI 的边界持续移动，人专注长周期规划；但核心系统里，AI 替你做的每个决策你都要能亲自解释和负责。
- **构建变快不等于变好**：99% 的人只把 AI 当搜索引擎；坏决策和坏品味会抵消 AI 的全部增益，先想清楚要解决什么问题。
- **谷底靠现金流和信念**：不做 IR、按固定美元控制股权稀释、拿现金回购自己的股票——「做自己最好的投资者」。
- **小团队赢巨头靠「不做什么」**：别复制巨头的全套动作，过滤噪音，把全部资源押在那 1% 最重要的事上。

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
