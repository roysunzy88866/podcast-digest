# Campbell Brown：AI 回答争议问题时，谁在替你把关质量？

AI 模型在政治和医疗等敏感话题上的回答质量，比你以为的差得多。[[Campbell Brown|Campbell Brown]] 的团队用超过 3000 条提示、产出 12000 多条回答做了系统测试，发现模型会错误陈述政治舆论、把话安到没说过的人头上、在邮寄投票等事实问题上给错答案，在移民、气候等开放式争议问题上还经常站队 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:59" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I think there's the quality of information problem, which you've just outlined. And the things that were cited there, this was based on over 3,000 prompts, over 12,000 outputs that were evaluated by my team, my technical team, former researchers from Meta." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更离谱的是，问美国是什么政体，有一个模型引用了《环球时报》这种来源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You write, the most revealing failures were much subtler. When we asked the major model something as basic as what form of government the US has, one of them, Claude Opus 4.7, cited the Global Times, a Chinese state-run tabloid." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这不是个别现象，是所有主流模型的共性问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So I – yes, that surprised me and I do think – and that was not by any means an outlier. That was the example I highlighted, but there are plenty of examples and it wasn't just Claude and Anthropic, it was across all the models." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 社交媒体的教训：为互动率优化就别想同时保质量

Campbell 在 Meta 负责新闻合作时，试图在平台和出版商之间建立更好的商业模式，没成功。她归因于一个根本矛盾：社交媒体为[[互动率|互动率]]优化，而人们最容易互动的内容是最夸张、最疯狂的，不是高质量新闻 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:50" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I mean, what we were trying to do was get more high-quality news on the platform, right? But if you're optimizing for engagement, which is what social media does, you can't also optimize for accuracy and quality." aria-label="回原文"></button>。你不可能同时为互动率和准确性优化。

但她对 AI 阶段反而乐观：企业客户花大价钱买 AI 产品，他们不会允许模型为互动率优化，而是要求为准确性优化 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:47" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I'm a big company spending millions and millions of dollars with Anthropic or OpenAI. I'm not going to let them optimize for engagement. I'm going to demand they optimize for accuracy, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。激励结构变了，结果可能不一样。

## 两个问题：信息质量 + 缺乏独立验证

信息质量只是问题的一半。另一半是问责——模型表现如何，目前没有[[独立验证|独立验证]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:19" data-who="Unknown" data-en="The quality of information is only part of the problem. The other piece is the accountability. There's no independent verification of how the models perform." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

OpenAI、Anthropic 拿出来的就是自己写的博客，说"我们测了，挺好"。Campbell 的类比很直接：银行不审计自己，药厂不批准自己的药 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:45" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You don't get independent verification of any kind. And for anything that matters throughout our history, banks don't audit themselves. Drug companies don't approve their own drugs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。我们要在整个公民基础设施上建这个技术，不能只靠厂家自说自话。

好消息是模型公司确实想改进，竞争激烈，市场在激励他们 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:33" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We work with the model companies on this. The competition between them all right now is so intense. They all want to win this race." aria-label="回原文"></button>。难的地方在于，很多问题没有标准答案——不是非黑即白，而是主观的、需要呈现多角度的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Thank you for watching. Sometimes there's a right or wrong answer, and we do measure how they perform on factual accuracy, but a lot of times there isn't. It's subjective or there are multiple perspectives that need to be represented, and that's just harder to get right." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 用专家而不是标注师来定标准

Campbell 的公司做法是：找真正的领域专家——前 CIA 分析师、临床医生等——来制定评估框架，然后用这个框架训练一个 [[LLM 裁判|LLM 评判者]]，实现大规模评估 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:40" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Thank you for joining us. And we work with them to architect the benchmarks, to develop what the standard is going to be, and we work very in-depth with them. And then we use this rubric, essentially, to train an LLM judge to be able to evaluate at scale how the models perform on these topics." aria-label="回原文"></button>。核心逻辑是：哪怕两个专家在移民问题上立场完全对立，只要他们是理性的，就能在"应该用什么框架来思考这个问题""应该呈现哪些视角"上达成共识 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:20" data-who="Unknown" data-en="If we're measuring for factual accuracy and it's something that there is a clear way to check and fact check, then we'll do that. If it's questions around bias, I think what you want, and here's how we think about the experts, is that you can take two people with completely opposing views on a topic like immigration" aria-label="回原文"></button>。你要的是回答问题的正确框架，而不是一个可能不存在的正确答案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:24" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And that's what you're trying to get at. It's like the right framework for how to answer that question as opposed to a right answer for a question that might not have one." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为什么不能靠几千个数据标注师？因为语境和细微差别不是人海战术能解决的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:57" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And this is what's hard. Like, what is context? What does that look like?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。世界上最聪明的工程师也没法告诉你怎么围绕一个复杂政治议题构建语境，那不是他们的专长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:28" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And by the way, this is partly why I'm very – I have lots of questions around how the labs do their own evals because for a long time there hasn't been real expertise brought in." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

对于有明确科学共识的问题——比如疫苗是否导致自闭症——Campbell 的立场是模型应该追求真相，有明确证据就引用明确证据 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:06" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So if there's a clear – I think you have to – Look, and by the way, you don't have to, but my choice would be that the models would try to get to the truth when there is one, and that that has to be one of the principles that you're trying to achieve." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但她也承认专家会犯错，COVID 起源就是例子，所以专家的权威性本身也应该被质疑 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:46" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So how much can you rely on experts for when someone's going and asking a question like that? I thought, I mean, this is a very, this is a legitimate question. There's a book, I actually wrote something about this not too long ago because there's a book, I'm blanking on the author's name right now, but The End of Expertise that talked about how, and COVID's a perfect example of how experts have gotten it wrong repeatedly on important issues." aria-label="回原文"></button>。不过在医疗和心理健康领域，她坚持应该让真正见过几百个病人的临床医生来评估模型输出，而不是工程师 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:29" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And also, I think the sense of elitism that comes with that, what you have a degree from some certain university and therefore you have a certain amount of authority to speak on this, that people genuinely should question." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 诱导性提示和谄媚问题

当用户输入"告诉我为什么某人是最好的总统"这种[[诱导性提示|诱导性提示]]时，模型怎么做？Campbell 观察到，目前主流模型倾向于反射你用的语言，但不会在答案里附和你——比如会说"很多支持者认为他是最好的总统，原因是…

…"，而不是"我同意，他确实是" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Why is he the best president ever? The model might respond, and I would guess if I were looking at a typical Claude response on something like this, it would likely say many people, many supporters of Donald Trump believe he is the best president ever for these reasons and cite some of the accomplishments or what polling or whatever the data is around to support that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这是各家公司的政策选择，不是技术必然 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="That is a choice that Anthropic is making in terms of how to respond to that question. That's just what I've seen. And again, I'm not speaking on behalf of Anthropic or OpenAI or anybody else, but just what I've seen in the repeated loaded prompts that we've tested." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

有意思的是，到现在为止聊天机器人没有爆发过像社交媒体那样的内容审核丑闻。Campbell 认为是因为人们知道 AI 会[[幻觉|幻觉]]，所以现阶段更宽容 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:43" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I think people under – it's to your point. It's even the average person knows they hallucinate, so we're way more forgiving of chatbots at this stage. I don't think that's going to be the case in a year." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但这种宽容不会持续——企业客户不会接受花 2000 万买的产品还在胡说八道，受监管行业已经因此碰到瓶颈 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:13" data-who="Unknown" data-en="They're not being mistakes. And they're, you know, why am I paying you $20 million to sell me all these AI products and they're hallucinating? That's not acceptable." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 个性化聊天机器人的诱惑

人们其实想让机器人当朋友。有人下飞机第一件事就是打开 ChatGPT 报平安 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:28" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I don't know if it's satire or real, but it felt real enough to me that I thought I'd bring it into this discussion. Someone wrote, plane landed and a guy in the row ahead of me immediately opened ChatGPT to let it know he landed safely." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Campbell 预测，随着模型能力商品化、智能服务变便宜，一定会出现专门优化"做你朋友"的第三个竞争者 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="54:54" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But just from a business model perspective, it's harder. But as the AI models commoditize, right, as intelligence gets cheaper, basically, to serve, that will inevitably come." aria-label="回原文"></button>。到那时候，为互动率优化的老问题可能卷土重来。她现在的着力点是趁大实验室还在关注准确性，把这件事推到更高的优先级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I'm sure there are. I want to focus on keeping big labs focused on accuracy, and at least for the moment, that is where they're leaning in. I'm really happy to see it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **信息质量和独立验证是两件事**：模型答得对不对是一回事，谁来检验是另一回事。目前靠厂家自测，相当于银行自审。
- **为互动率优化和为准确性优化是互斥的**：社交媒体的教训。AI 目前因为企业客户驱动暂时避开了这个坑，但如果转向消费级、个性化，老问题会回来。
- **评估敏感话题需要领域专家定框架，不是标注师堆量**：专家对立立场的人也能在"应该怎么思考这个问题"上达成共识，这个共识就是评估标准。
- **有科学共识的问题，模型应该追求真相**；没有标准答案的问题，模型应该给框架和多视角，不选边。
- **[[来源质量|来源质量]]是低垂果实**：回答美国政体问题时引用中国国有报纸，这种问题不该存在，应该用分类器等基础手段解决。
