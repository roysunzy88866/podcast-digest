# Claude Fable 5 亲测：最聪明模型的能干与不能干

<div class="pd-byl">2026-09-03</div>

<div class="pd-play"><button class="pb" type="button" aria-label="播放">▶</button><span class="tt"><span class="t1">听中文精华</span><span class="t2">AI 合成朗读</span></span><span class="bar"><i></i></span><span class="tm">00:00</span><audio preload="metadata" src="/audio/2026-06-09-howiai-claude-fable-5-review-what-the-new-mytho.mp3">你的浏览器不支持音频播放,或音频尚未生成。</audio></div>

[[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 的新模型发布了：[[Mythos|Mythos]] 级(全新一代、高于 Sonnet 和 Opus 的模型档位)的第一个正式版本，叫 **Claude [[Fable 5|Fable 5]]**。本集是 How I AI 的主持人拿早期访问权限，站在「普通用户、普通软件工程师」的角度做的第一手实测——不测生物学、网络安全那些高大上场景，就看日常干活到底好不好用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:26" data-who="" data-en="She's not working on biology quite yet, although give me give me a little time and some time to experiment. Maybe I'll get there. So this is really going to be focused on what the everyday user, what the everyday software engineer is going to think about when they're using this model." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

先说价格，免得你被基准测试冲昏头：**每个输入 token 10 美元、输出 50 美元**，是高于 Opus 的一个新档位 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:43" data-who="" data-en="Quick, it's not cheap. It's $10 per input token and $50 per output token. It's going to be a new tier above Opus." aria-label="回原文"></button>。而且它在设计上就是「token 大胃王」——官方说它消耗速率限制和 token 的速度约为其他模型的 **2 倍** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:03" data-who="" data-en="Now, guess what it also can do? It can consume those tokens. So Anthropic has said it consumes rate limits and tokens at about 2x the rate of other models." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

主持人全程用最高的 extra high 档跑任务，形容是「token 燃烧叠加 token 燃烧」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:14" data-who="" data-en="But it's just something to think about when you're working with any high intelligence model is how much intelligence does the task actually take? Now as I said before it is token intensive by design and I did most of my tasks on extra high and so it was like token burning on token burning and so they say that high is probably the sweet spot for most work I used extra high just because I don't want anybody in the comments saying Claire" aria-label="回原文"></button>。她最关心的问题是：这种 token 强度是否真的换来了对等的产出？「这正是我没法 100% 确定的地方」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:56" data-who="" data-en="My question for any of these models, this is not an Anthropic model question, this is not a Fable question, is does this token intensity actually output? And that's a place where I'm just not 100% sure." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 官方怎么介绍它

Anthropic 的说法是：全新模型类别、当前最先进、所有测试基准都大幅领先——**SweeBench Pro 上拿到 80%**,远超 Opus 4.8、GPT 5.5、Gemini 3.1 Pro <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:08" data-who="" data-en="It is exceeding every benchmark they tested by a significant amount. This 80% on SweeBench Pro, you'll look at that compared to some of the more recent models that have come out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。官方主打三个卖点：

- **极强自主性**：能运行持续数天的异步任务，启动子智能体(把任务拆给多个小智能体并行干)、保持多天会话 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:27" data-who="" data-en="Now, what are some things that earlier models couldn't do that they are saying now that Fablefy can do? It's very autonomous, including running days-long asynchronous tasks." aria-label="回原文"></button>。主持人没拿到模型那么多天，无法验证「跑四天」，但确实让它在几个任务上跑了几小时——她自己也承认这些任务「可能值、也可能不值这几个小时的努力」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:57" data-who="" data-en="I didn't have Fable for many days, so I cannot verify that it ran for days. I did get it to run, however, for several hours on some tasks that may or may not have merited that several hour effort, but it definitely seems like it has both the hardest and the intelligence capability to run for a very long time, if that's appropriate for your task." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **「工程师中的工程师」**：调查极其完整、搜遍所有角落、要 120% 确定才交付 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:25" data-who="" data-en="Unfortunately, if you have worked with a seasoned engineer, you know there's good to this and you know there's bad to this. So it is very complete in its investigation." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **[[视觉能力|视觉能力]]**：官方称 exceptionally good,主持人也承认这是「视觉能力前进了一步」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:49" data-who="" data-en="This is a place where I actually really loved the model. And you know me, I'm pretty critical of models, but I did see a step ahead of vision. So that's something we're going to dive into." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 一个核心认知：太聪明反而是负担

官方说 Fable「工作起来像个经验丰富的工程师」——主持人马上接了一句大实话：跟老工程师共事过的人都知道，这有好也有坏 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:18" data-who="" data-en="They explicitly say that Fable works like a seasoned engineer. Unfortunately, if you have worked with a seasoned engineer, you know there's good to this and you know there's bad to this." aria-label="回原文"></button>。它过度彻底，不总是服务于发布，更不总是服务于做出好产品；「有时候你想要的是一个稍微不那么彻底的工程师…

…稍微笨一点」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:47" data-who="" data-en="That's not always in service of launching and that's honestly not always in service of building a great product. So while you can give it a goal and it will be very autonomous and it will be very thorough, honestly, sometimes you want like a slightly less thorough engineer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

使用任何高智能模型前都得先问：**这个任务实际需要多少智能？** 老老实实把简单任务交给 Sonnet 或 Opus,可能才是对的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:06" data-who="" data-en="We'll talk about some of the prompting techniques it says and when to use this model. But it's just something to think about when you're working with any high intelligence model is how much intelligence does the task actually take?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 实测里惊艳的地方

**文档排版(PDF/视觉类任务)是最大惊喜。** 她用给七岁孩子做练字文档这种小事测试：Fable 5 排出的二年级练字纸间距刚好、留白足、清晰易读，而 Opus 排的版本又密、线都分不清该写在线上还是线下 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:21" data-who="" data-en="So this is super simple. But we've been doing these handwriting documents for my seven-year-old based on classic texts and classic poems. And on the right is Opus 4A and on the left is Mythos 5." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她拿一堆不同模型对比过，Fable 在 PDF 排版上确实最好，「非常简单的评估，但是非常非常好的一个」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:04" data-who="" data-en="Mythos 5 really did a good job. So very simple eval for me, but a very, very good one. Now, here's the problem, though." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**基准测试应该站得住。** 她虽然没测最尖端的用例，但「没发现它在技术上失败的地方」，认为这些跑分是真的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:40" data-who="" data-en="Very far ahead of these other models. While I wasn't testing the most advanced use cases, I didn't find something that technically it failed at. So I think these benchmarks are really going to hold." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 实测里翻车的地方

**写文档给人读，几乎没法读。** 她让 Fable 对自己的需求文档做对抗性审查(专门挑逻辑毛病的审读)，得到一份看起来很长很聪明的 Markdown,但全是内部引用、大段大段的文字，「非常详细，但不是那种能让你拉远看全局的方式」——见树不见林 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:25" data-who="" data-en="There are these big blocks of paragraphs, like look at, look at this. It is just really hard to see the forest for the trees in this particular model. And I saw this sort of like over and over again, working on it with specs is it was very complete." aria-label="回原文"></button>。结论：**写 spec、写 PRD 别用它**，退回 Sonnet/Opus;把 Fable 当「执行的编排器」——细节真正重要、但你不用去读的那些地方 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:43" data-who="" data-en="And that's a real challenge when working with these very, very high intelligence models. Again, I would actually suggest pulling back to maybe a Sonnet or Opus model for specs and then looking at Fable as an orchestrator of execution where that detail really matters, but you don't have to read it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**前端/设计出奇地差。** 让它设计一个技能注册表，结果连「AI 垃圾内容」式的差都不如——灰黑红配色、简单轮廓，「从根本上就很糟糕」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:24" data-who="" data-en="AI slop bad is like fundamentally terrible design. Gray, black, red, simple outlines, just really, really terrible. Now, the Anthropic team suggested that I just needed to be a little bit more detailed in my prompting." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Anthropic 团队建议提示词写详细点，但她说过去一年的前端模型从没需要过这样，而且加了细节依然平平。设计活还是掺个 Opus 进来吧 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:00" data-who="" data-en="I'm gonna have to keep testing it as it rolls out today, but this was a real disappointment in terms of design. So again, you might want to toss an Opus in the mix instead of relying on Fable for design." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**执行过于保守。** 让它交付一个「客户能拿到价值」的 MVP,它把「最小」牢记在心——产出非常窄、没什么实际用处 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:17" data-who="" data-en="So when I was trying to do that ambitious days long work, I took a spec and I said, can you ship the V0 of this, the MVP? I said enough that a customer could get value." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她怀疑这来自模型的安全调优，并指出从后期 Opus 开始她就发现这些模型「不是特别有雄心」，需要靠提示词把长时程产出和正确的产品雄心配起来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:36" data-who="" data-en="And it's been a challenge I've seen since the kind of later Opus models is they're not super ambitious. And so again, you'll have to think about how to prompt this to get that long-running outcome paired with the right product ambition." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**[[多智能体|多智能体]]有成功也有坑。** 多智能体编排能力确实在，她成功跑过一些，但也有大量停滞和报错——离开电脑三小时回来发现子智能体全卡死了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:15" data-who="" data-en="I walked away from my laptop and came back to these sub-agents that had stalled after about 3 hours? And so like, egg on my face." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她认为这更多是 [[Claude Code|Claude Code]] 的问题而非模型本身，但既然承诺了「跑数天」，技术上就得把这个结果交付出来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:32" data-who="" data-en="I think this is a Claude Code issue, not necessarily a model issue. Although with this promise of long-running, days-long prompts, you really got to deliver technically on the outcome." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 安全护栏与版本区别

这是很多人关心的：**Fable 5 算不算 Mythos?** 算，但有护栏 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:52" data-who="" data-en="Is this or is this not Mythos? It is Mythos. Fable has the safeguards." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

模型里内置了针对网络安全、生物、化学和蒸馏(别家偷模仿它的输出)的特定分类器，命中后不是直接拒绝，而是**优雅回退到 Opus 4.8**——这在 messages API 里也是个可选参数(按 Opus 价格用 4.8 兜底)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:06" data-who="" data-en="And so if you get classified into one of these categories, instead of saying like, do not pass go, you may no longer fable, it just falls you back to Opus 4.8. This is also a capability in the API now where you can do this graceful fallback to 4.8 if you're using a Mythos class model." aria-label="回原文"></button>。另有 30 天数据保留政策，仅用于抓滥用、不用于训练 Claude。

实际影响很小：**95% 的会话没触发回退** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:36" data-who="" data-en="So while it's still not training Claude, they do want to check the use of this model because they have been and will forever be very cautious about us normies using their intelligent models." aria-label="回原文"></button>。没护栏的完整 Mythos 目前只对 Project Glasswing 等少数企业伙伴开放，但底层是同一个模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:20" data-who="" data-en="I would suspect that at some point we get some access to a Fable 5 point whatever, or that the Project Glasswing class opens up. But for now, we get Fable, Project Glasswing, or these pre-selected companies get Mythos, but they are all fundamentally the same underlying model." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

配套发布还有两样：**[[Cloud Managed Agents|Cloud Managed Agents]]**(Anthropic 托管的沙箱，跑长时程智能体工作，Fable 开箱即用——不过主持人还在想它有什么好的用例)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:38" data-who="" data-en="A couple of product things that are also launching today, along with the Fable 5 model, Cloud Managed Agents are going into public beta. If you haven't paid attention, this is Anthropic's hosted harness, hosted sandbox for running long running agentic work." aria-label="回原文"></button>;以及**顾问策略**——把 Fable 5 当高级顾问、用更便宜的模型当执行层，今天就能在 API 和 Claude Code 里用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:03" data-who="" data-en="There's also a new advisor strategy where you can use Fable 5 as a senior advisor and use cheaper models as an execution layer. A lot of people are doing this with Opus and Sonnet." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **把难题交给它**：细节极其重要的高难度技术问题、长时程工作、以及视觉/文档解析类任务，是 Fable 5 的甜区 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:42" data-who="" data-en="So what's my takeaway? I would hand it hard problems. Of course, not cybersecurity, bio or chemistry problems, but hard technical problems where being extremely detailed matters, long horizon work." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **别把前端和策略/spec 交给它**：设计出奇地差，文笔几乎没法读，过度思考 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:04" data-who="" data-en="I was actually really surprised. I probably wouldn't hand it my front-end work, or I definitely wouldn't hand it my front-end work, and I definitely wouldn't hand it strategy or spec work." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **别无脑用最强模型**：先问「这个任务需要多少智能」，简单活留给 Sonnet/Opus,它比什么都贵、还吃 2 倍 token <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:19" data-who="" data-en="It's better than anything. And honestly, I still think there's a place for good old Sonnet. I think there's a place for Opus, and I think there's a place for other models in the ecosystem." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **低智模型的活它也能兼**：可用「顾问策略」让 Fable 当大脑、便宜模型当手，摊薄成本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:03" data-who="" data-en="There's also a new advisor strategy where you can use Fable 5 as a senior advisor and use cheaper models as an execution layer. A lot of people are doing this with Opus and Sonnet." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **安全回退机制对你影响不大**：95% 会话不会触发；真被分类器拦了也只是降级到 Opus 4.8 继续跑 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:36" data-who="" data-en="So while it's still not training Claude, they do want to check the use of this model because they have been and will forever be very cautious about us normies using their intelligent models." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **想用好它先读官方提示词指南**：核心就是「把你最难的问题交给它」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:31" data-who="" data-en="I'm going to test it. If you want to learn more, definitely look up the prompting guide for Fable. It's going to probably repeat a lot of what I said." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
