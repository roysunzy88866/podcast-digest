[[Ryan Carson|Ryan Carson]] 做了一个叫 Untangle 的产品，一开始面向消费者做 AI 离婚工具，结果没人买单——人们要的是真人律师。他转头去找律师聊，发现律师助理短缺、证据开示(离婚案中交换证据的法律流程)是噩梦，于是转型 B2B 卖给家事律师事务所，业务立刻爆发 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:02" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Turns out nobody wants to use AI for divorce. They want lawyers, people. I'm just going to say it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

一年前他上这档节目时，还在 IDE 里手把手地、一步步指令式地教[[智能体|智能体]]干活。现在他几乎不打开 IDE 了，全部工程工作搬到了[[云端智能体|云端智能体]] [[Devon|Devon]] 上，同时跑 10 到 15 个线程 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:12" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It was like three-step markdown file. We were in an IDE, which I haven't opened an IDE in months. We were in an IDE and we were really, you were very prescriptive and directive of your agents." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:12" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I was like, why isn't Cloud? Like, I feel like I should be working on the Cloud. And I thought, what about Devon?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他一个月在 Devon 上花了 5000 美元，后来涨到 20000 美元，最后去找 Devon 的团队谈了免费额度 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I started looking at that and was really impressed. So now I went all in, so much so I spent, at one point I was spending $5,000 on Devon, and then it went up to $20,000 one month." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 用文件夹管优先级，用纸管注意力

同时跑十几个线程，怎么不乱？他的方法是用文件夹按优先级分：P0 是必须推进的核心新功能，P1、P2 往下排，Bug 单独一个文件夹 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And you'll notice... I've got bugs, right? I've got P0, P1, P2." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="P0 is the big stuff. I'm not gonna show you that, but that's just like, these are the main things I need to move forward as the business, new features, usually. P1 is like, ah, stuff that's important." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他发现人能同时跟踪的并发任务大概就是四五个，多了就乱了，所以必须分桶 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:33" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I've got probably, you know, 10, probably 10, 15 threads. And I bucket them in folders because I remember one of your shows, you know, it was either you or your guest was saying it's sort of like four or five concurrent tasks you can actually keep track of." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

除了数字分桶，他还用一张纸写每周优先事项，贴在显示器旁边。他认为核心技能已经不是写代码，而是管理——就像管理 10 个、100 个员工一样，怎么排优先级、怎么委派，这些管理能力在智能体时代直接可以复用，而且回报是倍数的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:40" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is my real setup that I'm literally working on today. And, you know, I think one of my big messages for today is that all of us have to up-level our ability to manage agents." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The reason why I think folks of a certain level of experience are like ready to cook right now with AI is all those management skills that you have developed over your career can be applied right here." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Watchdog：让智能体帮你盯业务

他一个人兼客户成功、工程、销售，客户多了之后根本盯不过来。于是他搭了一个叫 Watchdog 的[[剧本|剧本]](一种预定义的指令模板)：针对每个客户账号，自动进去查自从上次检查以来的活动、Sentry 错误日志，然后过滤排序，列出排名前三的问题，再检查是不是已经被修了、有没有未合并的 PR <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's called Watchdog. And, you know, we're starting to pick up a lot of family law firms now and they have a lot of cases, right? And I'm literally doing customer success and engineering and sales, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And what it does is it basically... It has a skill that's repeated for each firm. So think of these as customers, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他感觉焦虑、不知道业务在发生什么的时候就跑一次 Watchdog，几分钟后拿到一份清晰的状况报告 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:02" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So Watchdog, what it does, and this is multiple iterations, everybody. So what you want is, like, when that moment where you feel like, I need to know what's going in the business right now." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 编码智能体不只是写代码

他认为人们严重低估了编码智能体的能力范围——Devon 不只写代码，还在帮他做报价、运营文档、客户分流 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:10" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And in fact, Devin runs DealDesk for me. So Devin's doing quoting, custom quoting for me, like setting out operations, documentation, customer triage." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他的思路是：如果一个人既懂你的代码库又能写代码解决任何业务问题，你会让他干什么？就这么用你的智能体 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So Devin's doing quoting, custom quoting for me, like setting out operations, documentation, customer triage. Like, I always think, you know, what would you do with somebody who both knew your code base and could write code to solve anything in your business?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本地 vs 云端：各有分工

他也还在用本地智能体 [[Codex|Codex]]，场景是：需要低延迟、需要人在旁边牵着走的大型前后端功能开发，以及用浏览器做前端验证——写用户故事、拉起预览分支、像用户一样走一遍、列出通过和失败的、自动修 Bug <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Yeah, cool. I'll take the subsidy. That, you know, plus goal really, really is good for when you have this kind of, I've got a feature, it's a lot of front-end UI, I just need you to grind for a lot and I need to be here and kind of hold your hand." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:19" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="A lot. So I say, write user stories, use Chrome, pull up the preview branch in Chrome, go through it as a user, list which user stories pass, identify bugs, fix them until you're good." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这类需要频繁干预的活，本地体验更好。

云端智能体则用来跑 Bug 自动修复、一次性小需求、运营类任务 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:33" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Those are kind of what I'm using Codex for. When I'm using background agents, it's a lot of like auto fixes on bugs. Why not like when I'm on calls with customers and they say, oh, do you have this MCP?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 自动审查和合并：Land PR 与 Merge Mommy

他在 Devon 里搭了一个叫 Land PR 的流程：智能体说 PR 做完了，就触发一次 Devon 内置的代码审查，最多跑两轮找 Bug 和未解决的评论，然后在浏览器里录一段带字幕的视频走查，展示测试红绿结果。他看完视频说"通过"，就合并 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:28" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I have something called LAN PR, where basically it's a playbook in Devon, but you can think of it as a skill. And usually it's when the agent comes back and says, okay, the PR is done." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="All right, find all the bugs, check that all the comments are resolved, and then there'll be some more that come up, do it again, does it twice. And then the thing is, is it records a video walkthrough." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

主持人这边则搭了一个叫 Merge Mommy 的智能体，部署在 Vercel 上：PR 打开并通过所有 CI 检查(包括 BugBot 审查)后触发，从爆炸半径、安全性等五个维度打分，低风险的自动批准合并，中高风险的在 Slack 里 ping 人类来审 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:25" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Yeah, so I did an episode on this a week or two ago. It's an EVE agent, so it's deployed on Vercel. And whenever a PR is opened and then passes all its CI checks, including BugBot, Merge Mommy gets triggered." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:38" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And whenever a PR is opened and then passes all its CI checks, including BugBot, Merge Mommy gets triggered. And then what she looks at is she scores the PR on like five aspects of risk." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

两人都反对"让智能体 24 小时跑、自动出产品"的做法。主持人认为现在的前沿模型根本不知道该发布什么，自动改进循环对产品来说是个笑话 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:13" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And two, I don't think it actually adds value. I agree. ## 用视频面试：不看人，看智能体管理能力

他在招第一位工程师时，不聊、不见面，只让候选人录一段全屏桌面视频：在一个已有应用上用智能体构建一个新功能，他要看的不是技术能力，而是"你是一个多好的智能体管理者" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I don't really care where you live. I just want to see how good of an agent manager you are. And the videos I'm getting are fascinating and very revealing about whether I would want to work with this person." aria-label="回原文"></button>。通过后给 Devon 权限做一个真实项目，同样录视频，最后才聊几句就招 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I'm liking it. So I've got, you know, a number of people that are probably going to move to phase two, where then what I'm going to do is say, okay, give them access to Devon and actually build a real thing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **用文件夹按 P0/P1/P2 分桶管理智能体线程**：人能同时跟踪的并发任务有限，必须分优先级，配合一张纸写每周重点保持注意力锚定。
- **搭 Watchdog 类剧本让智能体帮你盯业务**：针对每个客户自动查错误日志、活动记录，过滤排序后只给你看排名前三的问题及修复状态。
- **编码智能体当全能业务助手用**：不只是写代码，报价、运营文档、客户分流都能丢给它。
- **本地和云端按场景分工**：需要低延迟、频繁干预的前后端功能用本地；Bug 修复、一次性需求、运营任务用云端。
- **PR 审查和合并可以自动化**：用智能体做代码审查、浏览器视频走查、风险评估分级，低风险自动合并。
- **代码量不等于产品价值**：前沿模型不知道该发布什么，去找真人客户聊，别坐在椅子上让智能体自动转。
- **面试工程师看智能体管理能力**：让候选人录全屏视频展示他们如何用智能体构建功能，比聊技术栈更能看出实际工作方式。

> 【背景】Devon 是 Cognition 公司开发的云端编码智能体产品，转写稿中多处拼写为 Devon，实际应为 Devin。Claude Code 是 Anthropic 的命令行编码工具。Codex 是 OpenAI 的本地编码智能体。BugBot 是一个 PR 审查工具。Opus 5 指 Claude 的 Opus 5 模型。OpenClaw/OpenClaude 是一个第三方 Claude 包装客户端。Polly 是该客户端中的某个配置或实例名。
