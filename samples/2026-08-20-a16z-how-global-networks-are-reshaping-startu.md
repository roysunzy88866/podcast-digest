# 无国界创始人：为什么AI时代最好的创业筹码是"不在硅谷长大"

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Angela Strange</b> · a16z · 2026-08-20</div>

<div class="pd-play"><button class="pb" type="button" aria-label="播放">▶</button><span class="tt"><span class="t1">听中文精华</span><span class="t2">AI 合成朗读</span></span><span class="bar"><i></i></span><span class="tm">00:00</span><audio preload="metadata" src="/audio/2026-08-20-a16z-how-global-networks-are-reshaping-startu.mp3">你的浏览器不支持音频播放,或音频尚未生成。</audio></div>

风险投资历史上扎在硅谷，但 [[a16z|a16z]] 的投资组合里，40% 的钱投给了国际创始人——一半在美国，一半在别的国家 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:48" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="I think this trend is just going to continue. We actually ran the stats, and out of our apps, 40% of our investments were in international founders. Half of them were based here, half of them were based in other countries." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

所谓"[[无国界创始人|无国界创始人]]"，定义极其包容：任何有全球野心的国际人士都是，不管你先在本国起步还是直接来美国 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:06" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="And so, you know, like Americans technically could be borderless founders, but they tend to be connected here anyways. And so anyone international, whether you're building primarily in your home country to start, but that you have global ambitions, you're a borderless founder." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但这类创始人身上有一种共同的心态——他们往往有一种"要证明给世界看"的驱动力，不管出生在哪，都要成为基准 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="They tend to have a chip on their shoulder. It's the desire that they want to become the benchmark and prove something to the world that regardless of where they were born, they can still achieve greatness." aria-label="回原文"></button>。一位在德国小镇长大的创始人、一位从耶鲁回哥伦比亚的创始人，底层动力都是同一个东西：我不接受被出生地限定天花板。

## 三层别人拿不走的优势

Angela 和 Gabriel 把无国界创始人的优势总结为三样：人才、品牌、客户。这三样不是并列的，它们会互相放大。

**人才：不是"更便宜的人"，是"别人找不到的人"。** 巴西一家 AI 保险公司，最厉害的 AI 人才不在那所最知名的大学，而在一所你没听说过的学校——但那个学校有专门在全国搜罗顶尖学生的奖学金项目和机器人竞赛 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:07" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="So we have an insurance, AI insurance company in Brazil. And so you might think, oh, the best talent is at the top most well-known universities there. Actually, for AI talent, it's at a university that you haven't heard of." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

只有本地人才知道这些渠道。更关键的是飞轮效应：你很早招到 10 个极其聪明的人，当你去美国招第 11 个人——这个人手握 100 个 offer——他见到你的团队，反应是"天哪，他们怎么在那儿找到这么强的人？"<button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:25" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And it's actually these robotics competitions. And so if you are a company that gets 10 incredibly smart people really early, the 11th that you might be trying to recruit from the US that has 100 other offers meets your team." aria-label="回原文"></button> [[人才飞轮|人才飞轮]]就这么转起来了。

**品牌：在本国成为"那个代表"，能倒吸全球人才。** [[Adi|Adi]] 做到哥伦比亚四分之一人口的银行和支付后，成了哥伦比亚最热的科技公司，然后它就能把弗吉尼亚 Capital One 的信贷专家吸引到波哥大来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:47" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="And then, OK, how do you find a credit officer that can do billions of dollars of GMV? The best credit people tend to come from Capital One. How do you attract somebody from Capital One in Virginia to want to work down in Bogota?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

[[Eleven Labs|Eleven Labs]] 对波兰来说也是类似的符号——有创始人把这叫"AI 奥运会"，每个国家都想有自己的代表队 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:12" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And one of the things that has happened is that for Poland, Eleven Labs have become such an important name for them to be representatives in, you know, there's a founder that we know that calls this the AI Olympics." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这种品牌还会得到政府加持：瑞典政府支持本地 AI 公司，周边国家的企业看到政府背书，就直接把这家公司当成"已验证的选择" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:40" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="You know, in Sweden, we have seen a lot of happen where the Swedish government has supported a lot of lovable in Agora. And when this happens, it gives you such a jumpstart in validation with all the large enterprise companies that are part of your country." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**客户：在本国拿到第一个大客户，比在美国容易得多。** 美国没有银行或保险公司愿意当"第一家" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:54" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="And there's lots of opportunities in the U.S. to sell into banks and insurance companies. Nobody wants to be the first bank or the first insurance company. But via Borderless Founder Network, you can get a large bank and a large insurance company in another country much more quickly, and that will very much accelerate your thing in the U.S. And so I think you just have these levers to make things move faster." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但在另一个国家，通过本地网络，你可以更快拿到一家大银行或大保险公司的[[设计合作伙伴|设计合作伙伴]]关系，然后这会成为你回美国销售时的社会证明。反过来也成立：[[Cognition|Cognition]] 这家硅谷公司，早期的 GTM（走向市场）主力其实是巴西 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:18" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And even to Angela's point, this is like when you're trying to get started in that specific country. But we also see in the cases of really hot companies in Silicon Valley, like Cognition, where their early go-to market was actually Brazil." aria-label="回原文"></button>。桥是双向的。

## 侨民网络：比校友网络更强，只是缺组织

Gabriel 打了个比方：美国顶尖学校的校友网络很强大，但特定国家的[[侨民网络|侨民网络]]其实更强——只是历来缺组织 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:35" data-who="Angela Strange" data-en="It's interesting, the very sort of relatable American version of this is if you think of some of the schools, the alumni networks around the top schools are incredibly powerful." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

a16z 的做法是先找"当地杰出人士"（local luminaires）——不是找最成功的公司，而是找在当地生态系统里最受尊敬、最拼命把生态往上推的人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:35" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="What is that process like now? Yeah, so we have this term that we use called local luminaires. So it goes back to what we alluded before, but it's like people that are highly respected in the local ecosystem that have built companies." aria-label="回原文"></button>。瑞典有个叫 Fredrik 的人，做了很多天使投资，几乎投了瑞典所有相关 AI 公司，因为他同时跟年轻创始人和资深创始人都很熟 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:25" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="...in the middle of the AI boom in Sweden, right? But if you look at all the angel investments that Fredrik did, he's pretty much in all the relevant companies that came out of Sweden." aria-label="回原文"></button>。找到这些人，帮他们办活动、帮他们的公司招人，你就自然进入了那个国家最优秀的人才流。

然后在硅谷这边，找同国家的"后起之秀"——比如从瑞典高中辍学、去了 MidJourney 又成为 OpenAI 最年轻研究员之一的 Gabriel Peterson <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:46" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="...of the rising stars or who are these luminaires in Silicon Valley from that country, right? So, you know, one of these rising stars in Sweden is Gabriel Peterson." aria-label="回原文"></button>。每次有瑞典创始人想来硅谷，都想找他。把"国内的灯塔"和"硅谷的后起之秀"连起来，你就嵌进了这个国家创业者的人才流动网络里。

这个模式最早是个 WhatsApp 群：Gabriel 跑去拉美见了 25 个最重要的 CEO，把他们拉进一个群，里面还有 Marc Andreessen 和 Alex Rampell <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:41" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And not long after, I remember I got a WhatsApp from Gabriel, who had joined A6Z, totally on a different team. And he's like, hey, you haven't met me yet, but I just see this huge opportunity in Latin America." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这些独角兽创始人开始在里面互相交换项目，因为他们本身就是多产的天使投资人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:40" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And we put them all together. And one of the interesting things is like they were discussing topics about the region, but they also started exchanging deal flow in the group because they were all prolific angel investors." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 连续创始人：第一波没走到最后的人，第二波更凶

很多本地生态有第一波企业家，做到了 10 亿到 50 亿美金的规模，但因为生态本身太早期、太艰难，没能走到终局 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:45" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="But, you know, we, I think these local ecosystems had a wave, a first wave of entrepreneurs that got to some sort of scale that was meaningful, let's say within the 1 billion to 5 billion range usually." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这些人现在回来了，心态是"50 亿不够，我要用我攒的所有知识和人脉，做更大的事" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:29" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="See and fulfill their vision that gets them going and thinking about, okay, I'm going to do a next business, but this time around I'm going to apply all the knowledge that I have, all the network that I build, and go build something even bigger because I know I can." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

a16z 跟这类人建立关系的方式很具体：帮他们重新激活人脉（特别是想接上斯坦福最聪明的 AI 工程师）、帮他们跟潜在客户做深度对话来验证想法、然后在他们来硅谷的时候，直接帮他们约到 DoorDash、Lyft 等公司的高管 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:33" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="So, like, in the case, I'll give an example. We invested in Frederick Guillaume. He has a new company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Frederick Guillaume 做新公司 pip.com 的时候就是这样，来了一趟硅谷回去后愿景清晰了，最终选了 a16z，因为在"帮你把想法想清楚"这件事上，没人比他们做得更深 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:02" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And then after they came back from that trip, they had a pretty clear vision of what they wanted to do and prosecute. And then they decided to go with us because despite them having pre-existing relationships, because at that point, a lot of, you know, it's very obvious that a lot of investors want to invest in them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 来硅谷，至少待三到六个月

对想搬来硅谷的国际创始人，Gabriel 的建议很明确：来，但别只来几周 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:00" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="What are the kinds of questions that I should be asking myself? I think the answer varies a lot for everyone, but I think one of the things that is very common is definitely come and spend time in Silicon Valley." aria-label="回原文"></button>。几周时间你会经历"社交网络的死亡期"——很难约到人，容易气馁。三到六个月才能建立真正有价值的连接 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:42" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And I think just calibrating on the speed is really, really important. And, you know, one of the things that I personally would recommend is at the very least three to six months so you can kind of emerge." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

即使你最终决定回国，这趟也不白来。每个人回去都带着兴奋，更关键的是校准了对"速度"的认知——硅谷在运作速度上仍然领先所有其他生态系统 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:24" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="I never met somebody that came to Silicon Valley and was like, this was such a waste of time. Everybody goes back excited and it helps them calibrate as well on the level of speed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

实操层面，a16z 还会帮创始人解决签证问题（他们投资了一家叫 Extraordinary 的 AI 签证公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:31" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="And those are things that we help a lot of founders with. We're actually investors in a visa company, an AI native visa company called Extraordinary. And through Extraordinary, we partner a lot on helping these founders come to the U.S. to make sure that they have the right qualifications or help them understand what are the things that they need to come to the U.S. as fast as possible." aria-label="回原文"></button>），也会赞助类似 Palo House 这样的"创始人文理学院"——四个德国学生搞的，四个月里接待了 1200 人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:45" data-who="Gabriel Vasquez" data-en="So we decided to sponsor this house and, you know, over a period of four months. And over four months, they crowded the house too much. They received, I believe, through dinners and everybody that stayed, it was like 1,200 people that ended up staying." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **优势是三层飞轮，不是单点**：人才→品牌→客户，在本国拿到任何一个都会加速另外两个，最终倒吸美国的人才和客户。
- **找人才不要只盯知名大学**：去挖本国的奖学金项目、机器人竞赛、小众院系——这些是本地人才知道的暗池。
- **用本国大客户当跳板**：在美国没人想当"第一家银行"，但在本国通过侨民网络拿下一个大客户，回头就是美国市场的硬通货。
- **侨民网络比校友网络强，但要主动组织**：找到本国的"灯塔人物"和硅谷的"后起之秀"，把两端连起来，你就嵌进了人才流。
- **来硅谷至少三到六个月**：几周只会经历社交挫折；三到六个月才能建立真连接、校准速度认知，即使回国也不亏。
- **连续创始人是隐藏金矿**：第一波做到中等规模没走完的人，带着全部教训和人脉回来，帮他们接上最新的 AI 人才流，他们能跑得比第一次凶得多。
