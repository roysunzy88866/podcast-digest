<div class="pd-tldr"><b>一句话</b>一种看不见的光波长能灭活空气中病毒，南非试验已实现90%结核传播抑制，但目前全球年部署量仅几百台。</div>

南非一项试验里，病房空气中安装了[[222纳米|222纳米]]波长的灯之后，[[结核病|结核病]]的传播被压掉了90%。而结核病对这种光的耐受性，是流感或冠状病毒的10倍左右——换句话说，对常见呼吸道病毒的效果理论上比这还好得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:56" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And also I should point out that... We have some really encouraging results on tuberculosis specifically. There's a trial going on in South Africa that already has preliminary results and they're seeing 90% transmission suppression in these TB wards." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这东西的原理不复杂：222纳米远紫外线被病原体的DNA、RNA和蛋白质强烈吸收，让病原体在空气中失活、无法复制。关键在于，它同时也被人体最外层的死皮细胞（[[角质层|角质层]]，约20微米厚）大量吸收，光根本穿透不到活的皮肤细胞。

这跟254纳米的传统杀菌紫外线完全不同——那种能伤到活细胞，安装必须找专家，搞错了人眼会快速受损 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:28" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You've mentioned that it gets blocked by basically any proteins, so proteins in our skin and epidermis layer presumably, but how is it safe to, for example, look at?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。222纳米的安装门槛低得多，任何电工都能干 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:10" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Whereas you can't really mess up a 2-2-2 installation that badly because it's just innately a lot safer. And so the level of expertise is, like you said, any electrician can do it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

眼睛没有死皮层保护，所以比皮肤更脆弱一些。泪液层只能吸收约15%的[[远UVC|远UVC]]，其余被上皮层吸收。

低剂量可能引起眼部疼痛或不适，但嘉宾认为长期损害风险相当低，目前日本有一年期和三年期的眼部安全研究正在进行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:07" data-who="Unknown" data-en="The rest of the incident dose would be absorbed in the epithelium. And this is a, you know, so a relatively lower dose might produce eye pain or discomfort. The positive story there though, is that this is still, even though these are living cells, they're still full of proteins and the absorption" aria-label="回原文"></button>。需要强调的是，不是坐在房间里突然眼睛就疼了，而是得"爬上去盯着它看几分钟且不关掉"这种事故场景 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:03" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And just to clarify, it's not where you might be kind of just ambiently sitting in the room and suddenly your eye starts hurting. It's more like, did you accidentally climb up and stare right into it for a couple of minutes?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

部署方式现在是在房间角落装一个窄光束灯，指向对角，让光在空间里走最长的路径，从而最大化平均剂量。一个灯覆盖约250平方英尺，标准教室大概需要两到三个 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:00" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So for now... This is because these lamps have a fairly narrow beam angle, and that means that to maximize the average dose in the room, you want to maximize the pass lengths of that beam, which means usually you mount it in a corner and you point it to the opposite corner, and that gets you..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。未来理想形态就是普通天花板灯具，像烟雾报警器一样不起眼。

灯泡寿命方面，制造商保证至少10,000小时保持70%输出，实际数据可能到13到14,000小时。工作日每天开8小时的话，大约能用五到六年 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:29" data-who="说话人1" data-en="What's the average lifetime of these installations? Yeah, we think that the bulbs that we're using right now, the manufacturer will admit it'll last at least 10,000 hours." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

现在一台500美元，安装费大致等于灯本身的钱——找电工的话总共1000美元一个房间，自己动手插插座则安装费近乎为零 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:11" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And then once you're getting bigger, you're looking to spend maybe six figures on purely just on lamps and then installation also. So usually we say installation will cost you probably about what the lamps will cost you if you're hiring professionals to do it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。市场上有卖2000甚至3500美元一台的，嘉宾认为这不是行业固有的成本，而是小公司需要高毛利存活的人为产物 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:12" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And like I said, it's kind of high margin as a business model. ## 优先装哪儿

理论上收益最大的地方是小学和中学——通风差、儿童免疫经验少。但现实是学校会非常保守，早期更可能出现在私立学校，甚至单个家长就能出资装 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:12" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Can you talk about what the research suggests with respect to, as we're prioritizing the rollout, where should we prioritize? Right. So this is a tough one." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更现实的突破口是[[长期护理中心|长期护理中心]]和医院候诊室：那里社交混合少、人群停留时间短（不太可能累积高剂量），但总人流量大，而且机构有强烈的商业动机减少感染——护理机构的收入跟存活住户数挂钩 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:38" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So I think ultimately, you know, occupational settings of some sort are going to see it earlier, even though I don't necessarily think that's where the very largest benefit is." aria-label="回原文"></button>。大学也是先锋，因为不是儿童、决策链条短 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:36" data-who="Unknown" data-en="For really big institutions, if you're talking a university, you're probably looking at hundreds of lamps. But if you're talking for middle, I also think, so universities are kind of a front runner because again, because it's not children, it's like older students, it's a little easier to get installs set up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 真正的大招：掐灭下一场大流行

日常防感冒的效果其实不确定——很多感冒是近距离长时间接触传染的，环境干预空间有限 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So, you know, we're talking about like, oh, we're preventing colds and flus. My honest assessment is that I'm much more uncertain about our ability to prevent the average cold, which is just not very contagious." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但反直觉的是，越具传染性的病原体，远UVC的"拦截面积"越大。麻疹的[[基本传染数|基本传染数]]约20，COVID最严重时大概一点几，传统UVC当年成功控制过麻疹爆发 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:12" data-who="Unknown" data-en="More dangerous wavelengths as the 254 nanometer wavelength. That was used to control measles. And measles is just absurdly contagious." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

流行病学里，基本传染数R0从1.08降到0.99，就是"大流行"和"没有大流行"的区别 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:33" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, the math that is common to epidemiologists and people who market video games for a living is that the difference between the reproduction number, it's called R0 in epidemiology and the K factor in marketing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。在交通枢纽、集会场所做到中等覆盖，就可能把R0压到1以下。而且这不像疫苗或口罩需要每个人主动配合——建筑所有者单方面决定装上就行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:16" data-who="Unknown" data-en="No, I definitely think that's a huge benefit. I also think because it's part of the built environment, you need a lot fewer people to be involved in the prevention process, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

跟升级HVAC系统比，远UVC便宜得多，尤其对已有建筑来说，改通风管道可能根本不现实 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:07" data-who="Unknown" data-en="The sort of competing technologies, they're not quite competing. ## 最大风险：不是安全，是传播动力学

如果这东西最终没达到预期，最可能的原因不是安全问题，而是我们发现大多数常见呼吸道疾病的传播其实是极短距离的（两英尺内面对面说话直接大量暴露），环境干预插不上手 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:31" data-who="Unknown" data-en="If it fails to work, if this doesn't achieve the societal level benefits that we expect it to, why? What is the thing that we don't have confidence on that we could potentially learn more in the next couple of years in a way that would disappoint us negatively?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。那样的话它可能变成"只有大流行时才有用"的技术，而人们又讨厌为预防付费 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:22" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So I think that it is imaginable to me that we end up in this uncomfortable world where we can't do very much for, you know, quote, normal disease transmission, but it still is like totally crucial anti-pandemic technology." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

不过即便短距离暴露为主，降低空气中的病毒浓度仍可能降低感染严重程度——COVID已经证明了"初始病毒量越低，症状越轻" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:42" data-who="Unknown" data-en="But it's definitely going to be more challenging. You might still maybe reduce the severity of an infection just by reducing the viral dose. We saw this with COVID is that even if you don't fully prevent an infection, if you get less viruses, you're going to have a less severe infection." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

关于"减少接触病原体是否削弱免疫系统"的担忧：卫生假说的现代表述认为，免疫系统训练主要靠环境微生物和共生菌，不是靠得病。感染麻疹会摧毁免疫记忆，纯粹是坏事。

如果真需要"故意接触"，我们有疫苗这项技术 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="61:23" data-who="Unknown" data-en="You know, decreasing people's contact with pathogens is a net a bad thing. I will say, relative to other sort of infection prevention measures, this one is relatively easy to desist if we decide that the math doesn't work out in favor of it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 为什么还没铺开

全球目前年部署量可能低至几百台 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:17" data-who="Unknown" data-en="I don't think it's even that high. It might be as low as just a couple of hundred a year worldwide. When do we get to that tipping point? 商业路径上，比起说服公众，更可行的是找大雇主算经济账：员工生病或孩子生病导致照护缺勤的成本很高，Google这种公司有专人负责员工健康，不需要"说服大众"，只需要算数字 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="75:46" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Well, so I think the political angle here is, it is tough, but I think there's a completely non-political capitalist angle that's like pretty feasible. ## 本集带走
- **222纳米远UVC安全性的核心**：被20微米厚的死皮层（角质层）大量吸收，穿透不到活细胞；眼睛更脆弱但需要事故级暴露才会有不适，日常坐在房间里不会受伤
- **部署算账**：约250平方英尺一台灯，灯500美元+安装500美元（自己装则近乎零）；灯泡寿命约5-6年（每天8小时）；未来无技术突破也可能降到100美元/台
- **优先场景排序**：长期护理中心/医院候诊室 > 私立学校/大学 > 普通公立学校 > 普通家庭（家庭场景性价比最低，除非有婴儿或免疫功能低下者）
- **防日常感冒效果不确定，防大流行潜力巨大**：短距离直接暴露的环境干预空间有限；但R0从1.08压到0.99就是大流行与没有大流行的区别，交通枢纽中等覆盖可能就够了
- **没铺开的原因不是技术或监管**：全球年部署量可能仅几百台，核心瓶颈是"没人知道这东西存在"，信息传播是第一优先级
