[[Benedict Evans|Benedict Evans]] 曾在 A16Z 做了多年的常驻分析师。在过去六年里,他作为独立分析师追踪最重要的科技趋势,现在把所有精力都花在研究 AI 上。本期对话的核心是:作为和互联网、智能手机同等级别的平台级变革,AI 现在到底进行到哪一步了?Benedict 给出了一张极具启发性的全景图——前半部分聊「周期定位」与「自动化的本质」,中间聊「价值会在产业链的哪一段沉淀」,最后聊我们该如何面对反 AI 情绪与职业焦虑。没听过这集,也能顺着这条脉络读懂他的核心判断。

## 我们站在 1997 年:变化虽大,但别太早下定论
主持人开场提问:大家都知道 AI 是件大事,那么在即将到来的巨变里,我们目前最没有充分计价(pricing in)的是什么?
Benedict 给出了他口中「最具争议的观点」:AI 和互联网或移动技术一样大,**但也只和它们一样大** <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:46" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="Knowing that, what do you think people are still not fully pricing in when they think about the change that they're going to experience to their lives and their work?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他强调,智能手机和互联网本身就是天翻地覆的事,所以「和它们一样大」已经是极高的评价。但科技界总有人觉得这更像是工业革命,而在这群人之下还有另一群人觉得他完全低估了 AI。

如果把 AI 类比成互联网,Benedict 认为我们现在处于 1997 年:大家都很兴奋,但大部分东西还不工作,未来会怎样运行谁也说不清 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:19" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="We wouldn't be doing this if it wasn't for the internet. But then if you dig into that, if you're going to make the internet comparison, it's like we're a 1997. It's very exciting." aria-label="回原文"></button>。更麻烦的是,采用程度呈极端的两极分化——科技圈里的人可能早就买了 Mac mini 集群不再用 [[Google|Google]],但普通大众依然只是偶尔用用。即便是 13 到 18 岁的青少年群体中,也只有大约 15% 到 20% 是日活用户 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:50" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="A lot of other people are picking it up using it to varying degrees, but slightly puzzled. So, there's a bunch of survey data that I put in the presentation that, even if you look at like 13 to 18 year olds or something, it's still like 15, 20% of people are daily active users, and another 20% are weekly active users." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

> 【背景】1997 年正值互联网商业化的黎明期,网景等浏览器大战刚刚打响。Benedict 借此类比强调:站在变革开端时,你无法预测最终的赢家和产品形态(就像 1997 年没人能预测 Google 或 Facebook 的成功)。

既然现在像 1997 年,那我们离「一切彻底变样」还有多久?说完了宏观的历史定位,接下来是他怎么拆解具体的工作自动化。

## 软件开发者就像看见 VisiCalc 的会计师
Benedict 借用了一个绝佳的类比:想象你是 1970 年代末的会计师,第一次看到 VisiCalc(第一款电子表格软件) <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:40" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="What does this mean for us? One of the analogies I used in the presentation is, imagine you're an accountant seeing the first software spreadsheets in the late '70s, and this is mind-blowing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。你改一个利率,所有数字在 30 秒内全部更新,过去一周的活儿瞬间干完了。这对会计师来说如同魔法。但如果你是个律师或记者,你看到它的反应只会是:「这玩意很聪明,我的会计师该买一个,但这跟我的工作没关系。」这就是当前大众面对 AI 的真实处境。

软件开发者是今天看到 VisiCalc 的会计师。很多人经历了「[[Claude Code|Claude Code]] 之前」和「Claude Code 之后」的颠覆感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:38" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="&quot;Oh, my God, this changes everything.&quot; Before VisiCalc and after VisiCalc, before Claude Code and after Claude Code. A lot of other people are picking it up using it to varying degrees, but slightly puzzled." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但其他人只是在不同程度地使用它,并感到困惑。这引出了 Benedict 的核心论点:**在工作中,真正难的那部分到底是什么?**

> 【背景】VisiCalc 是 1979 年发布的世界上第一款电子表格软件,常被视为推动个人电脑走向实用的杀手级应用。

## 任务被自动化了,但工作还在
顺着「什么是工作中真正难的部分」,Benedict 深入探讨了自动化的本质。

Benedict 指出,我们常把「[[任务|任务]](task)」和「工作(job)」混为一谈。有些工作本身就是一个单一任务(比如早期的电梯操作员),这种很容易被彻底自动化 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:14" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And pulling that apart, sometimes the task is the job. The classic example is like an elevator attendant. If I live in a building that has an attended elevator, we have a manual elevator, so there's no button." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但更多时候,自动化的只是某个任务,这会引出经济学上的「[[杰文斯悖论|杰文斯悖论]](Jevons paradox,指资源利用效率提高后,总体消耗量反而增加)」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:42" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So there were some things where the button, the job was a task and the task got automated. What happens much more, and this is why people talked about the Jevons paradox is this pricey elasticity, because the Jevons paradox is just price elasticity, applied price elasticity." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他举了一个关于投行的玩笑:年轻人可能不信,但在 Excel 出现之前,初级投行分析师的工作时间非常长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:00" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="Or do you do more for more money because you've got new ROI? And if you look at something like the history of accounting, or indeed professional services, this is a joke I made on Twitter back when it was Twitter, was like, young people won't believe this, but before Excel, junior investment bankers worked really long hours." aria-label="回原文"></button>。现在有了 Excel,按理说应该提前下班了,但事实并非如此。软件开发也一样:以前开发者要自己写各种底层代码,现在写 iPhone 应用,90% 的代码 Apple 都替你写好了(比如驱动、文件系统),但这并没有减少对工程师的需求。

再看咨询业。你雇麦肯锡,是为了拿那个 75 页的演示文稿吗?AI 确实能给你生成一个极烂的演示文稿,但那不是你付钱买的。你真正买的是他们走遍全公司摸清内部政治、去和真实客户交谈以诊断问题的能力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:35" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And long way of answering a question, why do you hire McKinsey? Are you hiring them to get a 75-slide deck? Well, narrowly, Claude cowork will make a really, really crappy version of that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。PowerPoint 只是个任务,诊断才是工作。所以,即使是最前沿的 AI 公司,[[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 和 [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 也在疯狂招人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:45" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="Well, it's more complicated than automation. Even just looking at the most advanced AI companies, Anthropic, OpenAI, I just had Dan Shipper from Every on the podcast, everyone's just increasing headcount." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

既然自动化的只是任务,那长期来看经济规律会怎样起作用?这就引出了他对就业焦虑和底层逻辑的判断。

## 别信末日论者:历史、时间周期与劳动总量谬误
既然自动化的逻辑很复杂,那为什么还有那么多职场末日论?Benedict 给出了极其犀利且令人安心的反驳。

关于 AI 淘汰工作的速度,Benedict 认为很多人(他称之为 Twitter 上的「末日论者」)完全不懂世界是怎么运转的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:11" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So yeah, this is faster, but the internet was faster too. I think the other answer to this, and this kind of comes back to the professional services point is, you talk to these doomers on Twitter, and they would act like every big company is going to buy ChatGPT tomorrow, and then in two weeks time, they'll fire all their staff, and these people are mourners." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们以为大公司明天买了 ChatGPT,两周后就会开除所有员工。但现实是,企业软件的销售周期长达 18 个月,甚至比创业公司的两轮融资周期还长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:34" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And that was like the starting point why they then didn't understand anything else. Turn to a big company, enterprise software sales cycle, you'll know this better than me, enterprise software sales cycle is like 18 months, if you're lucky." aria-label="回原文"></button>。你不可能指望大公司明天就把 SAP 系统拔了换成 AI,这需要三、五甚至十年的时间逐个行业渗透 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:02" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So, I know people aren't just going to tear out SAP and replace it with X, Y, Z. Maybe in like three, five, 10 years, yes, that whole estate will look radically different, and all those jobs will have changed, but it will take two, three, four, five, 10 years, and it will take time sector by sector, and it will take time for people to work out, &quot;You could do that thing with this.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

在底层逻辑上,他相信「[[劳动总量谬误|劳动总量谬误]](lump of labor fallacy,即认为社会上总共只有固定数量的工作可做)」依然适用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:47" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And Mark can buy himself another Gulfstream, add to the fleet. I think the other answer, it's back to the lump of labor fallacy, and the last 200 years, that each of these technologies removes a bunch of jobs, creates a bunch of new jobs, creates a bunch of new value, unlocks prosperity for all of us, and that's painful as you go through it, but it always creates more value." aria-label="回原文"></button>。回到 1800 年,90% 的人是农民,天天担心庄稼歉收。之后两百年我们不断自动化掉旧工作,但 GDP 仍在增长,我们解锁了大量今天看来理所应当的新工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:04" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And then that automation, whether it's price elasticity, and the enablement of the fact that they became automated, unlock so much of new jobs. And so you go back to 1800, 90% of us were peasants, and our major concern was like, are the crops going to fail?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。每次阵痛期确实会摧毁一些城镇、让一部分人失业,但从宏观尺度看,走出来之后大家都变得更富裕了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:41" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="We've had this process over and over again since 1800. And each time you go through it, you get a bunch of frictional pain and dislocation, and a bunch of people do their jobs, and a bunch of towns get hollowed out, and it all sucks." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 模型公司没有定价权,价值将回归应用层
聊完了技术普及周期与宏观经济,Benedict 把视线拉回到了当下的科技产业:在这波变革里,钱到底会被谁赚走?

Benedict 举了电力和移动通信的例子。Sam Altman 曾说「我们将像卖水或电一样按表出售 AI 智能」,Benedict 对此毫不留情:你需要我给你科普一下公用事业的利润率结构吗?电视机公司不会给电力公司分成,博世也不会把洗衣机的收入分给发电厂 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:47" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And the other side, the point of my capital section in the presentation is there's this quote from Sam Altman where he said, &quot;We're going to be selling AI intelligence on a meter like water or electricity.&quot;" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

作为资深电信分析师,他给出了残酷的数据:全球移动行业年收入约一万亿美元,每年资本支出占收入的大约 15% 到 20% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:58" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="It's about 15, 20% of revenue every year. And if you look at a chart of mobile data consumption, it's an exponential curve, like perfect curve going spread up. And the number now, I think it's about 1,500 to 2,000 times what it was in 2010 globally." aria-label="回原文"></button>。移动数据消费量呈完美的指数级增长(现在是 2010 年的 1500 到 2000 倍),但这 25 年间,电信公司的股票根本没涨 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:11" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And the number now, I think it's about 1,500 to 2,000 times what it was in 2010 globally. And the stocks have gone nowhere in 25 years, because it's an X gross low margin commodity utility, where they're selling this objectively amazing piece of global technology infrastructure that has enormous complexity and enormous sophistication, but all the cool stuff is made by you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。因为它们最终沦为了卖低毛利大宗商品的管道,所有酷东西的价值都被在座的应用开发者赚走了。

这是目前围绕[[基础模型|基础模型]]最根本的问题:模型会赢者通吃吗?Benedict 认为不会,因为没有[[赢家通吃|赢家通吃]]效应,所以会无限期竞争 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:42" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So there doesn't seem to be a winner takes all effect where one of these will run away ahead of the other. So you should have competition indefinitely. If you have competition indefinitely, you don't have primary really radical differentiation of what the product is." aria-label="回原文"></button>。既然产品没有根本性差异化,模型公司就不会有[[定价权|定价权]]。所以,未来的生态应该更像云([[AWS|AWS]],用户不在乎底层跑在什么上面)而不是像 Windows(微软靠系统垄断通吃)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:10" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="Now that may be completely wrong, and one of the points I make in the presentation is like, imagine having this conversation about the internet in 1997, what would you have got right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。模型本身会沦为无差异的基础设施,真正的价值会在更上层的应用里爆发。

> 【背景】这里的 AWS 是亚马逊的云服务,Windows 是微软的操作系统。Benedict 用这个对比说明:未来 AI 模型可能只像云服务器一样在后台默默提供算力,而掌控用户、赚取高利润的将是那些做上层应用的公司。

既然价值在应用层,那竞争的焦点会变成什么?顺着产业链往上走,答案呼之欲出:[[分发|分发]]。

## 当产品变成大宗商品,分发才是护城河
明确了模型只是底层管道,接下来的问题就是怎么在应用层赢。

如果产品(模型)基本上是大宗商品,那么分发渠道就变得至关重要 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:55" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So yeah, I did spend some time talking about this in the presentation I did at the end of last year, that if the product is a commodity, then the distribution is what matters." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Benedict 拿浏览器打比方:浏览器本质上只是一个带有输入框和输出框的极薄外壳。浏览器设计上最后一次真正的创新是 20 到 25 年前的标签页浏览 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:26" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And which is like, what's the last innovation in browser design? Tab browsing, which was 20 years ago, 25 years ago. And every now and then somebody tries to innovate in browser design and it never works because you found the Platonic ideal." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当年微软靠 Windows 的分发优势打败了网景,但这最终毫无意义,因为浏览器的价值又往上层转移到了 Google 手里。今天同样的戏码在 AI 界重演:Google 正利用分发优势强推 [[Gemini|Gemini]],普通人根本分不清 Gemini 和 Claude 的区别 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:01" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="So clearly what's happening now is that Google is using distribution to drive Gemini. And what's the difference between Gemini and Claude? And if you're using this stuff all day, then you know, but like normal person, there's no difference." aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[Meta|Meta]] 的模型在业内被看扁,但靠着在所有产品上铺满入口,使用率竟然能紧跟 ChatGPT 和 Gemini <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:09" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="And if you're using this stuff all day, then you know, but like normal person, there's no difference. And the same thing with Meta, if you look at survey data on which our M people use, even before the news thing, like the Llama thing, Meta was behind, it was up there between ChatGPT and Gemini, which if you're in tech, people have completely written it off, but it was like they sprayed it on every service surface." aria-label="回原文"></button>。所以,OpenAI 去年的战略是「无处不在」,因为他们极度害怕被巨头用默认选项锁死 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:31" data-who="Benedict Evans" data-en="It was fine. So distribution of an adequate product when the field is basically commodity distribution on brand become a big deal, you could see that in the strategy, OpenAI strategy late last year was, people called it Everything, Everywhere, Yesterday." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

聊完了产业格局,视线必须转向社会层面。技术在狂飙,大众的反弹也在酝酿。

## 反 AI 情绪与不可避免的阵痛
技术方兴未艾,但社会的反噬也来了。作为分析师,他怎么看这些情绪?

## 本集带走
1. **AI 的影响力堪比互联网,但我们正处在 AI 的 1997 年**:大部分应用还没跑通,大众采用率很低。不要因为短期的狂热或泡沫就对终局下定论。

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
