产品愉悦感（[[delight|delight]]）不是在功能上撒一层快乐，不是摇一摇手机飘雪花那种「[[彩纸效应|彩纸效应]]」。它指的是同时满足用户的**功能需求**和**情感需求**——用 Nesrine 的话说，叫创造「[[情感连接|情感连接]]」。

理论层面，愉悦是「快乐」加「惊讶」两种情绪同时出现。落到实践上，它通过三个支柱实现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:13" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="How can we build products where we can achieve this delight goal? And in order to do that, we need to satisfy three main pillars. The very first one is..." aria-label="回原文"></button>：

**[[消除摩擦|消除摩擦]]**：找到用户情绪处于谷底的「低谷时刻」——焦虑、压力——用体验设计把它抹平。她举了自己的亲身例子：叫的 [[Uber|Uber]] 被司机无故取消，正要赶火车，心理上已经做好写长文申诉的准备，结果打开 App 只点两下就退了款。情绪从低谷瞬间翻转，这就是消除摩擦带来的愉悦 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:54" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="and how can we leverage the experience so we can reduce these valley moments? The best example I could share with you is actually something that did happen to me recently." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**[[预判需求|预判需求]]**：不等用户开口，在他们意识到需要之前就把东西递过去。她的例子是 [[Revolut|Revolut]]：一家人到新加坡发现手机卡没有国际漫游，丈夫打开 Revolut，一个标签页买了 eSIM，七欧元搞定。

一个银行 App 为什么要放 eSIM？因为 Revolut 的大批用户是国际人士，经常旅行——需求被提前预判了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:55" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="which are very high. And that was the moment when I looked at my husband, he opened his Revolut app. He went to a tab called eSIM, buy eSIM, seven euro, it's done." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**[[超出预期|超出预期]]**：在用户没有要求的情况下，给到比他想要的更多的东西。她老公用 Edge 浏览器买咖啡机，准备付 120 欧元时，浏览器自动弹出一张 15% 的优惠券并自动填充——他根本没在找优惠券 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:35" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="He put the coffee machine on like a for payment he was about to pay. And suddenly, actually, Edge suggested him for a complete what to feel feature, a coupon. Like he was about to pay something like 120 euro and suddenly you get 15% discount" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## B2B 也需要愉悦：别做功能，做「面向人」的产品

B2B 和 B2C 的分法过时了，Nesrine 提出一个概念叫 [[B2H|B2H]]——Business to Human（面向人类）。只要产品的终端使用者是人，情感需求就不能被忽略 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:32" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="I mean, it's all about bringing joy to the customers. So I came up with this concept of B2H, which is business to human. For me," aria-label="回原文"></button>。她在写书时专门采访了 GitHub、Atlassian、Snowflake 等公司的人，发现他们都有类似的产品原则——Dropbox 叫 Cupcake，Snowflake 叫 Superhero——名字不同，本质一样：给用户带来快乐 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:13" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="is that they do value emotional connection in a way or another. I mean, it's not something new. They just do it in a different way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她特别推崇一个思考方式叫**[[人性化|人性化]]**：问自己「如果我的产品是一个人，体验会怎么变得更好？」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:11" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="And I even said it's my favorite. It's about asking the following question. If my product was a human, how would the experience would be better?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

她在 [[Google Meet|Google Meet]] 时不跟 Zoom、Teams 比，而是跟「如果大家面对面开会，体验会怎么更好」比——标准直接拉到了真人交互的层面 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:43" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="conferencing tool, do you know what did we compare Google Meet to? That's the best, do you think?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Dyson 的产品负责人告诉她类似的话：不拿自家吸尘器跟别的吸尘器比，而是跟「如果我雇一个真人来打扫，真人能做到什么」比 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:18" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="I spoke with a head of product from Dyson and he said a very similar thing. Actually, when I spoke with Andy, He said, um, we don't compare our diesel vacuum cleaner to other vacuum cleaner." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

对「头发着火」的刚需市场，她承认产品「能用就行」，但马上补了一句：如果你不把情感维度做进去，一旦有竞争对手做了，对方会赢 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:54" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="that's probably gonna be okay. Honestly, maybe the product will work. And of course it has to work." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Buffer 的例子很说明问题：发现约 2% 的用户几个月没活跃，主动发邮件说「我们要给你退款」——亏钱，但建立信任和情感连接，结果很多人反而留下来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:41" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="And actually, he said something really interesting. He actually said that we realized that there are about 2% of our users who are not using Buffer. They are clearly inactive." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 四步法：怎么找到值得投入的愉悦机会

Nesrine 在 Google 做 [[Chrome|Chrome]] 和 Google Meet 时，本身就是「愉悦 PM」，但她知道不是每家公司都有这个奢侈，所以提炼了一个可嵌入现有流程的四步模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:38" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="Talk about that. Yes, so the thing that we did not really spoke about is the fact that over my product" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第一步：识别用户的动机（motivators）**。不光是功能动机（我要搜一首歌），更重要的是**[[情感动机|情感动机]]**（我想感到不那么孤独、我想改变心情）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:36" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="that we have a list of what we call like functional motivators and the emotional motivators. I'm pretty sure like most of the listeners are familiar with the functional" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

情感动机还分两层：个人情感动机——用户自己想有什么感觉；社会情感动机——用户希望别人怎么看待自己 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:43" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="Okay, great. And even when we talk about emotional motivators, we have the personal emotional motivators and the social emotional motivators." aria-label="回原文"></button>。[[Spotify|Spotify]] 的 Wrapped 就是一个典型：功能上没什么用，但满足了「我想让别人觉得我很酷」的社会情感动机 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:05" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="while using the product. I mean, think about wrapped, for example. A lot of people share their wrapped because they want to share how cool they are" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第二步：把动机转化为产品机会**。拿到功能动机和情感动机的列表后，用「我们如何能…

…」这类方法定义机会空间，放进产品战略里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:27" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="identifying emotional motivators also as well as functional motivators. Now, the second step is to convert those motivators. I mean, as soon as you have this list, how can I convert this into an" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第三步：用「[[愉悦网格|愉悦网格]]」分类方案**。横轴是功能动机，纵轴是情感动机，把所有方案放进去，会自然分成三类 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:32" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="we actually identified action motivators and emotional motivators, and I defined what I call the delight grid. So the delight grid is a matrix where we place" aria-label="回原文"></button>：
- **浅层愉悦**：只满足情感动机，没有功能性。

Wrapped、Apple Watch 生日气球属于这类 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:01" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="Because we are categorizing features into three types. There's the surface delight when a feature is only solving for an emotional, I mean, think about wrapped." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **[[低愉悦|低愉悦]]**：只满足功能动机，纯功能改进 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:40" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="The second type of delight is what I call low delight. It's when the feature is only solving for one of those functional motivators. And of course, the third type, which is the most powerful for me is the deep delight." aria-label="回原文"></button>。


- **深层愉悦**：同时满足功能动机和情感动机——这是最有力的类型。Discover Weekly 就是：功能上帮你发现新音乐，情感上让你感觉被理解、被看见 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:46" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="It's when the feature is only solving for one of those functional motivators. And of course, the third type, which is the most powerful for me is the deep delight." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第四步：用「愉悦清单」验证**。清单里有几项关键检查 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:38" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="we are not just creating only surface delight or we are just doing it in the wrong way? So I created something that I call the delight checklist." aria-label="回原文"></button>：
- **用户影响和商业影响**：愉悦不是借口，必须跟业务对齐。


- **可行性**：能不能做出来。
- **[[熟悉度|熟悉度]]**：不能让用户太震惊——这一点她用 Discover Weekly 的真实故事说明。

最初设计是完全推荐新歌，上线后指标很好，两周后发现有个 bug 会偶尔混入用户已经听过的歌。工程师修了 bug，结果指标全掉了。

用户喜欢的恰恰是那种「偶尔听到一首我认识的歌」的熟悉感。现在大家用的 Discover Weekly，本质上是一个「有 bug 的版本」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:44" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="which is something that actually created the success of Discover Weekly. So I'm not sure if you know about that, but I'm going to go ahead and share it. Yeah, let's do it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。


- **[[包容性|包容性]]**：让你开心的事不一定让所有人开心。她举了 Deliveroo 在法国母亲节的翻车案例：推送通知做得像妈妈打来的未接来电，本意是温馨，但对失去母亲的人来说是伤害，引发大量负面报道 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:36" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="And I can share some examples here as well. So the example that comes to my mind is actually something that happened last year. here in Paris or in France in general," aria-label="回原文"></button>。Apple 的手势烟花反应也有类似问题——有人跟治疗师视频展示受伤的手指，结果触发了满屏烟花 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="68:13" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="Can you share that? It's actually a feature that's very much known nowadays, but maybe people don't know exactly what's wrong or what's good or what's bad about" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 两个实战案例：Chrome 标签页和 Google Meet

Chrome iOS 端的标签页管理问题，看似是纯功能问题（内存、性能），但她们做了大量用户访谈，发现人和标签页之间有强烈的**情感关系**——有人让研究者看标签页时会道歉，说「我通常不会开这么多」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:02" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="And how did we do that? The way we did that is actually we interviewed a lot of users, trying to ask them," aria-label="回原文"></button>。基于这些发现，她们做了「不活跃标签页」功能：超过 21 天没碰的标签自动移入单独文件夹，界面变干净了，但标签没被关闭——用户对 Chrome 的信任没有被破坏。这既是功能优化，也解决了羞耻感和压力感，是典型的深层愉悦 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:28" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="these motivators or these emotional parts. So what we built actually was a feature that exists today on iOS, which is called inactive tabs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Google Meet 方面，疫情初期她们花三到四个月研究远程办公的情感影响，发现三个模式：无聊、低互动、[[Zoom 疲劳|Zoom 疲劳]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="51:31" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="and it was about three to four months, I had to understand the impact or the emotional impact of this new behavior. The fact that people are staying home and having these back to back." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她们没有找「动机」，而是找**负动机**——什么让人沮丧。

斯坦福的研究指出 Zoom 疲劳的一个主要原因是看到自己的画面（相当于有人在你旁边举一面镜子），于是做了「最小化自我视图」功能 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:57" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="So when we worked on Zoom fatigue, we actually realized and we get help from a study that was published from Stanford, by the way," aria-label="回原文"></button>。针对低互动和无聊，引入了表情反应（竖大拇指、挥手）——有时取消静音说「我同意」太有侵入性，表情则让人保持在场感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="53:49" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="which was related to low interaction and boredom, we tried to understand how can we bring in a little bit more of joy, but at the same time," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 路线图怎么排优先级：50-40-10

不要把愉悦和功能对立起来。Nesrine 提出一个 50-40-10 的配比建议 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="60:13" data-who="Nesrine Changuel" data-en="problems and create solutions in a way that emotion is taken into consideration into that. And by the way, I created this model called 504010. And 504010 is actually a guidance or a recommendation so that if you have done your" aria-label="回原文"></button>：
- **50%** 的功能放给低愉悦（纯功能），产品必须能用。


- **40%** 放给深层愉悦（功能加情感同时满足）。

## 本集带走
- **愉悦 = 功能需求 + 情感需求同时满足**，不是在功能上撒彩纸。摇手机飘雪花如果没有价值，就别做。
- **三个实操支柱**：消除摩擦（把预期很难的事变简单，如 Uber 两键退款）、预判需求（用户没开口就给，如 Revolut 内置 eSIM）、超出预期（用户没要求就多给，如 Edge 自动填优惠券）。
- **用「愉悦网格」分类你的方案**：浅层愉悦（纯情感）、低愉悦（纯功能）、深层愉悦（两者兼有）——资源重点投在深层愉悦上。
- **验证时必须检查「包容性」**：让你开心的事可能伤害别人（Deliveroo 母亲节通知、Apple 手势烟花的教训）。
- **路线图配比参考 50-40-10**：50% 纯功能、40% 深层愉悦、10% 浅层愉悦。
- **说服老板的技巧**：别「推销愉悦」这个词，搞清老板最看重什么，展示消除摩擦和预判需求怎么帮他达成那个目标。
- **警惕习惯化效应**：惊喜会消退，必须有持续升级计划（如 Google Meet 背景从模糊一路进化到 AI 生成）。

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
