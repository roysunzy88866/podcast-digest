Daniel Bloom 是一家叫 Melio 的公司的产品经理，他在 [[Claude|Claude]] Co-work（一种可以连接你各种工具、自动执行任务的 AI 助手）上搭了一套系统，现在他电脑前 70% 到 80% 的时间都通过这套系统来管理 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Thank you for watching! Today, I say I manage about 70% to 80% of my time in front of the computer with co-work alone. Almost everything I do here is through co-work, and I try to do more and more of it because anything that I don't do in co-work, then the context is not as well caught as if I do it through co-work, whether it's other applications, whether it's Jira, Slack, queries, anything generally." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他说自己现在一天能干完以前一周的活 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:57" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I'll say personally, I think. First of all, I'm really able to do in a day now what used to take me a week and I stand behind that. It's, and you know, it's something that sounds like a, like a hypey LinkedIn post headliner, but it's, it's, it's true." aria-label="回原文"></button>——不是靠加速，而是靠 AI 帮他把所有协调、追踪、整理的杂活全接了，让他能做更深层的工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:23" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I look at, um, I look at my, the way I work through these, like this week and oftentimes I can get through this week in a good day, uh, which is just mind blowing." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 系统的两个核心能力

Daniel 认为让这套系统真正管用的不是 Co-work 本身，而是两个条件：第一，系统能重写自己的核心文件，意味着它可以不断自我改进；第二，它跟你生态里尽可能多的工具有连接和集成 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:59" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I think Codex could be great for this as well, or ChatGPT work. I think the real importance is for a system to A, be able to rewrite its own core files, and that means that it keeps on improving, and B, just have connections and integrations to as much of your ecosystem." aria-label="回原文"></button>。满足这两条，才有真正的质变。

## 周准备：从混乱到清晰的周计划

整个系统从一个叫"每[[周准备|周准备]]"的周期性任务开始。周日早上自动运行，做三件事：梳理他的"首要关注"（脑子里一直想着的大项目）、排出"本周优先级"、清空"收件箱"——来自 Slack、邮件、日历里冒出来的杂事，全部由 Co-work 自动抓取并填进 [[Notion|Notion]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Perfect. So it all starts with one recurring task, which is a composition of several skills called the weekly prep. So the weekly prep is the very basic task that helps me start my week." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最后它还会过一遍这周的会议，逐个问他：这个需要认真准备吗（如果需要，就自动变成本周的一个优先任务）、还是快速提醒就行、或者根本不用准备 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And then finally, it helps me prep for my meetings. So it runs me through the meetings that I'm going to have and asks me how I want to prep for them, whether it's like a serious preparation that requires its own task in my weekly priority, whether it's a quick reminder or just meetings that don't really need any prep." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他确认完，一周的框架就立住了，全部自动推送到 Notion。

Notion 对他来说几乎是只读的——他只看它来获取焦点和优先级，偶尔手动勾掉已完成的事项，其他全交给 Co-work 操作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Do you actually spend time in the Notion or are you still navigating all this with Claude? So the main idea is that Notion is almost a read-only for me. I look at the Notion to get my focus, to remind me of my top of mind, to remind me of my priorities and focus this week." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 早间简报：最亮的一招——主动问你"这是什么"

每天早上跑的"[[早间简报|早间简报]]"里，Daniel 认为最厉害的不是过会议和行动项，而是一个[[上下文|上下文]]自学习机制 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And this is called the morning brief. And this is, I think, where the real magic happens. So actually, we're going to run this live right now." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

具体做法：Co-work 会扫他最近的 Slack 消息、邮件、会议笔记，然后去找它"看不懂的东西"——比如出现了一个它上下文里没有的术语、文件名、里程碑。然后它会主动问：这是什么？

重要吗？我要不要读一下？他确认后，Co-work 就把这个知识存进上下文文件 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:08" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So this is one of the ways I've thought of. So basically what it does is imagine in this daily brief, my coworker runs through my recent slacks, my emails, my granola notes." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

现场演示时它就拦住了一个叫"结算上限"的支付术语，问 Daniel 要不要存下来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So here it flagged something. Let's see what it is. A term I didn't know." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这个设计的价值在于：公司内部的大量定义、术语、目标根本不在模型的训练数据里，如果不主动让模型去发现并学习这些"盲区"，它的理解力永远上不去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And I want people to just take a pause and reflect on what this is, because it looks like for your context, you are making sure that Claude understands internal definitions, terms, goals, et cetera." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 上下文是怎么建起来的

这套系统不是开箱即用的。Daniel 花了大量时间手动做初始的上下文注入：围绕他工作的每个主题和领域都建了上下文文件，包括目标、同事关系等等 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I did a lot of research on like how to build, how to set up your ClaudeMD and the context files. And basically I set it up so there's a context file around every topic that I have and like every area of my work." aria-label="回原文"></button>。方式包括喂链接、喂文档，以及用语音口述（他提到用 Whisper）不停地"胡扯"自己知道的一切 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:27" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I spent a lot of time manually contextualizing in the beginning, whether through like feeding it links and different decks and stuff like that. And also just a lot of, you know, whisper, just dictating endless information and blabbering endlessly about everything that I can think of." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

之后系统会自动维护：每隔几周跑一次更新，根据这段时间发生的变化来刷新知识文件 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:41" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Which is just incredibly powerful and how much context you can give relatively easily and quickly. And then this system basically updates itself on a recurring basis." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

它还学会了 Daniel 的工作习惯。比如他知道 Daniel 是"收件箱归零"的人——如果 Slack 上存了一条消息后来消失了，系统就推断这事处理完了；Gmail 收件箱里没了的，就当他读过了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:48" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And part of the fact that it knows me really well is also that it knows how I work. So for example, I'm inbox zero, both on Slack and on email. So it knows that if something was saved on Slack and is no longer, it means it's likely done." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这种对个人工作模式的理解让协作顺畅很多。

## 自我改进循环：四个自动跑的模块

Daniel 搭了一个每周自动运行的"[[自我改进循环|自我改进循环]]"任务，包含四部分 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:45" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And basically, it's almost not self-improvement, but it's Claude helping me improve it. So I'll walk you through something called the self-improvement loop. It's a scheduled task that runs weekly, and it has several skills attached to it, and I'll walk through them." aria-label="回原文"></button>：

**第一，草稿差距分析。** 他用 Claude 写东西时，Claude 给出草稿，他改完直接发出去——但 Claude 不知道改了什么。这个模块会让 Claude 回去找它之前给的草稿，对比他实际发送的版本，从中学习他的写作偏好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I'm going to walk through this in retroactive. So first of all, the first part is how my drafts landed. So what I found over time is that when I write with Claude, it'll give me a draft and then I'll change it and I'll just send it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第二，新[[技能|技能]]发现。** Claude 持续观察他反复做的事情，自动建议"这个可以变成一个技能"。

他很多技能就是这么来的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:50" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Now this is a cool one. The second one, and this is skills that are worth building. So basically Claude on an ongoing basis looks for things that I do on a recurring basis and suggests turning them into skills." aria-label="回原文"></button>。比如它建议他建一个"设计交接"技能，把 Co-work 里的研究和设计跟原型构建串起来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I've been prototyping a bunch lately, so it's helping me. It's suggesting I create a Claude design handoff skill between co-work and where I do the research and design where I actually build a prototype and a bunch of other interesting skills that I've built." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第三，现有技能的摩擦收集。** 他所有技能和周期任务的文件里都内嵌了反馈收集逻辑。

平时他跟 Claude 交互时说的"这个不对""这里有问题"，系统会自动记录。每周汇总 surfaced 出来，建议怎么改 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:33" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is the second part. The third part is fixes to my existing setup. So basically all of my skills and recurring tasks have lines in their files to collect feedback and friction from my interaction with them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**第四，外部信息审计。** 他做了一个叫 Improve 的技能，专门用来处理社交媒体和博客上铺天盖地的"AI 使用技巧"。

他把看到的内容丢进 Slack 频道让 Claude 审：这是真的吗？有用吗？

跟我们现在的东西冲突吗？需要吗？

然后两人一起决定要不要采纳 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:35" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="At the same time, I want to be able to actually implement what's relevant. So I've built a skill called Improve, which is basically like a very critical auditor for these." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他甚至拿主持人 Claire 的一期节目跑了一遍，Claude 给出了批评并指出了可以适配的部分 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So here I ran it against Claire, your How I AI episode on designing loops. And it basically gave me like a criticism of the episode. It said it's like a good video." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 扩展到团队：Workstation 插件

Daniel 和同事把这套东西打包成了一个叫 [[Workstation|Workstation]] 的共享插件，从 PM 扩展到了全公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:34" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="For sure. So basically, I'll say that one of the main things that were important for us, and we were debating whether to do this with Claude Code, which was much more powerful, or with CoWork, and we decided on doing it with CoWork." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

关键决策：他们选择了 Co-work 而不是 Claude Code（更偏技术）。原因是他们让几位 PM 试用 Claude Code，光是装依赖就花了三天，体验太差 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:18" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="We really insisted in the beginning on a good UX. And after having a few PMs try to do it with Claude Code through the terminal and taking three days to install everything and run all the dependencies, we understood that we need to do something that's simple." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Workstation 的核心是一个内置在聊天流程里的引导式 UX：自动帮你连好所有工具、确认你的角色、映射你的同事和管理层、看你的日历和 Slack、帮你建立个人写作风格——从第一天起，AI 写出来的东西就像你本人写的，而不是一个陌生人的腔调 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So basically one of the key elements of this workstation is that it basically does everything for you. So it's a UX built into the flow, into the chat. So basically what it does is walks you through everything you need to do." aria-label="回原文"></button>。整个过程大约 15 分钟 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Some people won't, though. And so if everybody's starting from this, it takes, what, 15 minutes, something like that? Yeah." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Daniel 说这个"把上手体验做成技能"的思路，源于他之前的教训：他做了一个写产品规格的工具，自己用得很好，但分发给其他 PM 时发现完全不行——因为所有参数都是按他个人的工作方式调的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:00" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And after I built something that was really powerful and good for the way I was working and I was excited to distribute it, I had live demos with several PMs that I watched them operated from zero, and I saw how different." aria-label="回原文"></button>。从那以后他坚持：任何内部工具都必须有手把手引导的新手流程 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I thought it was already this complete, fully baked tool, and I saw them using it and struggling because it was completely adjusted to how I work. And that's when it started when into this gem, I built this UX that guides you hand in hand." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 还缺什么

他觉得差两块。一是真正的云端运行能力——现在 Claude 还得依赖他电脑开着、在线才能跑。

他通过一个 Slack 频道做了变通，但不是真方案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:53" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So it's a really, really good question. I think the missing piece, and my personal sense is that it's very close, is A, the ability to operate in the cloud, essentially, in a way that's unrelated to when my computer is open and running." aria-label="回原文"></button>。二是对简单任务的自主执行——他已经在教 Claude 识别"闭环场景"（比如 Slack 上有人问了问题、后来有了回复且消息被取消保存，就推断这事已解决），为将来让 AI 自己处理这类琐事打基础 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:10" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="in the cloud when i'm away from my computer when it's off and you know uh coordinate like act on things uh that that will be huge and also closely related is just the ability to actually like you know do do things so i've been building up the system in order to enable to to basically prepare for it" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

多出来的时间花在哪？三件事：跟客户和用户深聊、做研究、以及改进这套 AI 系统本身 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Where do you think the effort should go? I'm not going to say anything surprising, but just purely in-depth work, that's one. So talking to customers, talking to users, and a lot of research, I think those two are really, really important and given, and also just the standard things that NEPM aims to get more time for." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **系统要能改自己的文件**：不能自我修改的系统，价值有天花板。让 AI 能重写自己的核心配置和知识文件，是持续进化的前提。
- **让 AI 主动找它"看不懂的东西"**：不要只靠你手动喂知识。让它在日常扫你的消息和文档时，主动挑出它不认识的术语、文件、目标，问你"这是什么、要不要存"——这是填补训练数据盲区最高效的方式。
- **在技能里内嵌反馈收集**：每个技能的指令文件里加一行"记录用户反馈和摩擦点"，不用你主动整理，每周自动汇总出改进建议。

    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });

    });
