上周末，The Cognitive Revolution 的主持人 Nathan 参加了在伯克利举办的 The Curve 大会。这是少数前沿实验室内部人士与外部批评者同处一室的场合。

他带回了几个相当有分量的说法，其中最惊人的是：

一位所有人都能叫出名字的前沿实验室高管承认，AI 的智能可能有一个人类不该越过的上限。

本期节目还聊到内存价格暴涨、AI 如何改写芯片和软件行业，以及 AI 在音乐上的表现。

## 前沿实验室的人开始说不该再往前走了？

Nathan 观察到一个变化：两年前大家分歧巨大，如今在一点上趋于一致——AI 确实会变得非常强大，不能再指望泡沫自己破掉。

用 Helen Toner 的话说，长预期时间线已经变得超级短。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:22" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You know, there were very few perspectives that As Helen Toner once famously put it, long timelines have got super short. I would say another corollary to that was what is called normal in terms of expectations is getting increasingly pretty incredible." aria-label="回原文"></button>

那位高管透露了三件事。第一，[[预训练|预训练]]依然在起作用，模型基础能力持续变强，没有明显的停点。

方法之一是合成数据：让模型自己把[[推理|推理]]过程转化成下一代的训练数据——这本身就是一种[[递归自我改进|递归自我改进]]。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You give the model a bunch of data and think, what could I transform this data into that if I then pre-trained on it would make the model smarter? And you're converting these reasoning tokens, your test time compute, you're converting back into pre-training data, which then goes in, of course, to the next generation of models." aria-label="回原文"></button>

第二，他认为最新的前沿模型已经具备了做出范式级突破所需的研究品味，只是还没找到把它引出来的办法，所以才需要上万个[[智能体|智能体]]去撞千年大奖难题。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="His take actually went as far as he believes that the latest frontier models do have the ineffable research taste that would be needed for them to start making paradigm level breakthroughs." aria-label="回原文"></button>

第三句才是真正的新闻：「**很可能存在一个我们就不该越过的智能水平**。」

<button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:09" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="One was He believes that there is a level of intelligence or there is might be a little strong, but there, there likely is, let's say a level of intelligence that we just shouldn't go past." aria-label="回原文"></button> Nathan 追问：那你愿意签署一个限制下一次预训练所用算力的协议吗？对方的回答是，这可能确实是合理的。

这是他第一次从前沿实验室领导者口中听到如此明确的设限表态。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But what actually happened was he basically said, yeah, I think that could be reasonable. The end, not really much of a fight there at all. Again, the details will be like incredibly important if we go down that path because the more data you enrich, you know, the more flops you put in enriching the data, you know, you can kind of, you can definitely play a sort of shell game of hide the compute, I think these days." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 设上限意味着什么：算力扩建的终结？

联合主持人 [[Prakash Narayanan|Prakash Narayanan]] 指出其中的矛盾：

智能提升来自算力和数据精炼，限制智能上限就等于限制算力扩建和数据精炼，这影响太大了。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:54" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so if you say that we shouldn't go beyond that point, that also means that you shouldn't build compute beyond that point, or you shouldn't refine data further beyond that point, which means a lot, really." aria-label="回原文"></button>

但 Nathan 认为，算力需求不会消失，只是用途会变。英伟达的 Jensen Huang 说过，英伟达花 20% 的精力设计芯片，80% 用于验证和测试。

前沿实验室接下来也会转向：让模型安全、可靠。

未来的大部分算力可能会花在监控上——比如监控模型内部激活，或者修复训练环境里的漏洞：

让模型先去攻击自己的训练环境，找出能钻空子的地方并修好。

实验室发现，环境越干净，模型作弊越少，一条雏形中的[[缩放定律|缩放定律]]正在浮现。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:46" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And gradually they're driving down the rate of flaws, which means that they're also driving down the rate at which cheating would be rewarded, which in turn means you're gonna get less cheating from the models as they actually come online." aria-label="回原文"></button>

另一条消息是：会上几乎没人再谈 2028。大家的说法是，关键时期就是现在，未来几个月的决策和算力使用方式可能决定一切。

还有人预测，一年之内，不做最好的科学将离不开 AI。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:26" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="They were talking more about just next year. It was really a kind of 2020, like critical period is beginning now. RSI is kind of at hand." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 其他公司是怎么追上的？靠蒸馏

会上还流传一个判断：真正推动前沿的只有两三家公司，Elon 和 Zuck 的公司其实一直在间接[[蒸馏|蒸馏]] Claude 的能力。方式很隐蔽：

市面上卖训练环境的厂商，很多是用 Claude 来生成这些环境的，其他公司买回去训练，等于变相继承了前沿模型的能力。

有创始人说，如果前沿公司不发布模型、没有这些泄漏，[[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 当年到 2026 年没人能追上的预测可能真会实现。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:00" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And that's how they are constantly capturing the capabilities advances that the true frontier companies had. So there wasn't actually a ton of talk about Competition beyond the top three." aria-label="回原文"></button>

> 【背景】蒸馏指用强模型的输出数据来训练较小的模型，从而低成本获得类似能力。

## 内存价格一年涨了 4.5 倍，为什么大家还在买？

节目的第二部分采访了 [[Positron|Positron]] 的联合创始人 [[Thomas Somers|Thomas Somers]]。这家公司做 AI 推理芯片，最近融了 8.75 亿美元。

他给了个直观的数字：一年零一周前的内存报价，现在已经涨了 4.5 倍，明年可能再翻倍。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:57" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Prakash asked whether the sharp rise in memory prices had changed the economics. I was looking at a quote that we had a year and a week ago for memory, and it's gone up four and a half times since then." aria-label="回原文"></button>

但大家照买不误，原因很简单：同样多的内存，今天能跑的模型比去年强了不止 5 倍。

真正的瓶颈是[[上下文长度|上下文长度]]——他自己的后台智能体从几个月前的 2 到 4 个，涨到了 15 到 20 个，每个都要独立的上下文，内存需求爆炸式增长。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But then the other side, I think the main limiter today of AI being applied to most applications is context length and being able to hold more context You know, per user and then scaling that out to drastically more users." aria-label="回原文"></button>

Positron 的做法是用手机、笔记本电脑同款的廉价内存，靠架构设计榨干它：

英伟达 GPU 实际只能用到理论[[内存带宽|内存带宽]]的 30% 到 40%，而他们第一代产品做到了 93%。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:34" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You only actually are seeing something in the ballpark of like two terabytes per second of actual realized memory bandwidth. And that comes down to a whole bunch of GPU architecture details." aria-label="回原文"></button> 下一款芯片 Asimov 单颗能装 2.3 TB 内存，是 B300 的 288 GB 的 8 倍，一颗顶八颗。

更有意思的是 AI 对芯片开发本身的改变。

他说，从 9 月初 GPT-6 Astra 发布起，智能体可以完全闭环地操作他们的 Palladium 仿真机：自己查文档、自己做笔记、自己搭测试环境。

在验证这件事上，AI 的钱已经超过了人力成本——6 个月前 token 花销相当于 1 个员工，之后一度超过多个员工，峰值时一天花超过 10 万美元。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Like, we, we were peaking, you know, in the, in the days after GPT success, we're really trying to, to push its boundaries, et cetera. We're, we're spending over a hundred thousand dollars a day on tokens." aria-label="回原文"></button>

不过他承认，AI 还没有提出过让他意外的设计想法。他自嘲还能守多久：「大概再守几周吧。」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Not many people left standing in the AIs can't do what I do category, so congratulations on your place for as long as it lasts. For a couple more weeks, I believe." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 用 Claude 的垃圾交差？员工因此被绩效审查

第三部分是 [[Latent Space|Latent Space]] 的 [[Sean Wang|Sean Wang]]（SWIX）。他现在管着一支团队，对员工有句狠话：「我不想付钱让别人替我经历大模型精神病。

我可以自己付钱经历自己的。」

<button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:23" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Sean Wang, known as SWIX, who runs Latent Space and the AI Engineer Conferences. And you know, the phrase I've sort of recently taken to is, I don't want to pay for someone else to go through LLM psychosis." aria-label="回原文"></button> 他有两三名员工正被绩效审查，原因是交上来的全是 Claude 生成的垃圾——而当事人自己看不出来，还觉得挺好。

他的逻辑很直白：我自己也能去问 Claude，那要你干什么？

他的用人标准也变了：除了安全和后端扩容这类岗位，编程年限不重要，有时经验反而是负担。

现在要能同时管理 5 到 10 个进行中的事项，更像管理者而不是执行者。看数据比看代码值钱。

而且他允许代码里存在垃圾，前提是模块边界必须自己完全理解——他做 Slack 竞品时遇到一个 bug：

同样代码，消息有时加载有时不加载，查出来是两个编程智能体在不同时间各写了一条代码路径，人类从来不会这么干。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="where you don't even know and actually the code also gets confused. So I'm making my own Slack competitor and I saw a bug where the messages weren't loading. I refresh and the messages were loading and I refresh again and the messages were not loading." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 中端软件订阅服务的死期到了？

SWIX 搞了个叫 KillMySass 的悬赏：谁能替换掉他公司不愿继续付费订阅的某款活动软件，就能拿到赏金。

背景是他要为这个 [[SaaS|SaaS]] 订阅花 4 万美元——他说，如果把同样多的钱花在 token 上，能买到一大堆 token。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="54:51" data-who="SWIX" data-en="Basically, you can call this a bounty on mid-tier SaaS that should not exist. Basically, half a salary or one-third of a salary for a piece of software that I don't own that nobody enjoys using." aria-label="回原文"></button>

最顽固的反对者是他自己的活动团队——一群用表格办事、和工会打交道的传统专业人士。

一开始坚决不要随手编的代码，看完提交作品的质量后，主动提出换掉旧系统。现在的体验对比更残酷：

给 SaaS 公司提需求，答复是排进第三季度路线图，而且第一季度提的需求才刚上线；

给自己的代码提需求，接了 DevIn 之后一到两小时就改好。他的结论是：「**SaaS 相当完蛋了**。」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:16" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's on our Q3 roadmap. And we don't have confidence that it'll actually be done in Q3. And by the way, we requested for a thing in Q1." aria-label="回原文"></button>

但他不认为软件工程会萎缩。

他的逻辑是把整个市场看作表格和邮件——所有现在靠表格、电话、开会解决的事，都可以被增量地变成定制软件。

软件定制化的终点，可能像手袋和服装一样按品牌和品味来区分。

真正的天花板只有一个：全人类能拿到的 token 带宽，也就是能造出来的硅片总量。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="59:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I think what software engineering is basically only done when we basically have Kate Spade versus Louis Vuitton versus Coach for software. We're not even at the point of like..." aria-label="回原文"></button>

## AI 已经攻进不可验证的领域了

节目结尾放了一首歌：Prakash 用 Claude Fable 把几年播客里的名场面剪成了一首曲子。

有意思的是，这不太算生成式音乐——模型是通过 API 操作数字音频工作站，真的在编排多种乐器。

Nathan 的判断是：如果你以为 AI 只在可验证任务（比如数学、代码）上强，**这堵墙恐怕也守不住了**。<button class="pd-ts" data-t="80:11" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I do think that perspective is like somewhat valid, but also I do think this goes right to the heart of one of the big questions that everybody's been talking about recently, which is simply how good are these things going to get in domains that are not automatically or readily verifiable?" aria-label="回原文"></button>

## 本集带走

- 一位前沿实验室高管明确表示，AI 很可能有不该越过的智能上限，甚至愿意考虑限制预训练算力——这是头一次听到这种表态
- 追赶者们通过买 Claude 生成的训练环境间接蒸馏前沿能力，这是差距没有拉开的隐形原因
- 内存一年涨 4.5 倍，但同等内存能跑的模型价值涨得更快；一家芯片公司的 token 开销已超过全部人力成本
- 老牌 SaaS 的处境危险：4 万美元的年订阅，可以用同样的钱买 token 自己写，改动从排期到 Q3变成两小时
- AI 在音乐这类没有明确对错标准的领域也开始拿得出手，只擅长可验证任务这堵最后的墙正在倒塌

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
