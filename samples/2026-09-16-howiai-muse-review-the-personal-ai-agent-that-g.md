[[Meta|Meta]] 出了个个人 AI [[智能体|智能体]]，名字叫 [[Muse|Muse]]——对，就是 Facebook、Instagram 那个 Meta。上手测评的这位是 How I AI 的主持人 [[Claire|Claire]],一个有孩子的科技从业者。她给出的总评有点出人意料：Muse 不一定是最聪明、最强大的智能体，但可能是她很长时间以来用过的**设计最好**的个人智能体，「我在每一刻都感到愉悦，从来没有觉得烦」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And my reflection on Muse overall using it is that this is just a really well designed personal agent. I was delighted at every moment. I was never annoyed." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 它是谁的智能体？给有孩子的「年纪大的人」

Muse 的定位是「能帮你把事情办成的个人 AI 智能体」，而且[[目标|目标]]用户定位极其精准——Claire 看完落地页的判断是：这是给有孩子的千禧一代、甚至 X 世代的产品，「这是给年纪大的人的」。官方展示的典型场景包括填写孩子的家长同意书、买婴儿推车这类家庭琐事，而不是写代码、做报表<button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is... For me, this is for millennials with kids, maybe Gen X with kids. This is for the olds." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

它和其他智能体相同的部分：背后有一台专用、安全的 Linux 虚拟机，自带完整浏览器和足够的存储、CPU、内存——不过 Claire 也调侃，落地页这段技术描述「显然不是我妈会在意的东西」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:53" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Muse Secure VMs run on a persistent, isolated Linux virtual machine equipped with full browser and enough storage, CPU, and memory to do the kinds of things you do on your own computer." aria-label="回原文"></button>。桌面端、移动端之外，还能通过 WhatsApp 跟它沟通，充分利用 Meta 自家生态<button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I as an early adopter who was probably the target audience for Muse cares about this, but I don't think anybody else does. The other thing is Muse has desktop and mobile." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 开箱即带 OpenClaw 的影子

Claire 是 [[OpenClaw|OpenClaw]](一款开源个人智能体框架)的老用户，她给智能体起名叫 Polly——沿用她原来那个智能体的名字。登录后她发现，Muse 带着明显的 OpenClaw 痕迹：Identity(身份)区里有类似 SoulMD(灵魂文件，定义智能体的性格与处世方式)、记忆等文件，而且都可以手动编辑。她还喜欢给它挑一个可爱的、会动的头像，「我喜欢我的智能体是可爱的、有名字的」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:22" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="What does it have that I really love from OpenClaw? Well, over here in Identity, it actually has an IdentityMD, a SoulMD, and a Memory. And you know what?" aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I love an identity. I want my agents to be... Cute and named." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

几个 Claude、ChatGPT 们少见的原语(基础组件)：

- **[[活动流|活动流]]**：不只是任务列表，而是每一次工具调用、脚本的完整脉络。普通消费者未必看，但作为开发者能看到它每一步怎么思考、怎么干活，「我真希望在 Codex、Claude Code、GrokBot 里也有这个」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:32" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But it's really kind of nice as a developer to see the step-by-step that it went through on every task. And as I was watching this progress, I was thinking, man, I really want this in Codex." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **[[信息流|信息流]](Feed)**:一句提示词「做一个关于我兴趣的信息流，语气清晰直接、可快速浏览、避免标题党」，点一下设置，每天就推送精准的个人化新闻——推送的全是睡眠、健康这类跟她生活强相关的内容。她说这比她的 Facebook 信息流健康多了<button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:00" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Ensure it is quick to skim. Avoid clickbait. That's it." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="I do like to put a sprinkle of salt in my water because I have been electrolyte-pilled, but this little feed is nice. It's much healthier, I will tell you, Muse, Engineers, and much healthier than my Facebook feed, which is truly nightmare fuel." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **想法(Ideas)**:不用你面对空白画布想任务，开箱自带一批建议——修路边的坑洼、主持家庭问答之夜、安排和老友吃饭、管理收件箱。她预测这会成为健康与健身领域的杀手级应用，「我们会看到很多健康类 Muse」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:46" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It has obviously the productivity things like managing your inbox, managing returns, but also has awesome things like relationships where it can host a trivia night for you or set up a dinner with a friend that you haven't spent time with in a long time." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **目标(Goals)**:不是待办清单，而是「你的个人目标」——设定后会生成方案、每天早晚提醒你，并持续追踪。她用它设定了喝水目标和给八个月大的宝宝做睡眠训练。同样的事她在 OpenClaw 和 ChatGPT 上都做过，但 Muse 的体验好得多：语气温柔不谄媚(「不像 GPT-4O 的语音那样谄媚，但温柔、友善、以人为本」)，而且是被当作目标追踪，不是一段聊完就沉底的对话<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Okay, again, I, you know, this is a goal and I have done this goal with OpenClaw. I have done this goal with ChatGPT. I have just liked the experience of setting personal goals with Muse a lot better." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is what I need to hear from a personal agent. It's not that sycophantic like a GPT-4O voice, but it is gentle, kind, human first. I really do like the voice of Muse." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **[[资料库|资料库]](Library)**:它为你生成过的所有东西都在这里——PDF、网站、图片、视频，还有她完全没料到的：**播客**。一句提示词就生成了一期六分钟、双人对话风格的周末 AI 新闻播客，类似 NotebookLM 那种形式，还能发布到 Spotify 或 Apple Podcasts<button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:56" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's a six-minute podcast. You can actually publish it to Spotify or Apple Podcasts if you want. I'm presuming you can share it with people." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 一次成型的晨间简报，是全场高潮

Claire 家从 OpenClaw 时代起就一直打印晨间简报：孩子们的日程、家长提醒、适合孩子的新闻，放在餐桌上全家一起看。她给 Muse 的要求是：一份明天早上的单页 PDF 日程，含一周安排、每个孩子的要点、给父母的提示、从邮箱里挖出的信息，加上旧金山的本地新闻和天气，做成全家人都爱读的样子。

结果：**这是第一份一次生成、而且她真正喜欢设计的简报**——此前 Claude 和 Codex 生成的 PDF 前端要么无聊要么难看，这份却「易读、排版漂亮」，还主动标出了巴西柔术课和钢琴课时间撞车这种她都没要求的服务，新闻部分本地、适龄，还附带餐桌讨论问题(「如果你能给一个新发现的星云起名字，你会叫它什么？」)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:30" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is an idea that my husband had and we have been printing these out. And this is the first one that has been one shot in a design I actually like. So this has made me want to test some of the Facebook models because I have hated the front end design of a lot of these PDFs out of Claude." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:06" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It actually does call out, I didn't have to ask it to, where like Brazilian Jiu Jitsu slams up against piano class. If you're a parent, you really appreciate that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这让她对 Meta 自家模型的前端生成能力刮目相看，打算专门去评测一下 Facebook 的模型<button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:07" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="So I guess the devil you know. I also am going to try the Facebook models because I was impressed by the artifacts that were generated. And I think that's good indication that there might be really strong models on the meta side, which I haven't played with." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 权限设计：信任是这样一点点建立的

Claire 认为最值得行业学习的，是 Muse 请求权限的方式。它没有开发者工具里常见的「自动批准」「YOLO 模式」「每次都问我」这类开关，而是在**恰当的时机**问你恰当的问题：连上邮箱后，它不是说「我有你的邮箱了，我去读了」，而是「我有你的邮箱，我可以去找一些关于你的信息吗？

」——找到后又问「我可以使用这些信息吗？」。「它没有预设我想开 YOLO 模式，但也不烦人，就是真的不烦人」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:23" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You know, the second thing is permission granting. It does a really nice job of asking for permissions, especially at the appropriate level for consumers. So you don't see down here like auto approve." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:00" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And so it was just this like really lovely way to give trust to this agent. That didn't presume that I wanted to go YOLO mode, but also was not annoying, like just was not annoying." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

头像则是设计的点睛之笔：让它干活时，小头像会抱出一台毛绒绒的小笔记本开始工作；做图像生成时弹出一个工作小球。Claire 说这才是设计师「在技艺顶层运作」的样子——不是把按钮磨圆，而是「拿一个静态头像，用 ImageGen 让它动起来，做成按需加载的加载画面，这是以前从来做不到的」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="What I mean is this. I mean, you take a static avatar and you use ImageGen to animate it for an on-demand loading screen that you could have never done before. Now, look, it's giving me options for who slime can be." aria-label="回原文"></button>。她把智能体从红色龙虾形象改成了青绿色的小龙 Slime(她孩子最喜欢的毛绒玩具的名字)，全程问答、选项、审批的交互都极其顺滑。

## 翻车的地方：浏览器操作

购物和订票暴露了短板。让它买 New Balance 9060 运动鞋，配色、尺码全出错，卡在[[浏览器使用|浏览器操作]]上;Claire 自认是「Codex 浏览器使用那一派的女孩」，「Codex 一会儿就能把这事儿搞定」。查《Odyssey》IMAX 场次那次好一些——走完了选座、游客结账、请求 Stripe Link 付款的全流程——但中途它似乎没打开正确的电影页面，信息来路不明，「这是唯一让我没有产生信任的部分」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:02" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Codex would crush this in a hot minute. For some reason, Muse just really struggles with finding the right thing on the internet to buy, which stinks because I do want it to buy things for me." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:23" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Do I want it to get me tickets for one of these things? Now, what's concerning, and again, does not engender trust, and this has been the only part, the browser use has been the only part where I did not get trust, is like, it didn't seem to pull up the right movie, so I don't know where it's getting that information." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

不过她对一个架构取舍很赞赏：Muse 不把整个虚拟机暴露给用户，只暴露浏览器这一个子集——「消费者看到一台长得怪怪的电脑会很困惑、很害怕，但直接看到浏览器、用浏览器，是更容易上手的方式」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:03" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But what I do like about Muse Is instead of exposing this full virtual machine to an end user who probably really doesn't care about that, it exposes a subset of virtual machine, which is the browser, which is the thing I care about." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 给产品经理和设计师的作业

Claire 的结论：担心 Meta 隐私？「我当 Facebook 用户快 20 年了，他们有我的照片、我妈妈、我孩子的照片——这算是熟悉的魔鬼吧。」她自己会把晨报、健康追踪、购物、孩子活动和「追着市政府修东西」这类家庭场景迁到 Muse 上；幕僚长级别的工作任务还是太复杂，不指望它<button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:05" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="They've got everything about me. So I guess the devil you know. I also am going to try the Facebook models because I was impressed by the artifacts that were generated." aria-label="回原文"></button><button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:19" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="My life is just too complicated for that. But I will definitely use it for wellness tracking, kid activity stuff. I'll probably move my morning newspaper to it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而她最想喊话的是产品经理和设计师：我们职业生涯的大部分时间都不需要设计智能体，这是一件全新的设计课题，「你应该花大量时间研究 Muse,弄清楚他们做对了什么——暴露了多少、隐藏了多少、让多少变得透明」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="This is a really new thing to try to design and I do think you should spend a lot of time with Muse. Figuring out what they got right, because I think they got a lot right on the design here." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **个人智能体的差异化不在模型，在设计**：Muse 不一定最聪明，但身份/灵魂文件可编辑、活动流全程可追溯、头像会干活动画，这些巧思让它「讨人喜欢」且零烦躁。
- **权限请求的正确姿势**：没有自动批准/YOLO 模式开关，在恰当时机问「我可以吗？」、展示找到的信息再请求使用——建立信任又不烦人。
- **五个值得抄的原语**：活动流(暴露每次工具调用)、个性化 Feed(一句提示词生成)、想法(开箱自带任务建议)、目标(追踪+每日提醒，不是待办清单)、资料库(含一键生成播客)。
- **用词要消费者化**：不叫插件、连接器、待办、artifacts,叫应用、任务——语言选择本身就是设计。
- **浏览器操作仍是短板**：买鞋翻车、订票将就，虚拟机只暴露浏览器子集是正确的减法，但视觉能力还不到位。
- **晨间家庭简报是可复制的用例**：日程+每个孩子的要点+本地适龄新闻+餐桌讨论题，单页 PDF 打印放餐桌。
