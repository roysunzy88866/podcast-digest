自然流量正在消失，因为「新的网站访客」已经不是人，而是大模型。这一集 Practical AI 播客请来了 [[Discover Labs|Discover Labs]] 的两位联合创始人——营销出身的 [[Liam Dunne|Liam Dunne]] 和工程师出身的 [[Ben Moore|Ben Moore]]——聊一件事：当人们越来越多地在 [[ChatGPT|ChatGPT]] 这类答案引擎里做研究，品牌要怎么被看见？他们说，[[SEO|SEO]] 没死，但战场变了。

## SEO 没死，只是扩展了

Liam 开场就表态：他不认为 SEO 已死，只是这个领域变大了。工作还是那三样——站内（照看好自己的网站）、站外（建立品牌权威）、技术（让[[智能体|智能体]]和 [[Google|Google]] 系统能访问并理解你的网站）——变的是**战术优先级**。

比如「好内容」在 Google 和 ChatGPT 眼里的定义就不一样 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:16" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="Is it kind of, how do the how do the two interact? I I mean, this is always I guess this is also a fluid thing, but if I am a company, this you know, I'm I'm a company in 2026. Like, what, what what is most important and what how are people thinking about the effort they're they're putting into maybe traditional SEO versus these topics. Do you have any thoughts there?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。传统 SEO 和 [[AEO|AEO]]（答案引擎优化，也有人叫 GEO，他把它们视作同一回事）合起来，才是完整的「自然搜索」。

变化的第一性原因是买家行为：人们在 [[LLM|LLM]] 里消化信息，点击就没了，「零点击研究」从社交平台蔓延到了自然搜索 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:56" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="Yeah, so broad topic. So It is, sorry. I'd say No, no, no, it's all good. So I would say the first thing is buyer behavior, right? So people researching and consuming information inside LLMs has changed buyer behavior, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。于是很多公司开始琢磨给人类和给智能体各做一个版本的网站 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:56" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="Like how do we shape the navigation bar, like conversion rate optimization, all of these things for human visitor. Whereas now it sees agents visiting your website. And so I think there's some interesting considerations there of, well, what are they looking for and what state does your website need to be in for them to be able to access information and understand you. And so I think there's some considerations there. I don't know where this ends." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 大多数从业者根本没在正确地测量

Ben 的批评很尖锐：传统 SEO 是「按数字填色」——排名几乎确定性，关键词有限可控；现在变成了一个有非确定性、随机因素的系统，需要统计建模，而 SEO 行业缺统计素养 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:14" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="Yeah. As Liam said, it's a big question, but it's a great one. So I would say this is probably the thing that has probably shaken a lot of the SEO industry at its core a lot in the sense that everyone kind of showed up, you know, in the past with traditional SEO and said, look, great. Let's like kind of color by, is it like paint by numbers kind of thing? Like you fill in the colors and we do this, we do that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。正确的问题是：怎么给[[citation|引用]]率、提及率、声量份额这些指标约束不确定性，把误差压到 5% 以内？

「老实说，这个行业里没有人真正在做这件事，甚至没有人真正在思考它」——大家买个工具、跑几个提示词，数字涨了就归功于自己，跌了就赖 Google 核心更新 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:12" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="There are hints in the network traffic and some of the, if you can decrypt the packets, there's also information there. And there's also like distilled models, so when I look at the training data, you can also figure out like some information from the distilled models. And so the thing is to set up basically an experimentation, a kind of engine or pipeline, whatever you want to call it, and then say, well, how do I bound the you mentioned there, citation rate, mention rate, share of voice? How do I bound the uncertainty on those metrics and what do I need to basically bring that uncertainty under, let's say, 5% margin of error. And to be honest, no one in the industry is really doing this, or even really thinking about it, people go on, they buy a ProFound or some other thing, and they're like, Here's my prompt, let's go away, go do this." aria-label="回原文"></button>。很多营销工时和工程时间浪费在不具统计显著性的波动上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:48" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="So, lots of times we have to come to our clients and say so let's say I'll give you a pro example, right? ## 品牌是怎么被「说出口」的：四大支柱

Ben 拆解了品牌在答案引擎里出现的完整机制：提示词被分词、摄入，模型先推理、有内部标准，然后才调用[[检索|检索]]引擎、做一组「[[查询分流|查询扇出]]」（把用户问题拆成多个子查询），拉回约 60 个初始候选来源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:11" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="And the second source of randomness is the actual temperature itself, like deliberate temperature randomness that generates the next token. And then the final part of this is like, it's also speaking with Liam about this, there's now fingerprinting on these LMs. So they will also nudge and create hashing functions in the background to know where the content came from. Anyway, so it goes through this reasoning phase, let's say, you say, like, a best gardening service in, let's say, Dublin and Ireland, I'll use that example. And it'll then start reasoning and say, oh, it'll have a certain set of criteria, it'll do some reasoning before it makes any queries with its retrieval engine." aria-label="回原文"></button>。随后用一个类似 DeepMind「一致风格」算法的多数投票机制，在候选中找共识、防幻觉，再从清理过的上下文生成最终回答 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:48" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="And it's exposed to a series of tools, and one of them has also got to be the retrieval engine. I'm saying a series of tools because when we get to agents, they could be exposed to a whole host of other things that you're not aware of. Then it accesses this retrieval engine, creates a set of, basically, query fan outs, you've probably heard the term, and then pulls back in basically a set of initial candidates essentially to be pulled into the context window. But before that happens, essentially, they look basically it's not always exactly the same algorithm, but DeepMind had an agree style algorithm, it's called. And basically, it's a way to avoid hallucinations." aria-label="回原文"></button>。所以 [[AI 可见性|AI 可见性]]要盯四根支柱：**[[权重|权重]]、推理、检索、响应**——只看最终引用，等于只看冰山一角 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:03" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="But there's all this other part before we got there. So I talked about, you know, the weights, how you're showing up in the weights, we did some research on this recently. How is the model reasoning? Like, what is what is it thinking? Like, when it makes core decisions around your domain or be that bottom of funnel, top of funnel, whatever it is." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这带出了本集最反直觉的发现：**被检索 ≠ 被引用**。以 [[Reddit|Reddit]] 为例：他们研究当时 ChatGPT 的检索引擎，发现它把大约三分之一的基础检索槽位留给 Reddit，但这些 Reddit 内容大多在引用环节被拒绝——它们被用来「给答案提供依据、塑造模型思路」，最终引用的却是别处（比如 TechCrunch）那个说同样事情的来源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:06" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="Sure. Yeah. So on the Reddit front, so obviously, as I mentioned there, there's obviously the the fourth pillar of AI visibility, the actual response, there's the reasoning or retrieval stage. And then we did a piece of research on the ChatBeti retrieval engine at the time, and it had allocated, like, an inset about, like, almost a third of its basic retrieval slots for Reddit. So if it could find relevant threads for for that topic or that query, it would basically inject that into the context window at the time." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Daniel 用例子复述：10 个 Reddit 来源加 1 个 TechCrunch 说同一件事，答案引擎用 Reddit 确认，却引用 TechCrunch——Ben 确认「完全正确」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:23" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="But it doesn't cite the Reddit things. It cites the TechCrunch thing because it's, like, I don't know, for whatever reasons behind the scenes, cites Do I have the right understanding here?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 品牌该怎么下场：相关性、共识、一致性

Liam 把策略压缩成三个词。**相关性**：先搞清楚你是谁、目标客户是谁，再把它翻译成买家会在 LLM 里搜索的语言——这是确保内容被查询扇出命中前提 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:22" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="So and it really depends on what your current state is. Like, do you have a website today? That's gonna be super important and like all the foundational stuff really matters. Like the I used to run a paid ads agency and I was like, man, why does some companies like really succeed with ads and others don't? There's lots of factors that go into it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**共识**：内容只是「掷帽入场」，信任票才是关键；LLM 之前的搜索体验之所以糟糕，是因为「我们营销人毁掉了一切」，而买家长上下文式的提问创造了长尾查询，长尾竞争少，是小公司对抗 HubSpot 这类巨头的机会 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:29" data-who="/ 24:32 Liam Dunne" data-en="These are the attributes of the solution, you know, we're looking for a piece of software, must have a free trial, must charge monthly, must integrate with hub spot or CRM. All of these requirements, I can front load that. And then the LLM does its thing and it comes back with personalized recommendations. Now I'm not saying that marked as a, you know, that's our job to influence those recommendations, but it's just a far better deal than like clicking through all those websites and finding the information myself. Like me personally, I just use it for everything, Date night, you know, meal prep, like buying software, everything." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们看好 Reddit（很多 B2B 公司不会玩，所以有优势）、数字 PR、客户评价平台（SaaS 行业就是 G2），枪口抵头选一个就押 [[YouTube|YouTube]]——原生搜索渠道、Google 旗下 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:51" data-who="/ 41:17 Liam Dunne" data-en="Why should we trust them? Mark has always asked me like, well, hey, where should we focus our efforts? And my answer is everywhere. Really what we're talking about here is it's a budget and time constraint, right? Like if budget and time weren't a consideration then be everywhere." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**一致性**：各渠道用同一套词，让它们被嵌入模型和世界。外链时代的「拿到一条 do follow 链接」不再是关键——围绕你这个实体的描述文字更重要，因为「如果某个 LLM 选中这段话放回答案里，我们是否被放在有竞争力的位置上？」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:38" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="And then consistency, this is kind of where it closes the loop. What I kind of opened the thought with is you need to get really clear on what you want to be known as, as an entity, as a company, right? So if you go to our website, you're gonna see we use similar words across the place because I want those to be embedded in these models and the world. And so whether it's your activities on Reddit, you're posting videos on YouTube, you're posting content on LinkedIn, even like your company LinkedIn profile, content on your website and on your blog, always presenting yourselves with a consistent message. Right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>

关于 Reddit 玩法的一个具体观察：他们没发现点赞数与引用量相关，相关性最高的是**内容本身**——被抽取的段落经常在页面往下一半的位置、评论只有一两个赞，就因为那段话命中了查询里的实体 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="44:41" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="Yeah, so I'm not gonna speak in absolutes because I think these things are always changing. What we have observed is engagement in terms of upvotes. We haven't seen that correlate with increased citations. What we have seen highest correlation with is the actual content itself, right? So I always like to break things into like easy ways to understand because that's how my brain works." aria-label="回原文"></button>。不过随着 OpenAI、Reddit、Google 之间的商业协议推进，账号历史之类的信号未来可能变得重要 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:42" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="And so we found very often that the passage being extracted from Reddit is like 50% down the page and the comment has like one up or two up votes and it's because the passage was relevant. Now, I think that might change over time, right? Because similar to how relevancy is super important, but authority is becoming even more important. I think these models are gonna keep similar like, you know, how Google always releases a core update is, you know, they it's a cat and mouse game between these providers and marketers who are constantly trying to catch catch up. And I know there are commercial agreements between OpenAI, Reddit, Reddit, Google." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

还有几个可操作的小窍门：行业不敢公布价格？公布一个方向上大致正确的数字，效果「好得离谱」——因为存在信息空白，LLM 不想幻觉，就会引用你填的数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:36" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="Like, so if I have if I have this long tail query, basically, one of the query files we've been retrieved on, what other supporting materials are out there that are likely to get ranked on that query to support it. So that's like consensus. We have a number of audits we do on this, etcetera. And then the other side of it is, so once I pull in that, like, before, so I pull in that set of sources that basically add consensus that'll help. And also you want to avoid saying, let's say there's some like anti consensus information out there, you want to avoid that building up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

反过来，模型权重里嵌入的事实准确性、情感和共现（训练时注意力函数把你的品牌与哪些实体关联）都会影响引用时刻的判断，连品牌名是个词典里已有的英文单词都可能成为问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:22" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="Essentially that's gonna impact them. So if your brand is like a word that is like, let's say, already existing English word in the dictionary, for instance, that can be an issue. Or if it's like has negative connotations with it, or it doesn't align with the, like, basically overall, let's say it's a security platform and it's like openly or something like that, that could have a So negative there's multiple points there, both on the retrieval side, like consensus, as well as like how the model is, like, basically the sentiment it has towards your brand, and also what." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Ben 还顺带嘲讽了一下：他们做模型排名分析时，发现某个 AEO 工具自身在模型里呈现非常负面的情感 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:45" data-who="Ben Moore" data-en="So that's just a good concrete example of it. And similarly, competitor isn't talking about those, you can also, like, do something similar to kind of capitalize on. And the second thing is, well, even when it basically perceives that information it's got from your brand, like, how are you showing up in the weight? So it might have a very negative sentiment around your brand. And if it's looking at data from your brand, then let's say, for instance, it's quite funny when we did the model rank analysis, profound as a really negative sentiment in the score." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 下一个前沿：从只读到读写

Liam 看两个方向：一是站外第三方来源如何影响品牌在 LLM 里的感知——「归根结底，品牌是最终的护城河」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:27" data-who="Liam Dunne" data-en="Okay. So I'd probably say two things. Off-site, like how are those third party earned sources gonna influence how I am or our clients are perceived inside LLMs. I think there's just so much change happening there. And ultimately, brand is the final moat." aria-label="回原文"></button>；二是**智能体可访问性**。现在大家都在卷「可发现性」，但很快智能体不只会爬你的网站，还会在上面执行操作：已有客户报告「上周有个 AI 智能体预约了一次演示」，还有客户的用户通过命令行让智能体出去采购、装好工具回来说「已经给你装好了」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:36" data-who="/ 49:29 Liam Dunne" data-en="Now this is this is going back to my original point. ## 本集带走

- **别再只盯最终引用**：AI 可见性有四根支柱——权重、推理、检索、响应；被检索进上下文≠被引用，Reddit 大量内容是给模型「打底」却把引用让给了别处。
- **三词策略**：相关性（想清楚你是谁、翻译成买家语言）、共识（多渠道信任票：Reddit、数字 PR、评价平台、YouTube）、一致性（全渠道同一套词，让实体嵌入模型）。
- **吃长尾红利**：用户在 LLM 里前置加载全部上下文，产生竞争极少的长尾查询——这是小品牌绕开巨头垄断的「金钱关键词」的机会。
- **用统计思维测量**：先问「怎么约束这些指标的不确定性到 5% 以内」，别看到数字涨跌就归因；大量工时浪费在不显著的波动上。
- **填信息空白**：行业不敢公布的定价等空白地带，你发布方向正确的数字就极可能被引用。
- **准备读写环境**：智能体开始在网站上执行动作（订演示、代采购），网站的可访问性和可操作性是还没人卷的下个前沿。
- **押注真实人类信号**：指纹识别会让 AI 生成内容被溯源清理，权重内的印记比单次上下文曝光值钱数量级，而真实的品牌叙事是最难被复制的。

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;

  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
