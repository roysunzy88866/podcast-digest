机器早在 30 年前就在国际象棋上击败了人类,但 Chess.com 的数据表明,人类的胜负欲反而让这项古老游戏空前繁荣。他们的日活达到千万级,并在自举运营 20 年后确立了「让 10 亿人下棋」的宏大使命。但在繁荣背后,面对 AI 辅助作弊的威胁,这家全球最大的国际象棋平台正用更强大的统计模型与技术打一场看不见的保卫战。

### 20 年自举的「反共识」生意经
Erik 从破产拍卖中买下 [[Chess.com|chess.com]] 域名。当他试图在 Sand Hill Road（硅谷风险投资聚集地）寻求融资时，几乎所有人都告诉他这是一个「不可投资的小众激情项目」，让他去找份正经工作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:17" data-who="" data-en="They were teachers and were guests in classrooms I was in. So I had a lot of meetings, and basically, most of them said, this is uninvestable, you should get a real job." aria-label="回原文"></button>。最终他没有融一分钱，而是 2007 年上线产品，靠收取在线学习会员费在 18 个月内迅速盈利 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:59" data-who="" data-en="Thank you for watching! we launched in 2007 right as i graduated from school and then i think within like 18 months we were starting to charge memberships for like online chess learning and we pretty quickly became profitable and then we just grew you know as we had more revenue we would just hire somebody new whether it was a community manager a developer a system assist admin uh you know eventually designers and" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这种不走寻常路的模式贯穿了公司发展的始终:当硅谷的标准创业剧本是「雇名校技术合伙人、融一大笔钱、砸钱获客」时,Erik 偏偏找了圣何塞州立大学的朋友,全远程办公、零外部资金、不花一分钱获客,并且挑了一个极小的市场 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:34" data-who="" data-en="I think one of the curses of being a founder today is just how much founder advice there is out there. At the time when I was getting into doing this 20 years ago, the playbook was like, hire a technical founder from Stanford, raise a bunch of money, get an office, go after a big market, spend a bunch of money on customer acquisition." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这种专注于自身愿景、极致用户体验的做法让团队一步步做大。直到 2024 年,看着疫情期间涌入的用户潮在回落后依然维持在极高的基准线上,团队才真正确信:这是一门在不断复利增长的大生意 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:43" data-who="" data-en="And then when we came down again, we were so much higher and we maintained this baseline. And so kind of in 2024, we're like, oh my gosh, like this thing is actually sticking around." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

> 【背景】Sand Hill Road(沙丘路)是硅谷著名的风险投资机构聚集地,在语境中代指 VC 行业。

当公司估值达到数十亿美元的规模后,Chess.com 才开始引入 General Atlantic 和 CVC 等私募股权机构 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:08" data-who="" data-en="Obviously, General Atlantic was really the first private equity to believe in us, you know, back in 2020 or 2021. you know they saw our growing business and they had conviction that we you know we're we're a strong player in an interesting market and and they came in and you know we had another investor you know it's kind of interesting because if you go back in time like we were just this privately entirely privately held and then um you know someone comes along and is like oh i love chess i want to invest and we're like" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Erik 强调,这些投资全是「二级市场」交易(买断老股),公司业务本身仍不需要任何外部资本注入来维持增长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:29" data-who="" data-en="And we're as committed as ever to continue to grow the game and to follow our mission. But yeah, we're still, no capital needed to grow this business. How does it change?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。而引入 PE 的实际意义并非「被催促拼命工作」,而是帮助这家 650 人的全远程团队在运营预测、财务汇报等方面完成了组织架构上的成年礼 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:18" data-who="" data-en="They're in the weeds on the product and the community, and they've really been great at pushing us to be our best. not just saying you know do better but like here's some areas have you looked at this and it's all it's just made us stronger and then all the operational stuff all the forecasting and all the kind of operational excellence that that we've really brought to bear that we've needed to do to kind of grow up and do this it's been wonderful um and has made us better and stronger and as our reporting gets better we learn more about things and uh you" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

### AI 让国际象棋「先变无聊,再变惊艳」
[[Deep Blue|Deep Blue]](深蓝)当年击败 Kasparov(卡斯帕罗夫)时,许多人预测国际象棋将走向死亡,但这违背了基本的人性。「从根本上说,人类想做人类的事情。那是人与其他人的竞争,是人在制造东西,是人在解决问题。」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:24" data-who="" data-en="Yeah, it's so interesting. Fundamentally, humans want to do human stuff. That's humans competing against other humans." aria-label="回原文"></button> 

虽然早期的国际象棋引擎(如 [[Stockfish|Stockfish]])过于完美,导致顶级棋手纷纷模仿电脑下出极其无聊的平局残局,一度扼杀了比赛的观赏性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:10" data-who="" data-en="And that chess computer, like... actually made chess pretty boring for a little while because it was so perfect and all these players tried to play like the best computer they were like grinding out these boring end games and it like kind of just got boring but then neural nets came along" aria-label="回原文"></button>;但随后基于神经网络和自我强化学习的引擎(如 [[Leela Chess Zero|Leela Chess Zero]])出现,它们极具攻击性且不按套路出牌,反而重新点燃了棋手们的创造力,把这项运动推向了前所未有的精彩高度 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:26" data-who="" data-en="actually made chess pretty boring for a little while because it was so perfect and all these players tried to play like the best computer they were like grinding out these boring end games and it like kind of just got boring but then neural nets came along" aria-label="回原文"></button>。如今,AI 早已不是终结者,而是变成了口袋里的超级教练,通过复盘分析、定制谜题等方式,大幅加快了人类学习的速度 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:08" data-who="" data-en="And the players have gotten better. And really, computers are now able to help us unlock the game in ways that make our enjoyment of the game better, whether it's the AI kind of coach on game review that reviews your game and helps you learn from it, or playing against the computer or generating puzzles that are specific for you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。我们看到越来越年轻的棋手涌现,正是这种「智力普惠」的直接证明 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:18" data-who="" data-en="Like if you took a talented 10 year old today versus 30 years ago, like how much faster do you think they could reach ELO 2000 or master or something? I mean, we're seeing it already." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

### 没有捷径:如何练就顶尖技能
拥有海量各等级人类棋谱数据的 Chess.com,给出的「变强秘诀」却极其朴素:「只是像小积木一样的重复」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:36" data-who="" data-en="Oh, no. Yeah, it's just repetition of like the small building blocks. And so it is doing puzzles." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

即使有了强大的 AI 辅助,知识获取变得极度容易,人类依然必须实打实地把每一次练习存进自己的大脑里。「这就像当你训练一个神经网络时一样,你给它一堆数据。

但人类的大脑并不能那样并行工作。」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:11" data-who="" data-en="You have to store that. It's the same as when you train a neural net, right? You give it a bunch of data." aria-label="回原文"></button> 要在某个领域出类拔萃,靠的是习惯的「千日复利」:如果你每天做 5 个国际象棋谜题,乘以 1000 天,你就做了 5000 个谜题,你自然会变得好得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:49" data-who="" data-en="And one thing that I really love is the concept that if you just take any given day in your life and multiply that by a thousand, that's kind of what your life looks like." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

### 作弊:技术引发的问题,用更强技术解决
当机器能轻易产出超越人类的输出并将其伪装成人类表现时,在线作弊成为了一个严峻的挑战 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:58" data-who="" data-en="Maybe they will call it cheating or not, but it is. machines can create superhuman output and it can pass off as human performance right it's tough right i i wish humans didn't have this drive that like to take advantage of someone else or you know put themselves above somebody or steal from them but there's some segment of the population where" aria-label="回原文"></button>。尽管有人曾担忧这会是对游戏的生存威胁,但国际象棋界早就习惯了与作弊长期斗争 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:44" data-who="" data-en="you know either for you know financial gain where they're using AI tools and and and technology to steal from people or even if they're just getting on to online chess and use you know loading up a computer and to steal rating points or boost their ego or whatever it is it is sad you know it's um and it bums me out that that humans can't just you know get in there and and be honest and full of integrity at all times um" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Chess.com 不愿透露具体的[[反作弊|反作弊]]机制,但核心在于极其强悍的数据壁垒:他们完全掌握人类下棋的模式特征,也清楚计算机下棋的逻辑,并能通过统计模型和机器学习模型不断逼近检测的极限 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:22" data-who="" data-en="We have more data. We have more statistical models, machine learning models, and that really kind of track how people play chess. And again, we have access to all this data." aria-label="回原文"></button>。虽然没法钻进每个玩家家里装摄像头,但他们依然能通过这些模型高效地封禁作弊账号 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:06" data-who="" data-en="It's something we spend a lot of time on and we feel really great about our role to protect the game and we feel very confident that we're doing a great job. Cheating can't be prevented because you can't put a webcam in every single person's home, but we do do a really good job at catching it and banning people who do that and trying to educate them as well." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

### 扑克、AGI 与人类技能的终极价值
在扩张版图时,Chess.com 推出了 Gambit,进军扑克领域。其最大的创新在于引入了国际象棋的「评级思路」:剥离买筹码的财力优势,用一套基于对手实力、赢牌数和手牌数的综合算法,衡量你「实际上到底有多强」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:20" data-who="" data-en="How good are you really? Not just can you buy the most chips or can you use a bot or can you steal the most money, whatever it is, but how good are you really? And we're going to roll out other games as well because we like the playbook of what we've done." aria-label="回原文"></button>。当人们在乎自己的评级分数时,就会像在乎钱一样在乎它,这将成为扑克界的一大创新 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:01" data-who="" data-en="as people get used to like wait i actually care about my poker rating and i treat this as like something important um i think people are going to care about it as much as money because it's a reflection of like the value and their skill as a player and of course even in chess your rating goes up and it goes down and you have your wins and your losses but we think that people are going to care a lot about their poker rating i certainly do um" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

面对即将到来的 [[AGI|AGI]](通用人工智能)或 [[ASI|ASI]](超人工智能),Erik 相信超人类智能会以极快的速度降临,而这就需要设立明确的护栏和互动规则 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:10" data-who="" data-en="I do believe in superhuman intelligence and that it will move at a faster and faster pace. I think it will be a little bit scary if we don't put guard rules, guard rails around that and rules for how it should engage with humans." aria-label="回原文"></button>。AGI 能用于善也能用于恶,其走向取决于掌权者的选择,因此这是个深刻的文化问题,而非单纯的技术问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:21" data-who="" data-en="And it's really what we do with that as a culture more than what the tool is itself. So a superhuman intelligence, an AGI, ASI can be used for good or for evil. It's going to depend on the people who are in charge." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

即便机器全方位碾压人类,Erik 依然相信人类技能的绝对价值:国际象棋的热潮就是最强有力的证据——人类渴望习得人类技能,也会欣赏哪怕不如机器那样完美的杰出人类表现 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:06" data-who="" data-en="I think the interest and growth of chess is actually a solution to all the world's problems. But it's incredibly optimistic to me because it is so clear that people, they want to acquire human skills, even when the machine can outperform them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。那种认为「在超人类 AI 面前人类技能不再有意义」的虚无主义,纯粹是无稽之谈 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:49" data-who="" data-en="But I think the idea that human skill is no longer relevant in the world of superhuman AI just as a product of the technology itself is nonsense. Right, so there I think we're super aligned and there's a bunch of complexity around the guardrails and distribution of the fruits of that technology still." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **反共识的启动资金**:找一个你真正热爱的小众市场,靠早期收费实现正向现金流;在不需要重资产投入时,不拿 VC 的钱也能跑赢长达 20 年的马拉松。
- **让 PE 成为组织进化的催化剂**:在成熟期引入二级市场投资,不为续命,只为倒逼团队建立严谨的运营预测、深度数据汇报与流程规范,完成向大型企业的蜕变。
- **人机协作的最优解**:即便机器超越人类,其最大价值也是作为加速人类学习的「口袋教练」,通过实时复盘和针对性强化训练,缩短天才的成长周期。
- **对抗 AI 作弊的检测逻辑**:用「机器行为 vs 人类行为」的差异化特征作为核心判别指标。利用海量的对局数据不断优化统计与机器学习模型,以技术手段保护游戏生态。
- **[[评级系统|评级系统]]的降维打击**:把零和博弈的金钱游戏(如扑克)改造成以「客观技能实力」为导向的评级体系,通过剥离纯粹的财力优势,重塑用户在游戏中的核心驱动力。
- **微习惯的千日复利**:不要去寻找捷径。把你想提升的领域拆解为每日最小单位的动作(如 5 个谜题、1 幅草图),将一个普通日子乘以 1000 天,这就是你不可替代的专业壁垒。
