## 起点：自己被坑出来的生意

Aaron 是二次创业，上一家公司给财务顾问和机构做财富管理平台，创办后没几年就赶上融第一轮时正好撞上金融危机。他常说自己经历过后觉得现在创业都是送分题。上一家公司做了几年，员工想要自己的 401(k) 计划，他托销售的朋友、一个卖 401(k) 的顾问办了一个，结果发现「这太糟糕了，怎么会这么难、这么笨重、这么贵」——灯泡亮了，这就是 Vestwell 的起点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:00" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="You know, we were a few years into that business. Our employees wanted their own 401(k) program, so I was like, okay. So one of our sales guys had a buddy who is an advisor that sold 401(k) plans and he gave us a plan. And I was like, this is horrible. How can it be this hard, this clunky, this expensive and that's really when the light bulb went off. Like, hey, there's a problem to go solve here." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

> 【背景】上一家公司名为 Foliar Dynamics，创办于 2007 年 10 月（此信息不在本段原文中，出自节目其他部分/背景资料）。

2016 年创业时他做的第一件事不是写代码，而是设计一个企业主搭储蓄计划的决策树工作流。行业的默认做法是手把手人肉流程：在计划服务协议上勾一堆框，safe harbor、选择性缴款这些词「对大多数人像外语」；他当年自己问顾问该选哪个，顾问说法律上不许给建议 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:11" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="So the default was a very handheld process. Where someone would say, hey, let's talk through a whole host of options. Let's check a bunch of boxes on a planned services agreement document. Let's stand up that, you know, let's choose your investments. Let's, you know, do you want safe Harbor, elective, non elective, all of these things. They're like a foreign language to most people, right? And even me, when I was at my prior company the advisors, like, hey, do you want to do this or that? And I was like, I don't know, what should I do? And the advisor is like, I can't tell you what to do. I'm like, well, you're an advisor. Give me advice, that's the whole job, right? And he's like, well, legally, I'm not really allowed to. So then that was when I was thinking like, hey, we could actually help walk people through this on their own. Much more consumable, distilled fashion that allowed people to make conscious, educated decisions without having to be an expert. Because you don't expect someone to be an expert in that space, right? That's going too far, but it's like, OK, how do you give them the confidence and all the information they need to make those right decisions, for themselves and for their business. But do so in a really streamlined fashion that doesn't become operationally intensive. So that was a lot of what we saw, but we didn't build the core of it. We didn't build the core underlying record keeping sub accounting piece when we started the business and that was simply because, we didn't know enough and I didn't know enough. And I was like, if we go down this path, we're going to chew up millions of dollars. We're going to end up with either a half baked solution that doesn't work or we're going to end up with a solution that's so tightly boxed. It's going to be so hard to open up the aperture of who we want to actually engage over the life of this business. So we kind of slow walked it, if you will and it was painful to do that. But it also was really eye opening because we learned so much about what to do as well as what not to do. When we were going to build our own and then what we started to do over time was lift out pieces of that legacy software and just rebuild it from the inside out. And then, eventually just rip and replace and re-platform the entire thing. So that was kind of the evolution of it all. But we had to stand up an RIA, right? We had to become an SEC registered advisor. So we started that, I remember we stood up an RIA and we got audited by the SEC. I think the second we put on our first business on the platform and I was like, you got to be kidding me. We literally just started this thing and now I got to go through an SEC audit. But that was also really enlightening." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

一个关键决策：他们创办时**没有**自建底层的账务记录和子账务系统——因为「我们懂的不够」，怕烧掉几百万美元做出半成品或框得太死的方案，所以先租传统软件跑业务、边学边把部件一块块替换，最后整个重新平台化。他们还必须搭一个 RIA(SEC 注册的投资顾问实体)，结果第一个客户刚上平台就被 SEC 审计——「我们才刚起步呢」——但那次审计反而让他看清监管者在想什么，成了积极经历 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:44" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="You know, but it was fun. Because it ended up being a really positive experience. Because I started to see like, well, what were the regulators thinking about? And they were actually just trying to be helpful. They weren't coming in just trying to needle us, right? They were truly trying to educate themselves and be helpful about the future of where they saw, you know, regulatory compliance and those aspects going. So it ended up being a great thing at the end of it but it was pretty nerve wrecking to start, right? You got probably four or five employees at the time. Maximum and all of a sudden the SEC's showing up at your door saying, OK, time for an audit. You're like, oh gosh, we haven't even gotten to the starting blocks yet. So, this is how it goes, though." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 产品市场匹配时刻：拿下摩根士丹利

行业存在了五十多年，不能指望产品一上架就飞快卖掉。真正的 PMF 时刻是 2020-2021 年 COVID 期间，Vestwell 签下摩根士丹利，成为 [[Morgan Stanley|Morgan Stanley]] at Work 的核心组件，把他们的财务顾问和业务接上平台——市场从那时意识到这两家在认真思考职场储蓄的未来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:05" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="Yeah, sure. Our business, it's retirement, it's savings, it's college savings, disability, emergency, you know, all the tax preferred things you need for life, right? So to get your education, retirement and healthcare needs. But it's a very complicated industry. Complicated business, it's been around fifty plus years from an industry standard and, you can't just show up and have this thing that just flies off the shelf. So it takes a while to kind of entrench yourselves into some of these players and our go to market. As you mentioned, is through channel partners. So we go to market through financial institutions, we go to market through payroll HCM providers, and we go to market through government sponsored programs, out to individuals and businesses across the country. You know, we started the business, it was kind of like, let's test it with a few financial firms, advisors, RAs, and get the wheels going. But it really wasn't until about 2020, 2021, during COVID. Where we were able to ink a deal with Morgan Stanley to help be a core component of Morgan Stanley at work, and power their financial advisors and all of their businesses that they were supporting through Morgan Stanley onto the platform. That was really kind of a point in time where the market realized, oh, wow, these guys,  from a Vestwell standpoint, as well as a Morgan Stanley standpoint, are really thinking about the future of where workplace savings goes and how this becomes kind of a catalyst for the wealth savings that people are going to have over the course of their career." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**合同结构**：五到十年的长期合同，每年数百万美元的最低承诺且逐年递增，但门槛设在伙伴不用掏钱的水平——他们只需把业务带到平台。这给了 Vestwell 稳定性和对未来收入的可见性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:53" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="Yeah, so what we do typically is we'll do large, they're long term, you know, five to ten year contracts and there's a minimum commitment to that, right? And ultimately, it's no money out of the pocket of our partners. Because we set the bar at a very attainable level where they're just bringing the business onto the platform. But it gives us some assurance of the business is going to show up and we're going to manage and hire, and staff according to what the plan looks like. So it gave us a lot of kind of stability and visibility into the future of saying, OK, if we do this, we do this well, everyone's happy. This thing's going to continue to accrete and grow the business across all of their advisor networks. So that, you know, when we signed that it was a multi year deal with, multi million dollar minimum commitments year of year that continue to grow and we've just. Since then, we've just continued to expand our partnerships across Morgan Stanley and they're one of our best, and greatest partners that we have out there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

**收费模式**：按雇主每月(PEPM)+ 按储户每月的固定费用，部分含基点成分；费用由雇主和储户承担，摩根士丹利只承诺带来业务量，除非没达到合同门槛才补钱 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:57" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="Yeah, so the business model is really, we get paid off a per employer per month fee and a per saver per month fee. Typically a flat fee, something like a PEPM. Sometimes there's a basis point component depending, we're trying to over index on basis points and look like an asset manager or an advisory shop but sometimes we will do that depending on the services we're providing. So that's basically, those fees are borne by the employer and the savers on the platform. And then, Morgan Stanley really is just committing to continue to bring volume. They're not, or they're our enterprise partners. They're not necessarily going to write a check into us unless they miss the threshold of where we contractually agreed to." aria-label="回原文"></button>。核心 KPI 是平台上的储户数——目前超过 250 万人 actively saving(平台上有钱在积累)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:50" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="Right, so we have over two and a half million people actively saving. Meaning they have dollars on the platform. They're in there and accruing some growth out of those." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 打法核心：贴牌纯软件

为什么大机构需要他？传统上只有两条路：要么从 FIS、SS&C 这类传统厂商租软件、再雇几百人运营；要么把 Fidelity、Empower 一堆「货架产品」摆上去让顾问卖——但那些都是竞争对手，天天想把你客户的资产吸走。

Vestwell 的方案是：产品是你的品牌、你的顾问、你的资产管理，我们从头到尾全做完，你零开销 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:14" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="So they typically have something in house on the wealth side. The retirement side, specifically retirement, and there's other savings programs too. It's very complex when you get to the sub accounting and the record keeping. So traditionally what firms do is they have one of two models. Model one would be they go rent a piece of software from a legacy provider, which would be like an FIS or an SS&amp;C. They go hire a bunch of people, you know, a couple hundred people to support the business and then they hope that you have some offering that they can accrue beyond the headcount operational overhead as well as software license fees. So that's kind of, that's model number one. Model number two is you don't rent any software and you just put a bunch of providers on the shelf. So you go out there and you have, maybe a Fidelity and a Empower, you know, pick a name, right? That's out there in insurance brand, nationwide and they put a bunch of these on the shelf. And then they say, hey, if it's an advisor sold solution, here's what you're permitted to go sell, right? And they frame it or box it in some capacity. The model we took was a bit different and said, listen, why would a large global brand be putting their business with a core competitor that's really just trying to siphon that business away from that brand, be it a Morgan Stanley or whoever it is. You know, our approach was like, hey, you should have the most modern, elegant experience possible. It should be low cost and it should be yours. It shouldn't be, you know, trying to compete with someone else that you're putting on the shelf.  Because you can't operationalize it yourself. So we said, hey, listen, we'll put you in the market. It's your product, it's your brand, it's your advisors. It's, you know, your asset management if you want it and we'll do it all front to back, and you have zero overhead to do it. And that kind of really flipped the tables with how we were able to engage and got people to say, oh, I can do this myself. Yet I don't have to go hire, you know, an army of people and go rent legacy software, and try to grow a business out of it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这个「[[贴牌纯软件|贴牌纯软件]]」的定位翻转了谈判桌——大品牌不用雇一队人、租旧软件就能拥有自己的现代化储蓄产品。

## V1 的教训：框太死，八年后复活

V1 是给小微企业的「两三种口味」标准化 401(k) 盒子：预设投资阵容、内置受托服务，企业走完决策树点一堆按钮就完成注册。很快发现框得太死——客户总说「我不要这个选项，你能做这个吗」，于是这个工作流被下架了八年。

但去年他们从零重建了同样的工作流，现在是卖得最快的产品之一，尤其通过薪资合作伙伴做产品驱动增长。「想法上是对的，只是当年结构跑偏了」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:28" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="So it's kind of a funny story. So V1 was how do we create a, let's say, you get two or three flavors of a 401(k) solution for a micro or small business. So that would be some sort of box. It has a, you know, investment lineup that's already set up. It has all the fiduciary services and so on. And then, a business could walk and sign up, click a bunch of buttons. Think of it, I always think of it like a decision tree, right? You're just kind of like, okay, I want this option, I want this option, I want this option, I want this option. At the end of it, it's like, OK, great. Here's your document. Here's everything that's going to be submitted. There's a lot of other legal stuff that happens behind the scenes. But that was V1, right? So we went through that and people could sign up. Now, it's hard, right? You show up, it wasn't necessarily white labeled, right? When we started for everyone because we didn't really have anyone to white label for. We had a couple of clients, but they're like, who is this company? Who's his brand? Can I trust them, right? And this is a sensitive topic, right? You're asking someone for arguably the second most sensitive thing in their life outside of their family. You're saying, hey, give me your savings and let me take care of it for you. So, you know, you want to be a trusted, reliable source for that. So that was V1 that was funny. We quickly realized that we had boxed it too tight, right? That people didn't fit into those boxes all the time and they'd be like, oh, I don't really want that option. I want this option. Can you do this? And then, you start to open up the box and widen what you're doing. So we ended up sunsetting that kind of workflow. Because there were too many options that we wanted to offer. That we don't want to constrain people in that aspect. So we ended up not doing it because it got too messy. Now, fast forward last year, we rebuilt that same workflow from the ground up and it's one of our core things that flies off the shelf right now. So it was kind of like some of these early ideas that we were right with the idea. We were probably a little off with the exact structure of it. But those have kind of come full circle and now they're actually core to what we do day to day." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 生意为什么能滚雪球

这门生意有个独特属性：即使一单新业务都不进，它仍然自动增长——因为总有人在被支付、有缴款进来、有资产在累积，而定价里含基点成分(按资产规模的百分比收费)。Aaron 说「什么都不做」它也会长到几十亿美元量级的总 ARR,带 SaaS 级毛利率；他们只需要确保不流失，而流失率是行业最低 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:41" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="Yeah, I would say if we did nothing. Let's just say we sat here and we're now continuing to engage with folks and bring on new partners. This business grows into a couple billion of topline ARR, right? With SaaS level gross margins. So that's kind of the scale, like if you're pulling in now. It's not one of those businesses where you're doing a billion dollars of revenue a day, but you're bringing in several billion dollars a year of revenue and it will just continue to grow, right? It's one of those businesses that if the business stayed stagnant, we brought on no new business. It still grows because you still have people getting paid. You still have contributions coming in. You still have assets accumulating, right? And it just continues to ride up, even though we're. Because we do have some basis point component in most of our pricing. So there's an attribute to it, where the business grows just sitting here. So that's a unique thing where you're not just continuing to have to sell, sell, sell. You're like, just make sure the business doesn't churn. Our churn rate is industry low and we kind of reset the bar for how the industry operates." aria-label="回原文"></button>。对顾问的吸引力在于：财富顾问把 Vestwell 推给企业主客户后，能看到该企业所有员工的账户数据(可聚合外部账户)，等于一个获客引擎，从中筛出未来的高净值财富客户；而且职场储蓄计划比个人客户涨得快得多——一个从零涨过百万美元的计划，远快于带着十万的人涨到一百万 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:07" data-who="Aaron Schumm" data-en="So it depends on where they sit in the ecosystem. ## 本集带走

- **贴牌是切入大机构的杠杆**：别让客户在「租旧软件+雇几百人」和「摆竞品的货架方案」里二选一——做成它的自有品牌，零开销，谈判桌就翻了。
- **长期合同用「可达成的最低承诺」**：伙伴不用掏钱、只带量，你换来多年收入可见性，据此提前招人配资源。
- **企业级销售输标不是终点**：输后按季度做轻触点(说过的事做到了没有)，周期重复时单子会回头；并把安全审计、采购应答提前做掉。
- **警惕把产品框太死**：V1 标准化盒子被市场拒绝，八年后重建同款工作流反而成了卖得最快的 PLG 产品——想法可以等市场成熟。
- **招聘留降级空间**：高管从 VP 起步、给成长通道，不满再向上加层，避免被动降职谈判。
- **解雇的直觉测试**：当你开始向别人求证「该不该开」时，答案已经是该开。
