# Whatnot：直播电商如何让购物重新变有趣

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Grant LaFontaine</b> · Whatnot 的创始人 · 2026-08-19</div>

中国 30% 到 40% 的电商已经是直播电商，而美国还是个位数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:15" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Is that right? Yeah, I've heard anywhere around 30 to 40% of all commerce is live commerce in China. Yeah, and then what is it in the U.S. today?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。这个差距不是常态，而是起步时差的体现——因为直播本质上是在扩大需求，不只是把线下销售搬到线上更高效地做一遍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:13" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yes. And so if you look at e-commerce today, you have to know exactly what you're looking for. So all it's really done is take a bunch of offline sales and bring them online in a more efficient way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

[[Whatnot|Whatnot]] 的创始人 Grant 小学时在 eBay 上卖了第一张全息宝可梦卡，赚了 10 美元。买家邮寄汇票，他拿着汇票去邮局兑现后再寄卡——那时候互联网太初级，连在线支付都没有 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:03" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Okay, I want to start all the way back when you were a kid. So I understand that your first online sale was a holographic Pokemon card and that the buyer mailed you a money order and you cashed it at the post office and then you shipped the card." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这段经历后来和他做视频、做[[市场|市场]]的职业路径汇合，成了 Whatnot 的起点。

但 Whatnot 并不是一开始就瞄准直播电商的。2019 年底，Grant 和联合创始人 Logan 先做了认证的 Funko Pop 交易市场，每天跟客户聊。

然后他们[[发现|发现]]一个现象：客户自己在社交媒体上用直播视频卖东西，但体验很破碎——交易、物流、支付、发现、客服，各个环节都没整合好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:42" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We were building our Funko Pop marketplace and we were talking to our customers every single day. And then we saw our customers hacking social media in interesting ways and the way they were trying to hack it was selling items in live video." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们没去研究亚洲的直播电商趋势（当时也不知道），而是直接针对自己用户已经在做的事，做了一个更好的工具。Logan 把自己关在房间里几周，做出了第一个版本，Grant 上线卖了很多东西，就这样跑起来了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:08" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And we just thought we could build a better one of those and it would solve the problems of the customers we had in the platform. Logan locked himself in his room for a few weeks, came out with the first version of the product, and I went live, sold a lot of items, and the rest is sort of history from there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 用户不在乎「市场」，在乎体验

Grant 说了一句很反直觉的话：用户根本不在乎市场 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:51" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's incredible. Users don't give a shit about the market. They care about whether they're going to enjoy the thing that provides value." aria-label="回原文"></button>。消费者不会想「这是一个直播购物市场，我可以在里面交易各种东西」，他们想的是「你要给我什么价值」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:18" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Because... Again, like no one's, the consumer is not making a, going like, oh, this is a live shopping market. And, you know, I can transact all of these things in this market." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

如果你一门心思去构建一个「市场」，反而会分散对用户体验的注意力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:08" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah. And so there's this counterintuitive thing, which is if you try and build a market, it oftentimes distracts from, like, are you building a really great user experience?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们对市场不了解，反而成了优势——因为了解太多，大概率会去迭代已有模式，而不是做一个全新的东西 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:34" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So that was sort of like the state of the market. And by the way, I would just say, I think it's probably an asset, not a liability, that you guys didn't know much about the market." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 直播购物更像逛商场，不是搜索

传统电商要求你精确知道要买什么，这对「发现」很不友好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:31" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Thank you for joining us. You know, prior to us, you sort of know exactly what you want. It's not really great for discovery." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Whatnot 更像逛商场：你大概知道想要一类东西，但不确定具体是哪个，进去逛逛、看看、跟人聊，过程中获得乐趣 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:56" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah. Which is, I don't know, if I go to the mall or I go to a set of retail shops, I'm sort of like, I kind of know I want a thing, but I don't know exactly what that thing is." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

结果是用户每天在平台上花大约 95 分钟 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:48" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Is that right? Yeah, about 95 minutes. It's incredible." aria-label="回原文"></button>，跟社交娱乐平台一个量级。而且大多数人在任何给定的一天里根本不买东西——超过 80% 的人只是看和聊 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:45" data-who="Unknown" data-en="They're actually just going to have a good time and experience it. Most people don't, like, you know, greater than 80% of people don't purchase on a given day. They're just there watching, having fun." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这跟传统电商的指标逻辑完全不同。

## 供给侧：从收藏品到 100 多个品类

Whatnot 从收藏品起步，现在覆盖 100 多个品类 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:31" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So just talk about the category expansion evolution over time. Yeah, I mean, I guess if you look at what not today, we're selling things in 100 plus categories. And then we're always investing in new areas to help people grow their business." aria-label="回原文"></button>。女装已经是最大品类，模式类似线下的 TJ Maxx——过季库存、好价格 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Wow. And, you know, there's a huge range of stuff in there. I'd say that, you know, the dominant is actually sort of looks like..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。还有新鲜食品（从 San Diego 码头直采的鱼）、烧烤餐厅的成品、高尔夫用品（每年增长数倍）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:42" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And then we're always investing in new areas to help people grow their business. So some of the fun ones today are like fresh food and drink where you can buy fresh fish sourced from the dock in San Diego or" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

平台去年做了超过 80 亿美元的交易额，今年到目前为止已经超过 80 亿美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:24" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, you know, the biggest businesses on WhatNot are probably doing upwards of $100 million in revenue and, you know, with like very strong EBITDA margins, 20, 30, 40 plus percent." aria-label="回原文"></button>。最大的卖家年营收超过 1 亿美元，利润率（EBITDA）能达到 20% 到 40% 以上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:13" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So obviously you're empowering millions of small businesses, but if done right, a business owner can actually build something north of $100 million of revenue and highly profitable, all on the back of Whatnot." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Whatnot 只抽很小一部分，刻意不提费用——让卖家拿走大部分，平台跟着卖家一起长 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:35" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So, you know, very fast growth. And we don't, we take a small fraction of that. And we'd like to, we're not raising fees or doing that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Grant 讲了一个海鲜分销商的故事：那人在 LinkedIn 上联系他，第二周就上了 Whatnot 直播，试点阶段卖了 3000 到 4000 美元的鱼 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:30" data-who="Unknown" data-en="So one of my favorite stories is the recent one that you've told us about the seafood distributor who messaged you on LinkedIn. And first of all, what an inbound." aria-label="回原文"></button>。一个本地海鲜商，靠传统方式几乎不可能触达全国市场，但直播视频让买家能看到新鲜程度、问问题、了解打包方式，信任感完全不一样 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:04" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Buying food on the internet, like fresh fish, because it's one of those things you're like, well, fresh fish on the internet, how do I know if it's good, if I can get sick from it?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 信任与安全：40% 的员工做这件事

平台每天有数千万人使用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:17" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, I mean, you know, if you think about just how many concurrent users you have and how many transactions you process per day, Yeah, I always, the analog I like for trust and safety is, so, you know, tens of millions of people use WhatNot every day." aria-label="回原文"></button>，信任是生死线。Whatnot 的[[信任与安全|信任与安全]]团队占了全公司 40% 的员工 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:29" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And we invest a lot in it. So I think trust and safety in particular on Whatnot is about 40% of employees. Wow." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Grant 的类比是：这相当于在管理两个纽约市的警察和立法系统——那个规模的城市也不可能完美运转，但因为有视频留痕、卖家自己也要靠回头客做生意，实际出问题的比例远低于现实城市 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Yeah, yeah. And so, the trust and safety team is basically the police force and the legal system of WhatNot. You gotta write your policies, you gotta have your people who enforce your policies." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

后台有一个「[[规则引擎|规则引擎]]」，实时处理数十亿个数据点，监控发货及时性、退款率等指标，自动触发相应措施 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="And then, you know, the problems are pretty diverse and you have to make sure you're building the right set of things for it. So as an example, we have a system in the background." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 用来提效，不替代人

Whatnot 是一个本质上是人类连接的平台——你认识店主，认识常来的顾客，这种关系是乐趣和回头率的来源 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:43" data-who="Unknown" data-en="It's not good business. Because the core of whatnot is you visit a shop, You know the shop owner, and then you know the people who frequent that shop." aria-label="回原文"></button>。所以 AI 的定位不是替代人，而是帮卖家更高效地做生意：用 LLM 自动推断直播需要的元数据、提供经营分析告诉卖家哪里好哪里不好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:25" data-who="Unknown" data-en="We, you know, when you're setting up a live stream for the discovery systems, oftentimes you need to add a lot of metadata. We can infer all that metadata with LLMs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

虚拟形象？目前看不到需求，因为那不是人们来 Whatnot 的原因 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:09" data-who="Unknown" data-en="Look, at the end of the day, we'll put energy into what's going to be good for the buyer and what's going to be good for the seller. But today, we don't see a need for avatars because that's not why people come to Whatnot." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走
- **别研究市场，研究用户已经在做的事**：Whatnot 不是看了亚洲趋势才做直播，而是看到自己的用户已经在社交平台上笨拙地做直播卖货了。
- **直播电商扩大的是需求，不只是效率**：传统电商是把线下销售更高效地搬到线上，但直播让人发现原本不知道自己会感兴趣的东西，市场总量会变大。
- **80% 的人不买东西也正常**：95 分钟日均时长靠的是娱乐体验，不是购买转化率——这改变了平台的优化目标。
- **信任与安全是 40% 的人力投入**：在处理数千万日活的交易平台上，信任不是锦上添花，是基础设施，投入比例远超一般人的想象。
- **AI 增强人类卖家，不替代**：自动填元数据、给经营建议，但核心体验——认识店主、跟人聊天——保持人类主导。
