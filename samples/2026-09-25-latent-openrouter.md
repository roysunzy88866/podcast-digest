这一集聊的是 [[OpenRouter|OpenRouter]]——那个开发者用来一站调用各家大模型的「路由器」——从创立到被 [[Stripe|Stripe]] 收购的完整故事。两位主角：创始人 [[Alex Atallah|Alex Atallah]]([[OpenSea|OpenSea]] 联合创始人出身)，和他的老友、首位投资人 [[Anjney Midha|Anjney Midha]]([[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 最早的投资人之一，曾负责 [[Discord|Discord]] 平台，后加入 A16Z)。两人从斯坦福一份校报相识，一路见证了整个开放模型生态的崛起。

最反直觉的一点：当初几乎所有投资人都觉得这东西「说白了就是个[[套壳|套壳]]市场」，Anjney 却说，「[[缩放定律|规模定律]]」恰恰是 OpenRouter 会很值钱的原因，而不是缺陷 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:33" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="which is- Yes, this is why we're here. The scaling laws were never like, in my mind," aria-label="回原文"></button>。一个月后 Menlo Ventures 的 Matt Murphy 把估值上调了 10 倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:59" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="We're going to invest. And then I think a month later, Matt Murphy marked it up by 10x. I forget what the exact post money was and so on." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 起点：600 美元的 Alpaca 时刻

Alex 用他早期写下的「PubSub 产品原则」解释 OpenRouter 的形态：[[市场|市场平台]]本质是「发布-订阅」结构——供应商向 SKU 发布产品，消费者订阅；OpenRouter 就是把这套结构套在模型上，Model Slug 是可订阅的 SKU,再加上 auto router(自动帮你在模型间路由) <button class="pd-ts" data-t="00:49" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="And I wanted you to maybe explain how you think about what should exist in the world. Yeah, the PubSub piece, which was early 2023," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

顿悟来自 2023 年初的 Alpaca:斯坦福团队用合成数据[[微调|微调]] Llama,只花了 600 美元，就得到一个在很多情况下和 ChatGPT 分不出差别的模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:38" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="I was just on an airplane using it. In many cases, you could not discern a chat GPT versus an Alpaca result. And I figured if it was this easy to make a model," aria-label="回原文"></button>。Alex 的[[推理|推理]]是：如果做模型这么便宜，那「把有价值的数据压缩成一个模型」就成了全新的数据变现方式、全新的商业模式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:18" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="Compress it into a model. And so it's just a whole new business model for the economy. It also, of course, provides a way" aria-label="回原文"></button>。而模型一多，就需要一个市场来发现它们——当时互联网上没有任何 LLM 的「大本营」，连 Hugging Face 那时也没有闭源模型、也看不到谁在用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:20" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="of those products. There wasn't any place on the internet that like was like a home base for LLMs in terms of seeing how much they were being used and seeing who was using them" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Discord 的教训：你需要开放模型

Anjney 的顿悟来自 Discord。[[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 给了 Discord GPT-3.5 早期访问权限，他们想做两件事：内部助手 Clyde,和内容审核。

审核场景很具体——每个 Discord 服务器都有自己的社区规范，理想方案是把规范喂给 LLM,让它为每个服务器做「上下文内审核」，替代全球 5000 多人的外包审核团队 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:51" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="like observing each message in these communities. And these communities are like millions of users. So we had a 5,000 plus person team globally on the Discord content moderation team." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但模型动不动就拒绝：后训练的护栏太粗暴，一个哈利·波特粉丝社区的审核请求会直接被拒(因为涉及商标内容)，侦探小说里写暴力情节的一章也会被拒 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:34" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="And often the, um, The post-training prompts were super heavy-handed. It said, oh, anything about Harry Potter," aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他们找 OpenAI 要权重，得到的回复是「抱歉，我们是闭源公司」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:48" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="We need more reliability that the model will do what we need it to. And they said, well, sorry guys, that's not how this works. We're a closed source company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。「那是我第一次意识到我们需要开放模型，企业需要对能力有更多控制，最终需要某种控制平面来编排这些开放模型」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:52" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="We're a closed source company. And so that was my first realization that we needed open models and the enterprises would need more control over capabilities and" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 「大模型赢者通吃」和「就是个套壳」

融资时最大的质疑是「大模型赢者通吃」：像 Google 赢搜索那样，价值全归一家公司，路由器只能捡残羹 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:55" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="you can obviously talk about some of the early day stuff. Well, I was going to say that the biggest objection we got is big model win, which is all" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Alex 的反驳：LLM 不只是用户界面，更是构建全新业务的地基——如果真出现 Google 级的垄断，整个经济都依赖它，结局会疯狂到离谱；而且创造优秀竞争者的经济学更容易去中心化 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:53" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="But LLMs don't merely have a user interface. They're also ways of building entirely new businesses. You know," aria-label="回原文"></button>。Anjney 从投资人角度补刀：他是 Anthropic 最早的投资人之一，「苦涩的教训」(算力扩展总能赢)在 ML 朋友圈里是共识，所以他投了 [[Mistral|Mistral]]、Black Forest Labs、Luma——一个多模型生态正在形成 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:27" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="Everything Alex said is true. And I came at it from a completely different perspective, which is- Yes, this is why we're here." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更让他愤怒的是「就是个套壳」的说法：「这是最愚蠢的、过度简化的框架，显然出自一个没有任何一线部署产品经验的人」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:24" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="And if you're like, oh, an app is just a rapper on no model, then like... And OpenRouter is like this wrapper on top of other APIs. This is the most stupid reductive framework." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还顺嘴补了一句：「投资人不就是 LP 的套壳吗？全都是套壳，一路套到裸金属」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="26:40" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="There's some point, some wrappers have value. I mean, investors are wrappers on LPs, right? I mean, yeah, it's all wrappers all down to bare metal, I guess." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 实验室的盲区：训练完然后呢？

Anjney 看到的共同痛点：各实验室的研究团队极擅长造能力，却毫无开发者思维——checkpoint(训练完成的模型存档)做完、发个 API,然后鸦雀无声。「以 Claude 为例，第一个 Claude checkpoint 实际上比对外发布早了一年完成，ChatGPT 出来后他们才决定发布，而且没有任何让开发者尝试它的计划」——Claude 1 博客里的三个示例，一个是 Discord 机器人，一个是他妻子的公司，一个是 Notion,「全因为都是朋友，规划就是这么临时」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:20" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="the checkpoint is done, put it out as an API, done, and then there'd be crickets. In the case of Claude, the first Claude checkpoint was actually done a year" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而 Google 训完新模型，一夜之间就能推到十亿台 Android 设备上；这种看不见的分发优势，在 OpenRouter 出现前，每家实验室都得自己扛 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:02" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="Everywhere, even if I don't. Everywhere. No, but like on Android," aria-label="回原文"></button>。Anthropic 花了超过 12 个月才做到第一笔 1000 万美元收入；相比之下，BFL 找上门时，OpenRouter 一句「你上线那天，我们可以给你带去一百万开发者」——Anjney 说那是阶跃函数式的力量变化 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:24" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="At Anthropic, I think it took more than 12 months to get to our first 10 million in revenue. And in contrast with Blackforce Labs, I remember the early days, you guys had a conversation with the BFL team." aria-label="回原文"></button>。如今 OpenRouter 开发者超过 1000 万(已做大量去重)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:53" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="How many developers are on OpenRodder today? Over 10 million, but it's hard to... We do a lot of account deduping work, but no one..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Alex 补充另一面：模型是黑盒，不能像普通产品那样把功能罗列在网页上，需要有人照亮「大黑房间」的每个角落，而且必须是中立第三方——这正是市场平台在做模型营销时和实验室自卖自夸的本质区别 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:24" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="Models are not products where you can just enumerate all their features onto a webpage. They're all black boxes, So you need to like shine lights on all corners of this room" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 冷启动飞轮：加密是生成式 AI 的彩排

怎么冷启动？Alex 的方法论来自 OpenSea 时代：找到被某个具体问题卡死的社区，做他们真正想要的东西。

「用户主动分享链接，是非常明确的信号，说明有重要的事正在发生」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:42" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="It's constantly in that Discord. Like users sharing links is a really clear indicator that like something important is going on. So we spent," aria-label="回原文"></button>。LLM 早期那个问题就是 OpenAI 拒绝续写。

Anjney 则给了一个被低估的元观点：他们那批人幸运地与加密社区相邻，而「加密最终有点像是生成式模型的一次彩排」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:26" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="in those days because in hindsight, crypto ended up being kind of like a dress rehearsal for generative models. If you think about the Axie experience, Alex is totally right." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Discord 时代的 Axie Infinity 服务器(菲律宾约 10% 人口在上面，交易量几十亿美元)逼出了扩展基础设施；之后这些抽象恰好接住了 [[MidJourney|MidJourney]]——他很早就和 MidJourney 创始人 David 一起吃火锅玩万智牌，看着这个文生图机器人长成 Discord 增长最快的社区之一。

单机版网页留存极差，因为人对着空白输入框会不知所措；而 Discord 里能看到别人怎么用、照着抄，「MidJourney 从发布到 1 亿美元收入运行率，不到八个月」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:01" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="because I think when MidJourney launched, from launch to 100 million revenue run rate, it was less than eight months. And shortly thereafter, Stable Diffusion launched." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Claude 上线前是 Discord 内部机器人，Eleven Labs 的 TTS 也在——「Discord 成了早期应用创新的培养皿」，而 Alex 最早意识到这些应用需要互联网上的「自己的家」，OpenRouter 是这个社区需求的延续 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:28" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="Eleven Labs had a TTS model that we had on Discord as well. Discord became this petri dish for early apps to innovate. And I don't think it's a coincidence that they found a home there" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

为什么 OpenRouter 本身不以 Discord 为中心？Alex:MidJourney 的 Discord 就是用户体验本身；LLM 需要代码级集成、密钥治理、数据策略，一个 Discord 服务器装不下 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:48" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="getting your images and then sharing them and having fun. For OpenRouter, for LLMs, like you need a lot of user experience around LLMs. Make them, like, really usable." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Alex 甚至在 Discord/OpenSea 合作会议上，是唯一举手说「把用户弹出跳转不是好体验，应该内嵌 iframe 直接结账」的人——他还是利益受损的一方。Anjney:同时懂 ML 社区、开发者体验和终端用户体验的人非常罕见，「这就是为什么没人应该对 Stripe 决定收购 OpenRouter 感到惊讶」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:42" data-who="Anjney Midha" data-en="Yeah, you need an Alex. And this is why nobody should be surprised why Stripe decided they had to buy OpenRouter because it's a really rare combination of people who understand" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 增长里程碑：一部 AI 演进的电影

OpenRouter 的排行榜和增长曲线，基本就是这几年 AI 领域的编年史 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="68:26" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="But developers have lots of varied preferences for them. And then we saw the way our leaderboard has changed over time is kind of like a movie of how the AI space has changed over time." aria-label="回原文"></button>。主持人说，成年的标志是 Andre Karpathy 公开放话「我不再看 Local Llama 了，直接看 OpenRouter 排行榜」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="68:51" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="To me, the coming of age moment was Andre Karpathy was like, I no longer read Local Llama because I just go to Open Router's leaderboard. Which I remember." aria-label="回原文"></button>。关键节点：

- 2024 年 5 月前，大家主要在玩 prose 类应用，编码还不行；**Claude Sonnet 3.5** 中间发布，编码能力巨大飞跃，用量激增，用户开始盯着账单喊「我需要更便宜的等效模型」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="64:30" data-who="Alex Atallah" data-en="The creator of Dream Tavern now runs product at Cognition, Devon. Then in the middle of 2024, we saw Claude Sonnet 3.5. That came out," aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- 随后 **Mixtral** 发布，价格降了约 80%,[[开放权重|开放权重模型]]第一次被严肃地称为「世界最好模型」(有炒作成分，确实有很多例子超过 GPT-4)<button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:40" data-who="SPEAKER_00 / 44:17 Alex Atallah" data-en="Okay, and I just want to cap off the misdrawal side. ## 本集带走

- **「套壳」是最偷懒的判断**：模型是黑盒，中立第三方做的发现、路由、信任和分发，是实验室自己给不了的价值——判断一层是否有价值，看它解决什么一线问题，别用名词否定它。
- **实验室的共同盲区是「训练完之后」**：几亿美元训练完，缺的往往是密钥管理、版本控制、开发者分发这些被研究者视为「管道工程」的东西；分发优势(Google 一夜推十亿台设备)是隐形的胜负手。
- **启动飞轮的笨办法最好用**：找到被一个具体问题卡死的社区(早期就是模型乱拒绝)，做他们真正想要的东西；用户自发分享链接是最可靠的信号。
- **专注 + 明确的最高期望客户**：OpenRouter 服务开发者、Anthropic 从第一天只押编程、Arena 只服务实验室评估——「为多类客户同时超越期望」几乎不可能。
- **token 是新的价值单位，fraud 是它的影子**：凡是大量价值流过的地方就需要新的安全基础设施(支付→Stripe Radar,推理→代币经济的新警长)；而且下一波攻击者是 AI 智能体，不是人。
- **时机错了的正确想法值得删除重来**：MOM 融合模型 2024 年太早就删光代码，2026 年模型差距缩小后重建为 Fusion——「魔鬼在细节里，技术可能就是太早了」。
