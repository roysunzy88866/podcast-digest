# Gusto CEO Tomer：小企业的「伙伴」，不只是工具

[[Gusto|Gusto]] 是一家服务美国[[小企业|小企业]]的薪资与 HR 平台，做了大约 15 年，收入超 10 亿美元、客户超 50 万。来聊的人是它的联合创始人 Tomer，他爸在以色列开了一辈子服装店，他自己对此的感受是：小企业主没有部门、没有职业阶梯，只有一个人扛起获客、定价、库存、税务所有事——这塑造了 Gusto 的产品观。

## 不变的秘方：对客户偏执

Tomer 说，很多都变了，但一样东西没变：对小企业的同理心。「聚焦客户」听起来像废话，但公司一大就会被稀释——发布、定价、营销天天占据你的心智，花在客户身上的时间比例越缩越小。他的比喻是：摆柠檬水摊时你整天和客户聊天；10 年后你有了 5000 名员工，时间全花在运营、财务、人事上，客户就忘了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:17" data-who="Tomer" data-en="It goes smaller and smaller and smaller over time. It's kind of like, you know, think about it as a small business, right? Like if you're like, let's say, you know, you're selling, I don't know, like you're selling lemonade in the street, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Gusto 用几个硬机制对抗这种漂移：产品会上问「过去一周和客户聊过天的请举手」，没举手的人自己会有压力；每两周一次的全员大会都以一段客户对话开场，聊的不只是产品使用，还有他们的生活、担忧和对经济的感受 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:30" data-who="Tomer" data-en="We start every, so we have an all-hands meeting every two weeks. Every all-hands meeting starts with a conversation with a customer, whether it's a live conversation or like a recorded video talking with us about their business." aria-label="回原文"></button>。产品经理必须是离客户最近的拥护者，不只是转述 10 条调研引语，而是拍视频、把客户请进办公室和工程师面对面。招人也按这个筛：要「传教士」不要「雇佣兵」，发自内心关心小企业的人。

## Gusto Co-Founder：把软件变成一个可以发短信的人

[[AI|AI]] 时代 Gusto 的第一个大动作是 [[联合创始人|Co-Founder]]：一个 5 人团队 8 周做出的新界面——不用再登录 gusto.com 层层点导航，直接在 iMessage、Slack、WhatsApp 上给 Gusto 发短信就能改工资、跑奖金 payroll、批 PTO <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:19" data-who="Tomer" data-en="So CoFunder basically is this idea like, hey, what if instead of logging into gusto.com, you never needed to log in and you could just text Gusto and run your whole..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。关键是「主动」：它早上查看你的账户，发现 7 个 PTO 请求里 5 个很正常就直接替你批了，另外 2 个来问你；发现你招了不少工程师却没申报 R&D 税收抵免，会告诉你这能省 1.5 万美元并问要不要帮你办 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:58" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Looks over your account and asks itself, okay, what can I do to help, really? So it could be things like, hey, you have seven PTO requests that you need to approve before this payroll." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 数据：AI 在帮小企业，而且大企业反而吃亏

Gusto 内部有一块经济仪表盘，看招聘、工资、现金流，用有代表性的客户样本推算整体经济。7 月全美就业数据减少 2.3 万个岗位，Gusto 的数据里小企业却净增了 3.8 万个，跨地区、跨行业 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:50" data-who="Tomer" data-en="But, you know, when we looked at our picture, again, small businesses, the picture was really, really different. There was actually net growth of 38,000 net jobs in July." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Tomer 的判断是：用 AI 的小企业雇人更多、增长更快、更有韧性，因为大公司部署 AI 复杂得多，小企业几美元就能试用一个 AI 工具，而雇一个人哪怕低薪也是至少 3 万到 5 万美元的承诺 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:34" data-who="Tomer" data-en="You're responsible for livelihood for your team, even if it's a team of one or two. Yeah. And so if you hire someone, even on the low end, because there's a lot of taxes and everything, you're probably committing $30,000, $50,000 at a minimum of cash outweigh for the business." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他说 AI 提升的是小企业的「生存能力」，而且不分行业 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="19:38" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Using AI, whether it's, again, it's like making marketing more efficient, so you get more customers, whether it's service, to make a service more efficient, whether it's upsell, additional products you want to sell to your customers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 疫情：扔掉全年计划，NPS 冲到 88

COVID 初期，Gusto 的仪表盘里解雇率跳到历史未见水平，新招聘率跌到谷底，比最差时期还糟 5 到 8 倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:03" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Really? The rate of new hires dropped to all times low by far, like kind of like, I don't know, it was five or eight times, you know, worse than the worst times." aria-label="回原文"></button>。当时《华尔街日报》头版标题是「小企业的终结」，Tomer 说那是公司最疯狂的时刻之一。

Gusto 的反应是：刚做完的全年规划整个扔进垃圾桶，问「客户现在需要什么」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:37" data-who="Tomer" data-en="And then COVID happened and we looked at this and we looked at the world and we just threw the whole thing. We just threw it out into the trash can and we asked ourselves, okay, what do customers need right now?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。客户需要的是一本不存在的「疫情手册」，于是 Gusto 来写：把各州每隔一天冒出来的新项目整理成指南，甚至改掉产品左侧导航栏，第一项从「运行薪资」换成「COVID-19 应对」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:00" data-who="Tomer" data-en="We changed our left nav and the first item was not run payroll anymore. It was actually COVID-19 and what to do with it. So you click on it and then there was all these programs ready for you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

PPP 贷款方面，他们和银行深度合作，客户点一个按钮、信息全部预填，快速拿到钱。那段时间 [[NPS|NPS]] 跳到 88、89 并长期停留——而薪资 HR 行业 NPS 为正就已领先，Gusto 平时以 70 为目标。推荐者留言的大意是：我本来只当你是发工资的，你却帮我让生意撑过了疫情。

## SVB 倒闭与「冗余就是这个系统的名字」

[[SVB|SVB]] 倒闭那周正是发薪周。Gusto 自己支付处理一直用多家银行，所以没受影响，但不少客户的钱取不出来。

Gusto 提前识别出这些公司，主动打电话：放心发工资，我们替你垫，下周从你账户扣回 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:53" data-who="Tomer" data-en="They're just not accessible right now because of the whole SBB collapse situation. So we ended up extending credit for them. Basically, we paid payroll for them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Tomer 亲自花了几天逐个给客户打电话。

他由此总结金融级产品的铁律：给狗做社交网络宕机两天，主人一周后就忘了；但你在挪真金白银、算别人的税，冗余就是系统的名字、稳定就是这个游戏的名字 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:52" data-who="Tomer" data-en="The owners are gonna be pissed for a little bit, but they're gonna forget a week later. Yeah. But if you're running financial services, if you're moving real money, if you calculate people's taxes, you know," aria-label="回原文"></button>。更进一步，内部系统的标准应该高到「能独立成产品」——后来真做出了 [[Gusto Embedded|Gusto Embedded]]：把支付处理、税务处理作为嵌入式薪资产品卖给 JPMorgan Chase、US Bank、Xero 等大平台。

## 为什么发薪这事这么难

Payroll 不是打钱那么简单：年底要出 W-2、要代扣税、要按时向各政府机构付款申报。填错一个季报数字，可能一年半后员工报税时才被发现，而那员工可能已经离职搬去了别处，雇主还得负责找到他、寄 W2C 更正表、让他重新报税 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:20" data-who="Tomer" data-en="So the government comes back to you and say, hey, You made this mistake, and you need to go fix it, and here's the employee that's impacted, and you need to go and find that employee and tell them, hey, they're now moved, they're in a different part of the country, and you're responsible to make sure that they know that they need to fix, here's your new W2, the W2C, the correction for your W2, and you need to refile your taxes with it right now." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

美国体系高度碎片化：有些州按县征税，有些按学区，同一条尽端路的三栋相邻房子可能适用三套税则——Tomer 说「我得知道你住街道哪一边，才能正确算你的税」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:34" data-who="Tomer" data-en="And so those all might work at the same or different companies that then need to figure that stuff out, right, when they're paying. Exactly. So I need to know which side of the street you are on in order to know how to calculate your taxes correctly." aria-label="回原文"></button>。有些管辖区小到只有一个办事员，新政就贴公告板、登本地报。所以 Gusto 除了软件还有运营：干了几十年的员工知道该给那位女士打电话，先聊她孙子孙女，再问最近税则有没有变 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:40" data-who="Tomer" data-en="And like, these are, for example, people who've been in the industry for decades and they know to call that lady, they know their phone number. To ask her how her kids and grandkids are doing, and then ask her, did anything change recently that we should know about as a payroll processor?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

15 年前 Gusto 创立（当时叫 [[Zen Payroll|Zen Payroll]]）的契机，正是联合创始人 Josh 在老式发薪公司那里被要求拎公文包的西装销售约在咖啡店见面、填几小时表格才能给自己发工资——销售还给他买了一杯热可可，因为 Josh 不喝咖啡 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:34" data-who="Tomer" data-en="And for you to go actually pay yourself, you actually need to meet a salesperson in person in a random coffee shop in your area, in your neighborhood. It's like a dude in a suit with like a briefcase." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 增长：把小企业主当「像消费者的专业买家」

小企业软件难做，Tomer 的解释是它卡在中间：客单价撑不起企业销售团队，量又不够大到靠广告牌营销，大公司都尽快脱离这个市场去追企业客户。Gusto 的解法是看清一个本质：小企业主是单一决策者（像消费者），但决策是专业的、有商业目标的（像企业）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="65:25" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Yeah, so the thing that I think we realized is to think about the small business owner as a single decision maker. So that's like more like feels like consumer. But on the other side is professional." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

所以整个游戏就是[[口碑|口碑]]——你需要一个值得谈论、被人喜爱的产品。他们从一开始就拒绝「最小可行产品」（[[MVP|MVP]]，先发布能用的版本再说），坚持「最小可爱产品」：能用、直观只是 MVP 的门槛，还得加上「令人愉悦」——产品能预判你脑子里的问题，而不是让你去某个深层菜单里找福利对比工具 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="70:03" data-who="Tomer" data-en="And I think for that, these two things already kind of clear the bar for a minimal viable product for the MVP, right? But to really get to the minimal lovable product, that's kind of where you add the last piece of the puzzle, which I would call just delightful." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 把开发速度翻倍，还会再翻

Gusto 现在每个 PM、工程师、设计师都能直接问 [[Claude|Claude]] 关于匿名化客户数据的问题——NPS 评论、客服记录、销售笔记，原本要几周甚至几个月的用户研究现在几分钟起步 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="72:05" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Now every PM at Gusto, every engineer, every designer can just go to Claude and ask our data questions about customer behavior, customer questions, everything anonymized, of course, but you can ask- So this is like internal, like amplitude type of dashboard data, or this is like usage data inside Gusto?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。执行侧更是如此：他们过去一年发 4 到 6 个大功能，现在半年就做完同样数量，正走向每季度一次、最终每月一次，Tomer 认为还能再翻一番 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="74:17" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Yeah. We used to launch, call it like four to six major feature announcements a year. We now easily do the same amount in, call it like six months, like twice a year, and we're well on our way to do it once a quarter and I think once a month." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他还引用了 YC 的 Gary Tan 的说法：如果有时候值得「煮沸海洋」、同时做多个产品、提高野心，那就是现在，因为 AI 让造新东西容易得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="73:28" data-who="Tomer" data-en="Like it needs to be really, really focused and actually really agreed with it for the longest time. But his argument, which I think is a really good one, which is like, if there was a time to boil the ocean, if there was a time to actually go and try to build multiple products at the same time and increase your ambition, this is the time to do it because AI does make it much easier to build new things." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 收购 Guideline：只看「加速价值」

Gusto 收购了美国领先 401k 提供商之一的 [[Guideline|Guideline]]，标准只有一个：这家公司对 Gusto 路线图的加速价值是多少——是两年才能做成的事现在一年能成？客户群、进入市场、人才各自加速多少？<button class="pd-ts" data-t="78:20" data-who="Tomer" data-en="So in other words, like looking at our roadmap, where does whatever that company has, where does that sit in our roadmap and what would it mean for us if we make it, you know, bringing it now?" aria-label="回原文"></button>

。他透露了一个反复出现的有趣困境：算出某标的对 Gusto 值 5000 万美元，结果它自己营收就有 5000 万，市场给它乘倍数后远超这个数——「其实我完全不在乎他们那 5000 万营收，我买他们不是为了营收，是为了团队和产品」<button class="pd-ts" data-t="81:56" data-who="Tomer" data-en="We are excited about the product they build and the code base and the team and we want to bring that into Gusto and we're quickly using AI, make it available to all of our customers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。对创始人的隐含建议是：想在下一轮融资前被收购，对话要趁早开始——收入涨得太快，你反而不再是潜在标的。

## 本集带走

  //    pathname → spaNavigated 永远置不了 true。window 上用「未设置才记」守卫,只在**第一次**记真·落地路径,
  //    重执行/换页都存活;__pdSpa 一旦置 true 就 sticky。referrer 用整 origin 比对(new URL),防

<script>
(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
  function all(){ document.querySelectorAll('.pd-play').forEach(wire); }
  document.addEventListener('nav', all);
  if(document.readyState==='loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', all); else all();
})();
</script>
