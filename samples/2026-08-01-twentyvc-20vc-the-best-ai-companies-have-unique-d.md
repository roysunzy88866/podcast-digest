# Simile：用模拟人帮大公司避开五亿美元级失误

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Joon Sung Park</b> · Simile 创始人 · 2026-08-24</div>

两三年后，运行一次[[模拟|模拟]]会话可能花费一两千万美元，但客户愿意为此支付一亿美元——因为那次模拟帮他们避开了一个可能损失五亿美元的决策 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:20" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="In my vision, I think there's a world in which in about two, three years, we're running a single simulation session that's going to take $10, $20 million to run." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这就是 [[Simile|Simile]] 在做的事：不是预测未来，而是让你在投入真实世界之前，先在模拟环境里试错。

## 从小镇实验到人类行为基础模型

[[Joon Sung Park|Joon Sung Park]] 最早因"Smallville"项目出名：他们用 GPT-3.5 驱动 25 个 NPC（非玩家角色），让这些角色在一个虚拟小镇里自主醒来、规划日程、建立关系。模拟设定在情人节前一天，结果这些[[智能体|智能体]]自发组织了聚会、装饰咖啡馆——完全是涌现行为，没人写脚本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:36" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="And that was creating an entire lived experience of a town. So the idea here was we would make a game town, and we would populate it with 25 NPCs, so non-playable characters, except these characters would actually wake up in the morning, do their routines, go to work, have relationships, and do all that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这个实验贡献了两样东西：一是当时最早的智能体（agent，能自主行动的 AI 程序）范例之一；二是首次把"[[记忆|记忆]]、规划、[[反思|反思]]"明确作为智能体架构的组成部分 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:07" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="We thought that was really interesting. Now, two fundamental contributions from that work. One was it was one of the earliest examples of creating agents." aria-label="回原文"></button>。记忆的解法最初很朴素——把所有信息存成 markdown 文本文件。

但问题是体验量太大，上下文窗口装不下。所以他们加了"反思"机制：每隔一段时间，让智能体回顾自己的记忆碎片，提炼出更高层的认知——比如"我为什么这周吃了五次蛋卷？

我可能在忙" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:49" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="You want to make sense of that aside from, oh, I went to get omelet five times throughout the week or something like that. So we had this concept of reflection, which basically was every certain interval, it's like a shower thought, you ask agent explicitly to get bunch of their memory pieces and basically make sense of them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这样智能体逐渐形成人格和世界观，而不只是一堆行为日志。

Simile 现在做的事，是这个思路的规模化：构建[[人类行为基础模型|人类行为基础模型]]，用来模拟个人、亚群体，最终模拟整个生态系统甚至市场 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:05" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="When we think about then building a simulation model company, would you say Simile is a simulation model company? We are a company that is creating foundation model of human behavior that can then be used to create simulations of individuals, simulation of subpopulations, and down the line, the simulation of the entire ecosystem and even the market." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 不造更聪明的机器，造"跟你一样会犯错"的模型

Simile 和 OpenAI、Anthropic 这类前沿模型公司走的路完全相反。那些公司要造超级理性、擅长编码和数学的机器。

Simile 要的是：如果一个人在某个情境下犯了错，模型也应该犯同样的错；人有什么偏见，模型就有什么偏见 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:29" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="So this is a great question. So the way we see it is if you look at large-language model companies today, fundamentally the task they have at hand is to create super rational, intelligent machines that are good at coding, that are good at natural sciences and mathematics." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们要的是人类大脑里"主观的那一半"——价值观、偏好、品味 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:55" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="We want our models to be biased in the same way humans are. In a way, we want to be a representation of people's values, preferences, and taste, sort of their subjective half of their brain." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

数据策略也完全不同。大模型主要训练在"人们说了什么"的网页数据上，但人们说的和做的之间有巨大鸿沟。

Simile 收集行为数据、交易数据、观察数据，但 Joon Sung Park 认为观察数据只适合做相关性预测 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:08" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="A lot of what people say is different to a lot of what people do. How do you think about the chasm of what people say and what people do and how that impacts your models?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。他更看重的是[[因果机制|因果机制]]——[[随机对照试验|随机对照试验]]和 A-B 测试数据：让人做了 A 和做了 B 之后行为分别怎么变，拿这些数据来训练模型推理[[反事实|反事实]]（"如果当初做了另一个选择会怎样"）<button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:30" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="You need a model that can actually reason about causal mechanisms and counterfactuals. So the kind of data that we care deeply about is a lot of randomized control trials." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 预测没人真正关心，企业要的是"怎么改"

一个反直觉的论点：没有人真正关心预测。知道星冰乐销量两个季度后要暴跌，企业不会觉得满足，他们会问"那我们该怎么办？

" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:03" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="No one really cares about what's going to happen in the future unless you're trying to predict the stock market. What people actually care about is they want to shape the future." aria-label="回原文"></button>。他们要的不是预言，而是因果推理——现在做什么能改变那个未来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:23" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="What do we need to do now to change the future? And there, what you really need is causal mechanism. You need a model that can actually reason about causal mechanisms and counterfactuals." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这使得 Simile 的价值主张跟传统市场调研（比如 Qualtrics）有本质区别。调查和访谈只是工具层面的创新，模拟要解决的是：能不能创建一个足够通用的人类模型，大规模代表人的观点，甚至让很多人在模拟里互相交互，看决策的全部下游影响 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:34" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="How can we create a better interview tool? Simulation is fundamentally about something different, which is how can you create the most generalizable model of people so that we can represent people's viewpoints at scale?" aria-label="回原文"></button>？

## 数据飞轮：世界本身就是验证场

模拟的反馈机制比看起来更好。编程智能体有清晰的奖励函数——用户点"接受"或"拒绝"，马上知道对错。

模拟预测的是未来，验证似乎很难。但 Joon Sung Park 说，世界本身就是基本事实：每天生成数万个假设，每个假设映射到一个可验证的结论，然后观察世界，看哪些成真了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:17" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="It is true. At the same time, I actually think simulation has even better mechanism, which is the world is our ground truth. We live in the ground truth world." aria-label="回原文"></button>。一个月生成 100 万个假设，统计有多少成真——这就是最好的学习方式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:45" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="And we're basically watching the world every day, seeing which of those hypotheses are answerable at what time. And we can basically say, a month goes by, we generated a million hypotheses, x percentage of them came true." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他们在验证上也交出了硬数据：花了一年时间证明模型能以 85% 的准确率预测人的行为和态度，与人们自我复制的行为相当 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:55" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="So we spent about a year actually demonstrating that we can create models of people that are actually amazing and validated at predicting people's behaviors across surveys, behavior experiments, real environment." aria-label="回原文"></button>。这项工作在 2024 年底发布，开启了"[[合成小组模拟|合成小组模拟]]"这个领域 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:01" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="And we show that we can actually predict people's behaviors and attitudes 85% as accurately as people replicate their own. We put that work out at the end of 2024." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 大企业三个月就买单

Joon Sung Park 本来以为市场需要一到两年才会热起来，计划 2026 年底才激进推进。结果客户行动极快，世界上一些最大的企业在三个月内就完成了交易 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:42" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="That when they realized that there is or there could be an answer in this market for addressing some of those pains of very slow experimentation, budget, and so forth, they were ready to drop everything and try us out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。原因是他们在日常工作中感受到的痛太真实了——实验太慢、预算不够，大量决策靠直觉，而直觉往往被自己狭隘的经验扭曲 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:26" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="But what I've also found was the pain they were feeling in their day-to-day work was so real. It was way more acute than I could have imagined. That when they realized that there is or there could be an answer in this market for addressing some of those pains of very slow experimentation, budget, and so forth, they were ready to drop everything and try us out." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

获取第一批客户的一个关键场景：企业在第一次通话中，把大型咨询公司花三到六个月做的研究结论拿来，在 Simile 系统里重新跑，两分钟就出结果 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:22" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="This is an amazing way to gain evidence that is actually quite accurate. And one of the ways we actually got some of our first customers was in the first call. They actually had a finding from large consulting companies" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 模拟的伦理边界

模拟是科幻小说里和 AGI 并列的两大技术支柱，滥用潜力是真实的。Joon Sung Park 把 Simile 的北极星定义为"大规模代表性"——很多人的观点在做关他们利益的决策房间里根本不被考虑，模拟是让决策者真正大规模听到人的声音的方式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="17:29" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="And like with any powerful technology, the misuse, the potential for misuse is quite real. And the way we see it, simulation at its best ought to be representation at scale." aria-label="回原文"></button>。至于跟政治选举、民主制度相关的模拟，原则很重要，但他没有展开具体会跟谁合作、不跟谁合作的边界 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:53" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="These are the kind of questions that simulation ultimately can answer. Thinking about democracies failing, elections for a government, this would be an incredibly useful tool." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 模拟是智能的 GPU

Joon Sung Park 把现在的大语言模型比作"智能的 CPU"——一个很大的单体，很聪明，做复杂推理。模拟要做的是"智能的 GPU"——单个模型不需要特别聪明，跟人一样就行，甚至要一样会犯错；但当大量这样的模型聚集在一起，就能产生涌现现象，像人类社会一样做出惊人的集体成果 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="51:37" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="What I see today that's prominent in AI space is what I consider to be the CPU of intelligence unit. You have this one language model that's really large, that's very smart, that can do very complex reasoning tasks." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他认为未来每个人都可以有一个可复制的"数字孪生"在模拟世界行动，而社会可以基于这种细粒度的人的表征，设计全新的政策形式和组织形式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:37" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="I think it's going to be quite inspiring. What's the crazy prediction then that every single person will have a replicable twin that acts and behaves like them in a simulated world?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **别只预测，要因果推理**：企业不缺"未来会怎样"的预言，缺的是"现在做什么能改变那个未来"。Simile 的核心竞争力在反事实推理，不在预测准确率。
- **数据策略要有护城河**：Joon Sung Park 认为这代 AI 公司必须有一个独特的、可防御的数据策略。Simile 的不是网页文本，而是行为数据加随机对照试验数据 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:03" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="Data is an important piece of simile for sure. My fundamental thesis here is for AI companies of this generation, you need to have an interesting data strategy that's going to be defensible." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **"反思"机制让智能体有人格**：不是堆记忆，而是定期让智能体回顾记忆碎片、提炼高层认知——"我为什么总做这件事？"——这样才能形成稳定的观点和性格 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:49" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="You want to make sense of that aside from, oh, I went to get omelet five times throughout the week or something like that. So we had this concept of reflection, which basically was every certain interval, it's like a shower thought, you ask agent explicitly to get bunch of their memory pieces and basically make sense of them." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **世界本身就是验证场**：模拟的反馈循环不是封闭的，每天生成的假设可以在真实世界中被检验，形成[[数据飞轮|数据飞轮]] <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:17" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="It is true. At the same time, I actually think simulation has even better mechanism, which is the world is our ground truth. We live in the ground truth world." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **找研究者创业，看他们要"问题"还是要"影响力"**：迷恋问题本身的人可能做出有趣的科学项目，但成不了好公司；被影响力驱动的人才会去找到能触达用户、能产生收入的问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="41:33" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="If you were an investor analyzing a group coming out of academia, what would you look for that would give you confidence that they would be able to make the leap from research to starting a company?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **找"矛盾超能力"的人**：最稀缺的人才是拥有两种本不该共存的能力——比如既极其数据严谨又极具创意想象力的 CMO <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:30" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="Do my leaders and my team have two superpowers that's not supposed to coexist in one person? Any expert will usually come in with one superpower or even sometimes multiple superpowers, but they're all correlated." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
- **短期偏执 + 长期笃信**：最好的团队成员在日常执行中极度焦虑紧迫，但骨子里相信世界最终会站在自己这边。同时保持这两种状态很难，但正是这种张力驱动了成功 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:37" data-who="Joon Sung Park" data-en="But in my team, the kind of things that I also see as an archetype is, and I also categorize myself as one of these kind of people, On a day-to-day basis, for instance, Lainey, one of my co-founders, she's paranoid." aria-label="回原文"></button>。
