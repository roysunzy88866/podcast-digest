# AI 时代 Staff 工程师的核心挑战：判断力怎么教

<div class="pd-byl"><b>Malika Rao</b> · 2026-08-26</div>

<div class="pd-play"><button class="pb" type="button" aria-label="播放">▶</button><span class="tt"><span class="t1">听中文精华</span><span class="t2">AI 合成朗读</span></span><span class="bar"><i></i></span><span class="tm">00:00</span><audio preload="metadata" src="/audio/2026-08-26-beyondcoding-how-new-staff-engineers-build-judgment-w.mp3">你的浏览器不支持音频播放,或音频尚未生成。</audio></div>

实现正变得丰富，但[[判断力|判断力]]并非如此——这是 AI 给软件工程师层级带来的最大错位。初级工程师现在能用[[智能体|智能体]]大量产出代码，但他们缺乏的是判断力；而 Staff 工程师过去靠几年学徒制慢慢把判断力传下去的路径，被压缩甚至打断了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:13" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="But now junior engineers are set up for different kind of expectations. Implementation is becoming abundant, but judgment isn't. So staff engineers now have to deal with a lot of cognitive overload as to how do you teach judgment?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 判断力到底是什么

判断力不是尽快找到解决方案。它是保持步调、同时容纳多种替代思路的能力——真正辩论各自的优缺点，享受评估权衡的过程，对数据保持开放心态 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:14" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="And then thinking in systems where it's constant evaluation of trade-offs, right? Judgment isn't about getting to the solution as soon as possible. It's the ability to hold that pace and entertain two different alternate ways of thinking, really debating the pros and cons." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

有判断力的工程师会思考二阶效应：这个决定的一阶影响是什么，二阶影响又是什么？我们今天在做哪些假设，在未来会站不住脚？他们不只是在构建解决方案，而是在"预演未来" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:05" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="There are some first order implications. But what are the second order implications? What assumptions are we making today that will not hold in the coming future?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

[[品味|品味]]和判断力有关联但不同。品味是能在多个层次上思考——从 1 层到 3 层到 10 层再到 50 层——从不同角度看待问题并形成观点，同时保持开放心态，如果有新数据进来就愿意改变自己的想法 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="49:31" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Post a free job today at linkedin.com slash quality. Taste to me is the ability to be able to think in multiple layers, starting off with probably 1 and then 3 and then 10 and think about scaling that to about 50 layers, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。判断力则是反复正确地做出可信决策的能力，信誉由此而来 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="50:25" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="That to me is taste. And judgment is a combination of the ability of making credible decisions, making decisions correctly over and over again. That's where credibility comes from." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 怎么在没有学徒制的情况下压缩判断力培养

传统路径是：初级工程师加入团队，花几年从 SEV3（较低级别事故）一路处理到 SEV0（最高级别事故），逐步理解如何构建生产系统。Staff 工程师在这个过程中承担指导和辅导的角色。但现在这条路径被绕过了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:23" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Without having the scope for building that apprenticeship, right? So what was available to staff engineers as to, you know, junior engineers usually onboard on the team, they spend a few years developing" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

一个可行的方向是把"[[案例教学法|案例教学法]]"引入工程教育。不是只学数据结构和算法，而是拿真实的事故报告做深度分析——比如 2017 年 AWS S3 那次大规模故障，从技术决策、组织决策、产品决策到当天的运行手册和操作手册，全方位拆解 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:57" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="So how do we make sure that as part of our curriculum, when I mentioned the case studies, incident failures, for example, the AWS S3 failure is a classic one, where the 2017 one, right?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。挑七八个这样的案例，用一个学期的时间让学生经历严谨的思维训练，建立对模糊性的熟悉和从容 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:15" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Just by looking at the technical decisions, the organizational decisions, the product decisions, and on the day of really what happened, what were the runbooks, what were the playbooks?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这背后还有一个常被忽略的技能：精确沟通。案例教学应该把语言能力也纳入课程——能不能精确表达、能不能与人协作、能不能在解决复杂问题的同时保持共情 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:20" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="And the goal of that to me is I feel language is so important. Can we communicate precisely?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## Staff 级别的新核心挑战：认知协调

在 Staff 和 Staff Plus 层面，核心挑战已经转向[[认知协调|认知协调]]。更多的代码审查、更多的指导辅导——但辅导的本质也在变：不再是教人写代码，而是教人理解为什么五年前、三年前某个决定是那样做的，让新人不会无脑派智能体重写代码而抹掉那段历史 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:36" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="So how we think about the future also has to be nuanced so that we cater to the complexities at different levels. So at staff level, I feel like the core challenge is shifting towards cognitive coordination." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这种协调负荷不应压在单个 Staff 工程师身上。一个具体例子：现在会议上人们不再自己做笔记了，但 AI 生成的笔记无法捕捉潜台词和字里行间的含义。

从中提炼出真正的核心可执行项，这成了高级工程师的新负荷 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:23" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Collectively put some durable principles that can benefit, especially at that level. Some things that I have seen be effective is one at the leadership level, being cognizant that what happens in meetings today." aria-label="回原文"></button>。代码审查也是同理——代码产出量暴增，谁来审？

还要不要审？这些都不是个人该扛的问题，而是组织责任 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:27" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="This is just one example. Similarly about you know code reviews right like in an area where in a time where we are able to churn so much code one thing that I hear constantly is how do we deal with the load around code reviews who does it can we have does it mean that we have more senior engineers in the team do we need to do code reviews at all" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 一个反直觉的发现：工程师开始要更多"人的时间"

一个意料之外的现象：当工程师整天与智能体协作、认知负荷拉满之后，他们反而开始渴望更多人与人之间的交流——不是减少会议，而是想要更多面对面的对话、头脑风暴、设计讨论 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:15" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="And I wanted to make space for that. And I actually heard one of my ICs call out that they're actually looking forward for more human collaboration. Oh, really?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。这对管理者来说是第一性原理层面的重新思考：过去"减少会议、保护专注时间"是标准答案，现在这个答案不成立了。创造心理安全的讨论空间，让大家能真正辩论方案的优劣，这是领导者的核心组织责任 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:32" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="So that told me so much about the kind of future that we want to create and the basic first principles of being human which is conversations and how conversations really lead to better decision making, more brainstorming, sound boarding and creating those spaces is so much important as a leader." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 团队形态没有标准答案，但要看清自己处于哪个阶段

小团队（特遣队模式）适合早期探索：高度熟练的小规模专家团队，极大自主权，不需要冗余，快速冲刺出 MVP 或达成目标。但这个模式不总是能扩展 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:29" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="I'll give you this example. I was spinning up what started off as a tiger team. Very typical model to start building a team for something that is a very new surface or a new problem that the business wants to explore." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当产品过了发布期、被广泛采用后，第一性原则变了——你要的是可靠性、可解释性、可观测性、金丝雀发布（先小范围验证再全量推送）、安全回滚。到了这个阶段，你需要更多协作：人与人之间、智能体与人之间、各职能之间 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="32:51" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Sometimes it's about just scaling our teams in a way that it's supporting where business is going. So let's say our product surface is well received, we have gone past launch, it's fully adopted." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

职能边界也在模糊——产品经理在审代码，独立贡献者（IC）在提设计想法，数据科学在影响产品方向 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:45" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="A lot of the lines that I'm also seeing blur is between functions. My product managers are reviewing code. My ICs are coming up with a lot of design ideas." aria-label="回原文"></button>。但什么能进生产、怎么干预，必须高度可控，这依赖于基础设施和 AI 平台是否已经按平台化思路建好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:04" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="And everyone has a little bit more ability to build and shape that future. But when it comes to guardrailing, holding our constraints, what goes into production, how do we intervene, that should be highly monitored." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

关键判断是：你处于旅程的哪个阶段？5% 人力的小团队什么时候该扩展到 27%？这不是一个放之四海而皆准的答案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="34:47" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="That cross collaboration between different functions, different intelligent systems, I think goes through a journey. And part of the acumen is also about noting what part of the journey are we in?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 跟不上智能体节奏的人，缺的不是工具使用

有些工程师用智能体极其流畅，有些跟不上。差距往往不在于"会不会用工具"，而在于判断力——能不能意识到与其开 10 个并行工作流，不如按"规划"和"执行"两个主题拆分思考方式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="37:48" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Maybe, you know, needs a mindset shift sometimes as I coach my engineers is where does planning stop and where does problem solving start, right? Some of the archetype that you mentioned where, you know, sometimes they take a little bit to catch up." aria-label="回原文"></button>。把一大块规划工作、项目分解、项目管理一股脑塞进一个自主工作流，容易让人不堪重负；拆成迭代步骤、用第一性原理逐步推进，效果通常更好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:26" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Just dividing up as engineers, how do we do our work, right? Instead of taking a big planning work, doing the project breakdown, doing the program management, having the JIRAs, and then also trying to figure out how to do this particular job all in one go." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

一个有效的做法是设立"本地带头人"——那些更懂 AI 工作流的人，去辅导其他工程师找到正确的平衡点：哪里移交控制、哪里自己干预、怎么把判断力嵌入提示词 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:45" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="If we break them up as to execution and planning, that itself unlocks a very different way of thinking. And I've found a lot of value where I set up local champions who are more like AI savvy, more workflow savvy." aria-label="回原文"></button>。单是把"你想并行化到什么程度"这件事想清楚，就能带来二八法则级别的收益 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="39:46" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Thank you for your time. Figure out your workflows, how much you want to parallelize differently. That itself gives that 80-20 benefit in my opinion." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 深入还是广泛？回到第一性原理来回答

面对智能体工作方式，该深入钻研还是广泛涉猎？答案不在工具层面，而在一个更根本的问题上：未来五到十年，软件工程里最有价值的东西是什么？

复杂性正在往哪里转移？盯着那个方向走 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="42:14" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="What are your thoughts on that? I think that also gets us to that key question right which is what is going to be the most valuable thing in software engineering over the next let's say five to ten years or where is that complexity shifting and keeping an eye on it and that should answer whether we want to go deep or broad." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

不会变的东西包括：构建好产品、做复杂权衡、从做功能到建平台、建基础能力。如果深入某个领域（比如推荐系统、搜索、API 设计、AI 平台）能帮你建立判断力和品味，能让你对生产系统有更深的同理心，那就值得深入——但不是为了深入而深入 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="43:50" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="Whenever there is this new terminology reset, things changing all the time, models adapting, data changing, which is what we are going through in the AI journey right now." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

品味也可以在软件工程之外建立。研究绘画——先喜欢颜色，再注意到技术细节，再想自己创造，才发现那些层次的复杂——听音乐、读文学、学数学，这些都在培养模式识别和多层次思考的能力 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:20" data-who="Malika Rao" data-en="I also want to highlight that taste can be built outside of just your profession and your software engineering career, right. That's where I go back to music, mathematics." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

> 【背景】Staff / Staff Plus 是大型科技公司里独立贡献者（IC）路线的高级职级，大致对应"资深专家/首席工程师"，不走管理路线但影响力等同或超过管理者。SEV0 到 SEV3 是互联网公司对线上事故的分级，SEV0 最严重。

## 本集带走

- **判断力 ≠ 快速给答案**：判断力是能同时容纳多条路径、享受权衡评估的过程、思考二阶效应，并且"预演未来"
- **品味 = 多层次思考 + 可被新数据改变的开放观点**：可以通过参与设计评审、也可以通过艺术和文学来培养
- **案例教学法是压缩判断力培养的可行路径**：拿真实事故报告（如 AWS S3 故障）做多角度深度拆解，建立对模糊性的从容
- **认知协调是组织责任，不是个人负担**：AI 笔记替代不了潜台词捕捉，代码审查负荷不该压在单个 Staff 身上
- **工程师在与智能体高频协作后，反而需要更多"人的时间"**：头脑风暴、设计辩论、面对面讨论——"少开会"不再是标准答案
- **跟不上节奏的人，缺的往往不是工具熟练度，而是判断力**：先想清楚"规划"和"执行"怎么拆分，比开 10 个并行工作流有效得多
- **深入还是广泛？看那个方向能不能帮你建判断力和品味**：不会变的是构建好产品、做复杂权衡、从功能走向平台

  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
