# YC 合伙人 John：别找完美想法，选一个然后深入

<div class="pd-byl"><b>John</b> · YC 合伙人 · 2026-09-01</div>

这一集是 YC 合伙人 [[John|John]] 聊创始人怎么选创业方向——他见过太多人同时盯好几个想法、迟迟不投入，结果哪个都没推进。他最反直觉的一个观点是：追求一个极其雄心勃勃的想法，和追求一个温和的想法，成本大致相同，都极其困难、都极端消耗时间，那不如瞄准那个如果成功会重写整个行业的版本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:27" data-who="John" data-en="It should be the most ambitious version of itself. It may seem unintuitive, but the cost of pursuing a wildly ambitious startup idea and the cost of pursuing a modest one are roughly the same." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 两种最常见的心态陷阱

第一种是「我要找到完美想法再动手」。问题在于，完美想法不可能在脑子里想出来，只能通过接触现实、从客户那里拿到反馈才能逐渐看清 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:09" data-who="John" data-en="Startups are hard, so shouldn't you figure out the best idea before committing? The problem with this approach is that it's impossible to figure out the perfect idea in the abstract." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二种是「我是做这个的完美创始人吗」。[[创始人-市场契合度|创始人市场匹配度]]确实重要，但很多连续创业者会拿这个借口卡住自己，觉得得有十年领域经验才配动手。实际上，选一个你好奇的方向、极其深入地钻研、大量跟客户聊，短时间内就能建立起非凡的认知 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="01:50" data-who="John" data-en="The truth is you don't. If you pick an idea you're curious about, go extremely deep, And most importantly, talk to customers." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 选一个，烧掉其他的船

同时做几个想法看似能「试错」，实际会产生坏数据——每个都浅尝辄止，拿到的信号全是噪音，结果要么过早放弃好想法，要么在坏想法上浪费更久 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:47" data-who="John" data-en="There are a couple of problems with this approach. The most serious is that it tends to produce bad data. If you don't actually go deep on an idea but instead juggle it with several others you won't get good signal about whether what you're doing actually works." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

正确做法是：选一个，然后**烧掉其他的船**。不是偷偷留着退路，而是明确关掉其他选项——改公司名、改邮箱、改网站、改你跟别人描述自己使命的方式，彻底变成一个「几乎认不出来的自己」 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:24" data-who="John" data-en="What do I mean by going deep? The first thing is that you should burn the other boats. That is, you should explicitly foreclose your other startup idea options." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他举了一家叫 GovDash 的公司，转型了至少五次，每次都换名字换邮箱，有一次连联系人都找不到了。但正因为他们每次都彻底投入，第五次真正成了政府采购领域的专家，需求大到几乎接不住 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:59" data-who="John" data-en="This could mean changing your company's name, your emails, your website, and even your internal narrative about why you're building a startup in the first place." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 怎么判断自己够不够「深」

他给了一个很硬的标准：**你能不能直接跑去经营你客户的生意？** 不是「你聊过 20 个客户」，而是如果明天把你扔进一家清洁公司，你知道他们每天出什么危机、接电话到底是不是前五大问题、漏接一通电话到底损失多少钱、他们愿意付多少来解决 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:40" data-who="John" data-en="Once you've decided to fully commit to an idea and go deep, how do you know if you're actually doing it well? The high watermark I use to help founders answer this question is could you actually run your customer's business?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

换种问法：你能就这个问题开一门课吗？你是世界上对这个主题最了解的人之一吗 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:20" data-who="John" data-en="These are the kinds of questions you need to be able to answer with very high confidence. Another way to think about this is could you teach a class on the problem you're solving?" aria-label="回原文"></button>？

但别在写代码之前非要聊几百个客户。正确节奏是一个紧密循环：深入理解需求 → 交付产品 → 更深入理解需求，同时进行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:37" data-who="John" data-en="Getting to this level will involve lots of conversations with customers and sometimes even literally doing the job yourself. But don't obsess over needing to talk to hundreds of customers before writing code." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## AI 时代好想法的三个特征

除了客户拉动，他还补充了三个在当下特别重要的品质。

一是**处在[[模型能力的边缘|模型能力的边缘]]**——你的产品在当前最前沿模型上勉强能用，但模型一进步就明显变好。你要清楚卡住产品性能的瓶颈是什么，如果某个瓶颈迟迟解决不了，解决它本身可能就变成一家公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:21" data-who="John" data-en="The most obvious one is pull from customers, but there are several other qualities of good ideas in the AI era that you should look out for as you go. The first is that the idea sits at the edge of what models can do today." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

二是**[[垂直化|垂直化]]，卖结果而不是卖软件**。AI 时代生产软件的成本趋近于零，光卖「给 X 行业的软件」不再有价值。

真正值钱的是客户信任、许可证、监管许可和结果所有权。所以别给保险公司做软件，直接当保险商；别给银行做后台系统，直接当银行 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:52" data-who="John" data-en="This is a version of Paul Graham's well-known quote that you should live in the future and then build what's missing. The second quality of a good idea is that it should verticalize." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

三是**做最雄心勃勃的版本**。挑战监管最严的行业、挑战体量巨大的老牌 SaaS 公司、做太空组装机器人这类硬科技——因为最野心的版本也是最能挡住竞争对手、吸引最顶尖人才的版本 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:24" data-who="John" data-en="That brings me to the third quality of a good idea. It should be the most ambitious version of itself. It may seem unintuitive, but the cost of pursuing a wildly ambitious startup idea and the cost of pursuing a modest one are roughly the same." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 深入的真正目的：找到底下的更好想法

即使你全力深入后这个想法失败了，你也不会白干——你会对行业有深刻理解，对执行有更好的判断。更重要的是，深入的过程几乎一定会带你发现一个真正可行的新想法 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:12" data-who="John" data-en="Or building hard tech like robotics for space assembly. Now, what if you do all of this and the idea fails? The good news is..." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

大多数创始人一开始解决的是表层痛点，真正机会在更深层的结构性问题。深入主要不是为了验证你最初的那个想法，而是为了发现藏在上面的更好想法 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:46" data-who="John" data-en="But more importantly, you will often come away from the process with a new idea that will actually work. When most founders begin, they're solving surface-level pain points." aria-label="回原文"></button>。你会看到瓶颈、看到空白、看到没人造过的工具，其中之一可能就是真正的公司 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:09" data-who="John" data-en="This almost always happens, especially if you're at the forefront of what models can do today. You'll notice the bottlenecks, the gaps, the dev tools nobody's built, and one of those could turn out to be the actual company." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

早期就像走在浓雾里，只能看到前方十英尺，诱惑是每个方向都试探几步。但那几乎不产生任何信息。

真正有效的是选一个方向，快速走。不保证终点对，但单位时间内你获得的信息量大得多，而且走着走着，你可能抵达一个一开始根本看不到的、更好的目的地 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:31" data-who="John" data-en="Learn everything you can about the customer and try to execute for them. In the early idea fog, where you can only see 10 feet in front of you, the temptation is to take a few cautious steps in every direction." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 本集带走

- **别找完美想法，选一个就干**：完美想法不可能在抽象中想出来，只能通过接触客户和现实逐步看清。
- **烧掉其他船**：选一个方向后，改名、改邮箱、改叙事，彻底关掉其他选项，不留退路。
- **用「能不能经营客户生意」检验深度**：不只是聊过多少客户，而是你能不能直接跑去替客户干这行。
- **需求理解和产品交付要在一个紧密循环里同时做**，不要先聊几百人再写代码。
- **AI 时代卖结果，不卖软件**：软件生产成本趋零，直接当保险商、当银行，而不是给它们做软件。
- **追求最雄心勃勃的版本**：成本和温和版本差不多，但能挡竞争、吸人才。
- **深入的目的不是验证起始想法，是发现底下更好的想法**：你会看到瓶颈和空白，其中之一可能就是真正的公司。
