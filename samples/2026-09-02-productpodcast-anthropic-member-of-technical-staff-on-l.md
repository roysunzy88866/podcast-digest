拒绝一份薪水高三倍的 offer,跑去一家刚起步的团队带人——说这话的人是 [[Amandeep Khurana|Amandeep Khurana]],[[Anthropic|Anthropic]] [[前置部署工程|前置部署工程]](forward deployed engineering)里带团队的人。这一集是 Product School CEO Carlos 和他的一场公开对话：两人私聊过一百次，头一回当众聊。Amandeep 的路径不寻常：[[AWS|AWS]] 工程师出身，在 [[Cloudera|Cloudera]] 做了五年解决方案架构师，2016 年创业、公司被 Databricks 收购，然后在亚马逊干了五年半——期间在 2023 年主动放弃了管理职位，回去做 [[IC|IC]](个人贡献者，就是不带人的执行岗)。

## 从创业者到亚马逊，再到“就不想做管理”的那一刻

在亚马逊做到同时管产品和工程的职位之后，他却在 2023 年主动交还了管理职责。那时候“回去做 IC”还不像今天这么酷，没什么人这么干。

他的理由很实际：面前有个 IC 机会，正好在一个他想学但完全不懂的领域——如果当时摆着的是管理岗，他可能就选管理岗了。但如果继续把大块时间花在组织建设上，他学不到东西。他还找了几位从 VP 转回 IC、后来又回到领导岗位的人聊，得到的建议是：最坏的情况是学到新领域，最好的情况也是学到新领域 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:28" data-who="Amandeep Khurana" data-en="You're right, it wasn't cool. It wasn't something a lot of people were doing. I found myself in a place where there was this opportunity in front of me, which was an IC opportunity, and it was in a domain I wanted to learn and didn't know anything about. If there had been a management opportunity, I might have picked that over the IC opportunity. But I said, &quot;Look, I want to learn this thing. I want to get involved. Let me just jump in and see what happens, and let me learn,&quot; because I'm not going to learn a ton if I continue spending a large chunk of my time on org building or management work. I wanted to be hands-on. That was one. Second, I spoke with a few folks at AWS at the time who had done similar transitions, who had gone from a VP role to an IC role and then eventually back into a leadership role. I asked them how they thought about it, and they advised me with some perspectives. So I took that on and said, &quot;I'll give it a shot. I have nothing to lose here. Worst case, I'm going to learn about this new domain. Best case, I'm going to learn about this new domain. And as this team grows, there might be management opportunities I can take on.&quot; So that's how I thought about it. It wasn't where it is today. For me, it was: I want to learn that thing, I want to be hands-on, and we'll see what happens." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

离开亚马逊时，他本来的计划是再创一次业。辞职几周后，Anthropic 的 offer 来了，邀请他加入刚正式化的前置部署工程职能。

他当时居然主动要求“就让我做 IC [[前向部署工程师|前置部署工程师]]吧，这事太好玩了”，招聘经理拒绝了，要他进来带团队。他答应了。

## FDE 不是改名的专业服务

前置部署工程师这个头衔现在很火，很多公司拿它包装传统售后团队。Amandeep 先纠正了一件事：Anthropic 不是先驱——微软几十年前就干这个，这个术语的现行形态是 [[Palantir|Palantir]] 在 2008 年前后推广开的，他们是在向 Palantir 学习 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:33" data-who="Amandeep Khurana" data-en="I would not call us the pioneers of this. The concept of forward deployed engineering has existed for a very, very long time. Microsoft is the OG of this; they used to do it several decades ago. And the current incarnation of the term was popularized by Palantir back in the 2006, 2007, 2008 time frame, so from 2008 onwards. So we're not the pioneers at all. In fact, we are learning from them and seeing how we take all of that and adapt it to the business we're building. The way I think about it: in any company building and selling a product, there are two things, and everything else is a supporting function. What does selling a product entail? There's the technical selling of it: your typical solution architect or systems engineer persona who comes in, drills through the demos, shows you the use cases, and walks you through the architectures, depending on the product you're selling. So it's technical pieces tied to certain accounts or verticals, and they're incentivized to close the deal. Then there's post-sales, which could be one of two ways: &quot;I'm going to help you implement the thing you bought,&quot; or, &quot;Now it's implemented and I'm going to be the service or customer support team, so if something goes wrong, you call us and we'll help you fix it.&quot; Note that in both cases, the product team builds something technical, and the go-to-market teams, both pre- and post-sales, go make the customer successful with what the product team built. FDE, forward deployed engineering, is actually different. There are a couple of things that are different from traditional professional services, which is the post-implementation work. One: in typical professional services, I'm going to give you my time and you're going to give me money. The exchange is time and money, and you tell me what you want me to do and I'll do it for you. I'm simplifying it, but generally that's how it works. One of the core parts of FDE is that it's not that I'm going to just give you my time and do whatever you want. It's that we're going to align on an outcome. We might even spend time together to identify the right outcome for the business based on the overall goals. I'm going to align on that, and then I'm in it with you. We're going to make it happen. So there's a lot more ownership in that relationship, as opposed to, &quot;You just tell me what to do and I'm your implementation person.&quot; The second component is that whatever work we do will feed back into the product. In Anthropic's case, part of it feeds into the research as well, because that's how you build the flywheel. So FDE as a function is very closely tied to the product and engineering function, and that's where the value gets created. Every engagement should produce a couple of things for us. One, feedback into product. Two, it needs to create some sort of blueprint or playbook, so we don't have to do that type of engagement again. It's a first-of-a-kind, tip-of-the-spear problem that hasn't been solved before. We come in and help solve it, and then that becomes a template that either gets productized, or we give it to customers to build themselves if appropriate, or we give it to partners to scale up. There are a bunch of system integrator partners that will take those templates and say, &quot;This is how you do this in a bank.&quot; So that's how we think about it. We're not trying to build or scale a professional services arm. This is essentially to figure out how to bring this technology into the real world in the best possible way, and then take all those learnings, templatize them, and give them away, either as product or as blueprints. ## 挑客户的三个过滤器

这么精英的团队，什么客户才接？三个过滤器：一，是否与使命一致(把 AI 安全地带给世界、为人类福祉)；二，是否是首创、能否打开一组目前拿不到的使用场景或一个行业；三，这次合作能否给产品带来信号。他强调，商业回报不是唯一视角，这三件事比商业回报重要得多 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:32" data-who="Amandeep Khurana" data-en="That's a great question. If we look at the definition: one, is the work aligned with our mission as a company, our mission of bringing AI safely to the world, but not just bringing AI to the world, also for the benefit of humanity generally. That's one. Two, is it a first-of-a-kind, and will it open up a set of use cases or a vertical for us that currently isn't available? And three, is there some signal we think we'll get for the product out of this engagement? So we look at those three things and then decide, should we lean in here or not. The FDE team actually does not look at commercials as the only lens. In fact, these three things are much more important than the commercials." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

至于什么样的人能当 FDE:没有固定画像——有前工程师、有博士研究员、也有非工程背景的。真正筛的是：是否与[[使命对齐|使命对齐]]、是否动手造东西、能否在没人知道答案的复杂客户环境里自己把答案搞出来(“很高的[[主观能动性|主观能动性]]”)，同时维护客户的信任 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="14:33" data-who="Amandeep Khurana" data-en="I don't think I can say there's one kind of persona that makes for a good FDE. We've got FDEs who were engineers. We have FDEs who were researchers and have their PhDs. We have FDEs who come from non-engineering backgrounds as well. So the background, while important, is not the only thing that matters. What really matters is mission alignment. Are you hands-on and building things? Can you be in front of customers in complex environments and actually guide them through the journey? Because we have to help them, and oftentimes we don't even know the answers, so we have to go figure it out. Since it's never been done before, we're going to run into problems we never anticipated. So it's: do you have high agency? Are you going to go figure things out and really try to disambiguate, while maintaining the customer relationship, maintaining the trust of the customer, having their back, and doing it in a manner aligned with our culture and mission? That's really what we look for. The Talent Wars and Choosing Mission Over Comp" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 人才战争与“我不缺这笔钱，但不是妥协”

实验室的人才被当 NBA 球员抢，Anthropic 靠什么留人？Amandeep 说至少目前没遇到留存问题：公司涨得快，股票授予比你几个月前拿到的更值钱；竞争没造成流失，面试里会筛使命契合。他也承认，实验室的薪酬包比 FAANG 慷慨得多——这在五六年前不成立，那时付薪最健康的是 FAANG <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:03" data-who="Amandeep Khurana" data-en="As far as I know, we have not really had a problem from a retention perspective so far. Anthropic has been growing very, very fast, and the stock grants are worth more than what you got three or six months ago. Competition has not been a reason why we've seen any attrition, and Anthropic generally has not seen attrition, from what I understand. - **FDE 和专业服务的分界线**：不是卖时间，是先对齐成果再一起做成；且每次合作必须产出产品反馈 + 一份可复用的蓝图/模板，目标是“再也不用做第二次这类合作”。
