这一集是 a16z 的 [[Elena Berger|Elena Berger]] 和两位合伙人聊他们第七版「百大消费级 AI 应用报告」——[[Olivia Moore|Olivia Moore]] 是这份报告的作者，[[Josh Ellman|Josh Ellman]] 来自消费团队。

这一版首次加入了消费者刷卡支出数据，结果和光看流量的图景很不一样：

流量榜看似趋于稳定（网页加移动端合计只有 11 个新产品，是历版最少），但按支出排名的前 50 个产品里，有 29 个根本不在任何流量榜上 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="03:19" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="We only had 11 new products across the web and mobile list combined, which is the least we've had in any of the prior editions of the series. But the spend data really introduced a lot of new variables because of those 50 that ranked for spend, 29 of them were not on either traffic list." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

消费级 AI 现在本质上是一场重度用户的游戏。

## 付费极度集中：一个重度用户顶几十个普通用户

数据有多集中？

大约一半的美国人报告在用 AI，但按 Yipit 的样本组，只有约 4.5% 的美国消费者在为 AI 产品付[[订阅制转型|订阅]]费——虽然比一年前翻了一倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:22" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="It depends on the source. Our Yipit panel shows probably sub-5%, around 4.5% of U.S. consumers are paying a subscription to an AI product. Some other sources say as low as 2 or 2.5 or maybe a little bit higher." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而就在这付费的 4.5% 里：前 10% 的用户贡献了一半以上的收入；前 1% 占 20%，底部 50% 只占 16% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:43" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="But that spend is still extremely concentrated. So even within that top 4.5 percent of people who do spend, the top 10 percent of those are like more than half of the revenue." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

前 1% 的用户每月用个人信用卡在 AI 上花 903 美元，中位数用户只花 25 美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:14" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="This was the other interesting thing about revenue data is we were able to look at it kind of like on a almost person-by-person basis, what it looks like, who are these people who are spending on AI." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这些花大钱的是谁？

主要是开发者、创作者这类重度用户，花在开发者工具、生产力工具、创意工具上——用来构建、制作、销售的东西。

N8N、Granola、Higgs Field、Manus 在头部消费者中的占比都远超平均 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="16:50" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="And probably unsurprisingly, it was mostly developer tools, productivity tools, creative tools, things used to like build, make, sell. Things like N8N, Granola, Higgs Field, Manus, those all were way overrepresented amongst the top spenders." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Josh 的解读是：程序员天生会问「我怎么在这个东西上获得杠杆」，这是一种特定心态和世界观，目前还只体现在较小的群体里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="18:12" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="And I think Part of my understanding of these numbers is it's still a world view that gets reflected only among a smaller group of people and the question might be just like how do you expand that world view?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而且即便在消费级助手产品里，来自 Assistant Benchmark（追踪 170 多个[[智能体|智能体]]的网站）社群数据也显示，头号用例仍然是编程和技术自动化——所以「服务一个用户每月要几千美元」的成本讲得通；

面向主流消费者的产品成本则是几十美元量级 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:09" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="What are they talking about doing with agents? And the vast majority, still, the number one use case was coding and technical automation, even for these consumer-assistant products like Muse and Instinct." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 个人智能体：三个月从极客玩具走向妈妈们

最有趣的新趋势是个人智能体。

上一版报告还差点让 [[OpenClaw|OpenClaw]] 冲进榜单前排，这一版它已不见踪影——流量彻底下滑，团队据称被 [[OpenAI|OpenAI]] 收购，而被 GrokBot、[[Town|Town]]、OpenAI 新推出的 DOTS 等后来者取代 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:16" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="Yeah. It's funny, the last report that we did, it ended right before the cutoff of when OpenClaw traffic would come in. So we had done kind of like a retro before we published the report and we had found that it would have been very high up in the rankings." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

真正的新变化在消费端：

最近三周到一个月出现了一批「我敢推荐给家人放心用」的消费助手，增长极快但还没触达主流用户。

其中最大的两个是 [[Muse|Muse]] 和 [[Instinct|Instinct]]：Instinct 宣布约 10 万用户、日增 10%，前三周 40% 的用户绑了信用卡，首月平均消费超 1000 美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:56" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="Instinct had announced like 100,000 users growing 10% day over day. They'd announced 40% of users connected a credit card in the first three weeks and they were spending over $1,000 on average in their first month." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Muse 前 12 天约 50 万下载、25 万活跃用户——听起来猛，但和 Threads 同期（仅美加）1600 万下载相比只有约 500 万 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="07:37" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="So we grabbed the Threads, U.S. and Canada downloads only. In the first 22 days or so, Threads had about 16 million downloads and Muse is still sitting around 5 million over that same time period." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Josh 认为下次再做报告时会看到大规模采用 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:52" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="And I think that's what's really exciting about this consumer. I assume when we do this again next time, we're going to see massive adoption. But I think it hasn't needed to push as hard to have that early network effect." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

但两个瓶颈摆在面前。一是平台：Amazon 拒绝 Muse 在其站点浏览购物，而 Muse 已与 Shopify 等几百家产品建立合作 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:29" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="We've seen that cut both ways. Like Amazon is like, we're not going to allow Muse to kind of browse and purchase products on our site, but also Muse has Already hundreds of other partnerships with different products." aria-label="回原文"></button>——平台开放度决定智能体能力上限。

二是信任：

这种软件比以往任何产品都更亲密地了解你（邮件、信用卡、你倾诉的私事），产品越有用知道你越多，而你未必想把这种了解带进与其他人共处的情境——很多人连工作版和个人版 [[ChatGPT|ChatGPT]] 都要分开，还没有产品破解这个问题 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="09:49" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="I still don't feel like any of the assistant products have unlocked person-to-person network effects. And part of this for me is this software knows us more intimately than any other product in the past." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Josh 直言：如果你不信任 AI，这将是它成长为大规模消费应用的最大阻碍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="10:42" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="But on a very personal level, if you don't trust AI, The thing that you're giving it your most intimate access to your email or your life or your credit card, and it might do rogue things that you wouldn't expect or share things with other people you wouldn't expect." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 广告必须回来：订阅主导是消费互联网的反常

Olivia 抛出一个有争议的判断：她并不希望付费人群比例直接扩大——因为目前消费级 AI 收入几乎全部来自直接订阅：

网页榜约 85% 靠订阅，约 62% 靠积分/token 加量付费，只有约 13% 有[[广告|广告]]或其他模式 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:07" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="Substantially all of the consumer AI revenue thus far has come from direct subscriptions. Like on this list we looked and it was like, you know, 85% or something of our web list monetized via subscriptions, another 62% monetized via kind of credits or token extra usage payments." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

回顾消费互联网史，几乎所有真正的巨头收入都来自广告或交易费而非订阅——这是个相当不自然的倒置 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="21:29" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="And that's like a If you look back at the history of the consumer internet, that's like a pretty unnatural inversion. Like almost all of the really big consumer technology companies that we have now make the vast majority of their revenue from ads or from transaction fees, not from subscriptions." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

全球消费级订阅产品前 20 名里 ChatGPT 已上榜（三年半就做到了），其余几乎全是媒体公司或高频购买的大平台 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:09" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="If you look at the top 20 consumer subscription products globally. ChatGPT is already on there, which is crazy in like three and a half years of growth. Almost all the other ones are media companies or big platforms like Amazon or even Uber, things like that, where you have a very frequent purchase behavior." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

推理成本下降后，广告和交易费模式才可能真正跑起来。

广告的信号已经有了：OpenAI 的广告年化运行率已达 10 亿美元（8 月数据）——以前一家公司推出广告产品后要花很多年才能到这个水平 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="24:30" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="Your Consumer Top 100 report had a really, really interesting fact in there, which was the run rate that OpenAI has reached through advertising. They're at a billion dollars now in annual run rate on advertising." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

原因一是密度：12 亿周活；二是它对你了解极多，理论上能收更高价格、有更高转化率，登录与钱包功能还会强化这一点 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:21" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="And then the second thing, and what I'm fascinated to see in terms of the evolution of the ads product is Hypothetically, they should be able to charge more and have higher conversion rates because they just know so much more about you as a user." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Josh 亲眼见过一个自然融入对话、标注清晰的好广告，也用自己的经历说明商业化查询的价值：

他在 Google 上很难找到一款能分开代客钥匙的钥匙扣，最后在 ChatGPT 上来回对话找到了底特律一家工厂的产品——那次对话完全对广告开放，广告本可以帮他找到东西 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="27:46" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="So we don't, yeah. You know, I was doing a bunch of travel research recently and was going to be in New York for a few days and I just started seeing some ads for a couple things that were going on in New York that I might have wanted to do and I just found that really useful and like I've started using it more for commercial shopping." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

他甚至认为 OpenAI 的定向投放可能超过以精准著称的 Meta，因为那是实时对话 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="28:51" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="So interesting. I feel like we may see, as they lean into it, OpenAI being able to do crazy good targeting, like even better than I think Meta, which previously was like best in class for this, just because they knew so much about you." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

垂直产品同样适用：面向医生的 [[OpenEvidence|OpenEvidence]] 已覆盖全美 50-60% 的医生，这种高价值定向受众广告效率极高 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="29:25" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="So another company that has seen early success in ads is OpenEvidence, which is an AI product for doctors. And it already has 50, I think the most recent numbers were 50 to 60 percent density of all U.S. physicians on the product." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

前提是必须做得巧妙——在和极其个人化的东西对话时塞进「快速减肥」式广告，那是在摧毁信任 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="25:53" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="It's early days and I think, you know, there have been some really funny conversations about ads and AI. If you are talking to this incredibly personal thing and then it's like, let me tell you about health and let me introduce you to this like crazy product that can help you, you know, lose weight fast." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 三大实验室分化，垂直工具的护城河在模型之外

ChatGPT 仍是全球主导：网页端流量领先 [[Claude|Claude]] 约 6 倍、领先 [[Gemini|Gemini]] 约 2 倍 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:32" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="Everything below ChatGPT has seen, I think, more movements and more shakeups. So ChatGPT is now ahead of Claude around 6x on the web, around 2x ahead of Gemini on the web." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

最大的意外是：在美国付费订阅者数量上，Claude 已超过 Gemini——尽管 Gemini 背后有 Google 的天然分发和更大的用户基数 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="30:59" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="And probably that's one of the biggest surprises to me in this report was we got a panel of U.S. spenders And Claude has actually passed Gemini in terms of number of paid subscribers, which is kind of crazy given Gemini has a much bigger install base and user base overall." aria-label="回原文"></button>。变现路径也分化了：

ChatGPT 在变现上遥遥领先，美国付费消费者订阅数约为 Gemini 与 Claude 之和的三倍；

但 [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] 名言「不做广告」，订阅打得更激进——约 7.5% 的订阅用户在每月 100 美元以上的最高档，而 ChatGPT 和 Gemini 都只有约 1% <button class="pd-ts" data-t="31:41" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="The other main difference, I would say, in how these products are monetizing, and again, ChatGPT is still far in the lead by monetization. They have around three times more paid consumer subscribers in the U.S. than both Gemini and Claude." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

创意工具上，实验室没占满所有缝隙。

音频（文本转语音、音乐）它们基本没碰——实验室要建编码智能体、要冲 AGI，不值得去趟 [[Suno|Suno]] 那些版权泥潭——于是 [[Eleven Labs|Eleven Labs]] 和 Suno 稳居流量与消费榜前列 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="33:13" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="So there's a few categories of creative tools or maybe modalities is the better term where the labs have not focused energy and attention for many reasons we could discuss." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

图像领域 OpenAI 和 Google 抢走了独立图像生成器的散户流量，[[MidJourney|MidJourney]] 已完全跌出流量榜——但收入榜上它回来了：

重度用户要的是有品味、能精细调校的模型 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:21" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="It has fallen off the traffic rankings completely. But to your point in power users, if you look at the revenue rankings, MidJourney is back. So people, the power users still want the model that they feel has taste, has an aesthetic sense, and that they're able to tune in a really specific way." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

视频则是中国公司占优的荒野边疆，因为它们能用任何数据训练，而视频生成对数据量极其饥渴 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="35:40" data-who="Olivia Moore" data-en="And then that brings us to video, which to me is kind of like A wild frontier right now because Chinese companies that can train on any data and do have a real advantage." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Josh 补充一个大趋势：

智能体友好、可被智能体调用的创意工具会获得强劲顺风——他让 Astra 这类强大模型直接生成和编辑创意内容，效果非常好 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="36:16" data-who="Josh Ellman" data-en="I think more broadly in creative tools, I'd be curious if either of you have experienced this, but I have recently been asking Astra and other really powerful models to generate or create, generate or edit creative for me." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 初创公司为什么还有机会：界面等着被重新发明

(function(){
  function fmt(s){
    if(!isFinite(s)||s<0) s=0;
    var m=Math.floor(s/60), x=Math.floor(s%60);
    return (m<10?'0':'')+m+':'+(x<10?'0':'')+x;
  }
  function wire(box){
    if(box.dataset.wired) return; box.dataset.wired='1';
    var a=box.querySelector('audio'), pb=box.querySelector('.pb'),
        bar=box.querySelector('.bar'), fill=box.querySelector('.bar > i'),
        tm=box.querySelector('.tm'), t2=box.querySelector('.t2');
    if(!a||!pb||!bar||!fill||!tm) return;
    var total=0;
    function paint(){
      var cur=a.currentTime||0;
      fill.style.width=(total?(cur/total*100):0)+'%';
      tm.textContent=fmt(cur)+(total?' / '+fmt(total):'');
    }
    a.addEventListener('loadedmetadata',function(){
      total=a.duration||0;
      if(total&&t2) t2.textContent=Math.round(total/60)+' 分钟 · AI 合成朗读';
      paint();
    });
    a.addEventListener('timeupdate',paint);
    a.addEventListener('play',function(){ pb.textContent='❚❚'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','暂停'); });
    a.addEventListener('pause',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; pb.setAttribute('aria-label','播放'); });
    a.addEventListener('ended',function(){ pb.textContent='▶'; });
    pb.addEventListener('click',function(){ if(a.paused) a.play(); else a.pause(); });
    function seek(ev){
      if(!total) return;
      if(ev.clientX==null) return;
      var r=bar.getBoundingClientRect();
      var x=Math.min(Math.max(ev.clientX-r.left,0),r.width);
      a.currentTime=(x/r.width)*total;
      paint();
    }
    bar.addEventListener('pointerdown',function(ev){
      seek(ev);
      function mv(e){ seek(e); }
      function up(){ document.removeEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.removeEventListener('pointerup',up); }
      document.addEventListener('pointermove',mv); document.addEventListener('pointerup',up);
    });
    a.addEventListener('error',function(){
      box.classList.add('pd-play-dead');
      box.textContent='本集中文精华音频还没生成好,稍后再来听。';
    });
  }
