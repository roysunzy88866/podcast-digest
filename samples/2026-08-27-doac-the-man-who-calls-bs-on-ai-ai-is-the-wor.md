Ed Zitron 在科技行业做了 15 年公关，他不是那种"反技术"的人，但他认为[[生成式 AI|生成式 AI]] 从诞生的第一天起就不是作为诚实的软件出售的——公司用魔法的术语兜售它，说它会取代所有工作、治愈癌症，实际上它是极其昂贵、不可靠、且根本不赚钱的云软件 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="02:33" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Ed Zitron. I think generative AI is at its heart con. I don't think it is sold as honest software." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 一万亿美元砸进去，收入在哪？

六家领先的 AI 公司——[[Anthropic|Anthropic]]、Amazon、[[NVIDIA|NVIDIA]]、Microsoft、[[OpenAI|OpenAI]]、Google——它们的收入并不是真的来自 AI。直到最近，这些公司 AI 收入的 70% 来自 OpenAI 和 Anthropic，而这两家是亏损公司，如果没有上面那几家持续输血根本活不下去 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="You're saying that their fundamental business model is... Well, their revenues are not really coming from AI. Up until fairly recently, none of their revenues were coming from AI, like dribbles a bit." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Amazon 给 OpenAI 投了 350 亿美元，给 Anthropic 投了 50 亿，Google 给 Anthropic 投了 100 亿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:37" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Right now, 70% of all AI revenues across those three companies are from OpenAI and Anthropic, two unprofitable, unsustainable companies that literally cannot afford to exist without these very same companies giving them money." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

而未来三年半，分析师预计 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 会有 4000 亿或更多的收入预期——但这全部是投机的，完全没有现实基础 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="04:46" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Google sent $10 billion to Anthropic. And in the next three and a half years, OpenAI and Anthropic, based on actual sell-side analyst evaluations, their estimates, that inform whether stock is going to go up or down after earnings, they are expecting $400 or more billion of revenue." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

这些公司在[[资本支出|资本支出]]上已经花了超过一万亿美元，明年还想再花一万亿 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="There's some value, but it's not. They have spent over a trillion dollars in capex. What does capex mean?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。资本支出就是建数据中心、买 [[GPU|GPU]]（一种专门做 AI 计算的芯片）这种长期投资。

以 Stargate Abilene 数据中心为例，1.2 吉瓦的电力，每栋楼 5 万个 GPU，把相当于整个布里斯托尔市的用电量压缩到一个只有城市面积 1/1172 的空间里 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="06:42" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And because of how many of them you need, you need thousands of them, tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands in some case, you need a bunch of power. So an example, OpenAI and Oracle are building a data center in Texas, in Abilene, Texas." aria-label="回原文"></button>。除 Microsoft 外，所有公司都在举债建这些东西。

最关键的问题是：这些上市公司拒绝披露 AI 收入 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:08" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="30 or something percent of cloud growth just from these two unprofitable companies that will need to be given the money from somewhere. And on top of that, these companies have such low respect for the average investor, for the analysts, for everyone really, that they don't even disclose their AI revenues." aria-label="回原文"></button>。偶尔提到时，用的是"年化 run rate"这种从不定义的指标——可能是月乘以 12，可能是过去四周乘以 13，每次都不一样 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="05:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The few times they deign us worthy... They use something called a run rate, an annualized run rate, which means, well, nothing. They never define it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Ed 的逻辑很简单：如果这东西真赚钱、真值那个价，他们会迫不及待地告诉你数字 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And really, the simplest way to explain it is, If they were actually profitable, if they believed that these services were worthwhile and that they were worthy of the cost, they'd charge it." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 补贴下的"采用"不是真需求

ChatGPT 发布 60 天就有 1 亿用户，超过 60% 的美国成年人在三年内将 AI 工具融入日常——但这些数字有欺骗性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="38:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="weird system of pulleys and levers and such. But how come then, when you look at the stats around ChatGBT's growth, 100 million active users in just the first 60 days after launching?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当你打开 Google，Gemini 在你耳边尖叫；打开 Word，co-pilot 在烦你；打开 Amazon，Rufus AI 对你买的袜子发表意见。这是历史上最大规模的非自愿技术推送 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:19" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The first, let's start with this adoption. Is it honest adoption when you are forced to use generative AI when you load Google? When you load Google Docs, Gemini screams in your ear." aria-label="回原文"></button>。人们用 AI，很大程度上是因为媒体三年来的恐吓式宣传——"这会抢走你的工作，不用就落后了" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="08:41" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="ChatGPT, for example. Every single media outlet has been screaming about this for three years. They've been saying this will take your job." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

更核心的问题是成本。AI 服务按 [[token|token]]（约四分之三个单词）计费，但你用月度订阅时完全看不到实际消耗 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="11:43" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But then also there's the problem of cost. So when you use AI services, you burn tokens. And it's per million tokens." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Semi Analysis 发现，在每月 200 美元的 ChatGPT 订阅上，你可以烧掉价值 14000 美元的 token；在 Anthropic 上可以烧掉 8000 美元 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:36" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Now... Someone recently found, Semi Analysis actually found this, a big analyst group. They found that on a $200 a month chat GPT subscription, you can burn $14,000 worth of tokens." aria-label="回原文"></button>。OpenAI 去年亏损了 209 亿美元，因为人们可以无限制地消耗 token 而只付固定月费 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="12:52" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And on Anthropix, you can burn $8,000 for... 200 bucks that is how most and even on the 20 buck a month service you can burn 400 dollars now most people don't realize that most people have no idea what ai costs most people just think oh it's 20 bucks a month no all of these companies run a horrifying loss open ai lost 20.9 billion dollars last year because people can burn as many tokens as they want" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

当 2026 年 3 月左右，OpenAI 试图让 150 人以上的企业按实际 token 用量付费时，企业的反应是立刻恐慌 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:13" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="200 bucks that is how most and even on the 20 buck a month service you can burn 400 dollars now most people don't realize that most people have no idea what ai costs most people just think oh it's 20 bucks a month no all of these companies run a horrifying loss open ai lost 20.9 billion dollars last year because people can burn as many tokens as they want" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Uber 在三个月内烧掉了整个年度 token 预算，然后说"成本太高了，得控制" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="13:29" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's a huge issue, which is not really what the... heir apparent to tech's history is meant to be saying but the point is enterprises immediately started freaking out uber burned through their entire annual token budget in three months so suddenly after everyone's saying ai is the most productive thing ever it's amazing it's changing everything the moment people actually had to pay for it they go oh i don't know actually um maybe it's it's obviously we all love it it's all great right" aria-label="回原文"></button>。Ed 的总结：你用 [[LLM|LLM]]，不管得到的结果对不对，你都得付费 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="15:32" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And it's very simple. You pay when you use an LLM, regardless of whether you get what you want. When these things hallucinate, say you're doing something, you're coding something, and they go through a code base and they fuck up a bunch of stuff, they break a bunch of stuff, you're paying for that." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 和互联网泡沫不一样

主持人用《创新者的窘境》的框架反驳：汽车刚出来时也比马车差，但最终因为更高的增长上限而取代了马 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="20:04" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="And one of the things it talks about is how the innovation that ends up taking out or transforming an industry... often starts worse, doesn't make economic sense." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Ed 的回应是：汽车时代没有全世界政府喋喋不休、没有每家数学家和科学家涌入同一个行业、没有人类历史上最大规模的不真诚营销活动 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="22:24" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Now, here's the thing. The car example. Back then, you didn't have pretty much every mathematician and scientist going into the car industry." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

高盛的 Jim Covello 在 2024 年的报告中指出：在 iPhone 推出前，有数千场演示说明当各种无线电变小时，某种东西不可避免地会出现。但 AI 没有这样的路径 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="40:15" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Stuff that eventually happened. And actually, there's this guy called Jim Covello from Goldman Sachs in a report he did in 2024 that was Gen.AI, too much spend for not enough return." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

互联网泡沫后确实诞生了伟大的公司，但有两个本质区别。第一，互联网泡沫后的需求是真实的、未补贴的；生成式 AI 的需求主要是补贴的 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="45:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That's very different to demand for generative AI. Right now, the demand we have for generative AI is predominantly subsidized. Just let's start there." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

第二，互联网的基础设施（光纤、传输站）建好后，运营成本会下降；但 AI 数据中心建好后，2050 年的运行成本和今天一样贵，除非电力有突破 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="46:35" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="How does this turn into a post.com bubble thing? A data center built today is going to be as expensive to run in 2050 as it is today, unless there's some breakthrough in electricity." aria-label="回原文"></button>。NVIDIA 的新芯片 Vera Rubin 号称效率高 10 倍，但每兆瓦成本反而更高——如果真有成本突破，他们会第一个嚷嚷 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:02" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Right. Even NVIDIA with Vera Rubin, their more expensive new GPU system, even then they're like, yeah, 10x more efficient. It's more dollars per megawatt." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

所有这些数据中心只为一件事建：生成式 AI。不是蛋白质折叠，不是自动驾驶，不是机器人 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:02" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="No, and that's the funny thing. All those data centers that we're building, all of them are for just generative AI. They're not for all of the other stuff." aria-label="回原文"></button>。Sightline Climate 今年 2 月的数据显示，规划中的数据中心有 190 吉瓦，对应每年 1.6 万亿到 3 万亿美元的需求——而实际年度需求连 1300 亿美元都不到 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="48:57" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="But when it comes to these massive amount of data centers, Sightline Climate said in February, there's 190 gigawatts of data centers under planning, don't know about under construction." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 软件质量在下降，不是在提升

Amazon Web Services 今年因为 AI 编码工具宕机了两三次 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="52:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="catastrophically worse. Amazon Web Services went down multiple times because of their AI coding tool. How is Google worse?" aria-label="回原文"></button>。GitHub 的停机频繁到有人开玩笑说"应该在他们正常运行时发通知" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="23:48" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="GitHub, someone posted on Twitter earlier today, we should get a notification when GitHub is up rather than when it's down because that would be more reliable. Microsoft is one of the largest companies in the world and they can barely wipe their own ass when it comes to GitHub." aria-label="回原文"></button>。行业数据直接指向 AI 辅助编码的爆发是主要罪魁祸首：代码本身变得更多 bug，AI 活动的体量直接让底层基础设施崩溃 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="The short answer is yes. Tech downtime and software outages have demonstrably increased over the last few years, and industry data points directly to the explosion of AI-assisted coding as a primary culprit." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

开源社区也有同样的问题——用 LLM 学了点代码的人把不理解的东西推上去，达克效应加上 AI，GitHub 被AI代码淹没了 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="56:49" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="That's interesting. Yeah, it's a real mess as well because open sources have this problem as well because it's well-meaning people. They're like, oh, I learned a bit of code with an LLM." aria-label="回原文"></button>。人性就是：如果 AI 写的上一百行"大体对了"，下一百行你就不会查那么仔细 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="57:17" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It is. Because we're going, okay, look, I'll let it write the code for the last 100 lines. And it was broadly right." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## "AI 取代工作"没有经济数据支撑

OpenAI 自己发布的研究显示：AI token 支出和每位员工收入之间没有相关性 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="65:51" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="White-collar labor disruption is not happening. OpenAI had a study that came out, I think, like a week ago, that said there was no connection between spending on AI tokens and revenue per employee." aria-label="回原文"></button>。受影响的不是白领主力——是艺术总监、转录员、翻译员，而他们的老板本来就不在乎产出质量，本来就会找最便宜的方案 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="66:20" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="It's like, yeah, shit, it kind of sucks. But the people that are having their lives disrupted, work-wise, are art directors." aria-label="回原文"></button>。

真正被颠覆的是那些本来就会被廉价劳动力取代的合同工——这更像是"数字化全球化" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="79:44" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Will it replace some jobs? I mean, it's replaced some contract labor that would otherwise be replaced with cheap labor out in the global south. It's a digital globalization in that sense." aria-label="回原文"></button>。至于律师、会计师这些，你听到的永远是合伙人（高薪管理层）在说 AI 多棒，从没听到助理（真正做苦力的人）这么说 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="65:14" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="If we think about white-collar, you know, jobs, you know, lawyers and accountants, people sit here and they tell me that lawyers and accountants, the profession, right, I should say some of the skills within the" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

Ed 还指出一个术语陷阱：行业用"人工智能"这个大词，把蛋白质折叠、机器人、自主武器全混在一起，这样当你说"LLM 不能做某事"，他们就说"你忘了给我们布置作业" <button class="pd-ts" data-t="47:31" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="Yeah, and actually that's a good point to make. The reason they use the term artificial intelligence is so everyone would lump everything into it. They would lump protein folding, nothing to do with LLMs." aria-label="回原文"></button>。智能体 AI（agent）也是一样——听起来像自主的，实际上就是 LLM 和 LLM 对话，上面加个框架 <button class="pd-ts" data-t="82:58" data-who="嘉宾" data-en="if you think back through history there was someone's job just to sit in an elevator and press the buttons right that's an example of a job that humans probably shouldn't have been doing and as technology gets more advanced it takes on a lot of that sort of automated monotonous stuff right the thing is with this particular thing that i know that this is from it's a specific blog i wrote i was explicitly talking about generative ai though i was explicitly talking about people when they say this they are referring to that so you're not talking about" aria-label="回原文"></button>。

## 为什么他们能一直撑着？
